## Overview

Scott McTominay was sold by Manchester United in August 2024 for around £25m to balance the books. Ten months later he was Serie A's MVP, the scorer of 12 league goals including an overhead kick on the final day, and the man Naples had turned into a saint as [Napoli](/clubs/napoli) won their fourth Scudetto. "McFratm", as the city calls him, has become the most improbable foreign hero in Italian football since Diego Maradona himself, and he captains [Scotland](/nations/scotland) under Sébastien Pocognoli.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Lancaster, McTominay joined Manchester United's academy at five and came through as a striker before converting to midfield. José Mourinho gave him a debut in 2017 and, famously, invented an award for him. He spent seven seasons at Old Trafford as a reliable, under-appreciated midfielder, winning the 2023 League Cup and the 2024 FA Cup, and scoring the late brace against Brentford in 2023 that was one of the few highlights of Erik ten Hag's reign. He chose Scotland over England at youth level and scored seven goals in Euro 2024 qualifying.

## Peak years

Antonio Conte signed him for Napoli in summer 2024 and built a 4-3-3 and then a 3-5-2 around his late runs. He scored 12 Serie A goals, more than any Napoli midfielder in a generation, including the bicycle kick against Cagliari on the final day that sealed the title. He was named the league's MVP, the first Scot to win it, and finished in the Ballon d'Or top 20. In 2025-26, with Kevin De Bruyne arriving alongside him, Napoli were title contenders again under Conte before Inter reclaimed the championship.

## How he plays

McTominay's game is timing. He sits in the left half-space, waits for the ball to go wide and arrives in the box as the cross or cutback is played, finishing with his right foot or his head. At 1.93 m he is a huge aerial threat for a midfielder, and his shooting from the edge of the area is powerful. He covers ground, presses and tackles.

The weaknesses are in possession: his passing is functional, he is not a creator and he can be slow to turn under pressure. He needs a system that moves the ball around him rather than through him, which is exactly what Conte built.

## Evolution

A holding midfielder and occasional centre-back at United, where his attacking instincts were suppressed, then a left-sided mezzala at Napoli with licence to attack the box, which unlocked a goalscorer nobody at Old Trafford had seen. For Scotland, Steve Clarke and now Pocognoli use him as an advanced 8, and his goal record for his country reflects it.

The McTominay phenomenon in Naples is about more than goals. He arrived as a player who had been told for years that he was limited, embraced the city, learned the language quickly and played with an intensity that Napoli's supporters recognised as their own. Conte's system suited him perfectly, but the player has grown beyond it: his first touch has improved, he is more comfortable receiving under pressure, and he has started to create as well as finish. The 2025-26 season, playing alongside De Bruyne for the first time, brought a different kind of challenge, with less space in the box as defences adjusted to his runs, and he responded by varying his movement and scoring more from distance.
