## Overview

Serhou Guirassy spent a decade as a journeyman striker in France and Germany before, at 27, becoming one of the most prolific forwards in Europe. His 28 goals in 28 [Bundesliga](/leagues/bundesliga) games for Stuttgart in 2023-24 earned him a cut-price move to [Borussia Dortmund](/clubs/borussia-dortmund), where he was the Champions League's top scorer in 2024-25, including a hat-trick against Barcelona in a quarter-final Dortmund lost. He is the greatest player [Guinea](/nations/guinea) has produced since Pascal Feindouno.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Arles to Guinean parents, Guirassy came through at Laval and had spells at Lille, Auxerre, Cologne, Amiens and Rennes without ever being more than a solid Ligue 1 striker. A loan to Stuttgart in 2022-23 changed everything: 11 goals in a relegation fight, then a permanent move for around €9m, then the 28-goal season under Sebastian Hoeneß that took Stuttgart to second in the Bundesliga and the Champions League.

## Peak years

Dortmund paid around €18m in summer 2024. His first season under Nuri Şahin and then [Niko Kovač](/managers/niko-kovac) produced 34 goals in all competitions, including 13 in the Champions League, the most in the competition, and a hat-trick against Barcelona in a 3-1 second-leg win that was not enough to overturn a 4-0 first-leg deficit. He was Dortmund's top scorer again in 2025-26 as the club finished in the Champions League places. He led Guinea at the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations.

## How he plays

Guirassy is a complete penalty-box striker. He scores with his right foot, his left and his head in roughly equal measure, and his movement in the six-yard box, a check away and then across the defender, is textbook. He holds the ball up well, links with the wingers and has a reliable penalty routine. His first touch under pressure is better than most target men.

The weaknesses are pace, which was never great and is declining at 30, and pressing intensity. He is not a channel-runner and does not stretch defences in behind. His knee problems in 2024 and 2025 have occasionally disrupted his rhythm.

## Evolution

A back-up striker across Ligue 1, a relegation-fighting target man at Stuttgart, then a complete number 9 in Hoeneß's possession system and Kovač's more direct Dortmund. For Guinea, he has been the focal point since 2021.

Kovač's Dortmund are a more direct side than Hoeneß's Stuttgart were, and Guirassy has adapted by playing higher and earlier: fewer touches in build-up, more runs across the near post, more first-time finishes from the wide service of Julian Brandt and the full-backs. His conversion rate from inside the six-yard box remains among the best in Europe, and his ability to score with his head from deep crosses gives Dortmund an outlet in games where the midfield is overrun. The numbers over three seasons, roughly a goal every 90 minutes in the Bundesliga, are those of an elite striker rather than a late-career hot streak, and the fact that it took a decade for a major club to notice says more about scouting than about him.

## Current situation

Guirassy is Dortmund's first-choice striker and leading scorer in 2026-27 under Kovač, with [Karim Adeyemi](/players/karim-adeyemi) sold to Barcelona and a younger supporting cast around him. His contract runs to 2028. He remains Guinea's captain.
