## Overview

Jeremie Frimpong is what happens when a winger is asked to play full-back and refuses to stop being a winger. At [Bayer Leverkusen](/clubs/bayer-leverkusen) under [Xabi Alonso](/managers/xabi-alonso) he was the right wing-back in the side that went through the 2023-24 Bundesliga unbeaten, scoring and assisting at a rate no defender in Europe could match. [Liverpool](/clubs/liverpool) triggered his release clause in May 2025, days after [Trent Alexander-Arnold](/players/trent-alexander-arnold) confirmed he was leaving for [Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid). He is a different kind of right-back: less a passer, more a sprinter.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Amsterdam to Ghanaian parents, Frimpong moved to Manchester at seven and spent nine years in [Manchester City](/clubs/manchester-city)'s academy without a first-team appearance. [Celtic](/clubs/celtic) signed him in September 2019 for a nominal fee and he was an instant hit at Parkhead, scoring on his debut and winning the League Cup and Premiership in his first season. Leverkusen paid around €11m in January 2021.

## Peak years

Frimpong improved every year in Germany. Gerardo Seoane used him as a right-back; Alonso, who arrived in October 2022, made him a wing-back in a 3-4-2-1 with a licence to behave like a forward. The result was the 2023-24 season: 14 goals and 12 assists in all competitions, the league title without a defeat, the DFB-Pokal, and a run to the Europa League final. He was named in the Bundesliga team of the season and committed to the [Netherlands](/nations/netherlands) after a long youth career with the Dutch, playing at [Euro 2024](/tournaments/euro). His 2024-25 was slightly quieter as Leverkusen finished second, but his release clause of roughly £30m looked like a bargain and Liverpool paid it.

## How he plays

Frimpong starts high and wide, often level with the striker when his team has the ball. His first instinct is to run: diagonally in behind the full-back onto a through ball, or straight down the line onto a switch of play. He arrives at the byline faster than defenders can recover and his cut-back is low and accurate. He also finishes: he times runs to the far post for crosses from the left, which is where many of his Leverkusen goals came from.

He is 1.71 m and not a dominant header of the ball, so he is a liability in aerial duels at the back post, and his positional play in a back four has had to be learned. He is not a passer in the Alexander-Arnold mould; his crosses from deep are serviceable rather than special. His one-v-one defending is better than his reputation, because his recovery pace erases mistakes, and his stamina lets him repeat sprints for 90 minutes.

## Evolution

He was a right winger in City's academy, a conventional right-back at Celtic, and Alonso's wing-back at Leverkusen. Arne Slot's Liverpool in 2025-26 played a back four, so Frimpong was used as an attacking right-back and at times as a right winger when Liverpool wanted to push on, with Conor Bradley competing for the defensive version of the role. [Andoni Iraola](/managers/andoni-iraola), who replaced Slot in June 2026, favours aggressive full-backs who press high and attack the box, which fits Frimpong well.

## Current situation

Frimpong's 2026-27 role at Liverpool is described here as of the 2025-26 season: an attacking right-back competing with Conor Bradley, with his best use as a transition weapon alongside [Mohamed Salah](/players/mohamed-salah)'s successor on the right. Liverpool drew 2-2 at Newcastle on the opening day of 2026-27 and beat Ipswich 2-0 away, but sat outside the top five after nine games under Iraola. With the Netherlands under Ronald Koeman he competes with Denzel Dumfries for the right-sided role.
