## Overview

Joshua Kimmich has been at the centre of [Bayern Munich](/clubs/bayern-munich) for more than a decade, through nine [Bundesliga](/leagues/bundesliga) titles, the 2020 treble and a long argument about whether he is a right-back or a midfielder. He is both, and he has captained [Germany](/nations/germany) since 2024, including through the early exit at the 2026 World Cup that brought [Jürgen Klopp](/managers/jurgen-klopp) to the national team. At 31 he remains Bayern's on-pitch leader under [Vincent Kompany](/managers/vincent-kompany).

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Rottweil, Kimmich came through VfB Stuttgart's academy before moving to [RB Leipzig](/clubs/rb-leipzig) in 2013, where he played two seasons in the third and second tiers. Bayern signed him in 2015 for around €8m, and [Pep Guardiola](/managers/pep-guardiola) used him everywhere in his first season: centre-back, right-back, midfield. He debuted for Germany in 2016 and played every game at Euro 2016 as a right-back.

Under Carlo Ancelotti and Jupp Heynckes he became Philipp Lahm's successor at right-back, the best in Europe in that position by 2018. Under Hansi Flick in 2019-20 he moved into midfield permanently and Bayern won the treble.

## Peak years

The Flick era was his peak: 2019-20 and 2020-21 saw him as the Bundesliga's best midfielder, partnering Leon Goretzka in a double pivot, scoring the chipped winner at Dortmund in May 2020 that effectively decided the title, and starting the Champions League final against PSG. Germany, however, were a different story: group-stage exits at the 2018 and 2022 World Cups and a Euro 2024 quarter-final defeat on home soil, where Julian Nagelsmann moved him back to right-back.

He was named Germany captain in autumn 2024 and led the side to the Nations League finals in 2025. The 2026 World Cup ended in the round of 32 against Paraguay on penalties.

## How he plays

Kimmich's passing is the first thing. From the base of midfield he plays the driven, low diagonal to the far winger better than any German since Toni Kroos, and from right-back his early crosses, delivered before the full-back has set, were the source of dozens of Lewandowski goals. He is a superb set-piece taker from the right. Defensively he is tenacious, with excellent timing in the tackle for a player of his size.

He is not tall and loses aerial duels, and his lack of pace means he can be exposed in transition when he steps up. His intensity sometimes spills into dissent. He has been criticised, fairly or not, as the symbol of Germany's tournament disappointments.

## Evolution

Guardiola's utility man, then Lahm's heir at right-back, then Flick's 6, then back to right-back under Nagelsmann for Germany and under Kompany at times when the squad needed it. In 2025-26 and 2026-27 Kompany has used him mostly as the deepest midfielder, with licence to step into the right half-space.

## Best plays and moments

- 2016, Euro 2016, with Germany: a starter at right-back at 21.
- 2017, Confederations Cup final, v Chile: a winner's medal.
- 2020, Bundesliga, Dortmund 0-1 Bayern: the chip over Bürki that decided the title ([watch](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Kimmich+chip+Dortmund+2020+Burki)).
- 2020, Champions League final, v PSG: the treble in Lisbon.
- 2024, Euro 2024, with Germany: a return to right-back and a quarter-final.
- 2024, Bundesliga: named Germany captain.
- 2026, DFB-Pokal: a fourth cup winner's medal in Bayern's double season.

## Current situation

Kimmich is Bayern's vice-captain and starting holding midfielder in 2026-27, with a contract to 2029 signed in early 2025. For Germany he remains captain under Klopp, who has retained him as the senior figure in a rebuilt squad after the 2026 World Cup exit and [Manuel Neuer](/players/manuel-neuer)'s international retirement.
