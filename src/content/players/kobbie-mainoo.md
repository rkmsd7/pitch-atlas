## Overview

Kobbie Mainoo was the best news [Manchester United](/clubs/manchester-united) had in a bleak period. Born in Stockport and raised in the club's academy, he broke into the team in November 2023 as an 18-year-old, scored the goal that won the 2024 FA Cup final against Manchester City, and started the [Euro 2024](/tournaments/euro) final for [England](/nations/england) seven months after his first Premier League start. The two seasons since have been harder, with injury and a loss of status under Ruben Amorim, but his revival under [Michael Carrick](/managers/michael-carrick) and a five-year contract signed on 30 April 2026 have made him the centrepiece of United's rebuild.

## Early life and breakthrough

Mainoo joined United's academy at six and captained the Under-18s to the FA Youth Cup in 2022. Erik ten Hag gave him a debut in the League Cup in January 2023 and he impressed on the 2023 pre-season tour, but an ankle injury delayed his Premier League start until November 2023 at Everton, where he was man of the match. From there he was a fixture: a stoppage-time winner at Wolves in February 2024, a first England cap in March and a role in the FA Cup win. Gareth Southgate started him in a Euro 2024 side that reached the final, where he was one of England's better players against Spain.

## Peak years

The 2024-25 season was a comedown. He scored on the opening day against Fulham and was still a regular, but injuries interrupted him, Amorim arrived in November with a 3-4-2-1 that used him as a No.10 and then not at all, and United finished 15th with a Europa League final defeat. Transfer talk grew through 2025, and by the autumn he had lost his place entirely. The turning point was Amorim's sacking in January 2026: Carrick restored a midfield shape that suited him, Mainoo's form returned, and United climbed into the Champions League places. In April director of football Jason Wilcox called him "one of the most naturally gifted young footballers in the world" as he signed to 2031. He went to the 2026 World Cup, where England finished third.

## How he plays

Mainoo's gift is composure under pressure. He receives with his back to a defender, takes the ball on the half-turn and glides away with a drop of the shoulder and a quick change of direction, so that the press runs past him. He then plays simply: a short pass, a one-two, a carry of ten yards. In the final third he times his arrival at the edge of the box well and finishes with a quiet, side-footed strike, as at Wembley in 2024.

What he lacks is physical dominance. He is not a tackler, his recovery pace is ordinary and he can be bypassed when a team plays through United quickly. His passing range is modest: he rarely switches play or plays through balls, and his output of goals and assists has been low for a midfielder with his technique. The question for Carrick is whether he can become a controlling No.8 or whether he needs a destroyer beside him.

## Evolution

In the academy he was a deep-lying midfielder. Ten Hag used him as one of two No.8s in a 4-2-3-1, alongside Casemiro or Christian Eriksen. Amorim tried him as a No.10 in a 3-4-2-1 and then as a wing-back-adjacent midfielder before dropping him. Carrick has used him as the left-sided midfielder in a double pivot or three, with [Andrey Santos](/players/andrey-santos) or Youri Tielemans alongside and [Bruno Fernandes](/players/bruno-fernandes) ahead, a structure that lets him receive from the centre-backs and carry.

## Current situation

Mainoo's contract was the feel-good story of United's spring; the autumn has been less kind. United made their worst start to a league season in 40 years and were knocked out of the League Cup by Brighton, Carrick's position came under scrutiny and the midfield has been rotated constantly. Mainoo remains a regular starter and the academy's flagship, and for England under [Thomas Tuchel](/managers/thomas-tuchel) he is in the squad picture behind [Declan Rice](/players/declan-rice) and [Jude Bellingham](/players/jude-bellingham). He wears No.37, the number he has kept since his debut.
