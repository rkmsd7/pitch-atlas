## Overview

Marcus Thuram arrived at [Inter Milan](/clubs/inter-milan) in 2023 on a free transfer from Borussia Mönchengladbach as a winger-turned-striker with a decent Bundesliga record and a famous surname. Within a season he was half of the best strike partnership in Italy, with [Lautaro Martínez](/players/lautaro-martinez), and a Serie A champion. Two Scudetti and a Champions League final later, he is one of the most complete centre-forwards in Europe and a [France](/nations/france) regular under [Zinedine Zidane](/managers/zinedine-zidane-manager).

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Parma while his father Lilian was playing there, Thuram grew up in Italy and France and came through at Sochaux, debuting in Ligue 2 at 17. Guingamp signed him in 2017 and he scored 15 goals in two seasons as a winger. Gladbach paid around €12m in 2019 and under Marco Rose he scored 10 in his first Bundesliga season, then converted to a striker under Adi Hütter and Daniel Farke. He made his France debut in 2020 and played in the 2022 World Cup final as a substitute.

## Peak years

Simone Inzaghi signed him for Inter on a free in 2023 and paired him with Lautaro. He scored 15 goals and set up 13 as Inter won the 2023-24 title by 19 points, with a derby goal against Milan and an all-round game that made him the perfect foil. In 2024-25 he was Inter's top scorer in the Champions League on the run to the final, scoring against Bayern and in the semi-final against Barcelona. In 2025-26, under [Cristian Chivu](/managers/cristian-chivu), Inter won the Scudetto again.

For France, he started games at Euro 2024 and the 2026 World Cup, where France lost the semi-final to Spain and then the third-place match 6-4 to England.

## How he plays

Thuram is a striker who still thinks like a winger. He runs the channels at a speed unusual for a 1.92 m forward, holds the ball up with his frame and long first touch, and lays off for Lautaro or the arriving midfielders. His headed goals from the far post are a feature, and his finishing with the right foot is clean. He is an unselfish player whose assist numbers reflect his role.

The weaknesses are finishing consistency, a left foot that is rarely used, and occasional periods of invisibility in games where Inter are pinned back. His pressing is good rather than relentless.

## Evolution

A winger at Guingamp and in his first Gladbach season, a striker from 2021, a second striker who does the running for Lautaro at Inter, and for France a wide forward or substitute striker under Deschamps and Zidane. Chivu has occasionally played him as the lone 9 with Lautaro dropping.

Thuram's growth at Inter has been as much mental as technical. He arrived with a reputation for inconsistency and became, within a season, a player Inzaghi trusted to start every big match. His understanding with Lautaro is now instinctive: when one drops, the other runs; when one goes wide, the other attacks the space. He has added a second phase to his pressing, closing the centre-back and then the goalkeeper in a single movement, and his hold-up play has improved to the point that Inter can play long to him under pressure. For France, where he was a squad player under Deschamps, Zidane has given him a starting role, and his early performances in the 2026-27 Nations League suggested he intends to keep it.

## Current situation

Thuram is Chivu's first-choice striker alongside Lautaro in 2026-27, with a contract to 2028, and a starter for France under Zidane after the 2026 World Cup.
