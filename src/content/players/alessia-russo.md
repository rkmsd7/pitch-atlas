## Overview

Alessia Russo is England's first-choice striker and the focal point of the [Arsenal](/clubs/arsenal-women) side that won the 2025 [Champions League](/tournaments/womens-champions-league) and the 2026 FIFA Women's Champions Cup. Twice a European champion with the [Lionesses](/nations/england-women), she scored the equaliser in the Euro 2025 final against Spain and was named England Player of the Year for 2025-26 in October 2026 after moving into the top ten of the country's all-time scorers.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Maidstone to a family of Italian descent, Russo played for Charlton and Chelsea's youth teams before a short spell at Brighton and a college career at North Carolina, where she won two ACC titles. She signed for [Manchester United](/clubs/manchester-united-women) in 2020 and scored 22 league goals in three seasons while battling injuries, becoming an England regular under Sarina Wiegman.

## Peak years

Euro 2022 made her famous: four goals as a substitute, including the outrageous back-heel nutmeg against Sweden in the semi-final. A year later she started the World Cup final against Spain, having turned down a British-record bid from Arsenal in January 2023 before joining on a free that summer. At Arsenal she has grown into the elite: 12 goals and the FWA award in 2024-25, a Champions League final win over Barcelona in Lisbon, and in 2025-26 a hat-trick inside 30 minutes in a 5-2 north London derby, Arsenal's player of the season award and an MBE in June 2026. The Euro 2025 final equaliser, a header from Chloe Kelly's cross, was her defining international goal.

## How she plays

Russo is a number nine who does the unglamorous things brilliantly: holding up long balls, pinning centre-backs, occupying two defenders so wingers can isolate full-backs. She drops to receive and spins into the channel, and her first touch is the best among English strikers. In the box she is a strong header of the ball and a two-footed finisher, and under Renée Slegers at Arsenal she has added a poacher's positioning that lifted her goal count. The criticism, that she does not score enough for her chances, has faded since 2024.

## Evolution

An attacking midfielder at college, she became a pure striker at United under Casey Stoney, then a link striker at Arsenal where Jonas Eidevall used her with Stina Blackstenius. Under Slegers she is the undisputed nine with Mariona Caldentey and Kim Little feeding her. For England, Wiegman moved her from substitute in 2022 to starter in 2023 and she has played every game since.

## Best plays and moments

- Back-heel against Sweden in the Euro 2022 semi-final, Sheffield.
- Starting the 2023 World Cup final against Spain in Sydney.
- Winning goal contribution in the 2025 Champions League final campaign and the Lisbon final itself.
- Equaliser in the Euro 2025 final, Basel, July 2025.
- 30-minute hat-trick in Arsenal's 5-2 win over Tottenham, 28 March 2026.

## Legacy / Current situation

As of November 2026 Russo is Arsenal's leader and England's striker heading into the [2027 World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) play-offs against Ukraine in December. Still 27, she is approaching 40 international goals and a place among the Lionesses' greatest forwards.
