## Overview

Alexander Isak is the striker [Liverpool](/clubs/liverpool) paid a British record £125m for in 2025, and the player on whom [Andoni Iraola](/managers/andoni-iraola)'s 2026-27 side depends for goals. Long, elegant and quick, he had already proved at [Newcastle United](/clubs/newcastle-united) that he could score in every way a Premier League centre-forward needs to, and he arrived at Anfield as the most coveted No.9 in Europe. His first season was disrupted by the saga around his transfer and by fitness problems; his second has started with goals.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Solna to Eritrean parents, Isak came through AIK's academy and scored on his senior debut in 2016 aged 16. A year later he was Sweden's youngest ever international goalscorer, and [Borussia Dortmund](/clubs/borussia-dortmund) signed him for around €9m in January 2017, with the inevitable comparisons to Zlatan Ibrahimović. Dortmund was too early: he made only a handful of appearances before a loan to Willem II in the Netherlands in 2019, where 13 goals in 16 games rebuilt his confidence. [Real Sociedad](/clubs/real-sociedad) paid a reported €6.5m that summer, and over three seasons in San Sebastián he developed into a complete forward, scoring 17 league goals in 2020-21 and winning the Copa del Rey.

## Peak years

Newcastle paid a then club-record £63m in August 2022 and scored on his debut at Anfield. After an injury-hit first season he hit 21 league goals in 2023-24 and 23 in 2024-25, when he was the division's second-highest scorer behind [Mohamed Salah](/players/mohamed-salah) and scored in the Carabao Cup final win over Liverpool, the club's first domestic trophy in 70 years. That summer he made it clear he wanted to leave, sat out the start of 2025-26, and joined Liverpool on deadline day. The fee eclipsed Chelsea's purchase of Moisés Caicedo as the most expensive in British football until Enzo Fernández's move to Manchester City equalled it in 2026.

## How he plays

Isak's game begins with his first touch. He receives with his back to goal or on the half-turn, kills the ball dead and is already facing the defender before the defender has set himself. From there he carries at a pace that belies his height, using long, loping strides and a low centre of gravity for a man of 1.92 m, and he loves to drift toward the left channel where he can open his body and shoot across the keeper with his right foot.

His finishing is calm rather than explosive: side-footed placement, early shots before the goalkeeper is set, and a willingness to use his left foot when the angle demands it. He is a reliable penalty taker. In the build-up he offers subtle lay-offs and third-man runs rather than old-fashioned hold-up play; he is not a target man and should not be used as one. He presses intelligently in a structured system, arcing his runs to show play one way rather than sprinting blindly.

The weaknesses are durability, with muscle injuries interrupting most of his seasons, and aerial presence, which is modest for his size. He can also disappear in games where the supply from wide areas is poor.

## Evolution

At AIK and in his Dortmund cameo he was a raw runner. Real Sociedad under Imanol Alguacil taught him to play in a possession team, to come short, combine and finish moves rather than only start them. Eddie Howe's Newcastle made him the focal point of a fast, vertical side, with [Anthony Gordon](/players/anthony-gordon) and [Bruno Guimarães](/players/bruno-guimaraes) feeding his runs. At Liverpool under Arne Slot he was asked to lead the line in a more patient possession side, and under Iraola in 2026-27 he is the reference point in a 4-2-3-1 that presses high and attacks quickly, with [Florian Wirtz](/players/florian-wirtz) behind him and [Bradley Barcola](/players/bradley-barcola) arriving from the left.

## Current situation

Isak's first Liverpool season, 2025-26, was fitful: he arrived short of match fitness after a summer of standing off, Liverpool finished fifth, well behind champions [Arsenal](/clubs/arsenal) and [Manchester City](/clubs/manchester-city), and Slot was replaced by Iraola in June 2026. The new manager publicly challenged Isak and Wirtz to raise their levels in his first pre-season, and Isak has responded, with roughly six Premier League goals from seven starts by late October 2026 despite a short injury absence in September. With [Bradley Barcola](/players/bradley-barcola) signed for €125m and Salah gone, he is now the senior forward. For [Sweden](/nations/sweden) he remains the main striker alongside Viktor Gyökeres, a partnership that kept the national team in the conversation through the 2026 World Cup qualifying cycle.
