## Overview

Sophia Wilson, who played as Sophia Smith until her marriage to Michael Wilson in January 2025, is the most dynamic attacker in the [United States](/nations/united-states-women) squad. The first pick in the 2020 NWSL draft, she was league MVP and Championship MVP in 2022 at 22, won the Golden Boot in 2023, and formed the "Triple Espresso" front line with [Trinity Rodman](/players/trinity-rodman) and [Mallory Swanson](/players/mallory-swanson) that won Olympic gold in Paris in 2024. After missing 2025 on maternity leave, she returned in March 2026 and scored a career-best 15 goals for the [Portland Thorns](/clubs/portland-thorns).

## Early life and breakthrough

From Windsor, Colorado, Smith was a prodigy who won an NCAA title at Stanford in 2019 and left college early to be drafted by Portland. She scored on her international debut in November 2020 and by 2022 was the Thorns' and the national team's best forward.

## Peak years

The 2022 season was close to perfect: 14 league goals, the NWSL MVP, the opening goal of the Championship final against Kansas City and the US Soccer Player of the Year award. The 2023 World Cup brought two goals against Vietnam but a round-of-16 exit, and under Emma Hayes she found her best international form at Paris 2024, scoring in the semi-final against Germany in extra time and starting the 1-0 final win over Brazil. Her daughter was born in September 2025. On 6 March 2026 she returned in a 5-1 Concacaf win over Monterrey; her first goal since November 2024 came against Angel City on 26 April; and she finished the 2026 regular season third in the Golden Boot race despite a two-match suspension in October for pulling an opponent's hair.

## How she plays

Wilson is a one-v-one specialist. She takes the ball on the half-turn, facing the defender, and uses a short burst of acceleration to go past on either side, then finishes with a low shot into the far corner. She likes to start wide left and drift inside, but Hayes has used her centrally, where her pressing and runs behind suit a [high line](/tactics/high-line). She is less effective with her back to goal than Alessia Russo-style strikers and relies on being supplied in space. Her 2026 season added more goals from crosses and rebounds.

## Evolution

A winger at Stanford, she became a striker in Portland under Rhian Wilkinson and a central forward for Hayes. Motherhood and a year out have, by her own account, made her more efficient: fewer touches, more shots.

## Best plays and moments

- Goal and MVP, 2022 NWSL Championship, Portland 2-0 Kansas City.
- NWSL Golden Boot 2023 despite injury.
- Extra-time winner against Germany in the Paris 2024 Olympic semi-final.
- Olympic gold, Paris, 10 August 2024.
- First goal back from maternity leave against Angel City, 26 April 2026, and a 15-goal 2026 season.

## Legacy / Current situation

As of November 2026 Wilson is a Portland star and a USWNT regular with 65 caps and 26 goals, preparing for the Concacaf W Championship qualifiers in late November and the [2027 World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) in Brazil.
