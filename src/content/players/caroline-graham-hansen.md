## Overview

Caroline Graham Hansen is the dribbler of her generation. The [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona-women) right winger has won four [Champions Leagues](/tournaments/womens-champions-league) and seven consecutive [Liga F](/leagues/liga-f) titles, was runner-up to [Aitana Bonmatí](/players/aitana-bonmati) in the 2024 Ballon d'Or, and is [Norway](/nations/norway-women)'s creative heart with 127 caps and 54 goals. Her fifth Gullballen, the Norwegian player of the year award, came in 2024.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Oslo, she debuted for Stabæk at 15 and for Norway at 16, and was a star of the 2013 Euro, where Norway reached the final. After a brief spell at Tyresö she joined [Wolfsburg](/clubs/wolfsburg-women) at 19, winning three Bundesligas and five DFB-Pokals in five years and reaching the 2016 and 2018 Champions League finals.

## Peak years

Barcelona signed her in 2019 and she became the right side of the most feared front three in women's football. She assisted in the 2021 final win over Chelsea, scored in the 2023 semi-final against Chelsea and the 2024 final against Lyon in Bilbao, and in 2023-24 produced 20 goals and 20 assists in the league. In 2025-26 she won a fourth Champions League in Oslo, in her home city, and a seventh league. For Norway, she scored against Slovenia in April 2026 and remains the first name on Gemma Grainger's team sheet.

## How she plays

Graham Hansen is left-footed but plays on the right, which gives her two options every time she receives: the outside with her weaker foot, where her acceleration takes her past full-backs, or the inside onto her left, where she curls shots or plays through balls. Her dribbling is built on tiny touches that keep the ball under her body, so that defenders cannot commit. She is a superb crosser, especially the inswinging delivery to the back post, and a creator of chances for others as much as a scorer. Her pressing is intelligent rather than relentless, and injuries, including a heart arrhythmia diagnosed in 2021, have been managed carefully.

## Evolution

A central playmaker in Norway's youth sides, she became a winger at Wolfsburg and the complete wide creator at Barcelona under Lluís Cortés and Jonatan Giráldez. Under Pere Romeu, and now wearing the number 10 shirt, she has freedom to drift inside and run the game.

## Best plays and moments

- Euro 2013 final appearance at 18.
- Three Bundesliga titles and the 2016 and 2018 Champions League finals with Wolfsburg.
- Assist-laden 2021 Champions League final, Barcelona 4-0 Chelsea.
- Goal in the 2024 Champions League final, Barcelona 2-0 Lyon, Bilbao, and 20 goals plus 20 assists in Liga F that season.
- Fourth Champions League, in Oslo, 23 May 2026.

## Legacy / Current situation

As of November 2026 Graham Hansen, 31, is a Barcelona starter and Norway's key player for the December play-off against Wales that could send her to a first [World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) since 2019.
