## Overview

Michael Olise walked into [Bayern Munich](/clubs/bayern-munich) in 2024 as a €60m signing from Crystal Palace and within a season was the [Bundesliga](/leagues/bundesliga)'s most creative player. He has won two titles and a cup in Munich, supplied a large share of [Harry Kane](/players/harry-kane)'s record 73 goals in 2025-26 and became a [France](/nations/france) regular after choosing Les Bleus over England, Nigeria and Algeria. He plays as if the game were slower for him than for everyone else.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Hammersmith to a Nigerian father and a French-Algerian mother, Olise was in Arsenal's, Chelsea's and Manchester City's academies before joining Reading at 16. He debuted in the Championship at 17 in 2019 and in 2020-21 was the division's Young Player of the Year with seven goals and 12 assists. Crystal Palace paid around £8m in July 2021.

At Palace, first under Patrick Vieira and then Oliver Glasner, he was the Premier League's most watchable winger outside the top six, scoring 10 goals in 19 league games in the second half of 2023-24 despite hamstring problems. He chose France in 2024, won Olympic silver in Paris that summer as the tournament's standout player, and moved to Bayern in July 2024.

## Peak years

Olise's first Bayern season under [Vincent Kompany](/managers/vincent-kompany) produced 20 goals and 20 assists in all competitions, a league title and the Newcomer of the Season award. His second, 2025-26, was better: the double, a Champions League quarter-final, and the role of chief supplier to Kane. He made his senior France debut in 2025 and was part of Didier Deschamps's 2026 World Cup squad that lost the semi-final to Spain.

## How he plays

Olise plays slowly on purpose. He receives on the right touchline, waits, invites the full-back to commit, then uses a drop of the shoulder and a change of pace to go inside onto his left foot. From there he has three options and uses all of them: the curled shot into the far corner, the clipped cross to the back post, or the through-ball to the overlapping full-back. His set-pieces, especially inswinging corners from the right, are a major source of Bayern's goals.

His weaknesses are the ones you would expect of a player who does not sprint much: he is not a threat in behind, his pressing is selective, and in games where Bayern do not control possession he can be peripheral. Hamstring injuries in 2023 and 2024 are a concern that has receded.

## Evolution

Reading played him as a 10 and a right winger. Vieira and Glasner used him as an inverted right winger with freedom to drift central. Kompany has made him the right-sided of two 10s in a 4-2-3-1 that becomes a 3-2-5, with Konrad Laimer or Josip Stanišić overlapping. For France, [Zinedine Zidane](/managers/zinedine-zidane-manager) has used him on the right since July 2026, with [Kylian Mbappé](/players/kylian-mbappe) central.

## Current situation

Olise is a cornerstone of Kompany's 2026-27 Bayern, the right-sided creator in a front four with Kane, [Luis Díaz](/players/luis-diaz) and [Jamal Musiala](/players/jamal-musiala). His contract runs to 2029. For France, he is a starter under Zidane in the first season of the post-Deschamps era.
