## Overview

Yan Diomande is the fastest-rising young player in European football. In the summer of 2025 he was a teenager at Leganés with a handful of Segunda División and La Liga games; by the summer of 2026 [Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid) had paid [RB Leipzig](/clubs/rb-leipzig) around €125m for him, one of the biggest fees in history. He is 19, Ivorian, left-footed and plays on the right, which makes him the natural complement to [Vinícius Júnior](/players/vinicius-junior) and [Kylian Mbappé](/players/kylian-mbappe) in [José Mourinho](/managers/jose-mourinho)'s rebuilt attack.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Abidjan, Diomande came to Spain as a youth player and joined Leganés's academy. He debuted for the first team in 2024-25, the club's first season back in [La Liga](/leagues/la-liga), and his performances off the bench, all acceleration and long-striding carries, were enough for Leipzig to sign him in summer 2025 for a fee reported at around €20m.

In Leipzig, under Ole Werner, he was a regular from the autumn, scoring in the [Bundesliga](/leagues/bundesliga) and producing the kind of highlight-reel dribbles that go round the world in a day. By spring 2026 the clubs with the biggest budgets in Europe were circling, and Real Madrid moved decisively after Mourinho's appointment.

## Peak years

It is far too early to speak of peak years, but the 2025-26 season at Leipzig was the breakthrough that set the fee. He played on both wings, beat defenders at a rate few players in Europe matched, and scored enough to make the numbers match the eye test. His first months at Real Madrid in 2026-27 have been a mix of starts and substitute appearances as Mourinho manages the expectations around him.

## How he plays

Diomande is a left-footed right winger in the modern inverted mould, but his threat is not the curled shot so much as the carry. He receives on the touchline, takes a big first touch past the full-back and uses pure acceleration to get to the byline. From there he either cuts a low ball back or drives inside for a shot. He is strong for a winger and holds off challenges at speed.

The weaknesses are what you would expect at 19: the decision at the end of the run is often wrong, he over-dribbles when a pass is on, and his finishing is inconsistent. He is also a work in progress defensively, which matters less under Mourinho than it would under a pressing coach.

## Evolution

At Leganés he was an impact substitute. At Leipzig he was given a starting role in a counter-pressing 4-2-2-2 and 4-4-2, often as a wide forward. At Real Madrid Mourinho has started him on the right of a 4-2-3-1, asking him to stay wide and give Mbappé and Vinícius the central and left-sided space they prefer. For [Ivory Coast](/nations/ivory-coast), he is already a senior international and part of the 2026 World Cup squad.
