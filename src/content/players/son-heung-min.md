## Overview

Son Heung-min spent ten years at [Tottenham Hotspur](/clubs/tottenham-hotspur) and left as one of the club's greatest players: 173 goals, the 2021-22 Golden Boot shared with [Mohamed Salah](/players/mohamed-salah), a Puskás Award, and the captaincy that lifted the Europa League in May 2025, Spurs' first trophy in 17 years. In August 2025 he announced he was leaving, and Los Angeles FC made him the most expensive signing in [MLS](/leagues/mls) history. He is the record scorer and captain of [South Korea](/nations/south-korea), and played at the 2026 World Cup in North America at 33.

## Early life and breakthrough

Son was born in Chuncheon and coached relentlessly by his father, a former professional, who had him practise technique for hours before allowing him to play matches. He joined Hamburg's academy at 16, made his Bundesliga debut at 18 in 2010 and scored 12 goals in 2012-13. Bayer Leverkusen paid €10m in 2013 and he scored 29 goals in two seasons there, including a Champions League hat-trick, before Tottenham paid £22m in August 2015, a record for an Asian player.

## Peak years

A difficult first season under Mauricio Pochettino gave way to a decade of excellence. Son and [Harry Kane](/players/harry-kane) became the most prolific partnership in Premier League history, with Son scoring in double figures in the league every season from 2016-17 onwards. He scored the goal against Burnley in December 2019 that won the Puskás Award, running from his own half; reached the 2019 Champions League final; shared the 2021-22 Golden Boot with 23 goals, none of them penalties; and became captain in 2023 after Kane left. Under Ange Postecoglou he led the club through two volatile seasons and lifted the Europa League in Bilbao after beating Manchester United 1-0. For South Korea he won the 2018 Asian Games, which exempted him from military service, and played at three World Cups before the fourth in 2026.

## How he plays

Son's two-footedness is the foundation. From the left he can cut inside onto his right foot or go outside onto his left, and he finishes equally well with either, which makes him impossible to show one way. His finishing is early and low, often across the goalkeeper from the edge of the box, and he scores a disproportionate number of goals from fast breaks, where his acceleration onto a through ball and his composure at speed are decisive.

He presses hard, tracks back and has never been a player who sulks when the game turns against him. His weaknesses were always a modest aerial game and a creative output that was good rather than exceptional; late in his Spurs career the pace dropped and the goals became more about movement in the box than running from deep. In MLS his assists have outstripped his goals.

## Evolution

At Hamburg and Leverkusen he was a striker or wide forward in a counter-attacking team. Pochettino converted him into an inside forward on the left of a 4-2-3-1, occasionally using him as a striker. Under José Mourinho and Antonio Conte he was a pure transition weapon alongside Kane; Postecoglou pushed him to a more central, striker-like role and gave him the captaincy. At LAFC under Steve Cherundolo he plays as a left-sided forward in a 4-3-3, with more creative responsibility than he carried in England.

## Best plays and moments

- 2015, Champions League with Leverkusen: a hat-trick against Zenit in the group stage.
- 2018, Asian Games final v Japan: captains South Korea to gold.
- 2019, Champions League quarter-final v Manchester City: two goals in a 4-3 defeat that sends Spurs through on away goals.
- 2019, Premier League v Burnley: the Puskás-winning solo goal from his own half ([watch](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Son+Heung-min+Puskas+goal+Burnley+2019)).
- 2020, Premier League v Southampton: four goals, all assisted by Kane, in a 5-2 win.
- 2022, Premier League v Leicester City: a 13-minute hat-trick off the bench in a 6-2 win.
- 2022, Premier League final day v Norwich: two goals to clinch a share of the Golden Boot.
- 2025, Europa League final v Manchester United, Bilbao: lifts the trophy as captain ([watch](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Son+Heung-min+Europa+League+final+2025+Tottenham)).
- 2025, MLS debut for LAFC: an instant impact in a club-record deal ([LAFC highlights](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Son+Heung-min+LAFC+goals+assists)).

## Current situation

Son's first full MLS season in 2026 was unusual: LAFC started with four straight clean-sheet wins, Son went 237 days without a goal around the World Cup before scoring against the LA Galaxy in July, and by early November he had seven goals and 15 assists in the league, leading the assist charts, plus two goals and seven assists in the CONCACAF Champions Cup. LAFC secured a playoff place with a derby draw in late October and ended the regular season with a 3-1 home defeat to Minnesota on 2 November. He wears No.7. He captained South Korea at the 2026 World Cup, and his international future after the tournament is not confirmed on this page.
