## Overview

João Neves is 1.74 m and plays like he is two metres tall. Signed from [Benfica](/clubs/benfica) in August 2024 for around €60m at 19, he was a starter in [Luis Enrique](/managers/luis-enrique)'s midfield within weeks and has since won two [Ligue 1](/leagues/ligue-1) titles and two Champions Leagues, the second after a season in which he was among the most-used players in the squad. He wears 87 in honour of his year-group of friends and has become one of the most popular players at [Paris Saint-Germain](/clubs/paris-saint-germain).

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Tavira in the Algarve, Neves joined Benfica's academy at 12 and debuted at 17 in 2022 under Roger Schmidt. He was a regular in Benfica's 2022-23 title-winning side and the following season was the best young midfielder in Portugal, earning his first senior cap in September 2023. PSG won the race for him in summer 2024.

## Peak years

His first PSG season produced a domestic double and the club's first Champions League. He scored a bicycle kick against Toulouse, a brace against Lyon, and was a constant in the knockout run, including the 5-0 final win over Inter. His headed goal in the 2025 Nations League final against Spain was a key moment in Portugal's shoot-out win. In 2025-26 he was again central as PSG won the league and retained the Champions League against Arsenal in Budapest.

## How he plays

Neves's defining trait is intensity. He presses from midfield with the energy of a forward, tackles with precision despite his size and wins second balls through anticipation. In possession he is tidy and progressive, carrying the ball through pressure and playing quick forward passes. He arrives late in the box and, improbably, scores headers.

The weaknesses are physical and creative. He can be bullied by bigger midfielders in aerial duels and lacks the long passing range of a classic deep playmaker. His shooting from distance is a work in progress. Occasionally his intensity tips into rashness and cards.

## Evolution

Schmidt used him as a 6 and an 8 at Benfica. Luis Enrique uses him as the right-sided 8 in a 4-3-3 with [Vitinha](/players/vitinha) at the base, with freedom to press high and to drift wide to overlap [Achraf Hakimi](/players/achraf-hakimi). For [Portugal](/nations/portugal), Roberto Martínez and now Jorge Jesus have used him as a box-to-box midfielder next to Vitinha.

What separates Neves from other energetic young midfielders is the cleanliness of his technique under pressure. Luis Enrique's PSG play through the middle of the pitch against the best presses in Europe, and Neves's ability to receive on the half-turn, take one touch and play forward is what keeps the ball moving when opponents try to trap it. He also reads the second phase of a press well, stepping into the space behind the first wave to receive the pass out. His running statistics are consistently among the highest in the Champions League, and his tackle success rate is unusual for a player who competes in so many duels. Coaches speak about his attitude as much as his ability; he was training at full intensity within days of arriving in Paris and has barely missed a match since.

## Best plays and moments

## Current situation

Neves is a starter in Luis Enrique's 2026-27 midfield, with a contract to 2029, in a PSG squad reshaped by Bradley Barcola's sale and [Ferran Torres](/players/ferran-torres)'s arrival. For Portugal he is a regular under Jorge Jesus.
