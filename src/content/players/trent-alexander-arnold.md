## Overview

Trent Alexander-Arnold changed what a right-back could be. A Scouser from West Derby who came through [Liverpool](/clubs/liverpool)'s academy, he became the creative hub of [Jürgen Klopp](/managers/jurgen-klopp)'s great side from the right-back position, setting assist records for a defender, winning the Champions League and two Premier League titles, and taking the quickly-taken corner against Barcelona in 2019 that became one of Anfield's defining images. In June 2025, with his contract expiring, he left for [Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid), where he wears No.12 with "Trent" on his shirt. His second season there, under [José Mourinho](/managers/jose-mourinho), has been a struggle for minutes.

## Early life and breakthrough

Alexander-Arnold joined Liverpool at six and was a midfielder for most of his youth career. Klopp gave him a debut in October 2016 in the League Cup and his first Premier League start that December; by 2017-18 he was first choice, playing in the Champions League final against Real Madrid in Kyiv at 19. Gareth Southgate took him to the 2018 World Cup. In 2018-19 he set a Premier League assist record for a defender with 12, played in the 4-0 comeback against Barcelona and started the Champions League final win over Tottenham in Madrid.

## Peak years

From 2019 to 2022 he was arguably the best attacking full-back in the world: 13 assists and the PFA Young Player award as Liverpool won the 2019-20 title with 99 points, a cup double in 2022 and a third Champions League final. Klopp's late tactical shift, inverting him into midfield from the spring of 2023, produced his most complete football, and Arne Slot's 2024-25 title was built in part on his passing from a hybrid full-back role. He announced in May 2025 that he would leave; Liverpool reached a deal with Madrid for a small fee so he could play at the 2025 Club World Cup, and he signed a six-year contract. With [England](/nations/england) he started at Euro 2024, where he played in midfield, and was part of the 2026 World Cup squad that finished third.

## How he plays

Everything comes from the right foot. From a wide position he delivers in-swinging crosses with the pace and dip of a free-kick; from an inverted position in midfield he plays 60-yard diagonals that land on the winger's instep, and clipped balls over a defence for a runner. He takes corners, direct free-kicks and long throws, and his dead-ball delivery produced a steady supply of goals for [Virgil van Dijk](/players/virgil-van-dijk). He also scores from distance, usually with a driven strike from outside the box.

The weaknesses are real and well documented. He is not a strong one-v-one defender, he can be caught ball-watching when the ball is on the far side, and he lacks the pace to recover when beaten. These were manageable at Liverpool because of the structure around him; at Madrid, where Mourinho has said plainly that his qualities "are made for the full-back position, not for midfield", they have mattered more.

## Evolution

He was a right-back in a 4-3-3 who overlapped and crossed. Klopp then inverted him into a 3-2 build-up shape, effectively making him a deep playmaker, and Southgate experimented with him as an orthodox midfielder. At Madrid, [Xabi Alonso](/managers/xabi-alonso) in 2025-26 rotated him with Dani Carvajal and used him in both roles; Mourinho tried him in midfield against Inter in the Champions League and then went back to using Federico Valverde and summer signing Denzel Dumfries at right-back.

## Current situation

Alexander-Arnold's first Madrid season brought 35 appearances in 62 games, 27 of them starts, as the club missed out on [La Liga](/leagues/la-liga) to Barcelona and went out of the Champions League in the quarter-finals; Alonso left mid-season and Mourinho returned on 13 July 2026 on a contract to 2029. He started pre-season early and played 90 minutes against Fiorentina and Leganés, but since the October international break he has made only three appearances, with Valverde Mourinho's preferred right-back and Dumfries also competing. A midfield experiment against Inter was publicly dismissed by the manager. His contract runs to 2031 and his future at the Bernabéu is an open question.
