## Overview

Salma Paralluelo is the fastest forward in European football and one of the few players to have competed at national level in two sports. A Spanish 400m hurdles champion as a teenager, she chose football in 2022, won the [World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) a year later as [Spain](/nations/spain-women)'s game-changing substitute, and won three [Champions Leagues](/tournaments/womens-champions-league) with [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona-women), scoring twice in the 2026 Oslo final against the club she then joined: on 25 July 2026 she signed for [OL Lyonnes](/clubs/lyon-women) until 2030.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Zaragoza to a Spanish father and Equatorial Guinean mother, she was a European under-18 athletics medallist and a professional footballer with Villarreal at the same time, scoring 23 goals in 37 games while training for the track. Barcelona's offer in 2022 forced the choice. She had already won the Under-17 World Cup in 2018 and would add the Under-20 title in August 2022.

## Peak years

The 2023 World Cup made her famous: the extra-time winner against the Netherlands in the quarter-final, the opening goal against Sweden in the semi-final, both as a substitute, and the tournament's young player award. She finished third in the Ballon d'Or in 2023 and 2024, winning two Champions Leagues with Barcelona in between while managing a knee problem that cost her part of 2024. In 2025-26, with the knee behind her, she scored twice late in the 4-0 final win over Lyon and for Spain against Iceland in June and the United States in October 2026. Her Barcelona contract expired on 30 June 2026 and Lyon moved quickly.

## How she plays

Paralluelo is a vertical forward. She plays from the left or as a striker, stays on the shoulder of the last defender and runs onto through balls, where her acceleration and stride length leave defenders behind. Her finishing is powerful and right-footed, usually across the keeper, and she has improved her left foot and her heading. She presses with the energy of a sprinter. Her technical game in tight spaces is still developing, which is why she has often been most effective as a substitute against tired defences.

## Evolution

Athlete-footballer at Villarreal, super-sub at the 2023 World Cup, starter at Barcelona by 2024 and now, at Lyon under Jonatan Giráldez, the main source of pace in a possession team. Spain's coach Sonia Bermúdez uses her as a starting left forward.

## Best plays and moments

- Under-17 and Under-20 World Cup titles with Spain, 2018 and 2022.
- Extra-time winner against the Netherlands, 2023 World Cup quarter-final.
- Opening goal against Sweden in the 2023 World Cup semi-final.
- Two late goals in the 2026 Champions League final, Barcelona 4-0 Lyon.
- Goal against the USA in the 2-2 draw in Chester, 13 October 2026.

## Legacy / Current situation

As of November 2026 Paralluelo, 22, is Lyon's new star with two goals in four league games in the [Première Ligue](/leagues/d1-feminine) and a certainty for Spain's [2027 World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) squad in Brazil.
