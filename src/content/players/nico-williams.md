## Overview

Nico Williams is the other half of the best wing pairing in international football. Where [Lamine Yamal](/players/lamine-yamal) is guile, Williams is speed: a [Spain](/nations/spain) left winger who scored the opening goal of the Euro 2024 final, won the tournament's Young Player award and then, in July 2026, became a [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) winner. At club level he has stayed loyal to [Athletic Bilbao](/clubs/athletic-bilbao), signing a contract to 2035 in July 2025 after turning down [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona) for a second time.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Pamplona to Ghanaian parents who had crossed the Sahara on foot to reach Spain, Williams grew up in the shadow of his older brother Iñaki, who was already an Athletic regular when Nico was a boy. He joined Athletic's academy in 2013, debuted in April 2021 at 18, and by 2022-23 was a starter on the left of Ernesto Valverde's 4-2-3-1. Luis Enrique took him to the 2022 World Cup, where he played against Morocco in the round of 16.

In 2023-24 he helped Athletic win their first Copa del Rey in 40 years, scoring in the final against Mallorca's penalty shoot-out drama, and the celebrations on the Gabarra barge down the Nervión were the most emotional in modern Basque football.

## Peak years

Euro 2024 was his coming of age. Playing on the left of [Luis de la Fuente](/managers/luis-de-la-fuente)'s 4-3-3, he terrorised full-backs all tournament, scored against Georgia in the round of 16, and in the final against [England](/nations/england) scored the opener and set up the atmosphere of a Spanish win. He won the Young Player of the Tournament award over Yamal.

Barcelona pursued him that summer and again in 2025. He chose Athletic both times, and in 2025-26 led them in their return to the Champions League. At the 2026 World Cup he was Spain's left winger throughout the knockout stage, including the final against [Argentina](/nations/argentina).

## How he plays

Williams's game is built on acceleration. He receives wide on the left with the full-back facing him, pushes the ball past on the outside and is gone. Because he is right-footed on the left, defenders expect him to cut inside, so his willingness to go down the line with his weaker foot is a major weapon. His crossing has improved from erratic to reliable, and he has added a curled right-footed shot from the inside-left channel.

The weaknesses are in the final action. His finishing is streaky, his decision-making at the end of a dribble can be hurried, and he has periods where the end product does not match the threat. He has also suffered recurrent muscle injuries, including one in autumn 2026 that caused concern in the Spain camp.

## Evolution

Marcelino used him as an impact substitute. Valverde made him a starter and asked him to track back, which he does well. De la Fuente's Spain gives him almost total attacking freedom on the left, with [Pedri](/players/pedri) or Dani Olmo always inside for the simple pass, which is where his best football has come.

## Current situation

Williams remains Athletic's talisman in 2026-27 under Ernesto Valverde, although a muscle injury suffered against Valencia in the autumn interrupted his season; the severity should be checked against current reports. For Spain he is the first-choice left winger under a re-signed de la Fuente.
