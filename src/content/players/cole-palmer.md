## Overview

Cole Palmer is the rare player who improved by leaving [Manchester City](/clubs/manchester-city). A £42.5m deadline-day signing for [Chelsea](/clubs/chelsea) in September 2023, he became the [Premier League](/leagues/premier-league)'s outstanding attacking midfielder almost immediately: 22 league goals and 11 assists in his first season, the Young Player awards, and a style so unhurried that the "Cold Palmer" nickname and shivering celebration became part of the league's furniture. He scored twice in the 2025 Club World Cup final against PSG, and in 2026-27 he is the creative centre of [Xabi Alonso](/managers/xabi-alonso)'s new Chelsea.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Wythenshawe, Manchester, Palmer joined City's academy at eight and was the standout of the side that won the FA Youth Cup in 2020. He made his senior debut in September 2020, scored in the Champions League against Club Brugge in 2021, and was a regular substitute in the 2022-23 treble season, scoring in the Community Shield and Super Cup in August 2023. He wanted to start, City could not promise it, and Chelsea paid £42.5m on 1 September 2023.

## Peak years

Mauricio Pochettino made him Chelsea's No.10 and penalty taker and the goals came in a rush: four in one half against Everton in April 2024, a hat-trick against Manchester United, 22 league goals in total and the PFA Young Player of the Year award. Under [Enzo Maresca](/managers/enzo-maresca) in 2024-25 he was again Chelsea's leading scorer and creator in the first half of the season, scored four against Brighton, and then endured a long barren spell before finishing the season with the Conference League and a Club World Cup final in New Jersey in which he scored twice and assisted the third in a 3-0 win over PSG. With [England](/nations/england) he scored in the Euro 2024 final as a substitute.

The 2025-26 season was more troubled: a groin injury disrupted the autumn, Chelsea changed managers twice, and his numbers fell. He still travelled to the 2026 World Cup, where England reached the semi-finals and beat France 6-4 for third place.

## How he plays

Palmer's game is about time. He receives in the right half-space or between the lines, takes an extra touch that seems to invite pressure, and then, as a defender commits, plays the pass or takes the shot. He is left-footed, slim and deceptively tall, and his finishing is placed rather than powered: a curled shot into the far corner from the edge of the box is the signature. His penalties, with a stuttered run-up and a late look at the keeper, are among the most reliable in the league.

He creates with disguised through balls, cut-backs from the byline when he drifts wide, and set pieces from both sides. He does not sprint much, press much or tackle much. When Chelsea's structure gets him the ball in the right areas he looks the best player on the pitch; when it does not, he can be anonymous for long stretches, which is the argument against him. Discipline has occasionally been an issue, most recently a red card against Tottenham in October 2026.

## Evolution

At City he was a right winger who cut inside. Pochettino gave him the No.10 role in a 4-2-3-1 with freedom to drift right. Maresca pushed him into the right half-space of a 3-2-5 attacking shape, where he operated as an inside forward and the main chance creator. Alonso, who arrived in July 2026 after Liam Rosenior's interim spell, has talked of giving Palmer "freedom" and "responsibility" in a front line that now includes [Morgan Rogers](/players/morgan-rogers) and João Pedro, with Palmer playing closer to the striker and Rogers drifting from the left.

## Current situation

Palmer started 2026-27 in sharp form, scoring and assisting at Fulham on the opening weekend and reaching six goal contributions in his first six games across competitions, and said he felt "the best I've felt in ages". Alonso described him as "special" and said the better Palmer plays, the better Chelsea are. The autumn was harder: a five-game run without a goal, a red card in the 2-0 home defeat to Tottenham and a suspension that kept him out of the trip to Anfield. Chelsea sat fourth after nine games, having sold [Enzo Fernández](/players/enzo-fernandez) to City for £125m on deadline day and bought Rogers for £117m. Palmer's contract runs to 2033.
