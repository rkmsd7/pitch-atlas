## Overview

Lautaro Martínez is the heart of [Inter Milan](/clubs/inter-milan) and the striker who made Argentina's post-Messi future look safe. He won the [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) in 2022, scored the winning goal in the 2024 Copa América final and was the tournament's top scorer, and at Inter he has won three Serie A titles, including the 2025-26 championship under [Cristian Chivu](/managers/cristian-chivu), and captained the side in two Champions League finals. At 29 he is closing on the club's all-time scoring record.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Bahía Blanca, Lautaro came through at Racing Club and scored 27 goals in 61 games, including a hat-trick against Colo-Colo in the Copa Libertadores, before Inter paid around €25m in 2018. His first season under Luciano Spalletti was modest; under Antonio Conte, from 2019, he formed the "Lu-La" partnership with Romelu Lukaku that won the 2020-21 Scudetto, Inter's first in 11 years.

## Peak years

Under Simone Inzaghi he became the main man. He captained Inter to the 2023 Champions League final against Manchester City, scored 24 league goals in 2023-24 as Inter won their 20th title and was named Serie A's MVP, and in 2024-25 led the run to a second Champions League final, including an unforgettable 7-6 aggregate semi-final win over Barcelona, before a 5-0 defeat to PSG in Munich. In 2025-26, under Chivu, Inter reclaimed the Scudetto.

For [Argentina](/nations/argentina), he scored four goals at the 2022 World Cup qualifiers, was part of the squad that won in Qatar, and in 2024 scored five goals at the Copa América, including the extra-time winner against Colombia in the final. He started the 2026 World Cup final defeat to Spain.

## How he plays

Lautaro is small for a striker and plays big. He holds the ball up with his back to goal, using low leverage and strength, and he attacks crosses with a leap that makes him one of Serie A's best headers despite his height. His finishing is powerful and varied: acrobatic volleys, near-post drives, chipped finishes over the keeper. He presses relentlessly and often starts Inter's counter-press.

The weaknesses are periods of poor finishing, especially in the Champions League where his record lagged his league form for years, and a temper that spills into cards. He is not a dribbler in open space.

## Evolution

A second striker at Racing and in his first Inter season, a partner to Lukaku in Conte's 3-5-2, then the senior striker next to [Marcus Thuram](/players/marcus-thuram) in Inzaghi's 3-5-2 and Chivu's more flexible version. For Argentina, Lionel Scaloni uses him as the striker in front of Messi.

Lautaro's goal record has become one of the central storylines of Serie A. He passed 150 goals for Inter in 2025 and is closing in on the club's all-time list, with Giuseppe Meazza's record in sight if he stays through the end of his contract. His leadership as captain is of the visible kind: he confronts referees, lifts teammates after mistakes and celebrates every goal as if it were a final. Chivu, a former Inter captain himself, has leaned on him heavily in a season of transition, and the partnership with Thuram remains the first thing every Serie A coach plans to stop.

## Current situation

Lautaro captains Chivu's Inter in 2026-27, now partnered by Thuram with Pio Esposito and Ange-Yoan Bonny competing behind them, with a contract to 2029. For Argentina, he is the starting striker in a team whose coach Scaloni has a contract to December 2026 and an unresolved future.
