## Overview

Vinícius Júnior arrived at [Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid) at 18 as a €45m gamble, spent two seasons being told he could not finish and then became the most decisive winger in the Champions League, scoring the winner in the 2022 final and the second goal in the 2024 final. FIFA named him the world's best player in 2024, the year he finished a bitter second in the Ballon d'Or. He has also been, through no choice of his own, the face of the fight against racism in Spanish football.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in São Gonçalo, Rio de Janeiro, Vinícius came through at [Flamengo](/clubs/flamengo), where he was the star of Brazil's 2017 South American under-17 title. Real Madrid agreed to sign him before he had played a senior game, and he arrived in Madrid in July 2018 after his 18th birthday. He spent months with Castilla before Santiago Solari promoted him, and by early 2019 he was starting Champions League games, tearing through Ajax at the Bernabéu before an injury ended his season.

The 2019-20 and 2020-21 seasons were a running argument about his end product. He scored a league-title-sealing goal against Barcelona in 2020 and a brace against Liverpool in the 2021 quarter-final, but the misses were as memorable as the goals.

## Peak years

Everything changed in 2021-22 under [Carlo Ancelotti](/managers/carlo-ancelotti). With Karim Benzema as his foil, Vinícius scored 22 goals, delivered the goal that won the Champions League final against Liverpool and won [La Liga](/leagues/la-liga). In 2022-23 he added the Copa del Rey. In 2023-24, with [Jude Bellingham](/players/jude-bellingham) arriving, he scored 24 in all competitions, including a hat-trick against Dortmund, a brace in the Supercopa final against Barcelona and the second goal in the Champions League final at Wembley.

The 2024 Ballon d'Or went to Rodri in a vote that prompted Real Madrid to boycott the ceremony; FIFA's The Best award went to Vinícius weeks later. The 2024-25 season, with [Kylian Mbappé](/players/kylian-mbappe) alongside him, was less fluent as the two competed for the same left-sided space, and 2025-26 under [Xabi Alonso](/managers/xabi-alonso) and then an interim regime was turbulent for the whole club.

## How he plays

Vinícius starts wide left, often standing on the touchline with the ball coming from a long diagonal, and from there he attacks the full-back at pace. His acceleration from a standing start is elite, and because he can go either way with his right foot or outside with his left, defenders cannot show him anywhere. His finishing has improved from poor to good: the far-post curl with the right foot is now a reliable weapon, as is the near-post drive from the angle.

He is at his most dangerous in transition, where Madrid's long, direct counters find him in space. In a settled possession game he is less effective, sometimes over-dribbling and losing the ball in dangerous areas. His decision-making in the final third remains inconsistent, and his temper, understandable given the abuse he has received, has cost him cards and suspensions.

## Evolution

Zidane and Solari used him as a raw touchline winger. Ancelotti built the attack around his partnership with Benzema, who dropped deep to open the space Vinícius ran into. With Mbappé he has had to share the left, sometimes playing inside as a second striker, which has not always suited him. Under [José Mourinho](/managers/jose-mourinho), from July 2026, he is back on the left in a 4-2-3-1 built for the counter-attack, with [Yan Diomande](/players/yan-diomande) on the right, and the directness has brought the best out of him. For [Brazil](/nations/brazil), [Carlo Ancelotti](/managers/carlo-ancelotti) has made him a starter again.

## Current situation

Vinícius is a starter in Mourinho's Real Madrid in 2026-27, playing a more conventional left-wing role than in the Alonso era and benefiting from the counter-attacking structure. His contract situation was a long-running story through 2025 and 2026 and should be checked against the latest reports. For Brazil, he remains a key attacker under Ancelotti, with [Raphinha](/players/raphinha) and [Rodrygo](/players/rodrygo), once the latter is fit, alongside him.
