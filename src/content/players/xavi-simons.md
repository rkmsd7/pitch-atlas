## Overview

Xavi Simons has been famous since he was 10, when his La Masia haircut and highlight reels made him football's first social-media child star. The career since has been more interesting than the hype: a teenage move from [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona) to [Paris Saint-Germain](/clubs/paris-saint-germain), a breakout season at [PSV Eindhoven](/clubs/psv-eindhoven), two excellent years at [RB Leipzig](/clubs/rb-leipzig), a stunning goal against England in a Euro 2024 semi-final and then a move to [Tottenham Hotspur](/clubs/tottenham-hotspur) in 2025. He is a [Premier League](/leagues/premier-league) player, and since April 2026 an injured one, after tearing his ACL against Wolves.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Amsterdam to a Dutch father, the former footballer Regillio Simons, and raised in Spain, Xavi joined La Masia at seven and captained Barcelona's youth teams. In 2019, at 16, he left for PSG in a contract dispute; he later said Barcelona did not believe in him. He made a handful of PSG appearances under Mauricio Pochettino before joining PSV in 2022, where Ruud van Nistelrooy made him a starter: 22 goals in all competitions in 2022-23, the Eredivisie's joint top scorer at 19.

PSG exercised a buy-back clause and loaned him to Leipzig for two seasons, where he scored 21 goals and was one of the Bundesliga's best creators. He made his Netherlands debut in 2022 and played at the 2022 World Cup.

## Peak years

Euro 2024 was the high point: he started every game for Ronald Koeman's [Netherlands](/nations/netherlands) and scored a 25-yard strike against England in the semi-final, which the Dutch lost 2-1 to an Ollie Watkins winner. Leipzig signed him permanently in January 2025 for around €50m, and Tottenham paid around €60m in August 2025.

His first Spurs season, under Thomas Frank and then [Roberto De Zerbi](/managers/roberto-de-zerbi), was uneven: flashes of quality, a few goals, and a team in transition. The ACL tear in April 2026 ended it, and he had surgery on 5 May. He missed the 2026 World Cup.

## How he plays

Simons is a quick, low-slung dribbler who receives between the lines and drives at the defence. He is close to two-footed, shoots early from the edge of the box with either foot and plays one-twos in tight spaces. From the right he cuts inside; as a 10 he drifts to the right half-space. He presses intelligently and reads passing lanes.

The weaknesses are a slight frame that can be bullied, inconsistent end product and a tendency to over-dribble. Before the injury his Spurs form had not matched his Leipzig level, and the knee will inevitably be a question when he returns.

## Evolution

A number 10 at La Masia, a winger-forward at PSV, a right-sided 10 at Leipzig under Marco Rose, and a right winger and 10 at Spurs. Koeman uses him as the Netherlands' central 10. His return in 2027 under De Zerbi, a coach who demands high technical levels in possession, will be a test.

## Current situation

Simons is sidelined with the ACL injury suffered in April 2026 and was, as of October 2026, training individually at Tottenham with a return targeted for 2027. His contract runs to 2030, and reports in spring 2026 linked him with a return to Barcelona. He is a Premier League player; this profile is included for completeness.
