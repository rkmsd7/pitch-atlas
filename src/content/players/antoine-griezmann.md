## Overview

Antoine Griezmann is the most decorated French player of his generation after [Kylian Mbappé](/players/kylian-mbappe), and one of the most intelligent forwards of any generation. He was the best player at Euro 2016, won the [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) in 2018 and finished third in the Ballon d'Or twice. At [Atlético Madrid](/clubs/atletico-madrid), across two spells, he became the club's all-time record scorer with 211 goals. In March 2026 he announced he would leave at the end of the season, and in July he joined Orlando City in [MLS](/leagues/mls).

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Mâcon, Griezmann was rejected by every French academy that saw him for being too small. [Real Sociedad](/clubs/real-sociedad) took him to San Sebastián at 14, and he debuted at 18 in 2009, helping the club win promotion in his first season. He spent five years as a left winger in the Basque Country, scoring 16 league goals in 2013-14 as Real Sociedad qualified for the Champions League.

Atlético paid his €30m release clause in 2014. Under Diego Simeone he was converted from a winger into a striker and scored 22 league goals in his first season.

## Peak years

Between 2015 and 2019 Griezmann was the best forward in Spain outside Messi and Ronaldo. He scored in both legs of the 2016 Champions League semi-final against Bayern, missed a penalty in the final against Real Madrid, then went to Euro 2016 and scored six goals, winning the Golden Boot and the Player of the Tournament award as [France](/nations/france) lost the final to Portugal. In 2018 he scored in the Europa League final, then scored in the World Cup final against Croatia and was awarded the Silver Ball as France won in Moscow.

A €120m move to [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona) in 2019 never worked, though he won a Copa del Rey, and he returned to Atlético in 2021, winning the 2021 Nations League with France along the way. The second spell was, if anything, better than the first: he scored 24 goals in 2023-24, became the club's record scorer in October 2023 and remained a starter under Simeone into his mid-30s.

## How he plays

Griezmann's first touch is the foundation of everything. He receives in tight spaces with his back to goal and turns, or lays off first time, with a cleanliness that few forwards possess. His left foot is precise rather than powerful, and his finishing is about placement. He is an excellent set-piece taker, both direct free-kicks and corners, and his movement between the lines, dropping to receive and then spinning in behind, is what made him so hard to mark.

He is also a worker. Simeone's system demanded that forwards defend, and Griezmann's tracking of full-backs and pressing of centre-backs were genuinely elite. The weaknesses were aerial and physical: at 1.76 m he was never a target man, and in his 30s his pace in behind faded, pushing him into deeper roles.

## Evolution

A left winger at Real Sociedad, a striker under Simeone, a wide forward (unhappily) at Barcelona, then, in his second Atlético spell, a hybrid number 10 and second striker who dropped into midfield so often that Simeone sometimes listed him there. In Orlando he plays as a central attacking midfielder behind the striker. He retired from international football in September 2024 with 137 caps and 44 goals.

- 2016, Champions League semi-final, v Bayern Munich: goals in both legs.

- 2026, La Liga: farewell to the Metropolitano as record scorer.

## Current situation

Griezmann joined Orlando City in July 2026 on a deal through the 2027-28 MLS season with an option for a further year, and is the club's designated-player centrepiece. He left Atlético as its most beloved modern player and a near-certain future Hall of Famer, with the Champions League the one trophy that eluded him.
