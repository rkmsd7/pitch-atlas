## Overview

Mikel Oyarzabal is the one-club forward who scored the goal that ended Spain's 12-year wait for a major trophy: the 86th-minute winner against [England](/nations/england) in the Euro 2024 final in Berlin. Two years later, in New Jersey, he was part of the squad that won the [2026 World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup). At [Real Sociedad](/clubs/real-sociedad), where he has been since he was 14, he is the captain and is closing in on the club's all-time scoring record.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Eibar, Oyarzabal joined Real Sociedad's Zubieta academy in 2011 and debuted at 18 in October 2015 under David Moyes. By 2016-17 under Eusebio he was a starter on the left wing, and under Imanol Alguacil from 2018 he became the team's leader. He captained Spain's under-21s to the 2019 European title and was in the squad that won the 2020 Copa del Rey, scoring the penalty that beat [Athletic Bilbao](/clubs/athletic-bilbao) in the Basque derby final played in April 2021.

## Peak years

Oyarzabal's best club years were 2019 to 2022, when Real Sociedad were consistently in Europe and he was their main scorer and set-piece taker. He went to Euro 2020 and scored the decisive penalty in the shoot-out against Switzerland, then won Olympic silver in Tokyo. An ACL injury in March 2022 cost him most of a year.

He returned to the Spain squad under [Luis de la Fuente](/managers/luis-de-la-fuente), won the 2023 Nations League and went to Euro 2024 as a squad striker. He scored against Georgia and then, off the bench in the final, slid in Marc Cucurella's cross for the 2-1 winner. In 2024-25 he scored 18 league goals, the best season of his career, and he was in Spain's 2026 World Cup squad, contributing in the group stage and as a substitute in the knockouts.

## How he plays

Oyarzabal is a thinking forward. His movement in the box is timed rather than explosive: a drift to the far post, a dart across the first defender at the moment the cross is struck. His first touch is clean, he finishes with the inside of the left foot and he is one of the most reliable penalty-takers in Spain. As a winger he cuts inside to shoot or play the reverse pass; as a striker he drops to link.

He is not quick and never was, especially after the ACL injury, and he does not beat defenders one-on-one at pace. His right foot is functional. What he offers instead is reliability, leadership and the ability to play three positions well.

## Evolution

He began as a left winger, moved to a false nine and then a genuine striker under Alguacil as Real Sociedad's style became more possession-based, and since 2024 has often played as a number 10 behind a younger striker. For Spain, de la Fuente has used him as a substitute striker and occasionally a starter, the experienced head behind [Lamine Yamal](/players/lamine-yamal) and [Nico Williams](/players/nico-williams).

## Current situation

Oyarzabal is Real Sociedad's captain and leading scorer in 2026-27, the elder statesman of a young Basque side, and remains in the Spain squad under de la Fuente as a reliable option off the bench. His contract runs to 2028.
