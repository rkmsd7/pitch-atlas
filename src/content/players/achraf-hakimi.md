## Overview

Achraf Hakimi redefined the right-back position for the 2020s. He has the pace of a winger, the finishing of a forward and, since [Luis Enrique](/managers/luis-enrique) arrived at [Paris Saint-Germain](/clubs/paris-saint-germain), the positional intelligence to play as a midfielder when PSG have the ball. He scored the opening goal in the 2025 Champions League final against his former club Inter and won the trophy again in 2026. He captains [Morocco](/nations/morocco), the first African side to reach a World Cup semi-final, and was named African Footballer of the Year in 2025.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Madrid to Moroccan parents, Hakimi joined Real Madrid's academy at eight and debuted under Zidane in October 2017, winning a Champions League medal that season as a squad member. He chose Morocco over Spain at 17. A two-year loan to [Borussia Dortmund](/clubs/borussia-dortmund) in 2018 made him a star: 12 goals and 17 assists as a flying wing-back under Lucien Favre, including a brace at Inter in the Champions League.

[Inter Milan](/clubs/inter-milan) paid €40m in 2020 and Antonio Conte used him as a right wing-back in a 3-5-2 that won the 2020-21 Serie A title, Inter's first in 11 years. A year later, with Inter needing to sell, PSG paid around €60m.

## Peak years

Hakimi's PSG years have brought five Ligue 1 titles and two Champions Leagues. His best season was 2024-25, when Luis Enrique's system turned him into a goalscoring right-back: he scored against Liverpool, Aston Villa and Arsenal in the knockout rounds and then opened the scoring in the final against Inter. He was runner-up in Ligue 1's player of the year voting and sixth in the 2025 Ballon d'Or. In 2025-26 PSG retained the Champions League against Arsenal in Budapest.

For Morocco, he was the captain of the side that reached the 2022 World Cup semi-final, scoring the decisive panenka penalty against Spain in the round of 16. He led Morocco at the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations on home soil and at the 2026 World Cup.

## How he plays

Hakimi's defining attribute is speed in both directions. Going forward he overlaps or underlaps depending on the winger's position, and under Luis Enrique he often inverts into central midfield during build-up, forming a box with the two pivots. He finishes like a forward, especially from cutbacks at the far post, and his crossing is low and early. Defensively, his recovery pace covers for an aggressive starting position, and he is a strong tackler.

The weaknesses are positional: he can be caught high when the ball is turned over, and his aerial defending is average. His concentration in deep defensive blocks has been questioned. Off the pitch, a legal case in France has hung over him since 2023.

## Evolution

A wing-back at Dortmund and Inter, a conventional attacking full-back in his first PSG seasons, and then, under Luis Enrique, a hybrid full-back and midfielder who is as likely to be found in the centre circle as on the touchline. For Morocco, Walid Regragui uses him as a right-back in a compact 4-3-3 with freedom to attack.

- 2021, Serie A: a title with Inter as a wing-back under Conte.

- 2026, Champions League final, v Arsenal: a second European Cup in Budapest.

## Current situation

Hakimi is a certain starter in Luis Enrique's 2026-27 PSG and one of the squad's senior leaders alongside [Marquinhos](/players/marquinhos), with his contract running to 2029. For Morocco, he remains captain after the 2026 World Cup.
