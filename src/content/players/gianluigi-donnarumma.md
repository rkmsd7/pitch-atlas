## Overview

Gianluigi Donnarumma is a goalkeeper of extremes: a Serie A debutant at 16, a European champion with [Italy](/nations/italy) at 22 after saving two penalties in the Euro 2020 final shoot-out, the man whose saves against Liverpool and Arsenal carried [Paris Saint-Germain](/clubs/paris-saint-germain) to their first Champions League in 2025, and then, within weeks of that triumph, surplus to requirements. In September 2025 he joined [Manchester City](/clubs/manchester-city), where he won the FA Cup and the League Cup in his first season and remains first choice under [Enzo Maresca](/managers/enzo-maresca). He is, as of 2026-27, a [Premier League](/leagues/premier-league) player.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Castellammare di Stabia, near Naples, Donnarumma joined [AC Milan](/clubs/ac-milan)'s academy at 14 and was promoted to the first team by Siniša Mihajlović in October 2015, at 16 years and 242 days, the second-youngest goalkeeper to start in Serie A. He was Milan's number one for six seasons, won the 2016 Supercoppa and became Italy's youngest goalkeeper at 17. His refusal to sign a new contract in 2021 made him a villain at San Siro, and he left on a free transfer for PSG.

## Peak years

Euro 2020 was his first peak: saves in the quarter-final against Belgium, the semi-final shoot-out against Spain and the final shoot-out against England at Wembley, and the Player of the Tournament award, the first time a goalkeeper had won it. He won the Yashin Trophy that year.

At PSG he shared the role with Keylor Navas before becoming undisputed number one, winning four [Ligue 1](/leagues/ligue-1) titles. The 2024-25 Champions League run was his second peak: outstanding saves at Anfield in the round of 16, a man-of-the-match performance against Aston Villa and decisive stops against Arsenal in the semi-final, before a clean sheet in the 5-0 final win over Inter. He won a second Yashin Trophy in 2025. Luis Enrique, who wanted a keeper more involved in build-up, then signed Lucas Chevalier, and Donnarumma was sold.

## How he plays

Donnarumma's size, 1.96 m with an enormous wingspan, is the foundation of a style built on filling the goal. In one-on-ones he stays big, delays and spreads, and strikers see no space. His reflexes are elite, especially to his left, and he is one of the best penalty savers in the game, with a reading of the taker's hips that has won him shoot-outs for club and country. He commands his area on crosses.

The weaknesses are the ones that drove the PSG decision: his distribution under pressure is average, he is not comfortable as a sweeper far from his line, and he has produced high-profile errors with the ball at his feet. His challenge on Jamal Musiala at the 2025 Club World Cup, which broke the German's leg, was a rare moment of recklessness.

## Evolution

Milan's teenage number one became Italy's European champion, PSG's rotation keeper, then PSG's Champions League-winning keeper. At City, Maresca's possession-based system asks him to play short more often than at PSG, and the early results suggest he has adapted. For Italy, [Gennaro Gattuso](/managers/gennaro-gattuso) has kept him as captain.

## Current situation

Donnarumma is Manchester City's first-choice goalkeeper in 2026-27 under Enzo Maresca, who replaced Pep Guardiola in summer 2026, and captains Italy under Gattuso after the 2026 World Cup. He is a Premier League player; this profile is included for completeness alongside his former PSG teammates.
