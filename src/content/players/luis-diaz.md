## Overview

Luis Díaz spent three and a half years at Liverpool as the winger who did everything except score enough, then moved to [Bayern Munich](/clubs/bayern-munich) in July 2025 for around €75m and immediately became a different player: a [Bundesliga](/leagues/bundesliga) and DFB-Pokal winner in his first season, one of the main suppliers to [Harry Kane](/players/harry-kane)'s record 73 goals, and the most relentless presser in [Vincent Kompany](/managers/vincent-kompany)'s side. He is [Colombia](/nations/colombia)'s best player and the joint top scorer of the 2021 Copa América.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Barrancas, La Guajira, to a Wayuu indigenous family, Díaz grew up in poverty and was spotted playing in an indigenous football tournament. He came through Barranquilla and Junior, won two Colombian titles, and joined [Porto](/clubs/porto) in 2019 for a modest fee. Under Sérgio Conceição he scored 41 goals in three seasons, including 14 in the first half of 2021-22, and was the joint top scorer at the 2021 Copa América with Lionel Messi.

Liverpool paid an initial £37.5m in January 2022, and [Jürgen Klopp](/managers/jurgen-klopp) started him in the League Cup and FA Cup finals that season, both won on penalties against Chelsea, and in the Champions League final defeat to Real Madrid.

## Peak years

His Liverpool years were interrupted by a serious knee injury in 2022-23 and marked by the kidnapping of his father in Colombia in October 2023, after which he scored a brace against Brazil for Colombia with his father in the stands. He was a central figure in Arne Slot's 2024-25 title-winning side, with 17 goals in all competitions, and reached the 2024 Copa América final with Colombia.

The move to Bayern unlocked a new level. In 2025-26 he scored in the Supercup against Stuttgart in his first competitive game and was a fixture on the left of Kompany's attack, contributing a strong tally of goals and assists as Bayern won the double. He went to the 2026 World Cup as Colombia's captain and talisman.

## How he plays

Díaz is a wide forward rather than a pure winger. He starts on the left touchline but spends much of the game running the inside-left channel, timing runs behind the right-back and arriving at the far post for cutbacks. His pressing is extraordinary: he leads Bayern's counter-press from the front and wins the ball high up more often than any other forward in the squad. He dribbles with pace and a low centre of gravity, favouring the inside cut onto his right foot.

The criticism at Liverpool was finishing: too many good chances missed. At Bayern, with better service and more chances, the conversion has improved. He is not a creator from deep and his crossing is average. He is also 29 going on 30, and the volume of running his role demands is a question for the years ahead.

## Evolution

Conceição used him as a left winger in a 4-4-2. Klopp made him a pressing wide forward in a 4-3-3; Slot used him as a left winger and occasionally as a false 9. Kompany plays him as the left-sided of two wide forwards in a 4-2-3-1, with [Michael Olise](/players/michael-olise) on the right and Kane as the target. For Colombia, Néstor Lorenzo uses him on the left with freedom to roam.

## Best plays and moments

- 2021, Copa America: joint top scorer with Messi, including a bicycle kick against Brazil.
- 2022, FA Cup final, v Chelsea: a winner's medal in his first season in England.
- 2023, World Cup qualifier, Colombia 2-1 Brazil: two late goals days after his father's release.
- 2024, Copa America: a run to the final against Argentina.
- 2025, Premier League: a title winner with Liverpool.
- 2025, DFL-Supercup, v Stuttgart: a goal on his Bayern debut.
- 2026, Bundesliga and DFB-Pokal: the double in his first German season ([watch](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Luis+Diaz+Bayern+Munich+goals+2025+26)).

## Current situation

Díaz is a starter on the left of Kompany's attack in 2026-27, with a contract to 2029, and has begun the season with four Bundesliga goals in his first six league games. For Colombia he remains captain and the team's best player after the 2026 World Cup.
