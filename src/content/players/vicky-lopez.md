## Overview

Vicky López is the youngest star of the Barcelona dynasty and already a two-time winner of the women's Kopa Trophy, awarded to the world's best under-21 player, in 2025 and 2026. At 20 she has three [Champions League](/tournaments/womens-champions-league) medals, four Liga F titles and a regular place in a [Spain](/nations/spain-women) side ranked first in the world.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Madrid in July 2006, López came through Madrid CFF and made her Liga F debut for them in 2021-22 at 15, a record for the league. She lost her mother to cancer that year and has spoken of football as her way through it. In summer 2022 she joined [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona-women), initially for the B team, but Jonatan Giráldez used her in the first team almost immediately. That autumn she won the Under-17 World Cup with Spain and its Golden Ball.

## Peak years

Her breakthrough season was 2024-25: a regular starter on the right of the front three, double figures in goals, the 2024 Golden Girl award, and a first senior Spain cap. At Euro 2025 she played in every match as Spain reached the final, and in 2025-26 she scored in Champions League and World Cup qualifying games, including against Ukraine in March and April 2026, as Barcelona won every trophy available. The 2026 Kopa Trophy, presented in London on 26 October, was her second in a row.

## How she plays

López is a right-footed inside forward who starts wide right and drifts into the half-space, where she links with [Aitana Bonmatí](/players/aitana-bonmati). Her dribbling is based on a low centre of gravity and quick feints rather than raw speed; she beats defenders on either side and is comfortable receiving under pressure with her back to goal. She shoots early and increasingly accurately from 18 yards. Defensively she presses with enthusiasm, if not yet with [Mariona Caldentey](/players/mariona-caldentey)'s discipline. Her slight frame means she can be bullied in England-style physical games, something Barcelona have worked on.

## Evolution

A winger at Madrid CFF, she has been developed by Barcelona as a hybrid interior-winger, and Pere Romeu has used her in all three attacking positions and occasionally as a number eight. The next step is consistency of end product: her goal tally has grown each season, and the 2026 summer departures of [Alexia Putellas](/players/alexia-putellas) and [Salma Paralluelo](/players/salma-paralluelo) have handed her more responsibility.

## Best plays and moments

- Liga F debut for Madrid CFF at 15, the youngest in the league's history (2021).
- Golden Ball at the 2022 Under-17 World Cup in India.
- Solo goal and assist in Barcelona's 2024-25 title run-in that sealed the Golden Girl award.
- Spain's Euro 2025 run to the final in Switzerland as a 19-year-old regular.
- Goals in World Cup qualifiers against Ukraine, March and April 2026, and a second Kopa Trophy.

## Legacy / Current situation

As of November 2026 López is a Barcelona starter and a Spain regular heading towards the [2027 World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup). Her trajectory, like Lamine Yamal's in the men's game, has Barcelona planning a decade around her.
