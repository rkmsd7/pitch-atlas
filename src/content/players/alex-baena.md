## Overview

Álex Baena is the creator Diego Simeone had been looking for since [Antoine Griezmann](/players/antoine-griezmann) began to age. [Atlético Madrid](/clubs/atletico-madrid) paid [Villarreal](/clubs/villarreal) around €42m for him in summer 2025 after two seasons in which he was [La Liga](/leagues/la-liga)'s most prolific assist provider, and he has become the supply line to [Julián Álvarez](/players/julian-alvarez). In July 2026 he added a [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) winner's medal to the Euro 2024 and Olympic gold medals he had already collected with [Spain](/nations/spain).

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Roquetas de Mar in Almería, Baena joined Villarreal's academy at 10 and came through the B team. A loan to Girona in 2021-22 brought promotion through the play-offs and his first senior goals. Back at Villarreal under Unai Emery and then Quique Setién, he became a starter, scoring in the Conference League and the Copa del Rey. A spat with Real Madrid's Federico Valverde in 2023, which ended in a car-park confrontation, briefly made him a national talking point.

## Peak years

The 2023-24 season was the breakthrough: 14 assists, the most in La Liga, from the left of Villarreal's attack. He kept the standard in 2024-25 as Villarreal qualified for the Champions League, and Spain's coaches had noticed. He was in the Euro 2024 squad, played in the 2023 under-21 Euro final, and in Paris in 2024 was the creative heart of Spain's Olympic gold-medal side, scoring and assisting in the 5-3 final win over France.

At Atlético in 2025-26 he started slowly after appendix surgery, then grew into a central role as Griezmann's successor in the number 10 position. He went to the 2026 World Cup as part of de la Fuente's squad and contributed as a substitute during the knockout rounds.

## How he plays

Baena's left foot is his signature. From the left half-space he plays the pass defenders cannot see coming: a disguised ball through the line, usually to a striker running across the near centre-back. His set-pieces, both corners and free-kicks, are among the best in Spain, with a whipped, dipping trajectory. He also scores: a curled shot into the far corner from the edge of the box is a regular feature.

He presses intelligently and has the work-rate Simeone demands. The weaknesses are physical. He is slight, can be bullied in midfield, and lacks the pace to beat defenders on the outside, which limits him as a pure winger. His right foot is rarely used.

## Evolution

Emery used him as a left winger. Setién and Marcelino made him a free-roaming left-sided creator. Simeone has turned him into a genuine number 10 in a 4-4-2 that becomes a 4-2-3-1, with Baena behind Álvarez and licence to drift left. For Spain, de la Fuente uses him as a substitute creator and set-piece specialist.

Baena's adaptation to Simeone's demands has been one of the quieter stories of Atlético's 2025-26 and 2026-27 seasons. He arrived as a creator from a possession side and had to learn the defensive discipline of a team that spends long periods without the ball, and by spring 2026 he was pressing the opposition's pivot with the same commitment Griezmann once showed. His set-pieces have given Atlético a weapon they lacked, and his partnership with Álvarez, built on the striker's willingness to run across the near centre-back at the moment Baena shapes to pass, has become the team's primary route to goal.

- 2026, World Cup: a winner's medal with Spain.

## Current situation

Baena is Atlético's first-choice number 10 in 2026-27, the key creator for Álvarez in Simeone's reshaped attack following Griezmann's departure to Orlando. His contract runs to 2030. For Spain he is in the squad under de la Fuente, competing with Dani Olmo for the creative roles.
