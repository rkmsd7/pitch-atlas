## Overview

Anthony Gordon's move to [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona) in May 2026 surprised almost everyone except the player. He had been linked for months with [Liverpool](/clubs/liverpool), the club he supported as a boy, and with Arsenal, but Barcelona agreed a deal with [Newcastle United](/clubs/newcastle-united) before the window opened, paid around €80m and presented him on 29 May as the La Liga champions' first signing of the summer. Three seasons at St James' Park had turned a raw Everton winger into one of the [Premier League](/leagues/premier-league)'s most direct attackers and an [England](/nations/england) regular, and he had just played at the 2026 World Cup.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Liverpool, Gordon joined Everton's academy at 11 and made his debut at 16 in the Europa League in December 2017. He was a bit-part player for three seasons, with a loan at Preston in 2021, before Rafael Benítez and then Frank Lampard made him a starter in 2021-22, when he was the bright spot of a relegation fight. Chelsea bid for him in the summer of 2022; Everton refused. In January 2023 Newcastle paid around £40m, and that summer he was player of the tournament as England won the European Under-21 Championship.

## Peak years

Eddie Howe used him on the left of a 4-3-3 and he exploded in 2023-24, with 11 league goals and 10 assists, a senior England debut and a place at Euro 2024, where he was a substitute in the final. The 2024-25 season was quieter in output but ended with the Carabao Cup, Newcastle's first domestic trophy since 1955, and a return to the Champions League. In 2025-26 he was again a starter in Europe and for England, though by the spring Howe was publicly questioning his commitment and leaving him out as the transfer became inevitable. He played for England at the 2026 World Cup, where they finished third.

## How he plays

Gordon's first instinct is to run at the full-back. He takes the ball to feet on the touchline, pushes it past the defender with his right foot and races to the byline, or chops inside to shoot across the keeper. His acceleration over ten yards is among the best in Europe and he repeats sprints for 90 minutes. His low crosses and cut-backs are good, and his pressing and tracking are exemplary; Howe called him the best presser in his squad.

The flaws are in the last action. His shooting can be rushed, his goal tally has oscillated and he can over-dribble into traffic. He wins a high number of penalties, which has invited accusations of going down easily. [Hansi Flick](/managers/hansi-flick) has praised his ability to receive under pressure and keep possession, a quality not always credited to him in England.

## Evolution

At Everton he was used on both wings and as a No.10. Howe fixed him on the left, outside [Bruno Guimarães](/players/bruno-guimaraes), with a licence to stay high and wide. At Barcelona he began on the left of Flick's 4-2-3-1 with Raphinha, and has also been tried through the middle; he told reporters in August that he backed himself to become Barcelona's No.9. His early contribution has been as a creator: two assists in five minutes on his debut and four assists in his first four La Liga games.

## Current situation

Gordon signed a five-year contract and took the No.17 shirt after his preferred 10 proved unavailable because it belongs to [Lamine Yamal](/players/lamine-yamal). Flick said in September that he was "thrilled" with his displays and that the goals would come; the first arrived against Getafe on 10 October as Barcelona, with [Rodri](/players/rodri) newly installed in midfield, won their first six [La Liga](/leagues/la-liga) games. His exact goal tally beyond that is not confirmed on this page. For England under [Thomas Tuchel](/managers/thomas-tuchel) he remains in the squad, competing with [Bukayo Saka](/players/bukayo-saka) and others for the wide roles.
