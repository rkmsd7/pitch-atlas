## Overview

Mariona Caldentey is the quiet genius of the Spanish generation. A decade at [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona-women) brought three [Champions Leagues](/tournaments/womens-champions-league) and over 100 goals; a free transfer to [Arsenal](/clubs/arsenal-women) in 2024 brought a fourth European title in her first season, the WSL and PFA player of the year awards and, in 2026, the FIFA Women's Champions Cup. For [Spain](/nations/spain-women) she won the 2023 World Cup and two Nations Leagues and opened the scoring in the Euro 2025 final.

## Early life and breakthrough

From Felanitx in Mallorca, Caldentey played for Collerense in the Balearic Islands before Barcelona signed her at 18 in 2014. She became a regular as the club professionalised, playing wide left or as a number 10, and was part of the 2019 Champions League final side.

## Peak years

Between 2020 and 2024 she was one of Barcelona's most reliable performers, scoring in the 2021 final against Chelsea from the penalty spot and starting the 2023 and 2024 finals. At the 2023 World Cup she scored a penalty in the semi-final against Sweden and started the final. Her decision to leave for Arsenal in 2024 surprised many; within a year she had 16 goals, the league's player of the season award and a winner's medal in Lisbon after scoring in the semi-final comeback against Lyon. At Euro 2025 she scored the opener in the final against England before Spain lost on penalties; her kick was saved by Hannah Hampton.

## How she plays

Caldentey can play anywhere across the front line or midfield. At Arsenal under Renée Slegers she is the left-sided number eight, dropping to receive from Kim Little, playing one-twos with Alessia Russo and arriving in the box. She is left-footed, precise rather than powerful, and her first-time passing in tight areas is the best in the WSL. Out of possession she is the model presser, an inheritance from Barcelona's system. She takes penalties for club and country with a calm, placed technique and delivers corners. She is not fast and does not take players on at speed; she beats them with timing.

## Evolution

A winger in her early Barcelona years, she moved into midfield as the club's possession game evolved and became a complete interior. In England she has added physicality and goals, and the move has made her a leader rather than a supporting star.

## Best plays and moments

- Penalty in the 2021 Champions League final, Barcelona 4-0 Chelsea.
- Semi-final penalty against Sweden at the 2023 World Cup.
- Goal in the 2025 Champions League semi-final comeback against Lyon and the Lisbon final win over Barcelona.
- WSL Player of the Season 2024-25, the first Spaniard to win it.
- Opening goal in the Euro 2025 final against England, Basel.

## Legacy / Current situation

As of November 2026 Caldentey, 30, is an Arsenal starter with 104 Spain caps and a certainty for the [2027 World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) squad. Her move proved Spanish technicians can dominate the English league.
