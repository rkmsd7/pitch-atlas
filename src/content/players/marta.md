## Overview

Marta is the most decorated individual in women's football and, for most who watched her at her peak, the best player there has been. Six FIFA World Player of the Year awards, five of them consecutive from 2006 to 2010, 17 [World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) goals, a record for either sex until Miroslav Klose's 16 was passed, 122 goals for [Brazil](/nations/brazil-women) and three Olympic silver medals. At 40 she is still captain of [Orlando Pride](/clubs/orlando-pride) and came out of international retirement in 2025 to win the Copa América.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Dois Riachos, a small town in Alagoas, Marta grew up poor and was mocked for playing with boys. Vasco da Gama's women's section found her at 14; when it folded she played in the Brazilian interior before Swedish club Umeå signed her at 18 in 2004. She scored 111 goals in 103 league games there and won the UEFA Women's Cup in her first season.

## Peak years

The 2007 World Cup in China was her masterpiece: seven goals, the Golden Ball and Golden Boot, and a goal in the 4-0 semi-final win over the United States, dribbling past two defenders with a backheel and a turn, that is still shown as the women's game's greatest. Brazil lost the final to Germany. Olympic silvers in 2004 and 2008 followed, and she played in the American WPS, winning two titles, and in Sweden for Tyresö and Rosengård. She joined Orlando in 2017 and in 2024, at 38, won her first NWSL trophies, the Shield and the Championship, having scored in the semi-final. Sent off in the Paris Olympic group stage, she returned to help Brazil reach the final.

She announced her international retirement in April 2024, then returned in 2025 for the Copa América, scoring a stoppage-time equaliser in the final against Colombia and converting in the shootout. The FIFA Marta Award for the best goal of the year was named after her in 2024.

## How she plays

Marta's game is built on the dribble. She receives on the left, squares up the defender and uses a quick step-over or a drop of the shoulder to go either way, then accelerates. Her left foot produces curled finishes, driven shots and precise passes; she takes penalties and free-kicks. Fiercely competitive, she has always demanded the ball and the responsibility. In her thirties she moved into a deeper playmaking role, linking play and finding runners, and her reading of the game has compensated for the lost burst.

## Evolution

From an unstoppable winger in Sweden to a complete forward in her WPS years to a number 10 and captain at Orlando. The 2022 ACL injury at 36 seemed like the end; the 2024 season was instead a renaissance, and the 2025 Copa América a coda few expected.

## Best plays and moments

- UEFA Women's Cup with Umeå in her first European season (2004).
- Seven goals, Golden Ball and Golden Boot at the 2007 World Cup, including the solo goal against the USA.
- Record 17th World Cup goal against Australia in 2019 and her tearful plea to the next generation.
- 2024 NWSL Shield and Championship with Orlando at 38.
- Equaliser and shootout goal in the 2025 Copa América final against Colombia.

## Legacy / Current situation

As of November 2026 Marta is still Orlando's captain under interim coach Yolanda Thomas, married to team-mate Carrie Lawrence since January 2026, and has not ruled out playing at the [2027 World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) in her home country at 41. Her legacy is the game's global growth itself.
