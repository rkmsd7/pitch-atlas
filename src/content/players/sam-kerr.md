## Overview

Sam Kerr is the greatest Australian footballer of any gender and one of the most prolific strikers in women's football history. Captain of the [Matildas](/nations/australia-women) and their record scorer with 76 goals, she won five straight WSL titles with [Chelsea](/clubs/chelsea-women), finished as the club's all-time WSL scorer, and in 2026 came back to the [NWSL](/leagues/nwsl), where she had twice been MVP, to join [Gotham FC](/clubs/gotham-fc).

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in East Fremantle into an Australian rules football family, Kerr switched to soccer at 12 and debuted for Perth Glory and Australia at 15. She won the 2010 Asian Cup as a 16-year-old. A serious foot injury in 2013 cost her a year, and for a while she was a winger of promise rather than a striker of substance. The move to the number nine role at Perth and Sky Blue around 2015 changed everything.

## Peak years

From 2017 to 2019 she was the NWSL's best player, winning the Golden Boot three years running and the MVP award twice, while scoring freely for Perth in the Australian off-season. Chelsea signed her in January 2020 and she scored 116 goals in all competitions over six years, winning five titles and three Golden Boots, including a 2020-21 campaign that brought a Champions League final. At the 2023 home World Cup she missed the group stage with a calf injury and then scored a 25-yard screamer against England in the semi-final. An ACL tear in January 2024 kept her out for over a year; she returned in early 2026 to score the winner against the Philippines at the Asian Cup, reached the final, and in May broke Fran Kirby's Chelsea record before scoring in her final game against Manchester United. Gotham signed her on 29 June 2026 on a contract to 2030; within two months she had set the club's single-season scoring record and reached 80 NWSL regular-season goals, a first.

## How she plays

Kerr is a finisher before everything else. She times runs behind the last defender, attacks the space at the near post on crosses, and heads the ball with a power and technique few can match. She can play off a partner or alone, and her work rate in the press is high. She is not a creator and does not drop deep to build; her value is in the box, and her instinct for the loose ball is unparalleled. Since the ACL injury the explosiveness is slightly reduced and the positional intelligence more pronounced.

## Evolution

Winger to striker in her early twenties, then from a transition striker in the NWSL to a complete penalty-box forward in Emma Hayes's Chelsea, where she learned to play with her back to goal. At Gotham, Juan Carlos Amorós uses her as the spearhead of a possession team, feeding her through balls from Rose Lavelle.

## Best plays and moments

- Hat-trick for Sky Blue against Seattle in 2017 on the way to a first NWSL MVP.
- Goal in the 2021 FA Cup final and 2020-21 Chelsea quadruple season.
- Long-range goal against England in the 2023 World Cup semi-final, Sydney.
- Breaking Chelsea's WSL scoring record against Leicester, 3 May 2026.
- 80th NWSL regular-season goal and Gotham's Shield, August to November 2026.

## Legacy / Current situation

As of November 2026 Kerr, 33, is leading Gotham into the NWSL play-offs as top seed and still captains Australia, with the [2027 World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) in Brazil her stated goal.
