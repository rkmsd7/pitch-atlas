## Overview

Christian Pulisic carried the weight of American football for a decade and, at [AC Milan](/clubs/ac-milan), finally found a place where he could simply play. A €20m signing from Chelsea in 2023, he has been Milan's most consistent attacker ever since, their top scorer in 2024-25 and the player whose new contract to 2031, signed on 30 October 2026, the club prioritised above all others. He captained the [United States](/nations/united-states) at the home [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) in summer 2026.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Hershey, Pennsylvania, to a family of soccer coaches with Croatian roots, Pulisic joined [Borussia Dortmund](/clubs/borussia-dortmund) at 16 in 2015. He debuted at 17 under Thomas Tuchel, became the Bundesliga's youngest foreign scorer, and won the 2017 DFB-Pokal. By 18 he was the United States' best player, the youngest captain in the national team's history, and Chelsea paid £58m for him in January 2019, a record for an American.

## Peak years

Chelsea were four years of flashes and frustration: a hat-trick at Burnley, a goal in the 2021 Champions League semi-final against Real Madrid, a winner's medal in the final, and a succession of injuries and managers who did not trust him. The 2022 World Cup brought the goal against Iran that sent the US through and a hospital trip for a pelvic injury suffered scoring it.

Milan under Stefano Pioli made him a starter from day one. He scored 15 goals in 2023-24 from the right wing, then 17 in 2024-25 as a right winger and number 10, the best season of his career, and won the 2025 Supercoppa. In 2025-26 under Massimiliano Allegri he was again Milan's main attacking threat as the club finished fifth. The 2026 World Cup, played partly in his home country, ended for the US in the knockout rounds under Mauricio Pochettino.

## How he plays

Pulisic is quick, close-controlled and two-footed enough to go either way from the right. He receives in the half-space, drives at the defender and either shoots with his left foot across the keeper or plays the cutback. As a 10 he links play and arrives late in the box. He presses with intent and tracks back, which has made him a favourite of every Milan coach.

The weaknesses have historically been durability and physical strength; he has been muscled out of games in England. In Italy, with more space and more trust, both concerns have faded. His crossing is average and he is not a long-range shooter.

## Evolution

A left and right winger at Dortmund under Tuchel and Favre, a left winger and wing-back at Chelsea under Lampard and Tuchel, then a right winger at Milan under Pioli, a 10 under Fonseca and Conceição, a second striker under Allegri and now, under Ruben Amorim, a right-sided attacking midfielder in a 3-4-2-1. For the US, Pochettino made him the captain and central playmaker.

## Current situation

Pulisic signed a new contract with Milan to 2031 in October 2026 and is the key attacker in Ruben Amorim's first season, with the club in the Europa League and chasing a return to the Champions League. He remains the United States' captain.
