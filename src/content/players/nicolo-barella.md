## Overview

Nicolò Barella is the engine of [Inter Milan](/clubs/inter-milan) and the spirit of [Italy](/nations/italy). A Euro 2020 winner, three-time Serie A champion and twice the league's midfielder of the season, he plays every match as if it were a derby, covering more ground than anyone and complaining to the referee about most of it. He has been the midfield constant through Conte, Inzaghi and [Cristian Chivu](/managers/cristian-chivu), and is one of the first names in Gennaro Gattuso's Italy.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Cagliari, Barella came through at his hometown club and debuted at 18 in 2015. After a loan at Como he became Cagliari's best player, a midfielder who ran, tackled and scored from distance. He made his Italy debut in 2018 and Inter paid around €45m in 2019.

## Peak years

Under Antonio Conte he was the right-sided midfielder in a 3-5-2 that won the 2020-21 Scudetto, and he was named Serie A's best midfielder. That summer he was central to Roberto Mancini's Euro 2020 win, scoring against Belgium in the quarter-final and starting the final at Wembley. Under Simone Inzaghi he was the box-to-box engine of the 2023 Champions League final run and the 2023-24 title, again winning the midfielder of the season award. In 2025 he started the Champions League final defeat to PSG; in 2025-26 Inter reclaimed the Scudetto under Chivu.

## How he plays

Barella never stops. He presses from midfield, wins second balls, carries the ball forward with quick, short strides and plays the pass on the turn that releases Inter's wing-backs. His late runs into the box produce volleys and side-footed finishes, and his crossing from the right half-space is good. He is also a set-piece taker.

The weaknesses are temperament, a high card count and a tendency to lose his head in big games, and a slight frame that can be out-muscled. His passing range is good rather than elite, and he is not a creator of chances in the way a true playmaker is.

## Evolution

A box-to-box 8 at Cagliari, a right-sided mezzala in Conte's 3-5-2, the same role with more attacking licence under Inzaghi, and under Chivu a midfielder who sometimes plays deeper as the pivot. For Italy, Mancini, Spalletti and Gattuso have all built the midfield around him.

Barella's influence is hardest to measure in the moments between phases. When Inter lose the ball he is the first midfielder to react, and when they win it he is the first to run, often arriving in the opposition half before the attack has formed. Chivu's Inter press a little higher than Inzaghi's did, and Barella's ability to sustain that over 90 minutes is what makes the approach viable. He has also become a more disciplined passer with age, choosing the simple forward ball more often than the ambitious one, which has reduced the turnovers that used to frustrate Conte. His goal return has never been high, generally between three and six a season, but his assist numbers have grown every year since 2021.

## Current situation

Barella is Inter's vice-captain and first-choice midfielder under Chivu in 2026-27, with a contract to 2029. He remains central to Italy under Gattuso after the 2026 World Cup.
