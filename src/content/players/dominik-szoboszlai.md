## Overview

Dominik Szoboszlai is the kind of midfielder managers love and analysts argue about: a tireless runner with a spectacular right foot who has played No.8, No.10, No.6 and right-back for [Liverpool](/clubs/liverpool) since arriving from RB Leipzig in 2023 for £60m. He was a Premier League champion in 2024-25, when Arne Slot used him as a pressing No.10, and he is the captain of [Hungary](/nations/hungary), the first Hungarian to play regularly for a top English club in decades.

## Early life and breakthrough

Szoboszlai was born in Székesfehérvár, where his father Zsolt, a former player, coached him from the age of five. He joined the Red Bull system in Austria at 16, scored freely for the feeder club Liefering and made his Salzburg debut in 2018. Over three seasons he won three Austrian league and cup doubles, played Champions League football and scored a famous goal against Iceland in November 2020, a stoppage-time winner that took Hungary to Euro 2020. [RB Leipzig](/clubs/rb-leipzig) signed him for around €20m in January 2021, though a groin problem delayed his debut by months.

## Peak years

In Leipzig he won two DFB-Pokals and became an elite Bundesliga midfielder, with ten goals and 13 assists in his final season. Liverpool triggered his release clause in July 2023 as part of Klopp's midfield rebuild alongside Alexis Mac Allister and [Ryan Gravenberch](/players/ryan-gravenberch). He was immediately a starter, and under Slot in 2024-25 he was the team's first presser and most-run player as Liverpool won their 20th league title. The free-kick against Arsenal in August 2025 and a run of goals in autumn 2025 were the high points of his third season, in which he also filled in at right-back after [Trent Alexander-Arnold](/players/trent-alexander-arnold)'s departure to Real Madrid.

## How he plays

Szoboszlai's game is about volume. He covers more distance than almost any midfielder in the league, pressing the opposition pivot, then recovering to screen his own defence. In possession his first instinct is forward: a vertical pass into the striker, a diagonal to the winger, or a carry through the middle third. When the shot is on from distance, he takes it, and his technique, a clean strike with the laces and minimal backlift, produces goals from 25 and 30 yards.

His set pieces are a genuine weapon: whipped in-swinging corners, driven free-kicks and a left-footed delivery when needed. He is also strong in the air for a midfielder and has learned to time runs into the box for cut-backs. Out of possession his pressing angles are good enough that Slot made him the trigger of the entire press.

The weaknesses are the flip side of the running. His touch under pressure in tight central areas is less secure than that of a natural No.10, his decision-making can be hurried, and his long-shot volume occasionally comes at the expense of a better pass. He can also drift in games where the team needs control rather than energy.

## Evolution

At Salzburg under Marco Rose and Jesse Marsch he was an attacking midfielder in a pressing 4-2-2-2. Leipzig used him as an inside forward and later a left-sided No.8. Klopp's Liverpool played him as a right-sided No.8 in a 4-3-3, with licence to shoot. Slot moved him to No.10 in a 4-2-3-1 as a pressing forward, then used him as a No.6 and even as an inverted right-back when injuries bit. Under [Andoni Iraola](/managers/andoni-iraola) in 2026-27 he is one of the two central midfielders in a 4-2-3-1, with [Florian Wirtz](/players/florian-wirtz) ahead and Gravenberch alongside, and his engine is central to Iraola's man-to-man press.

## Best plays and moments

- 2018, Austrian Bundesliga v Rapid Wien: his first Salzburg goal, a long-range strike at 17.
- 2020, Euro 2020 play-off final v Iceland: the 92nd-minute winner that sends Hungary to their first Euros since 2016 ([Hungary goals](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Dominik+Szoboszlai+Hungary+goals)).
- 2022, DFB-Pokal final v Freiburg: a penalty in the shoot-out as Leipzig win their first trophy.
- 2023, Premier League v Aston Villa: the first of his Liverpool long-range goals, a thumping strike from distance ([best goals](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Dominik+Szoboszlai+best+goals)).
- 2024, League Cup final v Chelsea: part of the youthful side that wins 1-0 at Wembley.
- 2024, Euro 2024 v Scotland: captains Hungary to a stoppage-time 1-0 win in Stuttgart.
- 2025, Premier League v Arsenal: a 35-yard free-kick that wins the game 1-0 at Anfield ([free-kick](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Szoboszlai+free+kick+Liverpool+Arsenal+2025)).
- 2025, Premier League v Tottenham: part of the 5-1 win that clinched the title under Slot.

## Current situation

Szoboszlai remains a Liverpool starter in 2026-27, now in the double pivot of Iraola's 4-2-3-1, where his pressing and running make him the manager's most natural fit in the squad. Liverpool have started the season behind champions [Arsenal](/clubs/arsenal) and [Manchester City](/clubs/manchester-city), and he has been one of the more consistent performers while [Florian Wirtz](/players/florian-wirtz) and [Alexander Isak](/players/alexander-isak) find their feet. For Hungary he is captain and talisman under Marco Rossi, with the 2028 Euro qualifying campaign the next target after the country missed the 2026 World Cup.
