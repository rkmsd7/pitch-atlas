## Overview

Omar Marmoush's rise was one of the sharpest in recent European football. For six years he was a useful squad forward in Germany; then, in the autumn of 2024, he became the most dangerous attacker in the Bundesliga at [Eintracht Frankfurt](/clubs/eintracht-frankfurt), scoring 20 goals before Christmas. [Manchester City](/clubs/manchester-city) paid around £59m for him in January 2025 and he scored a 14-minute hat-trick against Newcastle within a month. Eighteen months later, squeezed out by City's £458m rebuild, he joined [Tottenham Hotspur](/clubs/tottenham-hotspur) on a season-long loan that becomes permanent next summer. He is also the main man for [Egypt](/nations/egypt) alongside [Mohamed Salah](/players/mohamed-salah).

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Cairo, Marmoush came through Wadi Degla's academy and moved to Wolfsburg's youth set-up in 2017 at 18. The route to the first team was slow: a handful of Bundesliga appearances in 2019-20, then loans at St. Pauli in the second division, where he scored eight goals, and at Stuttgart. He returned to Wolfsburg for 2022-23 and had his best season there, but his contract was running down and Frankfurt signed him on a free in the summer of 2023.

Dino Toppmöller's Frankfurt used him first as a left winger and then as a striker, and the second role transformed him. A dozen goals in 2023-24 was promising; the start of 2024-25 was extraordinary, with 15 league goals and nine assists by the winter break.

## Peak years

City had been looking for a forward who could play with or instead of [Erling Haaland](/players/erling-haaland), and Marmoush arrived in January 2025 as [Pep Guardiola](/managers/pep-guardiola)'s side tried to rescue a difficult season. The Newcastle hat-trick in February announced him. He was used across the front three and occasionally as a second striker, played in the 2025 FA Cup final and at the Club World Cup, but in 2025-26 he struggled for regular minutes as City finished behind [Arsenal](/clubs/arsenal). He went to the 2026 World Cup with Egypt, playing all five of their matches as they reached the last 16 before losing 3-2 to eventual finalists [Argentina](/nations/argentina).

## How he plays

Marmoush's defining quality is his speed over 20 metres, and he uses it in two ways: running in behind from the left half-space when the full-back steps up, and carrying the ball at pace on the counter. He wants to shoot. His right-footed strike from 20 to 25 yards is heavy and accurate, and he hits free-kicks with real whip, which is why Frankfurt and later City gave him dead-ball duties.

His first touch when receiving on the run is excellent; with his back to goal in traffic it is less secure. He is a willing presser who starts counter-presses from the front, and he works back along the touchline. The weaknesses are typical of a direct player: his passing in tight combinations can be hurried, and when he is asked to hold width and wait for the ball he can drift out of matches. He is at his best in transition and least effective against a packed defence.

## Evolution

At Wolfsburg he was a wide forward used as an impact substitute. Frankfurt moved him to striker and allowed him to run from deeper positions, which unlocked his scoring. Guardiola used him more as a left winger who could cut inside and as a partner for Haaland in a 4-4-2 shape without the ball. At Tottenham under [Roberto De Zerbi](/managers/roberto-de-zerbi), whose teams build patiently and then attack the box in numbers, he has been used as a wide forward and as a second striker, with the freedom to run beyond the centre-forward that suits him best.

## Current situation

Tottenham's summer spending passed £300m, with [Sandro Tonali](/players/sandro-tonali) the club-record arrival and Marmoush and Savinho completing a double raid on City in the final week of the window. The loan carries an obligation to buy reported at between £50m and £60m depending on the source. Spurs' start under De Zerbi has been uneven: a 2-0 home defeat to Newcastle, a 2-0 win at Chelsea in October and a 0-0 draw at Leeds on 7 November. Marmoush's exact goal tally for 2026-27 is not confirmed on this page. For Egypt he is the first-choice striker in the post-World Cup cycle, with the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations the next target.
