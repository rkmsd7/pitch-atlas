## Overview

Rodrygo is the [Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid) forward whose name is attached to more unforgettable European nights than players with twice his profile. Two goals in two minutes against Manchester City in the 2022 semi-final, the Copa del Rey final winner in 2023, a goal in the 2022 Champions League final build-up: for four seasons he was the quiet third man of Madrid's attack. In March 2026 he tore the ACL and meniscus in his right knee, missed the World Cup, and is not expected back until early 2027.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Osasco, São Paulo, Rodrygo came through at [Santos](/clubs/santos), debuting at 16 and scoring in the Copa Libertadores at 17, the youngest Brazilian to do so. Real Madrid agreed a €45m deal in June 2018 and he arrived in summer 2019. Zidane put him into the first team almost at once, and in November 2019 he scored a perfect hat-trick against Galatasaray at 18, the second-youngest Champions League hat-trick scorer ever.

## Peak years

The 2021-22 Champions League run made him. His late header against Chelsea took the quarter-final to extra time; his two goals in the 90th and 91st minutes against Manchester City turned a lost semi-final into an extra-time win; and he started the final against Liverpool. The following year he scored both goals in the Copa del Rey final against Osasuna and a brace against Liverpool at Anfield, and he was named in [Brazil](/nations/brazil)'s 2022 World Cup squad, where he played in the quarter-final.

In 2023-24 he scored 17 goals as Madrid won the double, playing on the right of a front two or three alongside [Vinícius Júnior](/players/vinicius-junior) and [Jude Bellingham](/players/jude-bellingham). The arrival of [Kylian Mbappé](/players/kylian-mbappe) in 2024 squeezed his role, and 2024-25 and the first half of 2025-26 were seasons of frustration, speculation about a move to the Premier League and reduced minutes under [Xabi Alonso](/managers/xabi-alonso). The knee injury in March 2026 ended his season and his World Cup hopes.

## How he plays

Rodrygo is technically the most complete of Madrid's forwards. He is genuinely two-footed, comfortable in tight spaces, and a natural combination player who plays one-twos around the box rather than taking defenders on at pace. His timing of runs into the area is excellent, which explains the late goals, and his finishing is calm, often with the inside of either foot.

He lacks the raw speed of Vinícius or Mbappé and does not dominate games physically. When he plays as a wide forward he can drift out of matches, and his pressing is intermittent. His best football has come as a second striker or an inside forward with a target to play off.

## Evolution

Zidane used him as a right winger. [Carlo Ancelotti](/managers/carlo-ancelotti) used him as a right winger, a second striker next to Benzema, and later as a false nine. Alonso used him sparingly. Under [José Mourinho](/managers/jose-mourinho) his role is unknown because he has not yet played; with [Yan Diomande](/players/yan-diomande) arriving on the right, the second-striker or impact-substitute role seems his most likely route back. For Brazil, [Carlo Ancelotti](/managers/carlo-ancelotti) has used him on the right and in the middle.

- 2024, Champions League final, v Dortmund: a second European Cup at Wembley.

## Current situation

Rodrygo underwent surgery in March 2026 and, as of November 2026, is meeting his rehabilitation milestones with a return targeted for early 2027. His contract runs to 2028. Mourinho has spoken of him as part of his plans, but with Diomande, Vinícius and Mbappé ahead of him the second half of 2026-27 will determine whether his future is in Madrid or elsewhere.
