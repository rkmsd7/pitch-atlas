## Overview

Chloe Kelly has scored the two most important goals in [England](/nations/england-women)'s history: the extra-time winner against Germany in the Euro 2022 final at Wembley, celebrated with her shirt swung above her head, and the winning penalty in the Euro 2025 final shootout against Spain. In between she came off the bench to set up Michelle Agyemang's equaliser and score the extra-time winner against Italy in the semi-final. At club level she won the 2025 [Champions League](/tournaments/womens-champions-league) and the 2026 FIFA Women's Champions Cup with [Arsenal](/clubs/arsenal-women).

## Early life and breakthrough

Raised on a west London estate as the youngest of seven, Kelly learned the game in cage football with her brothers. She came through Arsenal's academy, scored on her debut at 17, and was loaned to Everton, where she stayed permanently and scored nine goals in 2019-20. [Manchester City](/clubs/manchester-city-women) signed her in 2020 and she won the FA Cup and League Cup before an ACL tear in May 2021 cost her a year.

## Peak years

She returned in time for Euro 2022 and wrote herself into history. The 2023 World Cup final followed, and she scored the fastest-recorded penalty in a Lionesses shootout against Nigeria. By January 2025 she had fallen out with City's coaching staff and forced a loan to Arsenal, where she helped win the Champions League in Lisbon, then signed permanently. Euro 2025 was her masterpiece as a substitute. In 2025-26 she scored her first Arsenal hat-trick against West Ham and won the Champions Cup.

## How she plays

Kelly is an old-fashioned winger in the best sense. She stays wide right, isolates her full-back, and beats her on the outside with a quick feint before crossing low or lofted to the back post. Her delivery from corners and free-kicks is England's best. She also drifts to the left to cut inside onto her right foot, and she finishes cut-backs from the edge of the six-yard box. Her penalty technique, a stuttered run and a hard, high strike, is practised to the point of inevitability. Defensively she tracks diligently. The main criticism of her City years, inconsistency as a starter, has been answered by her impact as a game-changer.

## Evolution

Under Sarina Wiegman she has become the ultimate impact substitute, entering the pitch with a defined role in the last half-hour, and she has embraced it. Under Renée Slegers at Arsenal she is a starter again, used on either wing, and her 2026 numbers show a more consistent goal threat.

## Best plays and moments

- Winner against Germany, Euro 2022 final, Wembley, 31 July 2022.
- Fastest-recorded penalty in the 2023 World Cup shootout against Nigeria.
- Assist and extra-time winner against Italy, Euro 2025 semi-final, Geneva.
- Cross for Russo's equaliser and the winning penalty, Euro 2025 final, Basel.
- Hat-trick against West Ham, 21 March 2026, and the 2026 Champions Cup with Arsenal.

## Legacy / Current situation

As of November 2026 Kelly, 28, is an Arsenal regular and England's most reliable clutch player heading into the [2027 World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) play-offs. Few players have defined two tournaments the way she has.
