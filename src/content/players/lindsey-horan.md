## Overview

Lindsey Heaps, who has used her married name since 2025 after a decade as Lindsey Horan, is the captain of the [United States](/nations/united-states-women) and the most experienced player in Emma Hayes's squad, with 180 caps and 40 goals. She led the USA to Olympic gold in Paris 2024, won the 2019 World Cup, and spent four years at [Lyon](/clubs/lyon-women) winning five French titles and the 2022 [Champions League](/tournaments/womens-champions-league) before joining Denver Summit FC, the [NWSL](/leagues/nwsl)'s new Colorado franchise, on 12 January 2026 on a three-and-a-half-year contract.

## Early life and breakthrough

From Golden, Colorado, she turned down a scholarship at North Carolina to sign for [Paris Saint-Germain](/clubs/paris-saint-germain-women) at 18 in 2012, a radical move at the time. She scored 46 league goals in four seasons as a striker, debuted for the USA in 2013 and returned to the Portland Thorns in 2016, where Mark Parsons converted her into a midfielder.

## Peak years

She was NWSL MVP in 2018, won the Championship in 2017 and Shields in 2016 and 2021, and was US Soccer's player of the year in 2021. Her move to Lyon in 2022 brought the Champions League in her first season. She was the USA's best player at the 2023 World Cup, scoring a towering header against the Netherlands, and was appointed co-captain with [Alex Morgan](/players/alex-morgan) before the tournament. Under Hayes she captained the Paris Olympic campaign, and in 2026 she scored at the SheBelieves Cup and against Japan before winning a tenth Lyon trophy in the Coupe de France final and a fifth league title in May.

## How she plays

Heaps is a complete central midfielder. She can play as the deepest of three, where her passing range and tackling matter, or as the advanced eight, where her late runs and heading produce goals. For the USA she is the one who sets the tempo, often slowing the game when the forwards want to sprint. Her aerial ability from corners is a genuine weapon. Her limitations are mobility over long distances and occasional over-reliance on the forward pass; critics in the US have questioned whether she presses enough, which Hayes has answered by giving her the armband.

## Evolution

Striker at PSG, number eight at Portland, controller at Lyon under Sonia Bompastor and Joe Montemurro, and now the senior leader of a brand-new club. For the national team she has been a key player under four coaches.

## Best plays and moments

- 46 goals for PSG as a teenage striker.
- NWSL MVP 2018 and the 2017 Championship with Portland.
- Champions League winner with Lyon, Turin 2022.
- Header against the Netherlands, 2023 World Cup, Wellington.
- Captaining the USA to Olympic gold in Paris, 2024, and five straight French titles.

## Legacy / Current situation

As of November 2026 Heaps, 32, is captain of a USWNT preparing for the Concacaf W Championship and the [2027 World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup), and the face of Denver Summit's first season.
