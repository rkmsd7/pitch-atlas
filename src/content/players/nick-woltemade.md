## Overview

Nick Woltemade does not look like a striker who should be able to do what he does. At 1.98 m he dribbles like a winger, drops into midfield like a number 10 and finishes with a delicacy that made him the most talked-about German forward of 2025, when he scored 17 for [VfB Stuttgart](/clubs/vfb-stuttgart), won the Golden Boot at the under-21 Euros and became [Newcastle United](/clubs/newcastle-united)'s record signing at around £65m. A difficult first Premier League season followed, and on 2 September 2026 he joined [Juventus](/clubs/juventus) on a season-long loan, where he scored in a 5-0 Europa League win over NEC Nijmegen within weeks of arriving.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Bremen, Woltemade came through at Werder, debuting at 18 in 2020 as the club's youngest ever Bundesliga player. He struggled for minutes and was loaned to Elversberg in the 2. Bundesliga in 2023-24, where 10 goals in 33 games revived his career. Stuttgart signed him on a free transfer in 2024, and under Sebastian Hoeneß he became a star: 17 goals in all competitions including five in the DFB-Pokal, which Stuttgart won against Arminia Bielefeld in May 2025. He made his Germany debut in June 2025 and scored six goals at the under-21 Euros that month.

## Peak years

Bayern pursued him through summer 2025 but Stuttgart refused to sell to a domestic rival. Newcastle, having lost Alexander Isak to Liverpool, paid the fee on 30 August 2025. His debut season under Eddie Howe produced 11 goals in around 50 appearances, a return he admitted left him unsatisfied. Under new head coach [Matthias Jaissle](/managers/matthias-jaissle), appointed in August 2026, he fell behind William Osula and Yoane Wissa and was allowed to leave on loan, with Juventus paying a fee of around £3.5m with no option to buy.

## How he plays

Woltemade's first touch is the surprise. He receives with his back to goal and, instead of holding the ball up, turns and dribbles, using long strides and a surprisingly low centre of gravity. He plays one-twos around the box, finishes with either foot (the left is stronger) and scores more goals with placement than power. His height makes him a threat from crosses, though his aerial game is less dominant than his frame suggests.

The weaknesses are pace, pressing intensity and a tendency to drift out of games when the service is slow. He is not a channel-runner, and in a direct team he can be isolated. Premier League defenders found him easier to bully than Bundesliga ones had.

## Evolution

A winger-forward at Werder, a number 10 at Elversberg, a striker at Stuttgart who dropped deep, a conventional 9 at Newcastle that did not suit him, and now, under [Luciano Spalletti](/managers/luciano-spalletti) at Juventus, a striker in a possession system with licence to drop. For Germany, he debuted under Julian Nagelsmann; [Jürgen Klopp](/managers/jurgen-klopp), since July 2026, has kept him in the squad.

## Current situation

Woltemade is on loan at Juventus for 2026-27 under Spalletti, competing for the striker role in a side playing in the Europa League and chasing a return to the Champions League. Newcastle, who remain his parent club, have said they will stay in close contact. He is in Klopp's Germany squad.
