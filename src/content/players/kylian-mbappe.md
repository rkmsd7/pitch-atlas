## Overview

Kylian Mbappé has been the most-discussed footballer in the world for nearly a decade, and in 2026 he was, by most measures, the best attacking player in it. He scored ten goals at the [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) in North America to win a second Golden Boot, even as [France](/nations/france) fell to [Spain](/nations/spain) in the semi-final and then lost a bizarre 6-4 third-place match to England. He finished second in the 2026 Ballon d'Or behind [Harry Kane](/players/harry-kane), having scored at a rate [Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid) had not seen since [Cristiano Ronaldo](/players/cristiano-ronaldo). He is 27, wears the number 10, and has the one trophy he moved to Madrid for, the Champions League, still to win.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Paris and raised in Bondy, in Seine-Saint-Denis, Mbappé was a prodigy at AS Bondy, where his father coached, and at Clairefontaine. Every big club in Europe wanted him at 13; he chose [Monaco](/clubs/monaco). He debuted at 16 in December 2015, breaking Thierry Henry's record as the club's youngest ever player, and in 2016-17 he exploded: 26 goals as Monaco won Ligue 1 and reached the Champions League semi-final, scoring in both legs against Manchester City and Borussia Dortmund.

[Paris Saint-Germain](/clubs/paris-saint-germain) signed him in 2017 on a loan that became a €180m transfer, the most expensive teenager ever. He scored 21 in his first season and then, at 19, scored four goals at the 2018 World Cup, including one in the final against Croatia, the first teenager to score in a final since Pelé.

## Peak years

Mbappé spent seven years at PSG and scored 256 goals, the club record. He was Ligue 1's top scorer six times, won six titles and reached the 2020 Champions League final. In the 2022 World Cup final against Argentina he scored a hat-trick, including two goals in 97 seconds to drag France from 2-0 down, and still ended on the losing side on penalties. His eight goals won the Golden Boot.

After a saga lasting years, he joined Real Madrid on a free transfer in summer 2024. The first season was uneven for the team, with [Carlo Ancelotti](/managers/carlo-ancelotti) leaving after a trophyless league campaign, but Mbappé scored 31 in [La Liga](/leagues/la-liga) to win the Pichichi and the European Golden Shoe. In 2025-26, under [Xabi Alonso](/managers/xabi-alonso) and then, after Alonso left mid-season, an interim regime, he was again the top scorer in Spain. The 2026 World Cup brought the Golden Boot with ten goals, a single-tournament record for France.

## How he plays

The first thing is the acceleration. Mbappé's first three steps are the fastest in the game, and his first touch is designed to set them off: he receives already moving, with the ball played in front of him, so that the defender is beaten before he has reacted. His finishing is early and low, usually across the goalkeeper with the inside of the right foot, and he rarely needs a second touch in the box.

Over the last three years he has become a more complete centre-forward. He drops to link play, holds the ball up better than he used to and is more willing to play one-twos rather than always going alone. His left foot is adequate rather than strong. The weaknesses are structural: he does not press with conviction, he can drift out of games when the service is poor, and his body language under frustration has been a recurring theme at both club and international level. He has also been affected by a run of muscle injuries since 2025.

## Evolution

Leonardo Jardim used him as a right winger at Monaco. At PSG he was a left winger in a front three with Neymar and Cavani, then the central striker when Neymar and Messi arrived around him, and finally the whole attack. At Real Madrid Ancelotti played him through the middle, with [Vinícius Júnior](/players/vinicius-junior) on the left, which caused friction and a positional debate that lasted a season. Xabi Alonso's 3-4-2-1 freed him to drift left again. Under [José Mourinho](/managers/jose-mourinho), from July 2026, he has been used as a classic number 9 in a 4-2-3-1, with [Yan Diomande](/players/yan-diomande) and Vinícius wide. For France, Didier Deschamps built the national team around him; [Zinedine Zidane](/managers/zinedine-zidane-manager), who took over in July 2026, has kept him as captain.

## Current situation

Mbappé is the focal point of José Mourinho's Real Madrid in 2026-27, a team rebuilt around his goals, [Jude Bellingham](/players/jude-bellingham)'s running and the €125m arrival of Diomande from Leipzig. Mourinho's more direct, counter-attacking approach suits his instincts, and his scoring rate has not dropped. For France, he captains Zidane's new era, with the Nations League and Euro 2028 the next targets. The Champions League remains the missing trophy, and in Madrid that is the only thing that will define his legacy.
