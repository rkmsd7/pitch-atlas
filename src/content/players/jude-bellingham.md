## Overview

Jude Bellingham was a Championship footballer at 16, a Bundesliga regular at 17, a [Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid) talisman at 20 and a Champions League winner at 21. Still only 23, he has already played in two European Championship finals' worth of pressure and a [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) semi-final, and he carries himself, at club and country, like a captain. His 2023-24 season, 23 goals and the number 5 shirt once worn by Zidane, made him a global star; the seasons since have been about turning that into sustained leadership.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Stourbridge, Bellingham joined Birmingham City at eight and debuted at 16 years and 38 days in August 2019, the club's youngest ever player. He scored his first senior goal that month and started 32 Championship games in his only season, after which Birmingham retired his number 22. [Borussia Dortmund](/clubs/borussia-dortmund) beat Manchester United to him in summer 2020 for around £25m.

In Dortmund he was a starter from the first week, scoring on his debut in the DFB-Pokal, and he won that competition in 2021 under Edin Terzić. By 2022-23 he was Dortmund's captain on occasion at 19 and the Bundesliga's Player of the Season, as the club missed the title on the final day. He went to the 2022 World Cup as England's first-choice midfielder and scored against Iran.

## Peak years

Real Madrid paid an initial €103m in June 2023. [Carlo Ancelotti](/managers/carlo-ancelotti) rebuilt the team without Karim Benzema around Bellingham as a free-roaming number 10 in a 4-4-2 diamond, and the effect was immediate: 13 goals in his first 13 games, a stoppage-time winner at Barcelona in his first Clásico and a wide-armed celebration that became the season's signature image. Madrid won [La Liga](/leagues/la-liga) and the [Champions League](/leagues/champions-league), beating Dortmund 2-0 at Wembley, and Bellingham won the Kopa Trophy, Golden Boy and finished third in the 2024 Ballon d'Or.

Euro 2024 added an overhead-kick equaliser against Slovakia in the 95th minute and a runners-up medal. The 2024-25 season, with [Kylian Mbappé](/players/kylian-mbappe) arriving and Bellingham pushed back into midfield, was less prolific and ended with shoulder surgery that delayed his start to 2025-26. He returned in October 2025 under [Xabi Alonso](/managers/xabi-alonso) and, after Alonso's mid-season exit, was one of the few constants in a turbulent Madrid campaign. At the 2026 World Cup he was England's outstanding midfielder, scoring in the knockouts as [Thomas Tuchel](/managers/thomas-tuchel)'s side beat France 6-4 in the third-place match after losing the semi-final to [Argentina](/nations/argentina).

## How he plays

Bellingham's trademark is the late run. He sits in the left half-space, waits for the striker to drag a centre-back away and arrives on the edge of the six-yard box at the moment the cutback is played. His finishing is unfussy, usually a first-time side-foot. He is an exceptional ball-carrier through the middle, using his strength to ride challenges, and his pressing from midfield is relentless, often the trigger for Madrid's counter-press.

His passing is good rather than great; he is not a creator of chances from deep in the manner of a true playmaker, and his crossing is average. He collects yellow cards for dissent, and his temperament under provocation has been a talking point. The shoulder that was operated on in 2025 had been dislocating for two years. Physically, though, he is built for a long career.

## Evolution

At Birmingham he was a winger and attacking midfielder. Dortmund made him an 8 in a double pivot, where his defensive work matured. Ancelotti unlocked him as a 10, then as a left-sided 8 in a 4-3-3 when Mbappé arrived. Alonso used him as one of two number 10s behind the striker in a 3-4-2-1. Under [José Mourinho](/managers/jose-mourinho), from July 2026, he is the left-sided attacking midfielder in a 4-2-3-1, with licence to attack the box when Mbappé pulls wide. For England, Gareth Southgate used him as a 10; Tuchel has used him as a deeper 8 with [Harry Kane](/players/harry-kane) dropping to link.

## Best plays and moments

- 2019, Championship, v Stoke: first senior goal at 16, Birmingham's youngest ever scorer.
- 2021, DFB-Pokal final, v RB Leipzig: a winner's medal at 17.
- 2022, World Cup, v Iran: his first England goal in a 6-2 win.
- 2023, La Liga, Barcelona 1-2 Real Madrid: a stoppage-time winner in his first Clásico ([watch](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Bellingham+Clasico+winner+2023+Real+Madrid)).
- 2024, Champions League final, v Borussia Dortmund: the 15th European Cup at Wembley.
- 2024, Euro 2024, v Slovakia: a 95th-minute overhead kick to save England ([watch](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Bellingham+overhead+kick+Slovakia+Euro+2024)).
- 2024, Euro 2024 final, v Spain: runner-up in Berlin.
- 2026, World Cup third-place match, England 6-4 France: part of the strangest medal match in the tournament's history.

## Current situation

Bellingham is central to Mourinho's first Real Madrid season since 2013, in a side rebuilt with [Yan Diomande](/players/yan-diomande) on the wing and a more direct style. He is fit, scoring and increasingly the team's on-pitch leader alongside [Federico Valverde](/players/federico-valverde). For [England](/nations/england), he is a certain starter under Tuchel and one of the players expected to lead the next cycle alongside [Bukayo Saka](/players/bukayo-saka). His contract runs to 2029.
