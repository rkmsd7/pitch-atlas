## Overview

Ada Hegerberg is the record scorer in the history of the [Women's Champions League](/tournaments/womens-champions-league) and the woman who received the first Ballon d'Or Féminin in 2018. She has spent 12 years at [Lyon](/clubs/lyon-women), now OL Lyonnes, scoring over 185 league goals and winning six European titles, and she captains [Norway](/nations/norway-women), for whom she passed 100 caps in 2026 after a self-imposed five-year exile.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Molde and raised in Sunndalsøra, Hegerberg and her sister Andrine played for Kolbotn and Stabæk, where Ada scored 25 goals in a single Toppserien season at 17. A year at Turbine Potsdam followed before Lyon signed her at 19 in 2014. She was a Euro 2013 finalist with Norway at 18.

## Peak years

From 2016 to 2019 Hegerberg was the most lethal striker in Europe. She scored 20 goals in the 2015-16 Champions League, won UEFA's player of the year, and scored a hat-trick inside 30 minutes of the 2019 final against Barcelona in Budapest. The 2018 Ballon d'Or ceremony became notorious for the host asking her to twerk; her answer, no, and her speech urging girls to believe in themselves, defined the moment. In 2017 she withdrew from Norway in protest at the federation's treatment of the women's team and missed the 2019 World Cup. A ruptured ACL and a stress fracture cost her almost two years from January 2020. She returned in late 2021, scored in the 2022 Champions League final win over Barcelona, and rejoined Norway in March 2022 with a hat-trick against Kosovo. Lyon named her captain in 2023; she scored three goals in two World Cup qualifiers against Slovenia in April 2026 and reached 100 caps that year.

## How she plays

Hegerberg is a striker of the box. Her movement, especially the curved run across the near post, creates space where none appears to exist, and her finishing is economical: side-foot, head, either foot, from a yard or 20. She is strong in the air and in holding the ball, and she has become a vocal leader for Lyon and Norway. She does not dribble past players and does not create from deep; she is the end of the move, and the best teams she has played in have been built to supply her.

## Evolution

Fast and raw at Stabæk, she became a complete penalty-box striker under Gérard Prêcheur at Lyon. The injuries of 2020-21 took her explosiveness; she returned as a more static target, relying on positioning, and under Jonatan Giráldez she now shares the striking role with Marie-Antoinette Katoto while providing leadership. For Norway under Gemma Grainger she is the captain and penalty taker.

## Best plays and moments

- 25 goals in 18 games for Stabæk at 17 (2012).
- Record 20 goals in the 2015-16 Champions League campaign.
- Hat-trick in the 2019 Champions League final, Lyon 4-1 Barcelona, Budapest.
- First women's Ballon d'Or, Paris, December 2018.
- Goal in the 2022 Champions League final, Lyon 3-1 Barcelona, Turin, and the Norway return hat-trick against Kosovo.

## Legacy / Current situation

As of November 2026 Hegerberg is Lyon's co-captain with Wendie Renard, under contract to summer 2027, and Norway's captain for the December play-off against Wales that decides whether she reaches a first [World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) since 2015. Her Champions League record of over 60 goals may never be beaten.
