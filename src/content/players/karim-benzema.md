## Overview

Karim Benzema was, for a decade, the most underrated great striker in the world, the man who made [Cristiano Ronaldo](/players/cristiano-ronaldo)'s goals possible at [Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid), and then, after Ronaldo left, the best player on the planet. His 2021-22 season, 44 goals including hat-tricks against PSG and Chelsea and a Champions League winner's medal, won him the 2022 Ballon d'Or. He joined Al Ittihad in Saudi Arabia in 2023 and won the league and cup double in 2024-25. His contract there ended in summer 2026, and he is not listed in Al Ittihad's current squad. His next step was not confirmed in the sources available for this profile.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Lyon to Algerian parents and raised in Bron, Benzema came through at [Lyon](/clubs/lyon) and debuted at 17 in 2005. By 2007-08 he was Ligue 1's best player, scoring 31 goals as Lyon won their seventh straight title. Real Madrid paid around €35m in 2009, in the same summer as Ronaldo and Kaká.

## Peak years

His first Madrid years were spent as a foil: under Mourinho and then [Carlo Ancelotti](/managers/carlo-ancelotti) he was the striker who dropped deep to create space for Ronaldo and Bale, in the "BBC" front three that won La Décima in 2014. He was underrated because his numbers lagged theirs; the coaches understood.

When Ronaldo left in 2018, Benzema became the focal point. He scored 30 goals in 2019-20 and 2020-21, then 44 in 2021-22, the season that defined him: a hat-trick in 17 minutes against PSG to overturn the round of 16, a hat-trick at Stamford Bridge and the goal at the Bernabéu that beat Chelsea, two goals against Manchester City in the semi-final and the Pichichi. He won the Ballon d'Or in October 2022, missed the World Cup through injury and retired from [France](/nations/france) duty in December 2022.

## How he plays

Benzema's first touch was the finest of any striker of his generation. He received with his back to goal and turned, or laid off first time, with a weight of pass that made the next player's job simple. He dropped into midfield, played one-twos and arrived in the box late, and from 2018 onwards he added a scorer's ruthlessness: both feet, the head, the far-post finish across the keeper. His penalties, after a miss in the 2021 Euro shoot-out, became reliable.

The weaknesses were pace in behind, which he never had, and periods of low intensity. His relationship with the French public was complicated by a legal case in 2015 that cost him five years of international football.

## Evolution

A pure striker at Lyon, a false-nine-style link man in Madrid's BBC years, a complete number 9 and captain after Ronaldo left, and then, at Al Ittihad under Nuno Espírito Santo and Laurent Blanc, a goalscoring veteran with the freedom of a 10.

## Best plays and moments

- 2008, Ligue 1: 31 goals and a seventh straight Lyon title.
- 2014, Champions League final, v Atlético Madrid: La Décima.
- 2018, Champions League final, v Liverpool: a goal in the 3-1 win in Kyiv.
- 2022, Champions League round of 16, Real Madrid 3-1 PSG: a 17-minute hat-trick ([watch](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Benzema+hat+trick+PSG+2022+Real+Madrid)).
- 2022, Champions League quarter-final, Chelsea 1-3 Real Madrid: a hat-trick at Stamford Bridge ([watch](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Benzema+hat+trick+Chelsea+Stamford+Bridge+2022)).
- 2022, Champions League final, v Liverpool: a fifth European Cup.
- 2022, Ballon d'Or: the first French winner since Zidane.
- 2025, Saudi Pro League and King's Cup: a double with Al Ittihad ([watch](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Benzema+Al+Ittihad+goals)).

## Current situation

Benzema's Al Ittihad contract ran to June 2026 and he left the club that summer; as of November 2026 his next club, or a decision to retire, could not be confirmed. He has been retired from international football since December 2022. His status as one of the great modern strikers is secure regardless.
