## Overview

Raphinha is the proof that a career can peak at 28. Written off as a squad player after his first two seasons at [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona), he responded to [Hansi Flick](/managers/hansi-flick)'s arrival with the most productive season by a Barcelona player since [Lionel Messi](/players/lionel-messi) left, followed by a second league title and, in summer 2026, the captaincy. He is the first Brazilian to wear the armband at the club.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Porto Alegre, Raphinha never played for a major Brazilian club. He came through at Avaí in Santa Catarina, moved to Portugal with Vitória Guimarães at 19 and worked his way up: [Sporting CP](/clubs/sporting-cp), then Rennes in France, then Leeds United in 2020 for around £17m. At Leeds, under Marcelo Bielsa, he became one of the Premier League's most watchable wingers and earned his first [Brazil](/nations/brazil) caps, scoring twice on his first start against Uruguay in 2021.

Barcelona paid Leeds around €58m in 2022. His first two seasons were decent but uneven, with 10 and 10 goals, and by summer 2024 he was widely expected to be sold.

## Peak years

Flick kept him and made him a captain. In 2024-25 Raphinha scored 34 goals and set up 25 in 57 games, including 13 goals in the Champions League, a hat-trick in the 5-4 win at Benfica, and goals in both legs of the semi-final against [Inter Milan](/clubs/inter-milan). Barcelona won [La Liga](/leagues/la-liga), the Copa del Rey and the Supercopa, and he finished fifth in the 2025 Ballon d'Or.

2025-26 brought a second straight league title. In 2026-27, with the armband, he began the season on a remarkable scoring run, reaching double figures across all competitions by the end of September.

## How he plays

Flick's Barcelona play with a very high line and press aggressively, and Raphinha is the trigger. He is usually the first forward to jump at the centre-back, and his pressing angles are the best in the squad. In possession he plays on the left in a nominal 4-2-3-1, but he rarely stays wide: he drifts inside into the left half-space and arrives late in the penalty area, where many of his goals come from, while Alejandro Balde overlaps.

His finishing is about power and accuracy rather than finesse, often a low drive across the goalkeeper. His left foot is strong enough that defenders cannot simply show him onto it. The weaknesses are a tendency to over-hit in tight areas and a certain rashness in the tackle that produces yellow cards. He is also 29 going on 30, and the volume of sprints his role demands will eventually catch up with him.

## Evolution

At Leeds he was a right winger who cut inside. At Barcelona under Xavi he played both wings and often looked lost. Flick moved him to the left, gave him licence to attack the box and asked him to lead the press, and the transformation was immediate. For Brazil, [Carlo Ancelotti](/managers/carlo-ancelotti) has used him more conservatively on the right, which is part of why his international record has lagged his club form.

## Best plays and moments

- 2021, World Cup qualifier, v Uruguay: two goals on his first Brazil start.
- 2022, La Liga, v Real Madrid: a goal in the 2-1 Clásico win at Camp Nou.
- 2024, Champions League, v Bayern Munich: a hat-trick in a 4-1 win.
- 2025, Champions League, Benfica 4-5 Barcelona: hat-trick in a ten-man comeback ([watch](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Raphinha+hat+trick+Benfica+5+4)).
- 2025, Copa del Rey final, v Real Madrid: assist in the 3-2 extra-time win.
- 2025, Champions League semi-final, v Inter: goals in both legs of a 7-6 aggregate epic.
- 2026, La Liga: named captain and starts the season with a goal every game.

## Current situation

Raphinha is Barcelona's captain for 2026-27 following the departures of Ronald Araújo and Marc-André ter Stegen and Frenkie de Jong's knee injury. He has said he wants to finish his career at the club, and reports suggest an extension is close. With [Lamine Yamal](/players/lamine-yamal) on the right and [Karim Adeyemi](/players/karim-adeyemi) competing for minutes, he remains the first name in Flick's attack. For [Brazil](/nations/brazil), he was part of Ancelotti's 2026 World Cup squad and remains a regular.
