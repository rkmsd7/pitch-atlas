## Overview

Khvicha Kvaratskhelia arrived at [Napoli](/clubs/napoli) in 2022 as an unknown €10m signing from Dinamo Batumi and within a season was "Kvaradona", the best player in a title-winning side and Serie A's MVP. In January 2025 he moved to [Paris Saint-Germain](/clubs/paris-saint-germain) for around €70m and within five months had won the Champions League, scoring in the semi-final against Arsenal. A second Champions League followed in 2026. He is the greatest footballer [Georgia](/nations/georgia) has produced and the man who took them to their first major tournament.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Tbilisi, Kvaratskhelia came through at Dinamo Tbilisi and moved to Russia at 18, first on loan at Lokomotiv Moscow and then at Rubin Kazan, where he was one of the Russian Premier League's best young players. He left Russia in March 2022 after the invasion of Ukraine, returned to Georgia with Dinamo Batumi for a few months, and was signed by Napoli's Cristiano Giuntoli that summer as Lorenzo Insigne's replacement.

## Peak years

His debut Serie A season was one of the great individual campaigns in the league's modern history: 12 goals and 10 assists as Luciano Spalletti's Napoli won the Scudetto for the first time since Maradona, with a style built on his dribbling from the left and Victor Osimhen's running through the middle. He was named the league's MVP and finished 17th in the Ballon d'Or. In 2024 he led Georgia at Euro 2024, scoring against Portugal in a famous group win that took them to the round of 16.

A contract dispute with Napoli ended with the move to PSG in January 2025. [Luis Enrique](/managers/luis-enrique) used him on the left of a front three with Dembélé and Doué, and he scored against Aston Villa and Arsenal en route to the 5-0 final win over Inter. In 2025-26 he won a second league title and a second Champions League.

## How he plays

Kvaratskhelia's dribbling is built on deception rather than speed. He slows, feints, shifts the ball to the other foot and then goes, usually inside onto his right foot from the left, but often outside with his left. From the inside-left channel he shoots with a swerving right-footed strike or clips a cross to the far post. He is a creator as much as a scorer, and his through-balls after a dribble are a feature.

His engine is enormous, which lets him press and track back in a Luis Enrique system. The weaknesses are decision-making at the end of a dribble, a tendency to take one touch too many, and a finishing record that has fluctuated. He can be fouled out of games.

## Evolution

A left winger throughout, but Spalletti's Napoli gave him freedom to roam while Luis Enrique's PSG asks him to hold width in the first phase and press aggressively in the second. For Georgia, Willy Sagnol builds the team around him.

## Current situation

Kvaratskhelia is a first-choice winger in Luis Enrique's 2026-27 PSG, rotating with [Désiré Doué](/players/desire-doue) and [Ousmane Dembélé](/players/ousmane-dembele), with a contract to 2029. For Georgia he remains captain and talisman.
