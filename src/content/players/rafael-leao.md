## Overview

Rafael Leão was, for seven seasons, the most exciting and most frustrating player at [AC Milan](/clubs/ac-milan): the MVP of the 2021-22 Scudetto, scorer of the fastest goal in Serie A history, a winger who could win a game in a minute and disappear for the other 89. In summer 2026, after Milan missed the Champions League places by a point and changed coach, he joined [Galatasaray](/clubs/galatasaray), where he wears 27 alongside Victor Osimhen, Leroy Sané and İlkay Gündoğan in the most star-studded squad in Turkish football.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Almada, across the river from Lisbon, to Angolan parents, Leão came through at [Sporting CP](/clubs/sporting-cp) and debuted at 18 in 2018. The attack on Sporting's training ground by ultras that May led him to terminate his contract, and he joined Lille on a free, scoring eight goals in Ligue 1 at 19. Milan paid around €30m in 2019.

## Peak years

His first two Milan seasons were uneven, but under Stefano Pioli in 2021-22 he became the best player in Italy: 11 league goals and 10 assists from the left, the Serie A MVP award and Milan's first title in 11 years. In 2022-23 he scored in the Champions League quarter-final against Napoli and reached the semi-final. He signed a contract to 2028 in 2023 and remained Milan's talisman through the Pioli, Fonseca, Conceição and Allegri eras, winning the 2025 Supercoppa and the 2025 Nations League with [Portugal](/nations/portugal). In 2025-26 Milan finished fifth, one point behind Como, and he moved on in the summer.

## How he plays

Leão's gift is acceleration from a standing start. He receives on the left touchline with the full-back close, takes one touch past him and is gone, with the ball played far ahead of him so that his stride length does the work. He cuts inside to shoot with his right or goes outside to cross or cut back. In transition he is nearly unstoppable; against a deep block, far less so.

The weaknesses are well known: concentration, work-rate off the ball, decision-making in the final third and a body language that invites criticism. His finishing is inconsistent for a player who gets so many chances. His heading, for a 1.88 m winger, is poor.

## Evolution

A striker at Sporting and Lille, a left winger at Milan who occasionally played as a second striker under Allegri, and now, at Galatasaray, a left winger in a possession team with Osimhen as the target. For Portugal, Roberto Martínez used him as an impact substitute; Jorge Jesus's plans since 2026 are still forming.

The Galatasaray move is a gamble for both sides. For Leão it is a chance to be the star of a team built to win in Turkey and compete in Europe, in a league where his pace and power are overwhelming and where the Champions League nights at Rams Park are among the loudest in the competition. For the club it is a bet that a player whose consistency was the eternal question at Milan can be unlocked by a system with Osimhen as the target and Sané and Gündoğan as the creators. The early signs in the Süper Lig were promising, with Leão scoring and assisting in his first weeks, and the Champions League group stage is where the move will be judged.

## Current situation

Leão is in his first season at Galatasaray, playing in the [Süper Lig](/leagues/super-lig) and the Champions League, in a squad built to win in Europe. The transfer fee and contract details were not confirmed in the sources available for this profile. He remains in Portugal's squad under Jorge Jesus.
