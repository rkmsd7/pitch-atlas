## Overview

Trinity Rodman is the face of the new [United States](/nations/united-states-women). Drafted second overall by the [Washington Spirit](/clubs/washington-spirit) in 2021 at 18, she won the NWSL Championship and Rookie of the Year in her first season, scored the goal of the Paris 2024 Olympics against Japan, and in January 2026 signed a three-year contract worth more than $2m a year that made her the best-paid player in the women's game. Her 12 goals in 30 games in 2026 were a career high.

## Early life and breakthrough

The daughter of NBA Hall of Famer Dennis Rodman, she grew up in Southern California largely without him and has spoken frankly about it. Her college season at Washington State was cancelled by the pandemic, so she entered the 2021 draft and was an immediate star, scoring six goals as Washington won the title under chaotic circumstances. She made her USWNT debut in 2022 and was a starter at the 2023 World Cup.

## Peak years

Under Emma Hayes she became one of the "Triple Espresso" front three with [Sophia Wilson](/players/sophia-smith) and [Mallory Swanson](/players/mallory-swanson). At Paris 2024 she scored against Zambia and then, in the 105th minute of the quarter-final against Japan, cut inside from the right and curled a right-footed shot into the far top corner, the goal of the tournament. She started the 1-0 final win over Brazil. A back injury and a knee sprain limited her 2025 season to 15 league games, but the Spirit reached the Championship final again and won the Challenge Cup. In 2026, fully fit, she scored twice in January friendlies for the USA and led Washington to third in the regular season.

## How she plays

Rodman is a right winger who wants the ball at her feet facing goal. She uses a long first touch to take defenders on, a feint to open the inside, and then either a cross from the byline or a curled shot from the angle. Her strength lets her hold off challenges and her pace covers 60 yards in transition. She presses from the front with intensity. Her end product has fluctuated; the 2026 numbers suggest it has settled. She can also play on the left or as a striker.

## Evolution

A raw athlete in 2021, she has learned under Jonatan Giráldez and Adrián González at Washington to play inside a possession structure and pick her moments. For Hayes she has become a more disciplined presser and a more clinical finisher.

## Best plays and moments

- Rookie-season NWSL Championship, 2021, and Rookie of the Year.

- Olympic gold, Paris, August 2024.

## Legacy / Current situation

As of November 2026 Rodman, 24, is in the [NWSL](/leagues/nwsl) play-offs with Washington, which hosts the Championship on 21 November, and is central to Hayes's plans for the Concacaf W Championship and the [2027 World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup).
