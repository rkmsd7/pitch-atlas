## Overview

Clàudia Pina spent years as the best substitute in Europe before becoming one of its best starters. The [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona-women) forward was top scorer in the 2024-25 [Champions League](/tournaments/womens-champions-league), won the 2025-26 [Liga F](/leagues/liga-f) Golden Boot with 21 goals, and was named in FIFA's world eleven for 2025. For [Spain](/nations/spain-women) she scored in the Euro 2025 run and in World Cup qualifiers against Iceland and England in 2026.

## Early life and breakthrough

From Montcada i Reixac near Barcelona, Pina played for Espanyol's youth teams before joining Barça at 12. She was a phenomenon at youth level: 35 goals in 29 games for Spain Under-17s, the Golden Ball and Golden Boot at the 2018 Under-17 World Cup Spain won, and an Under-20 World Cup final the same year. She made her first-team debut at 17 in 2018.

## Peak years

A loan to Sevilla in 2020-21 gave her a full season of starts. Back at Barcelona she became the first option off the bench, scoring crucial goals, including in the 2023 Champions League quarter-final against Roma. The 2024-25 season changed her status: nine Champions League goals, the top-scorer award, a Team of the Season place and Barcelona's player of the year. In 2025-26 she started most games, won the Golden Boot and a third Champions League, and in the summer of 2026 the departures of [Alexia Putellas](/players/alexia-putellas) and [Salma Paralluelo](/players/salma-paralluelo) made her a permanent starter.

## How she plays

Pina is a left-footed inside forward who plays from the left wing or as a second striker, and her defining action is the early shot: she receives around the edge of the box, takes one touch to set it and curls the ball into the far corner before the keeper is set. She also scores from free-kicks and takes corners. Her small frame makes her hard to dispossess but limits her in aerial duels, and she is not a pacy runner in behind, which is why Barcelona have often paired her with Paralluelo or [Ewa Pajor](/players/ewa-pajor). Her pressing is diligent and her link-up with [Aitana Bonmatí](/players/aitana-bonmati) instinctive.

## Evolution

Youth striker, loanee, super-sub, Champions League top scorer, Golden Boot winner. Pere Romeu's decision to trust her as a starter in 2025 was vindicated, and under Sonia Bermúdez she has become a Spain regular rather than a late option.

## Best plays and moments

- Golden Ball and Golden Boot at the 2018 Under-17 World Cup.
- Quarter-final goal against Roma in the 2023 Champions League.
- Top scorer in the 2024-25 Champions League.
- 21 goals and the 2025-26 Liga F Golden Boot, plus a third Champions League in Oslo.
- Goal against England in Spain's 4-0 World Cup qualifying win, June 2026.

## Legacy / Current situation

As of November 2026 Pina, 25, is Barcelona's leading scorer and Spain's first-choice left forward heading towards the [2027 World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) in Brazil.
