## Overview

Vivianne Miedema is the most prolific scorer the WSL has seen and the [Netherlands](/nations/netherlands-women)' all-time leading scorer with 104 goals in 132 games. A European champion at 21 and a World Cup finalist at 23, she spent seven years as [Arsenal](/clubs/arsenal-women)'s talisman before moving to [Manchester City](/clubs/manchester-city-women) in 2024, where she became the first player to score ten or more goals in five different WSL seasons and won the 2025-26 league and FA Cup double.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Hoogeveen, Miedema scored 78 goals in 69 games for Heerenveen as a teenager and joined [Bayern Munich](/clubs/bayern-munich-women) at 18, winning two Bundesliga titles, including an unbeaten 2014-15. Arsenal signed her in 2017 and in her second season she scored 22 goals as the Gunners won the 2018-19 WSL, taking the PFA award.

## Peak years

From 2018 to 2021 she was arguably the best forward in the world: six goals and four assists in a single game against Bristol City in December 2019, the WSL scoring record passed in 2020, and for the Netherlands two goals in the Euro 2017 final against Denmark and a run to the 2019 World Cup final in Lyon. Ten goals at the Tokyo Olympics tied the record for a single Games. An ACL tear in December 2022 interrupted the run, and Arsenal let her leave in 2024 after she had scored 125 goals in 172 games. At City she rebuilt her game, switched to the number 10 shirt in August 2025, and scored twice in two minutes in a 3-0 derby win over United in March 2026 before adding the final goal of the FA Cup final against Brighton in May. A knee operation in June 2026 was declared successful.

## How she plays

Miedema is a striker who thinks like a midfielder. She drops into the space between the lines, receives with her back to goal and either turns or lays the ball off before spinning into the box. Her finishing is unfussy, placed rather than blasted, with both feet and a strong head. She is also a top-class creator, with assist numbers to match her goals. Her lack of celebration became part of the legend. Pace was never her weapon and the ACL injury reduced it further; her intelligence has compensated.

## Evolution

A pure number nine at Heerenveen and Bayern, a complete forward at Arsenal, and now a [false nine](/tactics/false-nine) or number 10 at City behind Khadija Shaw. For the Netherlands she has played every attacking role and remains the focal point under Arjan Veurink.

## Best plays and moments

- Two goals in the Euro 2017 final, Netherlands 4-2 Denmark, Enschede.
- Six goals and four assists against Bristol City, 1 December 2019.
- 2019 World Cup final appearance in Lyon.
- Ten goals at the Tokyo 2020 Olympics.
- Derby double against Manchester United, 28 March 2026, and the 2025-26 double.

## Legacy / Current situation

As of November 2026 Miedema, 30, is back from knee surgery, contracted to City until 2027 and a Netherlands regular heading into the December play-off against Slovenia. She is the benchmark every WSL striker is measured against.
