## Overview

Désiré Doué was 19 when he scored twice and set up another in the 2025 Champions League final, the most decisive performance by a teenager in a European final since Mbappé. A year later he collected a second winner's medal as [Paris Saint-Germain](/clubs/paris-saint-germain) beat Arsenal on penalties in Budapest. Two-footed, quick and tactically mature beyond his years, he is the most complete young French attacker since [Kylian Mbappé](/players/kylian-mbappe), and he plays for the same coach who turned [Ousmane Dembélé](/players/ousmane-dembele) into a Ballon d'Or winner.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Angers to Ivorian parents, Doué came through at Rennes alongside his older brother Guela and debuted in Ligue 1 at 17 in August 2022. He was a regular over two seasons, mostly on the wing or as a number 10, and was one of the stars of France's run to the Olympic final in Paris in 2024, scoring in the semi-final against Egypt. PSG paid around €50m in August 2024.

## Peak years

His first PSG season started slowly and finished as the best debut campaign in the club's history. [Luis Enrique](/managers/luis-enrique) moved him to the right wing in the new year, and from February 2025 he was unstoppable: a goal against Liverpool at Anfield in the round of 16, a brace against Aston Villa, and then two goals and an assist in the 5-0 final win over Inter in Munich, where he was man of the match. He scored 16 goals in all competitions and was named Ligue 1's Young Player of the Season.

The 2025-26 season brought a second league title and a retained Champions League, with Doué sharing the wide roles with [Khvicha Kvaratskhelia](/players/khvicha-kvaratskhelia), Bradley Barcola and Dembélé. He made his senior France debut in 2025 and was part of the 2026 World Cup squad.

## How he plays

Doué is genuinely two-footed, which makes him unpredictable on either wing: on the right he can cut in on his left or go outside on his right, and the finish is equally clean either way. His dribbling is elastic, close-controlled and sudden, with a feint that sends defenders the wrong way. He combines in tight spaces, playing one-twos around the box, and his shooting from 20 yards is a real threat.

He presses hard, which is why Luis Enrique trusts him. The weaknesses are consistency, as expected at 21, and a tendency to disappear from games when PSG's possession is slow. He suffered a serious leg injury in autumn 2025 that cost him several months.

## Evolution

Rennes used him as a 10 and a left winger. Luis Enrique moved him to the right, then rotated him across all three forward positions. In 2026-27, with [Ferran Torres](/players/ferran-torres) arriving as a striker, he competes for the wide roles with Dembélé and Kvaratskhelia. For France, [Zinedine Zidane](/managers/zinedine-zidane-manager) has used him as a substitute winger since July 2026.

## Current situation

Doué is a core member of Luis Enrique's 2026-27 squad, rotating with Dembélé and Kvaratskhelia in the wide positions, with a contract to 2029. For France, he is in Zidane's squad and expected to grow into a starter.
