## Overview

Federico Valverde is the player every [Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid) coach since Zidane has leaned on when a problem needed solving. Midfielder, right winger, right-back, emergency centre-back: he has done all of them in a season and been the team's most consistent performer in most of them. Two Champions Leagues, three league titles and a Clásico-winning Supercopa final have followed, and in 2026-27, under [José Mourinho](/managers/jose-mourinho), he is one of the dressing room's leaders.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Montevideo, Valverde came through at Peñarol and was signed by Real Madrid in 2016 at 18, after the 2015 under-17 World Cup. He spent a season with Castilla, then a year on loan at Deportivo La Coruña, before Julen Lopetegui and then Santiago Solari integrated him into the first team in 2018-19. Zidane, in his second spell, made him a starter: his tackle on Álvaro Morata in the 2020 Supercopa final, a red card that stopped a certain goal, was named the player of the match's defining act, and he was central to the 2019-20 title.

## Peak years

Valverde's best seasons were 2021-22 and 2022-23. In the first, he was the right-sided midfielder in [Carlo Ancelotti](/managers/carlo-ancelotti)'s 4-3-3 that won the double, and his cross set up [Vinícius Júnior](/players/vinicius-junior)'s winner in the Champions League final against Liverpool. In the second he played as a right winger for much of the season and scored 12 goals, many of them from 25 yards or more. He won the Copa del Rey and the Club World Cup that year.

In 2023-24 he moved back into midfield beside [Jude Bellingham](/players/jude-bellingham) and [Aurélien Tchouaméni](/players/aurelien-tchouameni) and won his second Champions League and third league title. In 2024-25 Dani Carvajal's injury pushed him to right-back for half the season, where he was excellent. He was named Uruguay's player of the year repeatedly and was a key figure in [Uruguay](/nations/uruguay)'s third-place finish at the 2024 Copa América under Marcelo Bielsa.

## How he plays

Valverde's defining quality is volume. He covers more distance than any teammate, makes more sprints and does it without obvious fatigue. In possession he carries the ball through midfield with long strides, often releasing it with a pass early, and his signature is the long-range strike: a flat, hard right-footed shot from the edge of the area that has produced some of Madrid's most spectacular goals.

He is also an elite ball-winner in the counter-press, and his tactical discipline is why coaches trust him at full-back. The weaknesses are creative. He is not a line-breaking passer, his crossing from the right is average, and his decision-making in tight spaces in the final third can be hurried. His finishing inside the box is less reliable than from outside it.

## Evolution

Zidane used him as a right-sided 8 and occasionally a right winger. Ancelotti used him everywhere: midfield, wing, full-back. [Xabi Alonso](/managers/xabi-alonso) in 2025-26 used him as a wing-back and as one of two central midfielders in a 3-4-2-1. Mourinho has settled him as the right-sided central midfielder in a 4-2-3-1, partnering Tchouaméni, with licence to overlap. For Uruguay, Bielsa plays him as a box-to-box 8 in a 4-3-3.

## Best plays and moments

- 2020, Supercopa final, v Atlético Madrid: the tactical foul on Morata that won the trophy ([watch](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Valverde+tackle+Morata+Supercopa+2020)).
- 2022, Champions League final, v Liverpool: the cross for Vinícius's winner.
- 2022, La Liga, v Barcelona: a thunderous goal in a 3-1 Clásico win.
- 2023, Champions League, v Chelsea: two goals at Stamford Bridge.
- 2023, Copa del Rey final, v Osasuna: a winner's medal.
- 2024, Champions League final, v Dortmund: a second European Cup at Wembley.
- 2024, Copa America, v Brazil: part of Uruguay's quarter-final win on penalties.
- 2024, La Liga, v Osasuna: a volley from distance that went viral.

## Current situation

Valverde is a certain starter for Mourinho in 2026-27, the midfield partner to Tchouaméni and the player Mourinho has publicly called his most important. He is a vice-captain and will likely inherit the armband. For Uruguay, he remains the heart of Bielsa's side after the 2026 World Cup.
