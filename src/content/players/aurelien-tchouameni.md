## Overview

Aurélien Tchouaméni is the quiet structure in a [Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid) team of loud attackers. Signed from [Monaco](/clubs/monaco) in 2022 for around €80m, he has spent four seasons alternating between holding midfield and centre-back depending on the injury list, and in 2026-27 under [José Mourinho](/managers/jose-mourinho) he has found his most settled role: the deepest midfielder in a 4-2-3-1, the man who lets [Federico Valverde](/players/federico-valverde) run and [Jude Bellingham](/players/jude-bellingham) attack.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Rouen to Cameroonian parents and raised in Bordeaux, Tchouaméni came through the Girondins academy and debuted at 18 in 2018. Monaco signed him in January 2020 for around €18m, and under Niko Kovač he became one of the best young midfielders in Europe, Ligue 1's Young Player of the Year in 2020-21 and a France international by September 2021. He played in the Nations League final win over Spain a month later.

Real Madrid beat Liverpool and PSG to his signature in June 2022, and he started the 2022 World Cup for [France](/nations/france), scoring a long-range goal against England in the quarter-final and playing in the final defeat to Argentina.

## Peak years

His first Madrid season under [Carlo Ancelotti](/managers/carlo-ancelotti) was good rather than spectacular; he won the Copa del Rey and the Club World Cup. The 2023-24 campaign was the breakthrough: with Éder Militão and David Alaba injured, he spent months at centre-back, excelled there, and still returned to midfield for the Champions League run, playing the final against Dortmund despite a foot injury. Madrid won the double.

In 2024-25 and 2025-26 he was again moved between positions, and his form was inconsistent at times as the team's structure changed. He was part of France's 2026 World Cup squad that lost the semi-final to [Spain](/nations/spain) and the third-place match 6-4 to England.

## How he plays

Tchouaméni is a duel-winner. He is tall, long-legged and quick enough to recover, and he reads the game well enough to intercept rather than chase. In the air he is dominant for a midfielder, which is why coaches have used him in defence. His distribution is safe and increasingly progressive, with a good switch of play, and he carries a genuine long-range shooting threat.

The criticisms concern tempo and risk. He can be slow to release the ball under pressure, occasionally plays sideways when a forward pass is on and has been caught in possession in dangerous areas. He is at his best when the players around him are mobile, which is why the Valverde partnership works.

## Evolution

Kovač used him as a box-to-box 8 at Monaco. Ancelotti used him as a 6, then as a centre-back, then as a 6 again. [Xabi Alonso](/managers/xabi-alonso) in 2025-26 used him both as the central defender in a back three and as a pivot. Mourinho has given him one job: anchor. For France, Didier Deschamps made him a starter in 2022, and [Zinedine Zidane](/managers/zinedine-zidane-manager), since July 2026, has retained him.

## Best plays and moments

- 2021, Nations League final, v Spain: a winner's medal at 21.
- 2022, World Cup quarter-final, v England: a 25-yard opener in Doha ([watch](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Tchouameni+goal+England+World+Cup+2022)).
- 2022, World Cup final, v Argentina: 120 minutes in the greatest final.
- 2023, Copa del Rey final, v Osasuna: a first Spanish trophy.
- 2024, Champions League final, v Dortmund: played through injury to win the European Cup.
- 2024, La Liga: a season as a centre-back that saved Madrid's title bid.
- 2025, Champions League, v Manchester City: a key figure in the play-off win.

## Current situation

Tchouaméni is Mourinho's first-choice holding midfielder in 2026-27 and one of the most-used players in the squad, partnering Valverde in a double pivot. His contract runs to 2028. For France he is a starter under Zidane.
