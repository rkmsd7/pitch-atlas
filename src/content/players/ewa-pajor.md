## Overview

Ewa Pajor is the striker who gave [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona-women) a cutting edge when Jonatan Giráldez's team needed one. Signed from [Wolfsburg](/clubs/wolfsburg-women) in 2024, she won the Liga F Golden Boot in her first season and in 2025-26 scored twice in the 4-0 [Champions League](/tournaments/womens-champions-league) final against Lyon, finishing as the tournament's top scorer with 11 and second to Alexia Putellas in the 2026 Ballon d'Or. She has captained [Poland](/nations/poland-women) for most of a decade and is their record scorer.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Uniejów in central Poland, Pajor scored 64 goals in 60 games for Medyk Konin as a teenager and won the Under-17 Euro with Poland in 2013, scoring in the final. Wolfsburg signed her at 18 in 2015 and she spent nine years there, scoring 96 league goals, winning five Bundesliga titles and nine consecutive DFB-Pokals, and reaching three Champions League finals.

## Peak years

Her best Wolfsburg season was 2022-23: top scorer in the Champions League with nine goals and a goal in the final against Barcelona in Eindhoven, where Wolfsburg led 2-0 before losing 3-2. She moved to Barcelona in summer 2024 for a reported fee around €500,000 and scored 25 league goals to win the Golden Boot in 2024-25. The 2025-26 season was better still, with a Champions League top-scorer award for the second time and the Oslo final brace. For Poland she scored the extra-time winner in a 3-2 World Cup play-off win over Belgium on 13 October 2026.

## How she plays

Pajor is a vertical striker in a horizontal team. Barcelona's possession game often lacked a runner in behind, and she provides it: constant movement across the back line, diagonal runs from the shoulder of the last defender and a willingness to shoot early. She presses the first line with intensity, which suits Barça's counter-press, and she is good with her back to goal for a 1.67 m player. Her finishing is powerful rather than delicate, and she scores with her head more than her size suggests. Weaknesses are a tendency to be offside and a less refined link-up game than Barcelona's wingers are used to.

## Evolution

A pure goalscorer in Poland, she became a pressing forward at Wolfsburg under Ralf Kellermann and Tommy Stroot, and at Barcelona has learned to combine in tight spaces with Aitana Bonmatí and Clàudia Pina. The Ballon d'Or runner-up finish in 2026 reflected a player who added goals to a side that previously spread them around.

## Best plays and moments

- Goal in the 2013 Under-17 Euro final for Poland.
- Nine goals and the top-scorer award in the 2022-23 Champions League, including the Eindhoven final.
- 25-goal Liga F Golden Boot season, 2024-25.
- Two goals in the 2026 Champions League final, Barcelona 4-0 Lyon, Oslo.
- Extra-time winner for Poland against Belgium in the October 2026 play-off.

## Legacy / Current situation

As of November 2026 Pajor, 29, is Barcelona's starting striker and the most important Polish footballer of her generation, with Poland in the second round of the [2027 World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) play-offs against the Republic of Ireland in December.
