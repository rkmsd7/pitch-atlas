## Overview

Lauren James is the most talented English footballer of her generation and, since taking the number 10 shirt at [Chelsea](/clubs/chelsea-women), the player the club builds its attack around. A European champion with [England](/nations/england-women) in 2025 and a four-time WSL winner, she has the rare ability to score goals nobody else on the pitch could attempt, and in March 2026 Chelsea tied her to a contract running until 2030.

## Early life and breakthrough

The younger sister of Chelsea men's captain Reece James, she grew up in west London, trained with Chelsea and Arsenal as a child and made her senior debut for Arsenal at 16 in 2017-18. A move to [Manchester United](/clubs/manchester-united-women) in 2018 as the reformed club's marquee teenager brought 28 goals in three seasons and a promotion. Chelsea paid a reported British record fee to bring her back to London in 2021.

## Peak years

Injuries slowed her first Chelsea season, but 2022-23 brought the PFA Young Player of the Year award and a starring role at the 2023 World Cup, where her goal and three assists against China were followed by a red card for stamping on Nigeria's Michelle Alozie in the round of 16. She returned for the final against Spain. Euro 2025 was her first major tournament win, playing through injury in the final before being substituted. Domestically she has four straight league titles and scored the opener in the 2026 League Cup final against United.

## How she plays

James's gifts are physical and technical in equal measure. She is strong enough to hold off defenders with her back to goal, balanced enough to turn them, and has a long-range shot that keepers fear from 25 yards. She is genuinely two-footed, cutting in from the right onto her left or driving down the line on her right. Sonia Bompastor uses her as a free number 10 who drifts wide, and England's Sarina Wiegman has started her on the right. The persistent questions are fitness, with hamstring and foot problems recurring, and temperament under provocation.

## Evolution

A striker at United, she became a right winger at Chelsea under Emma Hayes and a central playmaker under Bompastor. The 2025-26 season, with 41 Chelsea goals in all competitions now passed, showed a more consistent, less explosive player who contributes every week rather than in flashes.

## Best plays and moments

- Hat-trick of assists and a goal against China at the 2023 World Cup, Adelaide.
- Volleyed winner against Manchester United in the 2022-23 title race.
- Opening goal in the 2026 League Cup final, Chelsea 2-0 Manchester United, 15 March 2026.
- Euro 2025 final in Basel, starting through injury as England beat Spain on penalties.
- Goal in England's 5-0 World Cup play-off win over Greece, 13 October 2026.

## Legacy / Current situation

As of November 2026 James, 25, is Chelsea's creative centre and an England starter heading into the [2027 World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) play-offs. Her ceiling, if she stays fit, is the best player in the world.
