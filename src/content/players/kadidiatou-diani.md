## Overview

Kadidiatou Diani has been the most consistently dangerous French attacker for a decade. The top scorer of the 2023-24 [Champions League](/tournaments/womens-champions-league) with [Lyon](/clubs/lyon-women), the Silver Boot winner at the 2023 [World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup), and the holder of 127 caps and 33 goals for [France](/nations/france-women), she joined London City Lionesses on 15 July 2026 on a three-year deal reported at around £500,000, joining [Alexia Putellas](/players/alexia-putellas) in the Michele Kang-owned club's ambitious [WSL](/leagues/wsl) squad.

## Early life and breakthrough

Born in Ivry-sur-Seine to a Malian family, Diani came through Juvisy (now Paris FC), debuting in Division 1 at 16 and for France at 19 in 2014. Her pace made her a winger of obvious promise; her finishing took longer. She won the Under-19 Euro with France in 2013.

## Peak years

[Paris Saint-Germain](/clubs/paris-saint-germain-women) signed her in 2017 and she became the league's best winger, scoring 74 league goals in six seasons, winning the 2021 title that ended Lyon's 14-year run, and being named Division 1 player of the year. Her move to Lyon in 2023 was the first direct transfer between the rivals for years; in her first season she was the Champions League's top scorer with eight goals and won the league. At the 2023 World Cup she scored a hat-trick against Panama and finished with four goals, winning the Silver Boot. She was a regular for France at Euro 2025 and scored in a 9-0 win over Latvia in October 2026.

## How she plays

Diani is a power winger. She receives on the right, takes a touch to set herself and then runs, beating full-backs on the outside with pure acceleration. Her crossing is low and hard; her shooting, once a weakness, became a strength around 2020 when she started cutting inside and hitting the far corner. She presses aggressively and tracks back, which has endeared her to every coach she has had. She can lead the line, which Hervé Renard and Laurent Bonadei have used for France. Her weakness is decision-making at the end of long runs, which still occasionally lets her down.

## Evolution

A pure winger at Juvisy, she became a goalscoring wide forward at PSG under Olivier Echouafni and a Champions League top scorer at Lyon. At London City she is the senior forward in a team built to rise quickly through the WSL.

## Legacy / Current situation

As of November 2026 Diani, 31, is a London City starter and a France regular with the [2027 World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) in Brazil already secured. She is among the most-capped active French players and still climbing the list.
