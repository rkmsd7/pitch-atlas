## The idea

The false nine wears the centre-forward's shirt but does not play the centre-forward's game. Instead of pinning the centre-backs and waiting for service, he drops into the space between the opponent's defence and midfield. That creates a dilemma for the defenders. Follow him, and a hole opens behind for wingers to run into. Stay, and the false nine receives unmarked with the whole game in front of him.

It is a role for a playmaker with a striker's instincts, or a striker with a playmaker's brain. [Lionel Messi](/players/lionel-messi) under [Pep Guardiola](/managers/pep-guardiola) is the definitive example: 47 goals in 2009-10, 73 in 2011-12, all from a position where nobody could decide who should mark him.

## How it works on the pitch

**The drop.** As the ball reaches the midfield, the false nine moves towards it, into the pocket in front of the opponent's centre-backs. The midfielders behind him adjust: one eight pushes beyond him into the space he has vacated.

**The runners.** The wingers are the key. With no striker occupying the centre-backs, the wingers (Villa and Pedro at Barcelona, Saka and Martinelli at Arsenal) make diagonal runs inside and behind. The false nine's trademark pass is the through-ball to a winger cutting inside from the far side.

**The dribble.** Messi's version added the ability to turn and run at a retreating defence. Firmino's version at Liverpool was more about pressing and linking: he dropped, Mane and Salah ran.

**Pressing.** The false nine is the first defender. Because he already lives near the opponent's pivot, he is perfectly placed to press it. Firmino made the role as much about work rate as about goals.

**Set shape.** In possession the team looks like a 4-6-0 or a 4-3-3 with a hollow centre. The box is attacked late by runners from midfield.

```
   Opponent CBs:      5        4
                         |
                      9 (drops)
             11 ->              <- 7
                  8         10
                       6
```

## Origins and evolution

The withdrawn centre-forward is one of football's oldest tricks. Matthias Sindelar, "the Paper Man", played it for Austria's Wunderteam in the 1930s. Nandor Hidegkuti was the deep-lying nine of Hungary's Golden Team, who beat England 6-3 at Wembley in 1953 while England's centre-half Harry Johnston spent the game wondering who to mark. Roma's Luciano Spalletti played Francesco Totti as a false nine in a 4-6-0 in 2005-07, and Spain's Cesc Fabregas filled the role when they won Euro 2012 without a recognised striker.

The modern explosion came on 2 May 2009 at the Bernabeu. Guardiola moved Messi from the right wing to the centre against Real Madrid, Barcelona won 6-2, and three weeks later beat Manchester United in the Champions League final with the same idea. Messi stayed there for three years and broke every scoring record in the game. Roberto Firmino gave the role a pressing dimension at Jurgen Klopp's Liverpool (2015-2023), and Guardiola used Bernardo Silva, Phil Foden and Kevin De Bruyne as false nines in different seasons at Manchester City, including the 2021 Premier League title with no striker at all.

The [positional-play](/tactics/positional-play) school embraced the false nine because it is the purest way to create the free man between the lines. The counter-movement was the return of the big nine, Haaland, Kane, Isak, who pin defences in a way the false nine cannot.

## Who does it best

- **[Roberto De Zerbi](/managers/roberto-de-zerbi)'s [Tottenham Hotspur](/clubs/tottenham-hotspur)**: De Zerbi's build-up is designed to find a dropping forward in the pocket, and he has used false nines throughout his career.
- **[Hansi Flick](/managers/hansi-flick)'s [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona)**: Lewandowski is a classic nine, but when he rests, Dani Olmo or Ferran Torres drop into the Messi space and [Lamine Yamal](/players/lamine-yamal) and Raphinha run beyond.
- **[Luis Enrique](/managers/luis-enrique)'s [Paris Saint-Germain](/clubs/paris-saint-germain)**: Dembele as a false nine won the 2025 Ballon d'Or and two Champions Leagues; he drifts, drops and runs with the whole front line rotating around him.
- **[Spain](/nations/spain)** retain the heritage: the 2026 World Cup winners used Dani Olmo as a dropping ten behind a mobile nine.
- **[Mikel Arteta](/managers/mikel-arteta)** has used Havertz and Merino as hybrid nines who drop and link at [Arsenal](/clubs/arsenal).

## Weaknesses and counters

A false nine gives you no presence in the box, so the team relies on wingers and midfielders to score. Against a deep, disciplined [low block](/tactics/low-block) that refuses to follow the false nine out, the space he drops into is already crowded, and the attack becomes a game of crosses with nobody to meet them. Centre-backs who are comfortable stepping into midfield (the Stones type) can mark the false nine without opening the gap behind. A man-marking midfielder assigned to him (Vogts on Cruyff, Pirlo's shadow in the 2000s) can shut the role down. And it needs a very particular player; without one, a false nine is simply a team with no striker.
