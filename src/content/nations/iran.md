## History

Iran were Asia's dominant team in the 1970s, winning three straight [Asian Cups](/tournaments/asian-cup) in 1968, 1972 and 1976 and reaching their first [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) in 1978, where they drew with Scotland. The revolution and the war with Iraq isolated the team for a decade, and the next qualification came in 1998 after a dramatic play-off against Australia. In France Iran beat the United States 2-1 in Lyon, a match loaded with political meaning, with Hamid Estili and Mehdi Mahdavikia scoring.

[Ali Daei](/players/ali-daei), who held the international scoring record for years with 109 goals, led the 2006 campaign, and Carlos Queiroz's long tenure from 2011 to 2019 made Iran the best-organised side in Asia: 2014 and 2018 World Cups, a 1-0 win over Morocco in 2018 and a draw with Portugal that left them one goal short of the knockouts. The 2022 World Cup brought a win over Wales and a narrow defeat to the United States amid the protests at home.

Amir Ghalenoei, a domestic coaching icon, took over in 2023, reached the 2023 Asian Cup semi-final and qualified Iran for a fourth consecutive World Cup. At the 2026 tournament, played against a backdrop of travel-visa complications, Iran again qualified; the round they reached could not be verified at the time of writing.

## Philosophy and style

Queiroz built Iran around a [low block](/tactics/low-block), relentless defensive discipline and [counter-attacks](/tactics/counter-attacking) through Sardar Azmoun and [Mehdi Taremi](/players/mehdi-taremi). Ghalenoei, whose Sepahan teams were attack-minded, has given more licence to the full-backs and to Alireza Jahanbakhsh and Mohammad Mohebi on the wings, but the 4-1-4-1 shape and the physical edge in midfield, where Saeid Ezatolahi screens, remain the foundation. Alireza Beiranvand's long throws and kicks are a trademark.

Team Melli's matches are national events in a country where football is the one unifying passion, and the Azadi Stadium's 80,000 crowds give Iran a home advantage few Asian teams can match. The rivalry with Saudi Arabia, often politically charged, and with South Korea and Japan in qualifying define the Asian calendar. The 2026 World Cup was complicated by visa issues for travelling supporters and by the team's base in Mexico, and the players continue to carry the weight of the social tensions at home that overshadowed the 2022 campaign.

## Managers who shaped them

- Heshmat Mohajerani: the 1976 Asian Cup and 1978 World Cup.
- Jalal Talebi: the 1998 win over the United States.
- Branko Ivanković: the 2006 World Cup.
- Carlos Queiroz: 2011-19 and 2022, three World Cups.
- Amir Ghalenoei: from 2023.

## Current squad and setup

Beiranvand is in goal behind Shoja Khalilzadeh and Milad Mohammadi; Ezatolahi and Omid Noorafkan hold midfield with Saman Ghoddos creating; Jahanbakhsh captains from the right; Taremi, now at [Inter Milan](/clubs/inter-milan), and Azmoun lead the line. The 2027 Asian Cup in Saudi Arabia, where Iran have not won since 1976, is the overwhelming priority, with the under-23s aiming for a first Olympic appearance since 1976 in 2028.
