## History

The Netherlands were an amateur backwater until the 1970s, three Olympic bronzes their only distinction. Then Rinus Michels' [Ajax](/clubs/ajax) and [Johan Cruyff](/players/johan-cruyff) invented [Total Football](/tactics/total-football), and the 1974 [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) team took it to the world. They beat Argentina, East Germany and Brazil, scored in the final before West Germany had touched the ball, and lost 2-1. In 1978, without Cruyff, they lost another final to the hosts, Argentina, Rob Rensenbrink hitting the post in the last minute of normal time.

Michels returned to win [Euro 1988](/tournaments/euro) with Marco van Basten's impossible volley against the Soviet Union and the AC Milan trio of Van Basten, Gullit and Rijkaard. The 1990s produced beautiful teams that lost on penalties: the Euro 1992 semi-final, the 1998 World Cup semi-final against Brazil, Euro 2000 at home. Dick Advocaat and Marco van Basten could not break the pattern.

Bert van Marwijk's cynical 2010 side reached the final and lost to Spain, and Louis van Gaal's 2014 team finished third after a 5-1 demolition of the holders. The team then missed Euro 2016 and the 2018 World Cup altogether. Ronald Koeman, in his second spell from 2023, reached the Euro 2024 semi-final, but the 2026 World Cup ended in the round of 32 with a 1-1 draw and penalty defeat to Morocco in Monterrey. Koeman resigned within a day. On 12 August 2026 the KNVB appointed [Xavi](/managers/xavi-manager) on a contract through the 2030 World Cup, the first foreign coach of Oranje since Ernst Happel in 1978.

## Philosophy and style

The Dutch school, exported through Cruyff to [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona), is [Total Football](/tactics/total-football): positional interchange, pressing to win the ball high and a 4-3-3 with genuine wingers. Every Dutch youth academy teaches it. Xavi's version is [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) with a controlling pivot, in this case Frenkie de Jong or Tijjani Reijnders, inside full-backs and patient circulation until a winger is isolated. The counter-argument has always been the Dutch tendency to over-pass and the fragility of a [high line](/tactics/high-line), which [Virgil van Dijk](/players/virgil-van-dijk)'s reading of the game has helped mask.

Dutch football is in a period of reflection. [Ajax](/clubs/ajax) have declined from the 2019 Champions League semi-final, [PSV Eindhoven](/clubs/psv-eindhoven) have become the dominant domestic force, and the [Eredivisie](/leagues/eredivisie) remains a selling league, so the national team is drawn from Liverpool, Barcelona, Inter, Manchester City and Bayer Leverkusen. The KNVB's choice of Xavi, a Cruyff disciple who played under Van Gaal and Rijkaard, is an attempt to reconnect the senior side with the positional tradition the country exported.

The rivalries are deep: Germany, from the 1974 final through Rijkaard and Völler in 1990 to the Nations League meetings of 2024-25; Belgium, the Low Countries derby; and Argentina, opponents in the 1978 final, the 1998 and 2014 semi-finals and the bad-tempered 2022 quarter-final. The Netherlands have never won the World Cup, and the Dutch public, who have seen three finals lost, treat each new cycle with a mixture of hope and dread.

## Managers who shaped them

- Rinus Michels: Total Football, the 1974 final and Euro 1988.
- Guus Hiddink: the 1998 World Cup semi-final.
- [Louis van Gaal](/managers/louis-van-gaal): third place in 2014 and the 2022 quarter-final.
- Bert van Marwijk: the 2010 final.
- [Ronald Koeman](/managers/ronald-koeman): two spells, Euro 2024 semi-final, resigned 2026.
- [Xavi](/managers/xavi-manager): appointed August 2026.

## Current squad and setup

Xavi's first camps in September and October 2026 used a 4-3-3 with Bart Verbruggen in goal, Van Dijk, 35 and still captain, alongside Micky van de Ven, Jeremie Frimpong and Denzel Dumfries as attacking full-backs, Frenkie de Jong and Reijnders in midfield with Xavi Simons as the creator, and Cody Gakpo and Memphis Depay in attack. The task is Euro 2028 qualifying, beginning in 2027, and a Nations League campaign that will test whether the new positional structure can finally add a second trophy to the 1988 title.
