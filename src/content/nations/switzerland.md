## History

Switzerland hosted the 1954 [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) and reached the quarter-finals for the third tournament running, a 7-5 defeat to Austria still the highest-scoring World Cup match. The famous "verrou", Karl Rappan's bolt system, was an ancestor of [catenaccio](/tactics/catenaccio). Then came the wilderness: no World Cup between 1966 and 1994, when Roy Hodgson's team, with Stéphane Chapuisat and Alain Sutter, reached the round of 16.

Köbi Kuhn's side went out of the 2006 World Cup without conceding a goal, on penalties to Ukraine. Ottmar Hitzfeld's 2010 team beat eventual champions Spain in the group opener, and Vladimir Petković's generation of Xhaka, Shaqiri and Sommer reached the round of 16 at three straight tournaments before the great night of [Euro 2020](/tournaments/euro), when they beat France on penalties from 3-1 down.

Murat Yakin took over in 2021, reached the 2022 round of 16 and the Euro 2024 quarter-final, where England needed penalties. At the 2026 World Cup Switzerland beat Algeria 2-0 in the round of 32, eliminated Colombia on penalties after a 0-0 draw in the round of 16 and went out in the quarter-finals.

## Philosophy and style

Switzerland's identity is organisation and a multicultural talent pool; the Kosovo-Albanian, Cameroonian and Turkish diaspora has supplied most of the recent stars. Yakin's 3-4-2-1 defends in a mid-block with Manuel Akanji sweeping, [Granit Xhaka](/players/granit-xhaka) dictating from the pivot and Remo Freuler covering. In attack they are [counter-attacking](/tactics/counter-attacking) specialists, Dan Ndoye and Breel Embolo running the channels, with Rúben Vargas as a creative wide forward. Yann Sommer's reflexes and the aerial power of Nico Elvedi at [set pieces](/tactics/set-pieces) complete a team that rarely beats itself.

Swiss football has grown on the back of the Basel and Young Boys academies and a youth system that won the under-17 World Cup in 2009 with Xhaka and Shaqiri. The [Bundesliga](/leagues/bundesliga) and Serie A are the main export markets, and the national team's multicultural make-up, the subject of political debate at home, is its greatest strength. The rivalries with Germany and Italy are neighbourly rather than bitter, and the penalty win over France at Euro 2020 remains the nation's favourite night.

## Managers who shaped them

- Karl Rappan: the verrou and four spells between 1937 and 1963.
- Roy Hodgson: the 1994 World Cup return.
- Köbi Kuhn: Euro 2004, the 2006 World Cup and Euro 2008 at home.
- Ottmar Hitzfeld: the 2010 win over Spain and the 2014 round of 16.
- Vladimir Petković: Euro 2020 quarter-final.
- Murat Yakin: from 2021, the 2024 Euro and 2026 World Cup quarter-finals.

## Current squad and setup

Yakin's November 2026 squad is settled: Sommer or Gregor Kobel in goal, Akanji, Elvedi and Ricardo Rodríguez across the back, Xhaka, the captain, with Freuler in midfield, Vargas and Zeki Amdouni behind Embolo, and Ndoye as the game-changer. Euro 2028 qualifying begins in 2027, and the Nations League campaign in autumn 2026 doubled as a chance to integrate the under-21 generation.
