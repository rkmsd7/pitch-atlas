## History

Senegal did not reach a [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) until 2002, and then announced themselves by beating holders France 1-0 in the opening game in Seoul, Papa Bouba Diop scoring. Bruno Metsu's team, with El Hadji Diouf and Henri Camara, reached the quarter-finals and lost to Turkey on a golden goal, having also reached the 2002 [AFCON](/tournaments/afcon) final.

A long hangover followed, with two missed World Cups and a failed home AFCON in 2012. [Aliou Cissé](/players/aliou-cisse), the 2002 captain, took over in 2015 and built the strongest team in the country's history around [Sadio Mané](/players/sadio-mane), Kalidou Koulibaly and Idrissa Gueye: the 2018 World Cup, lost on fair-play points; the 2019 AFCON final; and finally the 2021 AFCON title, won on penalties against Egypt in Cameroon in February 2022. The same year Senegal reached the World Cup round of 16, losing to England.

Cissé was dismissed in October 2024 after the 2023 AFCON round-of-16 exit, and Pape Thiaw, his assistant, was confirmed as head coach in December 2024. Thiaw's team won the January 2026 AFCON final in Morocco, a result later overturned by CAF on appeal, and at the World Cup beat Iraq 5-0 to qualify from the group before losing 3-2 after extra time to Belgium in the round of 32. The federation dismissed Thiaw and his staff on 11 July 2026. Patrick Vieira was reported as the favourite to succeed him; the appointment as of November 2026 could not be verified at the time of writing.

## Philosophy and style

Senegalese football combines the physical gifts of a country that exports hundreds of players to Europe with the tactical discipline of coaches trained in France. Cissé's 4-3-3 was a compact mid-block with a [counter-attacking](/tactics/counter-attacking) front three; Thiaw added a higher press and more possession. Koulibaly's reading of the game allows a relatively [high line](/tactics/high-line), and Pape Matar Sarr and Lamine Camara give the midfield both energy and passing range.

Senegal's football culture runs through the Génération Foot and Diambars academies and a diaspora in France that produced Mané, Koulibaly and Gueye. The 2021 AFCON title, the first in the country's history, sparked celebrations that the president marked with a national holiday, and the 2025 final controversy with Morocco, overturned by CAF, has given the continental rivalry a bitter edge. The fixture with Egypt, the 2021 final and the 2022 World Cup play-off, is the other rivalry that matters most.

## Managers who shaped them

- Claude Le Roy: the 1990 AFCON semi-final.
- Bruno Metsu: the 2002 World Cup quarter-final.
- Aliou Cissé: 2015-24, the 2021 AFCON title.
- Pape Thiaw: 2024-26.

## Current squad and setup

The core in November 2026 is unchanged whoever holds the reins: Édouard Mendy in goal, Koulibaly, 35, still captain alongside Moussa Niakhaté, Pape Matar Sarr and Camara in midfield, Iliman Ndiaye as the creator, and Mané, Ismaïla Sarr and Nicolas Jackson or Habib Diallo in attack. AFCON 2027 in East Africa is the next major tournament, with the under-23s targeting the 2028 Olympics.
