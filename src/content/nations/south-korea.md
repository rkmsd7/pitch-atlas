## History

South Korea won the first two [Asian Cups](/tournaments/asian-cup) in 1956 and 1960 and were Asia's first representatives at a [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) in 1954, where they lost 9-0 to Hungary. They have qualified for every World Cup since 1986, a record eleven consecutive appearances to 2026, and the tournament they co-hosted in 2002 remains the defining moment: Guus Hiddink's team beat Portugal, Italy and Spain to reach the semi-final, with Ahn Jung-hwan's golden goal against Italy and the red-shirted crowds the enduring images.

Park Ji-sung's generation reached the round of 16 in 2010; the 2018 team beat holders Germany 2-0; Paulo Bento's 2022 side reached the round of 16 with Hwang Hee-chan's late winner against Portugal. Jürgen Klinsmann's chaotic spell ended after the 2023 Asian Cup semi-final, and the 2002 captain [Hong Myung-bo](/players/hong-myung-bo) took over in 2024.

The 2026 World Cup was a failure: one win and two defeats, including a loss to Mexico, and a group-stage exit that drew public criticism from President Lee Jae-myung. Hong resigned on 28 June 2026. The KFA ran a search for interim and permanent coaches over the summer, with Paulo Bento and Roberto Martínez linked and Gus Poyet reported as an interim candidate. The identity of the coach in charge as of November 2026 could not be verified at the time of writing.

## Philosophy and style

Korean football is built on running, pressing and collective sacrifice; the 2002 team covered more ground than any side in the tournament. The modern version adds genuine quality: [Son Heung-min](/players/son-heung-min)'s finishing, [Lee Kang-in](/players/lee-kang-in)'s dribbling and Kim Min-jae's aggressive defending. The default is a 4-2-3-1 that becomes a 4-4-2 out of possession, with [counter-attacking](/tactics/counter-attacking) through the wide forwards and a willingness to defend in a [low block](/tactics/low-block) against stronger sides.

Korean football culture is organised around the K League, the Seoul and Suwon rivalries and the Red Devils supporters' club that filled the streets in 2002. The export route runs from the K League to Germany and England, and Son's decade at Tottenham made him the country's most popular athlete. The rivalry with Japan is the defining fixture in Asian football, and the 2026 failure has been framed domestically as a crisis of leadership at the KFA rather than of talent, given a squad drawn from Bayern Munich, PSG and the Premier League.

- Hong Myung-bo: 2024-26.

## Current squad and setup

With the coaching position in flux, the core is unchanged: Kim Seung-gyu in goal, Kim Min-jae and Seol Young-woo in defence, Hwang In-beom and Lee Jae-sung in midfield, Lee Kang-in as the 10, Hwang Hee-chan and Son, 34 and playing for LAFC in [MLS](/leagues/mls) since 2025, around Cho Gue-sung or Oh Hyeon-gyu. The 2027 Asian Cup in Saudi Arabia, starting 7 January 2027, is the immediate target, with the under-23 side chasing a 2028 Olympic place.
