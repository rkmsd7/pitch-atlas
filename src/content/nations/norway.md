## History

Norway's one great early moment was the 1936 Olympic bronze and a 2-0 win over Germany in front of Hitler in Berlin. They then lost at the 1938 [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) to Italy after extra time and spent fifty years as amateurs on the fringe. Egil "Drillo" Olsen changed the country's football in the 1990s with a ruthlessly direct, zonal, long-ball system: Norway rose to second in the FIFA ranking in 1993, qualified for USA 94 and beat Brazil 2-1 at France 98 to reach the round of 16.

Euro 2000 was the last tournament for a generation. Through the 2000s and 2010s, despite Ole Gunnar Solskjær, John Carew and John Arne Riise, Norway kept missing play-offs, losing the Euro 2020 play-off to Serbia in extra time. Ståle Solbakken was appointed in December 2020 to build around two extraordinary talents, [Erling Haaland](/players/erling-haaland) and [Martin Ødegaard](/players/martin-odegaard). The 2022 and Euro 2024 campaigns fell just short, but the 2026 qualifying group was a procession, Haaland scoring freely as Norway topped a group containing Italy and qualified directly for their first World Cup in 28 years. The round they reached in North America could not be verified at the time of writing.

## Philosophy and style

Drillo's legacy is a national comfort with [direct play](/tactics/direct-play), and Solbakken's Norway still move the ball forward fast. The 4-3-3 asks Sander Berge and Patrick Berg to win it and release Ødegaard, whose passes into Haaland's runs or to the wide forwards Antonio Nusa and Oscar Bobb produce most goals. Alexander Sørloth gives a second aerial target. Defensively Norway sit in a mid-block and are vulnerable to teams who press their build-up, hence Solbakken's efforts to add a more patient [positional](/tactics/positional-play) phase.

Norwegian football's rise is tied to Haaland and Ødegaard but underpinned by a coaching culture that emphasises long-term player development, from the Bryne and Drammen academies to the export route through Molde, Bodø/Glimt and the Eredivisie. Bodø/Glimt's European runs in the 2020s and the women's team's long success have given Norway a football profile beyond its five million people. The rivalry with Sweden and Denmark in the Nordic derbies is friendly but intense, and 1998's win over Brazil remains the benchmark every team is measured against.

## Managers who shaped them

- Egil Olsen: 1990-98 and 2009-13, the long-ball revolution and France 98.
- Nils Johan Semb: Euro 2000.
- Lars Lagerbäck: 2017-20, the Euro 2020 play-off.
- Ståle Solbakken: from 2020, the 2026 World Cup qualification.

## Current squad and setup

Ørjan Nyland is in goal, Kristoffer Ajer and Leo Østigård form the centre-back pair, Berge and Berg protect, Ødegaard captains from the number 8 or 10 role, and Haaland, [Manchester City](/clubs/manchester-city)'s record-breaking striker, leads the line with Sørloth, Nusa and Bobb around him. Solbakken's contract runs through Euro 2028 qualifying, which begins in 2027, and Norway hope to send Haaland and Ødegaard's generation to a first Euro since 2000.
