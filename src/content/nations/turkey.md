## History

Turkey played their first international in 1923 and reached the 1954 [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) through a drawing of lots against Spain, then waited 48 years for a second appearance. The breakthrough generation of the late 1990s, built on Galatasaray's 2000 UEFA Cup winners, reached the [Euro 2000](/tournaments/euro) quarter-finals and then produced the greatest result in the nation's history: third place at the 2002 World Cup under Şenol Güneş, beating Senegal with a golden goal and South Korea 3-2 in the bronze match, where Hakan Şükür scored after 11 seconds, still the fastest World Cup goal.

Fatih Terim's Euro 2008 side became famous for late comebacks, scoring in the final minutes against Switzerland, the Czech Republic and Croatia before losing the semi-final to Germany. The long gap that followed, with failures to reach the 2010, 2014, 2018 and 2022 World Cups and a dismal Euro 2020, ended under Vincenzo Montella, appointed in September 2023. Turkey reached the Euro 2024 quarter-finals, Merih Demiral scoring twice against Austria and [Arda Güler](/players/arda-guler) announcing himself, and qualified for the 2026 World Cup through the March 2026 play-offs. They were drawn in a group with the United States, Paraguay and Australia; the round they reached could not be verified at the time of writing.

## Philosophy and style

Turkish football is emotional and attacking, with a tradition of technical midfielders from Lefter to Emre to [Hakan Çalhanoğlu](/players/hakan-calhanoglu). Montella's 4-2-3-1 presses in a [gegenpressing](/tactics/gegenpressing) style, with Orkun Kökçü and Çalhanoğlu as the double pivot, Güler as the creative hub and Kenan Yıldız and Barış Alper Yılmaz as inverted wingers who cut inside to shoot. Ferdi Kadıoğlu and Zeki Çelik push high from full-back. The weakness is defensive concentration; the strength is that Turkey never stop attacking.

Turkish football culture is among the loudest in the world. The Istanbul derbies between [Galatasaray](/clubs/galatasaray), [Fenerbahçe](/clubs/fenerbahce) and Beşiktaş, the [Süper Lig](/leagues/super-lig)'s recruitment of ageing stars and the diaspora in Germany that produced Güler's predecessors Mesut Özil and İlkay Gündoğan all shape the national team. The 2024 Euro run brought scenes in Istanbul and Berlin not seen since 2008, and Montella's young squad, with Güler and Yıldız both under 22, is the most promising the country has had since 2002.

## Managers who shaped them

- Fatih Terim: four spells, Euro 1996 qualification and the Euro 2008 semi-final.
- Şenol Güneş: the 2002 World Cup bronze.
- Mustafa Denizli: the Euro 2000 quarter-final.
- Stefan Kuntz: 2021-23.
- Vincenzo Montella: from 2023, Euro 2024 quarter-final and 2026 qualification.

## Current squad and setup

Uğurcan Çakır is in goal, Demiral and Çelik lead the defence with Kadıoğlu at left-back, Çalhanoğlu, the captain, and Kökçü sit in midfield, and Güler, Yıldız, Kerem Aktürkoğlu and Yılmaz rotate across the attacking positions. Turkey and Italy will co-host Euro 2032, and the near-term aims are Euro 2028 qualifying from 2027 and a Nations League promotion push.
