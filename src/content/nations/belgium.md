## History

Belgium were an early European power, Olympic champions in 1920 at home and quarter-finalists at the first two [World Cups](/tournaments/world-cup) in name only, going out in the first round. The team of Guy Thys, with Jan Ceulemans, Enzo Scifo and Jean-Marie Pfaff, reached the [Euro 1980](/tournaments/euro) final and the 1986 World Cup semi-final, where Maradona's Argentina ended the dream.

A lean period followed a run of six straight World Cup qualifications to 2002, and Belgium missed every tournament between 2004 and 2012. The golden generation changed that: Eden Hazard, Kevin De Bruyne, Romelu Lukaku, Vincent Kompany and Thibaut Courtois took Belgium to the 2014 quarter-final, the 2016 quarter-final and, under Roberto Martínez, third place at the 2018 World Cup after a famous 2-1 comeback against Japan and a win over Brazil. Belgium sat top of the FIFA ranking for most of 2018-22 without winning anything.

The 2022 World Cup brought a group-stage exit and the end of Martínez's reign. Domenico Tedesco reached the Euro 2024 round of 16; Rudi Garcia replaced him in January 2025 and led Belgium through the 2026 World Cup, beating the United States 4-1 in the round of 16 and Senegal 3-2 after extra time in the round of 32 before a 2-1 quarter-final defeat to eventual champions Spain. Garcia's contract was not renewed and on 24 July 2026 the federation appointed Mark van Bommel, the former [Bayern Munich](/clubs/bayern-munich) and PSV midfielder, on a contract to Euro 2028.

## Philosophy and style

Belgium's identity is transition football. The coaching reforms of the 2000s, driven by Michel Sablon, standardised a 4-3-3 and dribbling-heavy youth development, which is why the country keeps producing wingers like [Jérémy Doku](/players/jeremy-doku). The senior side has played a [back three](/tactics/back-three) under Martínez and a 4-2-3-1 under Garcia, in both cases built on [counter-attacking](/tactics/counter-attacking) through [Kevin De Bruyne](/players/kevin-de-bruyne)'s passing. Van Bommel is expected to bring a more aggressive pressing structure in the Dutch tradition.

## Managers who shaped them

- Guy Thys: 1976-89, the Euro 1980 final and the 1986 semi-final.
- Marc Wilmots: the 2014 revival.
- Roberto Martínez: third place in 2018 and the top of the world ranking.
- Rudi Garcia: the 2026 quarter-final.
- [Mark van Bommel](/managers/mark-van-bommel-manager): appointed July 2026.

## Current squad and setup

Van Bommel, assisted by Boudewijn Zenden, started with Nations League matches against Italy in September 2026. Thibaut Courtois remains in goal, Arthur Theate and Maxim De Cuyper lead a young defence, Amadou Onana and Youri Tielemans, now captain, hold midfield, and De Bruyne, 35 and playing at [Napoli](/clubs/napoli), still starts as the 10 behind Loïs Openda, with Doku and Charles De Ketelaere wide. The long-term brief is Euro 2028 qualifying, which starts in 2027, and the 2028 Olympics for the under-23s.
