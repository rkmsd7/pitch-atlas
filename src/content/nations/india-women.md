## History

India's women were an Asian power before Asian women's football was a power. The team finished runners-up at the [AFC Women's Asian Cup](/tournaments/asian-cup) in 1980 and 1983 and third in 1981, when the field was small and the continent's giants had not yet organised. As Japan, China, Korea and Australia professionalised, India drifted: the last Asian Cup appearance of that era was 2003, and the federation offered little support for two decades.

The modern revival is built on Manipur, the north-eastern state that produces a remarkable share of the squad. Bembem Devi captained the side to the first SAFF Women's Championship in 2010, and India won the next three editions as well. [Bala Devi](/players/bala-devi), the country's record scorer with 48 goals, became the first Indian woman to sign a professional contract abroad when she joined [Rangers](/clubs/rangers) in 2020. India were to host the 2022 Asian Cup but withdrew after a COVID-19 outbreak in the squad, a bitter episode that cost a home tournament.

Crispin Chettri took over as head coach in 2025 after Joakim Alexandersson's interim spell and immediately qualified for the 2026 Asian Cup in Australia, India's first appearance since 2003. The tournament itself was harsh, with defeats to Vietnam (2-1), [Japan](/nations/japan-women) (11-0) and Chinese Taipei (3-1), but a sixth SAFF title followed in 2026, with an 11-0 win over Maldives, 3-0 and 3-1 wins over Bangladesh and a 1-0 semi-final against Bhutan.

## Philosophy and style

Chettri's India press hard and play quickly through the middle against South Asian opposition, where the squad's technique and fitness advantages are decisive. Against top Asian sides the plan is a deep [low block](/tactics/low-block), rapid [counter-attacking](/tactics/counter-attacking) through Manisha Kalyan and Pyari Xaxa, and set-piece threat. The Indian Women's League, now in its second decade, and the growth of clubs such as Gokulam Kerala, Sethu FC and East Bengal are slowly improving the depth; the lack of a fully professional league remains the biggest obstacle.

## Current squad and setup

India did not qualify for the [2027 World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup), with the Asian Cup group-stage exit ending that route. Sweety Devi captains, Ashalata Devi has become the first Indian woman to reach 100 caps, and Bala Devi, now in her mid-30s, remains the figurehead. The next targets are the 2027 Asian Cup qualifiers and the Asian Games. Chettri's record in his first 19 matches was 10 wins and 9 defeats. Ranked 69th in June 2026, India are trying to climb back inside the top 50 for the first time since 2013.
