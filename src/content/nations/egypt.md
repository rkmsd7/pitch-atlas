## History

Egypt were the first African nation at a [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup), in 1934, and the first African champions, winning the inaugural [AFCON](/tournaments/afcon) in 1957 and retaining it in 1959. The 1986 title at home began a golden run that peaked under Hassan Shehata, when Mohamed Aboutrika's generation won three consecutive AFCONs in 2006, 2008 and 2010, a feat no one has matched, with the 2008 and 2010 sides among the finest African teams ever assembled.

World Cup qualification has been the recurring failure. Egypt reached Italia 90, held the Netherlands and Ireland and went out narrowly, then waited 28 years for Russia 2018, where an injured [Mohamed Salah](/players/mohamed-salah) could not prevent three defeats. They lost the 2017 and 2021 AFCON finals, the latter on penalties to Senegal, and failed to reach Qatar 2022 after another shoot-out defeat to Senegal in the play-off.

[Hossam Hassan](/players/hossam-hassan), the country's record scorer, was appointed in February 2024. Egypt qualified comfortably for 2026 and produced their best World Cup: out of the group, a penalty win over Australia after a 1-1 draw in the round of 32, and a 3-2 defeat to Argentina in the round of 16. Hassan's position after the tournament could not be independently verified at the time of writing.

## Philosophy and style

Egyptian football is technical and possession-minded, the Al Ahly and Zamalek schools prizing short passing and control. Shehata's champions played a patient 4-3-3; modern Egypt is a 4-2-3-1 that defends in a [low block](/tactics/low-block) and transitions through Salah on the right and Omar Marmoush through the middle. Hossam Hassan has made the team more direct and physical, using Mostafa Mohamed as a target and the wide men for [counter-attacks](/tactics/counter-attacking). Set pieces, with Ahmed Hegazi and Mohamed Abdelmonem, are a strength.

Egyptian football is the most intense club culture in Africa: Al Ahly, the continent's most decorated club, and Zamalek supply the national team's domestic core, and the Cairo derby shapes dressing-room dynamics. Salah's status, the Premier League Golden Boots and the 2025 Premier League title with Liverpool, has made him the face of the Pharaohs for over a decade, and the 2026 round of 16 was the result he had waited for. AFCON rivalries with Senegal, who beat Egypt in the 2021 final and the 2022 World Cup play-off, and with the North African neighbours Algeria and Morocco, are the most heated fixtures.

## Managers who shaped them

- Mahmoud El-Gohary: the 1990 World Cup and 1998 AFCON.
- Hassan Shehata: three straight AFCONs, 2006-10.
- Héctor Cúper: the 2017 AFCON final and 2018 World Cup.
- Carlos Queiroz: the 2021 AFCON final.
- Hossam Hassan: from 2024, the 2026 round of 16.

## Current squad and setup

Mohamed El-Shenawy is in goal, Hegazi and Abdelmonem lead the defence, Mohamed Elneny and Imam Ashour hold midfield, Trezeguet and Salah, 34 and [Liverpool](/clubs/liverpool)'s record-breaking winger, play wide, and Marmoush and Mostafa Mohamed share the central striking role. AFCON 2027 in East Africa is the immediate goal, and Egypt also hope to field a strong under-23 side at the 2028 Olympics after finishing fourth in Paris.
