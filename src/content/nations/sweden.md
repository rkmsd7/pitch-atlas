## History

Sweden were an amateur power, Olympic champions in 1948 with the Gre-No-Li trio of Gren, Nordahl and Liedholm, third at the 1950 [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) and runners-up at home in 1958, when a 17-year-old Pelé scored twice in the final. Professionalism abroad stripped the team for years, but Tommy Svensson's 1994 side, with Tomas Brolin, Kennet Andersson and Thomas Ravelli's penalty saves, finished third in the United States, two years after a [Euro 1992](/tournaments/euro) semi-final at home.

The 2000s belonged to Henrik Larsson and [Zlatan Ibrahimović](/players/zlatan-ibrahimovic): a 2002 World Cup group win over Argentina, the Euro 2004 quarter-final and consistent qualification. Janne Andersson's unfancied team, without the retired Ibrahimović, reached the 2018 World Cup quarter-final, losing 2-0 to England, and the Euro 2020 round of 16.

Then the slide: a failed 2022 play-off against Poland, relegation to Nations League C and missing Euro 2024. Jon Dahl Tomasson was sacked in October 2025 during a poor World Cup qualifying campaign, and the former Brighton and Chelsea coach Graham Potter was appointed. Sweden entered the March 2026 play-offs via their Nations League ranking; whether they reached the 2026 World Cup could not be verified at the time of writing.

## Philosophy and style

The Swedish model, codified by Lars Lagerbäck and Andersson, is a compact 4-4-2 [low block](/tactics/low-block), zonal defending and quick [counter-attacks](/tactics/counter-attacking). Potter, who built fluid possession teams at Östersund in Sweden's lower leagues, favours a [back three](/tactics/back-three), wing-backs and patient build-up, with Lucas Bergvall and Hugo Larsson as progressive midfielders. The firepower is the point: [Alexander Isak](/players/alexander-isak), Viktor Gyökeres and Dejan Kulusevski give Sweden an attack few nations can match.

Swedish football culture has been shaped by its exports, from Gre-No-Li at AC Milan through Larsson at Celtic and Ibrahimović's career across Europe to Isak and Gyökeres. The Allsvenskan is a selling league, and Malmö, AIK and Hammarby supply the next generation to the Eredivisie and the Bundesliga. The rivalry with Denmark is the one with real heat, and the 2017 play-off win over Italy remains the most celebrated result since 1994. Potter's appointment was seen in Sweden as a homecoming after his years at Östersund.

## Managers who shaped them

- George Raynor: Olympic gold in 1948 and the 1958 final.
- Tommy Svensson: third place in 1994.
- Lars Lagerbäck: 2000-09 with Tommy Söderberg, four tournaments.
- Janne Andersson: the 2018 quarter-final.
- Graham Potter: from October 2025.

## Current squad and setup

Robin Olsen is in goal, Victor Lindelöf captains the defence with Gabriel Gudmundsson at wing-back, Larsson, Bergvall and Yasin Ayari share the midfield roles, Kulusevski and Anthony Elanga play off the front two, and Isak, [Liverpool](/clubs/liverpool)'s record signing, partners [Arsenal](/clubs/arsenal)'s Gyökeres. Euro 2028 qualifying, starting in 2027, is the priority after a decade without a European Championship.
