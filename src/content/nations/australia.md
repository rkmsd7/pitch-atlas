## History

Australia's isolation in Oceania shaped everything. The Socceroos reached the 1974 [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) under Rale Rasic, lost two play-offs in the 1990s and then produced the most famous night in the country's football history, beating Uruguay on penalties in Sydney in November 2005 to qualify for Germany 2006. Guus Hiddink's team, with Tim Cahill, Harry Kewell and Mark Viduka, beat Japan, drew with Croatia and lost to Italy in the round of 16 to a stoppage-time penalty.

The move to the AFC in 2006 transformed the fixture list. Australia lost the 2011 [Asian Cup](/tournaments/asian-cup) final to Japan, won the 2015 edition at home under Ange Postecoglou with a 2-1 extra-time victory over South Korea, and have qualified for every World Cup since 2006. Graham Arnold's 2022 team beat Tunisia and Denmark to reach the round of 16, losing 2-1 to Argentina.

Arnold resigned in September 2024 after a poor start to qualifying and Tony Popovic, the former Western Sydney Wanderers coach, took over. Australia secured direct qualification in June 2025, and at the 2026 World Cup reached the round of 32, where they drew 1-1 with Egypt and lost on penalties. Popovic was in charge for the October 2026 friendlies, including a 2-1 defeat to the United States.

## Philosophy and style

The Australian tradition is fighting spirit, aerial strength and [set pieces](/tactics/set-pieces); Cahill's headers and Mile Jedinak's penalties were the archetype. Postecoglou briefly imposed an attacking possession game, and Popovic, a former [Crystal Palace](/clubs/crystal-palace) centre-back, favours a [back three](/tactics/back-three) with wing-backs, disciplined pressing triggers and quick, direct transitions through Nestory Irankunda and Riley McGree.

The Socceroos' supporter culture, the green and gold of the 2005 night in Sydney and the Qatar 2022 street parties in Melbourne's Federation Square, has grown with each tournament. The A-League's financial struggles mean the national team is largely drawn from the Championship, Scotland and the Bundesliga, where Souttar, Irvine and Metcalfe play. The 2026 World Cup and the 2027 Asian Cup in Saudi Arabia, where Australia lost the 2011 final to Japan, are chances for Popovic's team to build on the Arnold years.

## Managers who shaped them

- Rale Rasic: the 1974 World Cup.
- Guus Hiddink: the 2006 round of 16.
- [Ange Postecoglou](/managers/ange-postecoglou): the 2015 Asian Cup.
- Graham Arnold: 2018-24, the 2022 round of 16.
- Tony Popovic: from 2024.

## Current squad and setup

Mathew Ryan, the captain, remains in goal; Harry Souttar anchors the back three; Jackson Irvine, Aiden O'Neill and Connor Metcalfe run the midfield; McGree, Craig Goodwin and Irankunda create; Mitchell Duke and Kusini Yengi share the number nine role. The 2027 Asian Cup in Saudi Arabia (7 January to 5 February 2027) is the next tournament, with the Olyroos chasing a 2028 Olympic place.
