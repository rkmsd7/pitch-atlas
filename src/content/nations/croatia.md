## History

Croatia played as an independent nation only from 1990 and were admitted to FIFA in 1992, yet within six years Miroslav Blažević's team had finished third at the 1998 [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup), Davor Šuker winning the Golden Boot and the 3-0 quarter-final win over Germany becoming a founding myth.

The 2000s brought disappointment, group exits in 2002 and 2006 and a Euro 2008 quarter-final under Slaven Bilić lost on penalties to Turkey, though Bilić's team had beaten England home and away to deny them Euro 2008 qualification. Zlatko Dalić took over in October 2017 and oversaw the greatest era: the 2018 World Cup final, reached through three extra-time wins and lost 4-2 to France; the 2022 third-place finish after beating Brazil on penalties; and the 2023 Nations League final lost to Spain on penalties. [Luka Modrić](/players/luka-modric), Ballon d'Or winner in 2018, was the constant.

Euro 2024 ended at the group stage, and at the 2026 World Cup Croatia lost 2-1 to Portugal in the round of 32. Dalić stepped down after almost nine years and on 13 July 2026 the federation reappointed Bilić, who had been coaching Al Fateh in Saudi Arabia.

## Philosophy and style

Croatia's identity is the midfield. Modrić, Rakitić, Kovačić and Brozović controlled matches through [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) with a patient tempo; the team rarely pressed high but suffocated opponents by keeping the ball. Dalić's 4-3-3 defended in a mid-block and relied on extraordinary stamina, hence the extra-time record. Bilić's own teams have been more direct and aggressive, with a [high line](/tactics/high-line) and quicker transitions, and he has promised to build around Joško Gvardiol and the next generation of Dinamo Zagreb products.

Croatian football's achievements rest on a tiny base: fewer than four million people, a domestic league dominated by Dinamo Zagreb and Hajduk Split, and a federation that has had to manage scandal and fan protest. Yet the youth system, built on street football and the Dinamo academy, keeps producing midfielders. The under-21 team reached the 2023 Euro quarter-final and the 2025 edition, and Martin Baturina and Luka Vušković are the next exports. The rivalry with Serbia, played out mostly in qualifiers and politics, and the friendly rivalry with England, beaten in the 2018 semi-final, frame the team's recent history.

## Managers who shaped them

- Miroslav Blažević: third place in 1998.
- [Slaven Bilić](/managers/slaven-bilic-manager): 2006-12, and again from July 2026.
- Zlatko Dalić: 2017-26, a World Cup final and two further semi-finals.

## Current squad and setup

Bilić's first squads have been a bridge between eras. Modrić, 41, announced his international retirement after the World Cup and Dominik Livaković, Gvardiol and Josip Stanišić now form the spine. Mateo Kovačić and Lovro Majer carry the midfield with Martin Baturina and Luka Sučić as the creative successors; Andrej Kramarić and Petar Musa lead the line. The Nations League campaign of autumn 2026 is a laboratory for Euro 2028 qualifying, which begins in 2027.
