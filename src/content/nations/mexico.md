## History

Mexico played their first international in 1923 and entered the first [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) in 1930, losing all three games. For decades they were there but not competitive; the first World Cup win came in 1962. Hosting in 1970 and 1986 produced the two quarter-final finishes, the second ending in a penalty defeat to West Germany with Hugo Sánchez at his peak.

The 1990s and 2000s brought regional dominance, [Gold Cup](/tournaments/gold-cup) after Gold Cup, the 1999 Confederations Cup win over Brazil at the Azteca and a run of seven consecutive World Cup round-of-16 exits from 1994 to 2018, the "quinto partido" obsession with reaching a fifth game. The 2012 Olympic gold, won against Brazil at Wembley, was the greatest international achievement.

Qatar 2022 broke the streak in the wrong direction with a group-stage exit, and Javier Aguirre returned for a third spell in 2024 with [Rafael Márquez](/players/rafael-marquez) as his assistant and designated successor. Mexico won the 2023 Nations League, the 2023 and 2025 Gold Cups and, as 2026 co-hosts, beat South Korea to reach the knockouts before losing 3-2 to England in the round of 16 at the Azteca on 5 July 2026. Aguirre stepped down as planned and Márquez was confirmed as head coach on 8 July 2026 for the cycle to 2030.

## Philosophy and style

Mexican football values technique and possession, the influence of Guadalajara and América academies and of the Spanish-schooled coaches who have passed through. Aguirre's 4-3-3 pressed high with Edson Álvarez as the anchor and used the wingers Hirving Lozano and Alexis Vega to create for Santiago Giménez. Márquez, a Barcelona-educated defender, favours [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) with a patient build-up and [inverted full-backs](/tactics/inverted-full-backs), and has promoted teenagers such as Gilberto Mora, the star of Mexico's 2025 under-17 and under-20 campaigns.

## Managers who shaped them

- Javier Aguirre: three spells, 2002, 2010 and 2024-26.
- Ricardo La Volpe: the 2006 possession team.
- Miguel Herrera: the 2014 side and the passion on the touchline.
- Juan Carlos Osorio: the 2018 win over Germany.
- Gerardo Martino: the 2019 Gold Cup and the 2022 disappointment.
- [Rafael Márquez](/managers/rafael-marquez-manager): from July 2026.

## Current squad and setup

Márquez's first autumn featured friendlies and the CONCACAF Nations League, with a Gold Cup at the end of 2026 as his first tournament. Luis Malagón is in goal, César Montes and Johan Vásquez marshal the defence, Álvarez captains from midfield, and Giménez, Raúl Jiménez, Lozano and Roberto Alvarado share the attacking roles, with Mora the breakout prospect. Mexico sit tenth in the October 2026 FIFA ranking; the 2028 Olympics and Copa América 2028, to which Mexico expect an invitation, are the medium-term goals.
