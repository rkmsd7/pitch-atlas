## History

Italy were an early European power, reaching the Euro final in 1993 and 1997 with Carolina Morace, the first great Italian striker, and reaching the quarter-finals of the inaugural [World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) in 1991. Then came two decades in the wilderness: no World Cup between 1999 and 2019, group-stage exits at every Euro, and a domestic league that was amateur until 2022.

The 2019 World Cup in France was the turning point. Milena Bertolini's side, led by [Cristiana Girelli](/players/cristiana-girelli) and [Barbara Bonansea](/players/barbara-bonansea), beat Australia and Jamaica to top their group and reached the quarter-finals, losing 2-0 to the Netherlands, and the public interest that followed forced Serie A to turn professional. The 2023 World Cup brought a group-stage exit and Bertolini's departure.

Andrea Soncin, appointed in 2023, delivered the best tournament in a generation at [Euro 2025](/tournaments/womens-euro). Italy beat Belgium, drew with Portugal, beat [Norway](/nations/norway-women) 2-1 in the quarter-final with Girelli scoring both goals, and were 96 minutes from the final against [England](/nations/england-women) before Michelle Agyemang's equaliser and Chloe Kelly's extra-time winner.

## Philosophy and style

Soncin plays a 3-5-2 that can look like a [back three](/tactics/back-three) [low block](/tactics/low-block) without the ball and a 3-4-1-2 with it. Manuela Giugliano is the metronome at the base of midfield, Arianna Caruso arrives late in the box, the wing-backs provide width, and up front Girelli, still scoring at 36, partners the quick Sofia Cantore, who moved to the [Washington Spirit](/clubs/washington-spirit) in 2025. The team's strength is organisation and [counter-attacking](/tactics/counter-attacking); its limit is a thin pool of elite talent behind the first eleven, something the growth of Juventus, Roma and Inter is slowly fixing.

## Current squad and setup

Italy finished second in [2027 World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) qualifying Group A1 behind Denmark with 9 points, drawing twice with the Danes and losing only to Sweden in March 2026. Belarus were beaten 3-1 on aggregate in October, and the round-two play-off against Finland is on 1 and 5 December 2026. Girelli, of Juventus, remains captain and top scorer; Elena Linari organises the defence, and Bonansea, now at Utah Royals in the [NWSL](/leagues/nwsl), scored the 90th-minute winner that knocked San Diego out of the 2026 play-offs on 6 November.

## Legends
