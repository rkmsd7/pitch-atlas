## History

Canada's football history is one of long gaps. The 1904 Olympic gold, won by Galt FC, was followed by decades of irrelevance behind hockey. The 1985 [Gold Cup](/tournaments/gold-cup) (then the CONCACAF Championship) qualified Tony Waiters' team for the 1986 [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup), where they lost all three games without scoring. The 2000 Gold Cup, won under Holger Osieck with Craig Forrest in goal, was a surprise that led nowhere.

The turning point was the 2015 Women's World Cup and the growth of MLS clubs in Toronto, Vancouver and Montreal. John Herdman's team, with [Alphonso Davies](/players/alphonso-davies) and Jonathan David, topped CONCACAF qualifying and reached Qatar 2022, where they lost all three matches but scored their first World Cup goal through Davies. Herdman left for Toronto FC in 2023 amid the drone-spying scandal that later cost the women's team points at the Paris Olympics.

Jesse Marsch, the former [RB Leipzig](/clubs/rb-leipzig) and Leeds coach, was hired in May 2024. He took Canada to the 2024 Copa América semi-finals and, after a four-year extension signed in May 2026, into the home World Cup. Canada beat Qatar 6-0 in Vancouver for a first World Cup win, defeated South Africa 1-0 in the round of 32 for a first knockout victory, and lost 3-0 to Morocco in the round of 16.

## Philosophy and style

Marsch is a product of the Red Bull school and Canada play its [gegenpressing](/tactics/gegenpressing) football: a narrow 4-2-2-2 or a 3-4-3, immediate counter-pressing on loss, direct forward passes and runners attacking the space behind. Davies at left wing-back and Tajon Buchanan on the right provide the pace; Stephen Eustáquio and Ismaël Koné cover the ground in midfield; David, now in Serie A at [Juventus](/clubs/juventus), finishes.

Canada's co-hosting of the 2026 World Cup, with matches in Toronto and Vancouver, was the catalyst for a national football infrastructure. The Canadian Premier League, launched in 2019, gives a domestic pathway for the first time, and the three MLS clubs supply the squad's depth. Davies, [Bayern Munich](/clubs/bayern-munich)'s left-back and the country's first global star, and David, who scored over 100 goals for [Lille](/clubs/lille) before joining Juventus in 2025, are the faces of a programme that rose from 122nd in the FIFA ranking in 2014 to the top 30.

## Managers who shaped them

- Tony Waiters: the 1986 World Cup.
- Holger Osieck: the 2000 Gold Cup.
- John Herdman: 2018-23, the 2022 qualification.
- Jesse Marsch: from 2024, the 2026 round of 16.

## Current squad and setup

Marsch called 26 players into a September 2026 camp for three friendlies, blending seventeen World Cup squad members with newer faces. Maxime Crépeau is in goal, Moïse Bombito and Alistair Johnston anchor the defence, Eustáquio and Koné hold midfield, Davies captains from the left, and David, Cyle Larin and Jacob Shaffelburg lead the attack. The CONCACAF Nations League and the 2026 Gold Cup come next, with the 2028 Olympics and Copa América 2028 as the longer-term targets.

- [Julian de Guzman](/players/julian-de-guzman): the 2007 Gold Cup player of the tournament.
