## History

Morocco were the first African nation to reach a [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) second round, topping a group with England, Poland and Portugal in 1986 under José Faria. The 1976 [AFCON](/tournaments/afcon) title, won in Ethiopia, remains the only continental championship, a source of frustration for a football-mad country that has produced Mohammed Timoumi, Mustapha Hadji and Noureddine Naybet.

The 1998 World Cup ended with a group-stage exit despite a win over Scotland, and Morocco then missed four straight tournaments. The investment of the 2010s, the Mohammed VI academy and a strategy of recruiting the diaspora, paid off in Qatar 2022. Walid Regragui, appointed only three months before the tournament, took the Atlas Lions past Belgium, Spain and Portugal to the semi-final, the first African team to get there, before defeat to France.

Morocco won Olympic bronze in 2024 and the under-20 World Cup in 2025, and reached the home AFCON final in January 2026, losing to Senegal in a result CAF later overturned on appeal. Regragui and the federation parted ways on 5 March 2026 and Mohamed Ouahbi, the under-20 world champion coach, was promoted. At the 2026 World Cup Morocco drew 1-1 with Brazil, beat the Netherlands on penalties in the round of 32, thrashed Canada 3-0 in the round of 16 and lost 2-0 to France in the quarter-final.

## Philosophy and style

Regragui's Morocco perfected the [low block](/tactics/low-block): a 4-3-3 that becomes a 4-1-4-1, with Sofyan Amrabat screening, the wingers tracking back and Yassine Bounou making saves. Out of it came devastating [counter-attacks](/tactics/counter-attacking) through [Achraf Hakimi](/players/achraf-hakimi) on the right and the dribbling of Brahim Díaz or Hakim Ziyech. Ouahbi, whose under-20s played a more front-foot pressing game, has nudged the senior team towards a higher line while keeping the defensive discipline. Set pieces, with Youssef En-Nesyri and Nayef Aguerd, are a major weapon.

Morocco's investment is unmatched in Africa: the Mohammed VI complex outside Rabat, the modernised stadiums for the 2025 AFCON and the 2030 World Cup, and a diaspora recruitment strategy that brought Hakimi (born in Madrid), Ziyech (Dronten), Amrabat (Huizen) and Brahim Díaz (Málaga) into the fold. The Atlas Lions' 2022 run made them a symbol across the Arab world and Africa. Rivalries with Algeria, whose 2026 round-of-32 opponents were Switzerland, and with Egypt and Senegal in AFCON are the continental fixtures that matter most.

## Managers who shaped them

- José Faria: the 1986 round of 16.
- Henri Michel: the 1998 World Cup and the 2004 AFCON final.
- Hervé Renard: the 2018 World Cup qualification.
- Walid Regragui: the 2022 semi-final and 2025 AFCON final.
- Mohamed Ouahbi: from March 2026, the 2026 quarter-final.

## Current squad and setup

Ouahbi's 4-3-3 in November 2026 has Bounou in goal, Hakimi, the captain and [Paris Saint-Germain](/clubs/paris-saint-germain) star, at right-back with Aguerd in the centre, Amrabat and Azzedine Ounahi in midfield with Bilal El Khannouss advanced, and Brahim Díaz, Eliesse Ben Seghir and En-Nesyri or Ayoub El Kaabi in attack. The next targets are AFCON 2027 in Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania, the 2028 Olympics, and above all the 2030 World Cup, which Morocco will co-host with Spain and Portugal.
