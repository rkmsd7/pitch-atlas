## History

The United States finished third at the first [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) in 1930 and beat England 1-0 at Belo Horizonte in 1950, the most famous upset in the tournament's early history, but then failed to qualify for forty years. Soccer survived in the NASL of Pelé and Beckenbauer and in college programmes until the 1990 qualification and the 1994 World Cup at home, where Bora Milutinović's team reached the round of 16 and launched [MLS](/leagues/mls).

Bruce Arena's 2002 team beat Portugal and Mexico to reach the quarter-finals, losing narrowly to Germany. Bob Bradley's side won their group in 2010 with Landon Donovan's stoppage-time winner against Algeria, and Jürgen Klinsmann's 2014 team escaped a group with Germany, Portugal and Ghana. The 2018 failure to qualify, after a defeat in Trinidad, was a national embarrassment that prompted a generational reset.

Gregg Berhalter's young team, with [Christian Pulisic](/players/christian-pulisic), Weston McKennie and Tyler Adams, reached the 2022 round of 16 and won three Nations Leagues. Mauricio Pochettino was appointed in September 2024 and took the hosts through the 2026 group stage, beat Bosnia and Herzegovina 2-0 in the round of 32 for the first knockout win since 2002, and lost 4-1 to Belgium in the round of 16. US Soccer then confirmed Pochettino would stay through the 2030 World Cup, though he will not coach the 2028 Olympic team.

## Philosophy and style

American football has always been athletic, organised and direct. The modern team, with players spread across Europe's top leagues, has layered a [gegenpressing](/tactics/gegenpressing) edge on top: Adams and McKennie hunting the ball, Antonee Robinson bombing forward on the left and Pulisic cutting in from the right. Pochettino's 4-2-3-1 asks for aggressive [pressing traps](/tactics/pressing-traps) and vertical passing, with Folarin Balogun or Ricardo Pepi running in behind. Possession-based control remains a work in progress.

## Managers who shaped them

- Bora Milutinović: the 1994 round of 16 at home.
- Bruce Arena: the 2002 quarter-final and two spells.
- Bob Bradley: the 2009 Confederations Cup final and 2010.
- [Jürgen Klinsmann](/managers/jurgen-klinsmann): 2011-16, the 2014 escape.
- Gregg Berhalter: the 2022 round of 16 and the Nations League titles.
- [Mauricio Pochettino](/managers/mauricio-pochettino): from 2024, extended to 2030.

## Current squad and setup

Pochettino's post-World Cup squads, 4-2-3-1 as standard, feature Matt Turner in goal, Chris Richards and Robinson in defence, Adams and McKennie in midfield, Malik Tillman and Diego Luna in the creative roles, Tim Weah wide and Balogun up front, with Pulisic as captain and talisman. A 1-1 draw with Ecuador and a 2-1 win over Australia in October 2026 kept the United States 16th in the FIFA ranking. The CONCACAF Nations League quarter-finals come in November 2026; the home Olympics in Los Angeles in 2028 and the 2028 Copa América are the next big dates.
