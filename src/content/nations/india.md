## History

India's golden age came in the decade after independence. Syed Abdul Rahim's team won the 1951 Asian Games at home and the 1962 Asian Games in Jakarta, finished fourth at the 1956 Olympics, the best by any Asian side, and were runners-up at the 1964 [Asian Cup](/tournaments/asian-cup). The story that India qualified for the 1950 [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) and withdrew over boots is half-true: they withdrew, but for cost and priorities, and FIFA had already banned barefoot play.

The decades after Rahim's death in 1963 were a slow decline. Cricket took the nation's attention, the domestic structure stagnated and India fell out of the top 100. [Bhaichung Bhutia](/players/bhaichung-bhutia) and I.M. Vijayan kept the flame in the 1990s, and the 2011 Asian Cup qualification, achieved by winning the 2008 AFC Challenge Cup under Bob Houghton, ended a 27-year absence.

[Sunil Chhetri](/players/sunil-chhetri), the third-highest active international scorer during his career, carried the team through the 2010s. Stephen Constantine's second spell took India to the 2019 Asian Cup and a 4-1 win over Thailand, and Igor Štimac qualified them for the 2023 edition, where they lost all three games. The [Indian Super League](/leagues/indian-super-league), launched in 2014, professionalised the game but the national team's FIFA ranking drifted back into the 130s. Manolo Márquez's short spell ended in July 2025, and Khalid Jamil, the first Indian head coach in over a decade, was appointed, with Chhetri coming out of retirement in 2025 to help the 2027 Asian Cup qualifying campaign.

## Philosophy and style

Indian teams have historically been technical but physically overmatched, and most coaches have chosen a defensive, [low-block](/tactics/low-block) approach. Jamil, who won the I-League with tiny Aizawl in 2017, is a pragmatist: a compact 4-4-2 or 4-2-3-1, Gurpreet Singh Sandhu in goal, Sandesh Jhingan organising the line and a reliance on [set pieces](/tactics/set-pieces) and the pace of Lallianzuala Chhangte and Manvir Singh on the [counter-attack](/tactics/counter-attacking). The ISL's foreign coaches have raised the technical level of the younger midfielders such as Apuia and Sahal Abdul Samad.

## Tournament record

India have never played at a World Cup. They entered qualifying for every edition from 1986 onwards without progressing past the second round.

## Managers who shaped them

- Syed Abdul Rahim: 1950-63, the Asian Games golds and the 1956 Olympics.
- Bob Houghton: the 2008 AFC Challenge Cup and 2011 Asian Cup.
- Stephen Constantine: two spells, the 2019 Asian Cup.
- Igor Štimac: 2019-24, the 2023 Asian Cup.
- Khalid Jamil: from July 2025.

## Current squad and setup

India's 2027 Asian Cup qualifying campaign in 2025-26 was the focus of Jamil's first year; whether they reached the finals in Saudi Arabia (7 January to 5 February 2027) could not be verified at the time of writing. The core is Sandhu in goal, Jhingan and Rahul Bheke in defence, Anirudh Thapa, Apuia and Brandon Fernandes in midfield, Chhangte and Manvir Singh wide and Chhetri, 42, still available as a mentor and finisher. The SAFF Championship and the 2026 Asian Games under-23 campaign are the other near-term targets, with Mumbai City, Mohun Bagan and Bengaluru FC the main suppliers of players.

## Legends

- [Sunil Chhetri](/players/sunil-chhetri): more than 90 international goals, record caps.
- [Bhaichung Bhutia](/players/bhaichung-bhutia): the Sikkimese Sniper, the first Indian to play professionally in Europe.
- [I.M. Vijayan](/players/im-vijayan): the Kerala striker of the 1990s.
- [P.K. Banerjee](/players/pk-banerjee): the 1962 Asian Games winner and FIFA Order of Merit holder.
- [Chuni Goswami](/players/chuni-goswami): captain of the 1962 champions, also a first-class cricketer.
- [Sailen Manna](/players/sailen-manna): captain of the 1951 champions.
- [Tulsidas Balaram](/players/tulsidas-balaram): the 1956 Olympic and 1962 forward.
- [Peter Thangaraj](/players/peter-thangaraj): Asia's best goalkeeper of the 1950s and 1960s.
