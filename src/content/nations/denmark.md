## History

Denmark were amateur purists who won Olympic silver in 1908 and 1912 and refused professionalism until the 1970s, which is why the early [World Cups](/tournaments/world-cup) passed them by. Sepp Piontek's Danish Dynamite of the mid-1980s changed everything: [Euro 1984](/tournaments/euro) semi-finalists, a 6-1 win over Uruguay at Mexico 86 and the elegance of Michael Laudrup and Preben Elkjær.

Then came 1992. Called up late when Yugoslavia were expelled, Richard Møller Nielsen's team beat the Netherlands on penalties and Germany 2-0 in the final to win the European Championship, with Peter Schmeichel immense and Brian Laudrup the star. The 1995 Confederations Cup and the 1998 World Cup quarter-final, where Bo Johansson's side lost 3-2 to Brazil, confirmed the golden decade.

Morten Olsen's fifteen years in charge brought the 2002 round of 16 and a possession identity. The 2018 round of 16 ended on penalties against Croatia, and Kasper Hjulmand's team reached the Euro 2020 semi-final, weeks after Christian Eriksen's cardiac arrest on the pitch in Copenhagen. Euro 2024 ended in the round of 16 against Germany. Brian Riemer, the former Anderlecht coach, was appointed in October 2024. Denmark's 2026 World Cup qualifying campaign ended in the March 2026 play-offs; whether they reached the finals could not be verified at the time of writing.

## Philosophy and style

Danish football is tactically literate. Olsen built a possession game inspired by his Ajax years, Hjulmand a 3-4-3 with wing-backs and [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) principles, and Riemer, whose Anderlecht pressed high, has leaned into coordinated pressing and quick transitions. Pierre-Emile Højbjerg's control, [Christian Eriksen](/players/christian-eriksen)'s passing and [Rasmus Højlund](/players/rasmus-hojlund)'s running in behind are the constants, and the [set-piece](/tactics/set-pieces) delivery from Eriksen and the aerial threat of Joachim Andersen are reliable sources of goals.

Danish football culture is about the collective: the roligans, the 1992 fairy tale and the way the nation rallied around Eriksen in 2021. The Superliga clubs, particularly FC Copenhagen, Midtjylland and Nordsjælland, run some of Europe's most productive academies, and the national team is now drawn from the Premier League, Serie A and the Bundesliga. The rivalry with Sweden, the 2004 Euro draw that eliminated Italy and the abandoned 2007 qualifier, is the fixture with the most edge, and Germany, who beat Denmark at Euro 2024, is the big neighbour.

## Managers who shaped them

- Sepp Piontek: the Danish Dynamite, 1979-90.
- Richard Møller Nielsen: Euro 1992.
- Morten Olsen: 2000-15, the 2002 round of 16.
- Kasper Hjulmand: the Euro 2020 semi-final.
- Brian Riemer: from October 2024.

## Current squad and setup

Riemer's 3-4-3 in the autumn 2026 Nations League featured Kasper Schmeichel in goal, Andreas Christensen and Andersen in the back three, Patrick Dorgu and Gustav Isaksen as wing-backs, Højbjerg and Morten Hjulmand in midfield, Eriksen, 34 and captain, and Mikkel Damsgaard behind Højlund. Euro 2028 qualifying in 2027 is the next goal, and Denmark will send an under-23 team to the 2028 Olympics if they qualify through the 2027 under-21 championship.
