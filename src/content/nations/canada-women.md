## History

Canada's story is inseparable from [Christine Sinclair](/players/christine-sinclair), who scored 190 international goals between 2000 and 2023, more than any player in history, and dragged a modest programme into the elite. The breakthrough came at the 2012 London Olympics: a 4-3 semi-final defeat to the [United States](/nations/united-states-women) that is still the greatest women's match many have seen, Sinclair scoring a hat-trick, followed by a bronze medal against France. A second bronze came in Rio 2016 under John Herdman.

Bev Priestman then delivered the summit. At the delayed Tokyo Olympics in 2021, Canada beat the USA 1-0 in the semi-final, their first win over their neighbours since 2001, and then [Sweden](/nations/sweden-women) on penalties in the final, Julia Grosso converting the winning kick. It was the first Olympic gold for a Concacaf side other than the USA.

The fall was sudden. At the 2023 [World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) Canada went out in the group stage, and at Paris 2024 the programme was engulfed by the drone-spying scandal: Priestman was sent home and later banned, Canada were docked six points yet still won all three group games before losing a quarter-final to Germany on penalties. In January 2025 the federation appointed Casey Stoney, the former [England](/nations/england-women) captain who had built [Manchester United Women](/clubs/manchester-united-women) and coached San Diego Wave.

## Philosophy and style

Stoney's Canada press higher and pass more than the Herdman and Priestman versions, but the foundations remain defensive: [Kadeisha Buchanan](/players/kadeisha-buchanan) and Vanessa Gilles are among the best centre-back pairings in the world, Kailen Sheridan is an elite goalkeeper and Ashley Lawrence can play anywhere down the left. [Jessie Fleming](/players/jessie-fleming), the captain, is the engine, and the attack has changed shape with Olivia Smith, who became the first million-pound signing in women's football when she joined [Arsenal](/clubs/arsenal-women) in 2025, and Jordyn Huitema providing pace. Set pieces and [counter-attacking](/tactics/counter-attacking) remain the surest route to goal.

## Current squad and setup

Canada have not yet qualified for the [2027 World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup). The Concacaf W Championship, played in the United States from 27 November to 5 December 2026, sends its four quarter-final winners to Brazil; Canada are among the eight quarter-finalists alongside the USA, Mexico, Jamaica, Costa Rica, Panama, Haiti and El Salvador. The 2026 build-up included a 4-1 win over Colombia and a 1-0 loss to the USA at the SheBelieves Cup in March and a 1-0 defeat in Brazil in April. Fleming captains; Buchanan plays for [Chelsea](/clubs/chelsea-women) and Gilles for [Bayern Munich](/clubs/bayern-munich-women).
