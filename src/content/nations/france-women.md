## History

France have produced as much talent as any nation in Europe and converted less of it. The club game explains both halves of that sentence: [Lyon](/clubs/lyon-women) won eight [Champions Leagues](/tournaments/womens-champions-league) between 2011 and 2022 and [PSG](/clubs/paris-saint-germain-women) became a serious power, giving Les Bleues a core of serial winners, yet the national team's best World Cup finish remains fourth in 2011, when Bruno Bini's side lost the semi-final to the [United States](/nations/united-states-women) and then the bronze match to Sweden.

The pattern became cruel. Quarter-final exits on penalties to [Germany](/nations/germany-women) in 2015, to the USA in Paris in 2019 as hosts, and to [Australia](/nations/australia-women) in 2023 after a 7-6 shootout. At the Euros there was a semi-final in 2022 under Corinne Diacre, lost to Germany, and a quarter-final exit on penalties to Germany again in 2025 after leading against ten players. Diacre's tenure ended in 2023 after senior players, led by [Wendie Renard](/players/wendie-renard), refused to play under her; Hervé Renard took the team to the Paris Olympics (quarter-final defeat to Brazil) and reached the 2024 Nations League final, lost 2-0 to [Spain](/nations/spain-women).

Laurent Bonadei, Renard's assistant, was promoted in August 2024. He made the hardest call in French football, leaving Renard, Eugénie Le Sommer and Kenza Dali out of the Euro 2025 squad to reset the group around younger players.

## Philosophy and style

France play a 4-3-3 with a strong emphasis on the wings. Delphine Cascarino and [Kadidiatou Diani](/players/kadidiatou-diani) attack one-v-one, Marie-Antoinette Katoto is a penalty-box finisher, Selma Bacha and [Sakina Karchaoui](/players/sakina-karchaoui) are among the world's best attacking full-backs, and Grace Geyoro drives from midfield. [Griedge Mbock](/players/griedge-mbock), the captain, organises a line that can defend high. Bonadei wants a quicker game than his predecessors, more vertical passes and more [counter-attacking](/tactics/counter-attacking) from turnovers, and the 2026 qualifying campaign showed a team scoring freely.

## Current squad and setup

France won [2027 World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) qualifying Group A2 with 13 points, sealing their place on 9 June 2026 with a 1-0 win over the Republic of Ireland, despite losing 2-1 to the [Netherlands](/nations/netherlands-women) in April. A 9-0 win over Latvia in October 2026, with Diani among the scorers, showed the attacking depth. Diani left Lyon for London City Lionesses in July 2026 and Katoto now plays for Lyon; Mbock, Karchaoui and Geyoro are PSG stalwarts. Renard, 36, still captains Lyon but her international career appears over. Bonadei's contract runs through the World Cup in Brazil.
