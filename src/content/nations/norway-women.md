## History

Norway were there at the beginning. They lost the first [World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) final in 1991 to the United States, won the second in 1995 in Sweden, beating Germany 2-0 with goals from Hege Riise and Marianne Pettersen, and completed the set with Olympic gold in Sydney 2000, Dagny Mellgren's golden goal beating the USA in the final. Euro titles in 1987 and 1993 round out a trophy cabinet no other European nation can match in variety.

The decline was slow and then sudden. A Euro final in 2013 was followed by a group-stage exit as the tournament's lowest scorers in 2017, a disastrous Euro 2022 that included an 8-0 defeat to England, and [Ada Hegerberg](/players/ada-hegerberg)'s five-year international exile from 2017 to 2022 in protest at the federation's treatment of the women's programme. The 2019 and 2023 World Cups ended in the quarter-finals and round of 16 respectively, and [Euro 2025](/tournaments/womens-euro) in a quarter-final defeat to Italy.

Gemma Grainger, the English coach who had rebuilt Wales, was appointed in January 2024 and has steadily restored order, with Hegerberg back as captain and [Caroline Graham Hansen](/players/caroline-graham-hansen) at the peak of her powers for [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona-women).

## Philosophy and style

The 1990s Norway played a famously direct, physical game, a kind of [direct play](/tactics/direct-play) built on long balls, second balls and corners. Grainger's version is a 4-3-3 with more structure in possession, Ingrid Engen and Vilde Bøe Risa at the base, Guro Reiten and Frida Maanum arriving in the box, Graham Hansen isolated against a full-back and Hegerberg attacking crosses. Signe Gaupset, who scored twice at Euro 2025 as a 20-year-old, is the next star.

## Current squad and setup

Norway finished second to [Germany](/nations/germany-women) in [2027 World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) qualifying Group A4 with 12 points, a 4-0 home defeat to the Germans in March 2026 the decisive result. Romania were beaten 8-0 on aggregate in the October play-off, and the round-two tie against Wales on 1 and 5 December 2026 decides whether Norway go to Brazil. Hegerberg, who passed 100 caps in 2026 and scored three in the April qualifiers against Slovenia, captains; Graham Hansen, Engen and Maanum provide the quality. Reiten plays for [Gotham FC](/clubs/gotham-fc) after leaving Chelsea.
