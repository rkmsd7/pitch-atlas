## History

Germany played their first international in 1908 and reached a [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) semi-final in 1934, but the national team's identity was forged in 1954. Sepp Herberger's West Germany beat the Mighty Magyars of Hungary 3-2 in Bern, the Miracle of Bern, a result that announced the post-war republic to the world.

Helmut Schön's teams of the 1970s, with [Franz Beckenbauer](/players/franz-beckenbauer) as the original libero and [Gerd Müller](/players/gerd-muller) finishing everything, won [Euro 1972](/tournaments/euro) and the 1974 World Cup at home against the Dutch. Beckenbauer returned as coach to win Italia 90 with Lothar Matthäus as captain, and Berti Vogts added Euro 96 with the first golden goal.

The 1998-2004 period was the low point, group-stage exits at Euro 2000 and 2004 prompting a root-and-branch reform of coaching and academies. Jürgen Klinsmann's 2006 World Cup at home restored the fun, and Joachim Löw's 2014 team won the fourth star in Brazil after a 7-1 semi-final destruction of the hosts and Mario Götze's extra-time winner against Argentina.

Three straight early exits followed: the group stage in 2018 and 2022, the round of 16 at Euro 2020. Julian Nagelsmann rebuilt a joyful side that reached the Euro 2024 quarter-final at home, but the 2026 World Cup ended in the round of 32, 1-1 with Paraguay and a 4-3 penalty shoot-out defeat. On 24 July 2026 the DFB appointed [Jürgen Klopp](/managers/jurgen-klopp).

## Philosophy and style

Germany were once caricatured as efficient and physical; the modern identity, built after 2004, is technical and structured. Nagelsmann's team used a 4-2-3-1 with [Florian Wirtz](/players/florian-wirtz) and [Jamal Musiala](/players/jamal-musiala) as twin tens inside [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) principles, Joshua Kimmich dictating from deep. Klopp's arrival brings the [gegenpressing](/tactics/gegenpressing) he perfected at [Borussia Dortmund](/clubs/borussia-dortmund) and [Liverpool](/clubs/liverpool): a high press, fast transitions and a front three attacking space rather than feet. Germany's set-piece culture and tournament mentality remain the foundation.

The 2026 exit prompted a familiar German self-examination. The DFB had already launched a second youth reform after the 2018 and 2022 failures, focused on individual technique and small-sided games, and the 2024 under-19 and 2025 under-21 cohorts suggest the next generation, led by Pavlović, Tom Bischof and Paul Wanner, is strong. Klopp's appointment was partly symbolic: a coach whose teams play with intensity and emotion, after a decade in which the national team was accused of being sterile.

Rivalries are a map of German football history: the Netherlands, from 1974 and 1988 to the 2-1 and 3-3 of 2024-25; Italy, who Germany never beat at a tournament until 2016; England; and Argentina, opponents in three World Cup finals. The DFB's ambition is Euro 2028, which Klopp has described as the first real checkpoint, with the 2030 World Cup as the destination.

## Managers who shaped them

- Sepp Herberger: the Miracle of Bern.
- Helmut Schön: Euro 1972 and the 1974 World Cup.

- [Jürgen Klinsmann](/managers/jurgen-klinsmann): the 2006 renewal.
- Joachim Löw: fifteen years, the 2014 World Cup.
- Julian Nagelsmann: Euro 2024 and the 2026 campaign.
- [Jürgen Klopp](/managers/jurgen-klopp): appointed July 2026.

## Current squad and setup

Klopp has started with a 4-3-3 and a pressing front line of Nick Woltemade, Leroy Sané and Kai Havertz, with Wirtz drifting in from the left. Kimmich captains from midfield alongside Aleksandar Pavlović, Antonio Rüdiger and Jonathan Tah lead the defence and Marc-André ter Stegen is in goal when fit. Musiala's return from injury is the key storyline of 2026-27. Next: Euro 2028 qualifying from 2027 and the Nations League, with Germany also preparing an under-23 side for the 2028 Olympics.
