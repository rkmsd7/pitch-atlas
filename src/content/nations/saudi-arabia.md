## History

Saudi Arabia emerged in the 1980s, winning the [Asian Cup](/tournaments/asian-cup) at the first attempt in 1984 and again in 1988, with Majed Abdullah the first Saudi superstar. At USA 94, their first [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup), Jorge Solari's team beat Morocco and Belgium, Saeed Al-Owairan's 70-yard run against the Belgians becoming one of the tournament's iconic goals, and reached the round of 16.

A third Asian Cup followed in 1996, but the World Cups of 1998, 2002 (an 8-0 defeat to Germany) and 2006 went badly, and the team missed 2010 and 2014. Juan Antonio Pizzi's 2018 team lost 5-0 to Russia in the opener but beat Egypt. Then came Qatar 2022 and the shock of the tournament: Hervé Renard's side beat eventual champions Argentina 2-1 with goals from Saleh Al-Shehri and [Salem Al-Dawsari](/players/salem-al-dawsari), a result that earned a national holiday.

Renard left for the French women's team in 2023 and Roberto Mancini's brief, unhappy spell ended in October 2024, when Renard returned. Saudi Arabia qualified for the 2026 World Cup through the Asian fourth round in October 2025, as the domestic [Saudi Pro League](/leagues/saudi-pro-league) boomed with Ronaldo, Benzema and Neymar. The round they reached at the 2026 World Cup could not be verified at the time of writing.

## Philosophy and style

Saudi football has always been technical and quick, the heritage of Al-Hilal and Al-Ittihad's passing sides. Renard's innovation was a fearless [high line](/tactics/high-line) and [pressing traps](/tactics/pressing-traps) that caught Argentina offside ten times in 2022, married to rapid transitions through Al-Dawsari and Firas Al-Buraikan. Mancini tried a more patient possession game that the players never embraced; Renard's return restored the 4-3-3, the energy and the dressing-room unity.

The 2022 win over Argentina and the 2034 World Cup award have made football the centrepiece of Saudi Arabia's sporting ambitions. The Saudi Pro League's recruitment of Cristiano Ronaldo, Karim Benzema, Neymar and dozens of European internationals since 2023 has raised the standard the national team's players face every week, though it has also squeezed the minutes available to young Saudis, a tension Renard has spoken about openly. The rivalry with Iran and with the Gulf neighbours Qatar and the UAE gives the Asian Cup its edge, and a home title in 2027 would be the ideal prelude to 2034.

## Managers who shaped them

- Khalil Al-Zayani: the 1984 Asian Cup.
- Jorge Solari: the 1994 round of 16.
- Nelo Vingada: the 1996 Asian Cup.
- Hervé Renard: 2019-23 and from 2024, the win over Argentina.
- [Roberto Mancini](/managers/roberto-mancini): 2023-24.

## Current squad and setup

Nawaf Al-Aqidi is in goal, Hassan Tambakti and Ali Al-Bulaihi anchor the defence with Saud Abdulhamid at right-back, Mohammed Kanno and Musab Al-Juwayr run midfield, Al-Dawsari captains from the left and Al-Shehri or Al-Buraikan leads the line. Saudi Arabia host the 2027 Asian Cup from 7 January to 5 February 2027 and want a first title since 1996, as a staging post to the 2034 World Cup at home.
