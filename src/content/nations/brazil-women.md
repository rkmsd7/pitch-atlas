## History

Brazilian women played under an actual legal ban until 1979 and without federation support for decades afterwards, which makes what [Marta](/players/marta), Formiga and Cristiane achieved all the more extraordinary. The golden generation announced itself with Olympic silver in Athens 2004 and then dismantled the [United States](/nations/united-states-women) 4-0 in the 2007 [World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) semi-final, Marta scoring one of the tournament's most famous goals, before losing the final 2-0 to [Germany](/nations/germany-women). Another Olympic silver followed in Beijing 2008. Both finals were lost to teams with far greater resources.

Marta won five consecutive FIFA World Player of the Year awards from 2006 to 2010 and a sixth in 2018, but the national team drifted: quarter-final defeats at home in the 2016 Olympics, round-of-16 exits at the 2019 and 2023 World Cups, and a run of short-lived coaches. Pia Sundhage, hired in 2019, was sacked after the 2023 group-stage exit. Arthur Elias, who had built [Corinthians](/clubs/corinthians) into the dominant club in South America, took over in September 2023 and his young side reached the Paris 2024 Olympic final, knocking out France and Spain before losing 1-0 to the USA. Marta, sent off in the group stage, came back from suspension to play what was meant to be her farewell.

It was not. She returned in 2025 to win a ninth Copa América Femenina, scoring a stoppage-time equaliser in the final against Colombia before Brazil won on penalties.

## Philosophy and style

Elias's Brazil is more organised than any previous version. The 4-3-3 presses as a unit, Tarciane and Rafaelle Souza defend a higher line than their predecessors, and the attack combines quick wingers, Gabi Portilho and Kerolin, with Debinha's intelligence between the lines. The samba stereotypes undersell it: this is a team that beat the USA 2-1 in São Paulo in June 2026 through pressing and structure. The flair is still there in the final third, where the one-v-one quality remains South America's best.

## Current squad and setup

Brazil qualify automatically as hosts of the [2027 World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup), the first in South America, and will open at the Maracanã on 24 June 2027 before games in São Paulo and Brasília. The 2026 schedule was a tour of serious opponents: wins over South Korea, Zambia and Canada in April, a split series with the USA in June, a 3-1 win and 2-2 draw against Argentina in October, and two matches in Japan on 29 November and 5 December. Marta, 40, is still playing for [Orlando Pride](/clubs/orlando-pride) and has not formally closed the door on 2027; Rafaelle captains.
