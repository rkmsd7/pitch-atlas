## Biography

Edin Terzić was born in Menden, near Dortmund, on 30 October 1982, to Croatian and Bosnian parents, and grew up a [Borussia Dortmund](/clubs/borussia-dortmund) supporter who stood on the Südtribüne. Few managers have lived the club they coached as completely as he did between 2020 and 2024. In 2026 he took a different kind of job: head coach of [Athletic Bilbao](/clubs/athletic-bilbao), the Basque club that fields only locally raised players, succeeding Ernesto Valverde on a contract to June 2028.

## Playing career

Terzić was a lower-league forward for Westfalia Herne, Wanne-Eickel and SV Menden, never rising above the fifth tier. He combined playing with a sport science degree in Bochum and was coaching youth teams by his mid-twenties. He is a textbook example of the modern coach whose playing career matters far less than his years of study.

## Coaching career

He joined Dortmund's scouting department in 2010, coached in the academy and became Slaven Bilić's assistant at Beşiktaş (2013-16) and West Ham (2015-17). He returned to Dortmund as Lucien Favre's assistant in 2018 and took over as interim in December 2020, winning the DFB-Pokal in May 2021 with Erling Haaland and Jadon Sancho. After a year as technical director he was appointed head coach in 2022.

The 2022-23 season ended in agony: a draw with Mainz on the final day handed the [Bundesliga](/leagues/bundesliga) title to [Bayern Munich](/clubs/bayern-munich) on goal difference. In 2023-24 Dortmund finished fifth domestically but produced a famous [Champions League](/tournaments/champions-league) run, beating PSV, [Atlético Madrid](/clubs/atletico-madrid) and [PSG](/clubs/paris-saint-germain) to reach the final at Wembley, where they lost 2-0 to [Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid). He resigned days later. After two years out of the dugout, during which he turned down several offers, Athletic announced in 2026 that he would replace the departing Valverde for 2026-27.

## Tactical identity

Terzić coaches in the Dortmund tradition of [gegenpressing](/tactics/gegenpressing): a 4-2-3-1 or 4-3-3 that presses in a coordinated block and attacks with verticality the moment the ball is won. His Dortmund were at their best as a [counter-attacking](/tactics/counter-attacking) side in Europe, soaking pressure against PSG and Atlético and breaking through Sancho, Adeyemi and Füllkrug. He favours a [high line](/tactics/high-line) in domestic games but showed in the 2024 run that he can lower it and defend a box with discipline.

At Athletic he inherits a squad made for his style: [Nico Williams](/players/nico-williams) and Iñaki Williams for transitions, a hard-running midfield and an Estadio San Mamés crowd that lives for pressing. He has emphasised [set pieces](/tactics/set-pieces) as a source of goals for a team without the budget of its rivals, and has adapted the build-up to Spanish refereeing, which allows less physical pressing than the Bundesliga. The key evolution he is attempting is to add more sustained possession to a side that under Valverde was already well organised.

## Achievements

- DFB-Pokal winner 2021 as Dortmund's interim coach.
- Champions League finalist 2024, Dortmund's first final since 2013.
- Bundesliga runner-up 2022-23 by goal difference.
- Assistant coach at Beşiktaş and West Ham before leading his boyhood club.

## Storyline

Athletic's 2026-27 season is a transition handled with Basque care. Valverde left at the end of 2025-26 after a third spell that included the 2024 Copa del Rey and a European return, and the club chose a foreign coach for the first time in years, trusting Terzić's record with young, athletic squads. The appointment was agreed before the summer and he was presented at the start of the new season, with a contract to 30 June 2028.

The early months have been about keeping the club's European level while adjusting to [La Liga](/leagues/la-liga), where [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona) are two-time champions under [Hansi Flick](/managers/hansi-flick) and [José Mourinho](/managers/jose-mourinho) has returned to Real Madrid. The Williams brothers, Nico fresh from winning the 2026 [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) with [Spain](/nations/spain), are the attacking pillars, and keeping them is the recurring Bilbao storyline. As of November 2026 Terzić is still in the honeymoon phase, and the question in the Basque Country is whether a coach who nearly won the Champions League with Dortmund can make Athletic a regular in it.
