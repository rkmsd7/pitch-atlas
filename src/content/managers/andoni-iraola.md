## Biography

Andoni Iraola Sagarna was born in Usurbil, Gipuzkoa, on 22 June 1982 and is as Basque as a footballer can be: 12 seasons at [Athletic Bilbao](/clubs/athletic-bilbao), a Basque Country international, and a coaching style built on the region's traditions of intensity and running. He is soft-spoken and unshowy, which disguises how radical his football is. His appointment at [Liverpool](/clubs/liverpool) in June 2026 made him the club's first Spanish manager since Rafael Benítez.

## Playing career

Iraola came through Athletic's academy at Lezama and made more than 500 appearances for the club between 2003 and 2015, mostly as an attacking right-back with a fine delivery. He played in the 2009 and 2012 Copa del Rey finals and the 2012 Europa League final under Marcelo Bielsa, whose influence on him is obvious. He won seven caps for [Spain](/nations/spain) and finished his career with a season at New York City FC in 2015-16.

## Coaching career

His first job was at AEK Larnaca in Cyprus, where he won the cup in 2019. At Mirandés in the Segunda División he reached a Copa del Rey semi-final with a second-tier side, then took Rayo Vallecano up to La Liga in 2021 and kept them there in style, with another cup semi-final and a reputation for beating the big clubs at Vallecas, including [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona) and [Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid).

[Bournemouth](/clubs/bournemouth) hired him in June 2023 in place of Gary O'Neil. After a slow start, his side became one of the most intense in Europe: a record Premier League points total in 2023-24, another in 2024-25 with a ninth-place finish, and a sequence of results against the elite, including wins over [Arsenal](/clubs/arsenal), [Manchester City](/clubs/manchester-city), [Tottenham Hotspur](/clubs/tottenham-hotspur) and [Manchester United](/clubs/manchester-united). The club sold Dean Huijsen, Milos Kerkez and Illia Zabarnyi for huge fees and he kept the team competitive in 2025-26 regardless.

Liverpool's decision to sack [Arne Slot](/managers/arne-slot) on 30 May 2026, a year after Slot's title, following a fifth-place finish, led directly to Iraola. He was appointed in June 2026 and the club backed him immediately with [Bradley Barcola](/players/bradley-barcola) from [Paris Saint-Germain](/clubs/paris-saint-germain) for around €125m.

## Tactical identity

Iraola's football is [gegenpressing](/tactics/gegenpressing) at its most extreme. His Bournemouth team pressed man-to-man across the pitch, with the back line stepping up to squeeze the space and the goalkeeper a long way off his line behind a [high line](/tactics/high-line). The aim was to force long balls and win the second ball, then attack immediately. The shape was nominally a 4-2-3-1 but became a 4-4-2 in the press, and [pressing traps](/tactics/pressing-traps) on the touchline were a constant.

In possession his teams are vertical rather than patient: quick switches, early crosses, [direct play](/tactics/direct-play) to runners and a flood of bodies into the box. The style asks a huge physical price, and his Bournemouth sides were the league's leaders in distance covered. At Liverpool, with [Mohamed Salah](/players/mohamed-salah), [Alexander Isak](/players/alexander-isak) and Barcola, he has slightly tempered the man-marking and used more of a zonal mid-block to protect ageing legs, but the first instinct remains the same: win it back now.

## Achievements

- Cypriot Cup with AEK Larnaca in 2019
- Promotion with Rayo Vallecano in 2021 and two seasons of comfortable survival
- Two Copa del Rey semi-finals with second-tier and modest sides
- Bournemouth's record Premier League points and highest finish

## Storyline

Liverpool's summer of 2026 was one of dramatic change. Slot, champion in 2025, was gone within a year after a disappointing title defence. Iraola inherited a squad with Isak, Salah, Florian Wirtz, Hugo Ekitike and Ryan Gravenberch, and the club added Barcola to give him the wide pace his system craves. The sale of some of the Klopp-era veterans completed the turnover.

The 2026-27 season has started under a mix of excitement and scepticism. Supporters warmed to the aggressive style, which recalls the best of [Jürgen Klopp](/managers/jurgen-klopp), but early reports suggest the transition has not been seamless, as a system built on relentless running takes time to embed in a squad used to Slot's control. The Merseyside derby against [David Moyes](/managers/david-moyes)'s [Everton](/clubs/everton) and the contest with the champions [Arsenal](/clubs/arsenal) and the enriched Manchester City under [Enzo Maresca](/managers/enzo-maresca) define the competitive picture.

Iraola was also a target for [AC Milan](/clubs/ac-milan) and [Real Betis](/clubs/real-betis) in the spring of 2026 before Liverpool moved, and his stock has never been higher. The job is the biggest of his life by a distance. His supporters say the gap between his resources at Bournemouth and his results was the largest in the league; Liverpool have closed that gap and will expect the results to follow.
