## Biography

Carlo Ancelotti was born in Reggiolo, Emilia-Romagna, on 10 June 1959, the son of a farmer. He is the calmest great coach in the history of the game: a man who has won the Champions League five times and the league title in Italy, England, France, Spain and Germany without ever appearing to raise his voice. In May 2025 he left [Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid) to become the first foreign coach of [Brazil](/nations/brazil) in more than half a century, and as of November 2026 he remains in charge after the 2026 World Cup.

## Playing career

Ancelotti was an elegant midfielder for Parma, Roma, where he won the 1983 Scudetto, and [AC Milan](/clubs/ac-milan), where he was part of Arrigo Sacchi's European Cup-winning sides of 1989 and 1990. He won 26 caps for [Italy](/nations/italy) and played at the 1986 and 1990 World Cups. Knee injuries curtailed him, and he retired in 1992 to become Sacchi's assistant with the national team.

The Milan years built his reputation: Kaká, Andrea Pirlo, Paolo Maldini and the 2003 and 2007 Champions Leagues, with the 2005 Istanbul final the great wound. He won the double in his first season at [Chelsea](/clubs/chelsea), delivered La Décima at Madrid in 2014 and returned in 2021 to win two more European Cups with Karim Benzema, [Vinícius Júnior](/players/vinicius-junior) and [Jude Bellingham](/players/jude-bellingham). Brazil's federation chased him for years before he finally said yes in 2025.

## Tactical identity

Ancelotti is the great adapter. He built the 4-4-2 diamond at Milan with Pirlo as a deep playmaker, invented the Christmas-tree 4-3-2-1 to fit Kaká and Clarence Seedorf, and at Madrid preferred a 4-3-3 that became a 4-4-2 in defence with Bellingham as a second striker. His sides are typically compact in a mid-block and lethal in transition, so [counter-attacking](/tactics/counter-attacking) is the closest label, although the 2024 Madrid side used a [box midfield](/tactics/box-midfield) in possession and his Bayern and PSG teams played more [positional play](/tactics/positional-play). His genius is in man-management: he fits the system to the players, keeps dressing rooms calm and trusts his best footballers to decide games.

- FIFA Best Men's Coach 2022; UEFA Coach of the Year 2003, 2007, 2014, 2022

## Storyline

Brazil's 2026 [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) started with a 2-1 win over [Japan](/nations/japan) in the round of 32, decided in stoppage time, a reminder of how much the Seleção still rely on individual moments from Vinícius, Raphinha and Estêvão. The tournament ended before the final that Brazil wanted, and the inquest in the Brazilian press was predictably noisy, but the CBF had hired Ancelotti for the long term and he remains in charge for the start of the 2030 cycle.

What comes next is the 2027 [Copa América](/tournaments/copa-america), the CONMEBOL qualifiers for 2030 that begin in 2027, and the renewal of the squad: Neymar's era is over, Casemiro and Marquinhos are at the end, and a generation led by Estêvão, Endrick, Vitor Roque and Bremer takes over. Ancelotti's calm is exactly what a federation that has sacked a coach every two years needed. The question is whether a man who has spent 30 years with the best club players in the world can shape a national team in short camps.
