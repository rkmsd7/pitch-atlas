## Biography

Unai Emery Etxegoien was born in Hondarribia, in the Basque Country, on 3 November 1971, into a family of goalkeepers: his grandfather Antonio and father Juan kept goal for Real Unión and Real Sociedad. Emery became an outfield player instead, but the family trade left its mark in his obsession with preparation. He is famous for watching hours of opposition footage and for individual video sessions with players, and he has coached in four countries while remaining recognisably the same meticulous, restless figure.

## Playing career

A left-sided midfielder, Emery came through Real Sociedad's academy and made a handful of La Liga appearances before a long journeyman career in the Spanish second and third tiers with Toledo, Racing Ferrol, Leganés and Lorca Deportiva. He retired in 2004 after a knee injury, aged 32, and was handed the Lorca manager's job almost immediately.

## Coaching career

Emery's rise was fast. He took Lorca to promotion, then guided Almería into La Liga and to an eighth-place finish in their first top-flight season. [Valencia](/clubs/valencia) hired him in 2008 and he delivered three consecutive third-place finishes while the club sold David Villa, David Silva and Juan Mata. A short, unhappy spell at Spartak Moscow followed before [Sevilla](/clubs/sevilla) gave him the platform for his defining achievement: three straight [Europa League](/tournaments/europa-league) titles between 2014 and 2016.

[Paris Saint-Germain](/clubs/paris-saint-germain) recruited him in 2016, where he won the 2017-18 domestic treble but was also in charge for the 6-1 collapse at [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona). His time at [Arsenal](/clubs/arsenal) (2018-19) ended with a Europa League final defeat to [Chelsea](/clubs/chelsea) and a sacking that November. [Villarreal](/clubs/villarreal) revived him: a fourth Europa League, beating [Manchester United](/clubs/manchester-united) 11-10 on penalties in 2021, and a Champions League semi-final in 2022.

[Aston Villa](/clubs/aston-villa) appointed him in October 2022 with the club 16th. He took them to seventh, then fourth and a first Champions League campaign in 41 years, where Villa reached the quarter-finals in 2025 before losing narrowly to PSG.

## Tactical identity

Emery is often filed under [counter-attacking](/tactics/counter-attacking), and his Villa are devastating in transition, but the label undersells him. The base is a 4-4-2 that becomes a 4-2-2-2 in possession, with the wingers tucking inside to form a box in midfield and the full-backs supplying width. Villa are patient in build-up, with John McGinn and Youri Tielemans dropping to receive, before accelerating through [Morgan Rogers](/players/morgan-rogers), until his 2026 sale, and [Ollie Watkins](/players/ollie-watkins) running in behind.

Out of possession the famous feature was the extreme [high line](/tactics/high-line) of 2023-24, which caught opponents offside more than any team in Europe, paired with [pressing traps](/tactics/pressing-traps) that funnelled play wide. Emery has since become more pragmatic, happy to drop into a compact [low block](/tactics/low-block) in European knockout ties. [Set pieces](/tactics/set-pieces) are drilled relentlessly, and his half-time adjustments are a consistent source of second-half goals.

## Achievements

- Four Europa League titles, a record for a manager
- Ligue 1 2017-18 plus two Coupes de France and two Coupes de la Ligue
- Aston Villa's first Champions League qualification since 1982-83
- Champions League quarter-final with Villa in 2025
- La Liga Manager of the Year 2021 (Villarreal)

## Storyline

The summer of 2026 tested Emery's model. Morgan Rogers, the player he had turned from a Middlesbrough prospect into an [England](/nations/england) regular, left for [Chelsea](/clubs/chelsea) in a £117m British-record deal to work under [Xabi Alonso](/managers/xabi-alonso). Villa's finances under Profit and Sustainability rules have long forced them to sell, and Rogers followed Jhon Durán and Jacob Ramsey out of the door.

Emery's answer has always been structure over stars. Villa have been a top-six side for three seasons running, and his contract to 2027 makes him one of the longest-serving managers in the [Premier League](/leagues/premier-league), behind only [Mikel Arteta](/managers/mikel-arteta) and [Fabian Hürzeler](/managers/fabian-hurzeler)'s predecessors at Brighton. Early reports in 2026-27 suggest Villa remain in the European places, with Watkins still the focal point and Emiliano Martínez, a World Cup finalist again in July 2026 with [Argentina](/nations/argentina), behind them.

The question around Villa Park is whether the ceiling has been reached. Emery has been linked with [Real Betis](/clubs/real-betis) and [Sevilla](/clubs/sevilla) whenever a Spanish job opens, and there is a sense that his work at Villa is complete rather than unfinished. For now he keeps turning up with his laptop, keeps winning games he is expected to lose, and keeps Villa relevant in a league whose top end has never been more expensive.
