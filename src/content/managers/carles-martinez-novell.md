## Biography

Carles Martínez Novell was born in Catalonia on 13 August 1984 and belongs to the generation of Spanish coaches who never played professionally but grew up inside the method. He spent years on the staff of the Spanish Football Federation's youth teams, absorbing the positional principles that later produced the [Spain](/nations/spain) side that won the 2026 [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup), before an unlikely detour to Toulouse made his name. There, in a club run on data by the American group RedBird, he won the 2023 Coupe de France within months of his first senior appointment and then kept a selling club comfortably in [Ligue 1](/leagues/ligue-1) for three seasons. [Bayer Leverkusen](/clubs/bayer-leverkusen) appointed him on 1 July 2026, on a contract to June 2028, as the successor to [Kasper Hjulmand](/managers/kasper-hjulmand).

## Playing career

Martínez Novell did not play at a professional level. Like a number of the best coaches of his age group, among them [Julian Nagelsmann](/managers/julian-nagelsmann) and [Fabian Hürzeler](/managers/fabian-hurzeler), he moved into coaching in his early twenties and built his reputation through academy work, video analysis and the Spanish federation's coaching pathway rather than on the pitch. He has said this made him more curious, and more dependent on explaining ideas clearly, than coaches who can fall back on what they did as players.

## Coaching career

His formative years were spent with Spain's youth teams, working across age groups as an assistant and analyst while the federation's possession model was being passed from the senior side down to the under-15s. He arrived at Toulouse in 2022 as assistant to Philippe Montanier, part of a technical staff recruited to match the club's data-led recruitment, and was promoted to head coach in the spring of 2023 when Montanier was dismissed.

The impact was immediate. Toulouse beat Nantes 5-1 in the Coupe de France final at the Stade de France in April 2023, the club's first major trophy since 1957, and qualified for the Europa League, where they beat [Liverpool](/clubs/liverpool) 3-2 in Toulouse in November 2023. The following seasons were about sustainability: a mid-table finish while selling his best players every summer, then a ninth-place finish in 2025-26 that drew interest from bigger clubs. Leverkusen, who had finished sixth under Hjulmand and missed the [Champions League](/tournaments/champions-league), decided they wanted a younger coach with a clearer model and a track record of improving players on a budget.

## Tactical identity

Martínez Novell is a [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) coach who adapted the Spanish model to the physical demands of Ligue 1. At Toulouse he used a [back three](/tactics/back-three) in a 3-4-2-1, with wing-backs providing the width and the two attacking midfielders tucking inside to form a box with the double pivot ([box midfield](/tactics/box-midfield) in all but name). Build-up is patient and short, the goalkeeper is a genuine eleventh outfield player, and the aim is to draw the opponent's press before releasing a runner in behind.

Out of possession his teams are compact rather than reckless. He sets [pressing traps](/tactics/pressing-traps) on the touchline and prefers a coordinated jump to an all-out high press, which was a sensible choice at a club that could not afford elite athletes. At Leverkusen the ingredients are richer, and he has inherited the back-three structure that [Xabi Alonso](/managers/xabi-alonso) made the club's identity and that Hjulmand kept, so the early signs are of evolution rather than a rebuild: more width from the wing-backs, a full-back or centre-back stepping into midfield ([inverted full-backs](/tactics/inverted-full-backs)), and a sharper emphasis on set pieces, which were a Toulouse strength.

## Achievements

- Coupe de France 2023 with Toulouse, the club's first major trophy in 66 years, and Europa League qualification.
- A 3-2 Europa League win over Liverpool in November 2023.
- Three seasons of Ligue 1 stability at a selling club, finishing ninth in 2025-26.
- Appointed Bayer Leverkusen head coach from 1 July 2026 on a contract to June 2028.

## Storyline

Leverkusen's decision to sack Hjulmand after a sixth-place finish in 2025-26 was a statement about ambition. Sporting director Simon Rolfes had hired the Dane to steady the club after Alonso, [Florian Wirtz](/players/florian-wirtz) and others left in 2025, and stability was achieved; what the board wanted next was a return to the Champions League places and a recognisable style, and Martínez Novell was chosen for both. The contract, to June 2028, is short enough to signal that results matter but long enough to let him shape the squad.

The context is demanding. [Bayern Munich](/clubs/bayern-munich) under [Vincent Kompany](/managers/vincent-kompany) are the champions, with [Harry Kane](/players/harry-kane) fresh from winning the 2026 Ballon d'Or on 26 October after a 73-goal season; [Borussia Dortmund](/clubs/borussia-dortmund) under [Niko Kovač](/managers/niko-kovac) and [RB Leipzig](/clubs/rb-leipzig) under [Ole Werner](/managers/ole-werner) are the other established powers; and the [Bundesliga](/leagues/bundesliga) is watching how [Jürgen Klopp](/managers/jurgen-klopp)'s new Germany set-up, in place since 24 July 2026, affects its German internationals. Leverkusen are in the 2026-27 [Europa League](/tournaments/europa-league), which brings Thursday-Sunday rhythms the coach knows well from Toulouse.

The start has been uneven: as of 7 November 2026 Leverkusen sat sixth in the Bundesliga on 13 points from eight games, with four wins, a draw and three defeats, including a 3-0 loss at Hoffenheim and a 4-2 home defeat to Stuttgart in late October. That is roughly where Hjulmand left the club, and the patience of a board that sacked a coach for sixth place will be tested if the table does not move by the winter break. Martínez Novell's case is that his teams improve as his ideas take hold; the next months will show whether a coach who thrived with limited resources can make a richer squad play his football.
