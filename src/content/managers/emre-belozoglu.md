## Biography

Emre Belözoğlu, born in Istanbul on 7 September 1980, was the most gifted Turkish midfielder of his generation and is now the man charged with restoring order at [Fenerbahçe](/clubs/fenerbahce). He was announced as head coach on 3 November 2026, succeeding İsmail Kartal, who had resigned after a fourth spell lasting barely four months, with Dirk Kuyt having held the fort as interim coach through a [Champions League](/leagues/champions-league) tie against [Liverpool](/clubs/liverpool). It is Emre's second time in the Fenerbahçe dugout, after a short caretaker spell in 2021.

## Playing career

Emre broke through at [Galatasaray](/clubs/galatasaray) as a teenager, winning three league titles and the 2000 UEFA Cup under Fatih Terim before [Inter](/clubs/inter-milan) signed him in 2001. He spent four seasons in Serie A, then joined [Newcastle United](/clubs/newcastle-united) in 2005, where his left foot and set-piece delivery made him a cult favourite. He returned to Turkey with Fenerbahçe in 2008, winning the title in 2011 and captaining the club, with later spells at [Atlético Madrid](/clubs/atletico-madrid), where he won the 2012 Europa League under Diego Simeone, Başakşehir and a final Fenerbahçe return before retiring in 2020.

For [Turkey](/nations/turkey) he won 101 caps, played at the 2002 [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup), where the team finished third, and at Euro 2008. He was a deep-lying playmaker of exceptional vision, tempo control and temper.

## Coaching career

Emre's coaching career began almost by accident. Serving as Fenerbahçe's sporting director in 2021, he stepped in as caretaker for the final stretch of the season after Erol Bulut's departure. Başakşehir then appointed him in 2021 and gave him his first full project; the 2022-23 season brought a fourth-place finish and European qualification, with the team praised for its organised possession football. Spells at Ankaragücü and Antalyaspor were shorter and less successful, which is why his appointment at Fenerbahçe was greeted with a mixture of hope and scepticism in Istanbul.

The context is extraordinary. [José Mourinho](/managers/jose-mourinho) led Fenerbahçe in 2024-25, left in 2025, and the club has churned through coaches since. Kartal returned for a fourth spell in June 2026, offered his resignation after a Champions League draw with Roma in September, was initially persuaded to stay and then departed after a derby loss to Galatasaray. Kuyt kept the team ticking over until the November break, and the board, having briefly been linked with Sergio Conceição, settled on a club legend.

## Tactical identity

As a coach Emre favours the kind of football he played: patient, possession-based and built through midfield. His Başakşehir side used a 4-2-3-1 with a deep playmaker dictating from the base, full-backs who tucked [inside](/tactics/inverted-full-backs) to create a 3-2 build-up shape in the manner of [positional play](/tactics/positional-play), and a number ten who linked rather than finished. The defensive line was [high](/tactics/high-line) and the press organised around [traps](/tactics/pressing-traps) in the opposition half.

At Fenerbahçe he inherits a squad assembled for several different managers' ideas, and his first task is simpler: structure and belief. Expect a 4-2-3-1, a stronger emphasis on controlling tempo, and a reliance on senior players to steady a nervous dressing room. The season is too young, and his tenure too new, to say more with confidence.

## Achievements

- Fourth place and European qualification with Başakşehir in 2022-23.
- As a player, league titles in Turkey, Italy and Spain, the UEFA Cup with Galatasaray in 2000 and the Europa League with Atlético in 2012.
- 101 caps for Turkey and a World Cup bronze in 2002.

## Storyline

Fenerbahçe's 2026-27 season is a search for stability in a city where [Okan Buruk](/managers/okan-buruk)'s Galatasaray have won title after title. The Kartal-Portuguese coaching cycle, as the Turkish press has dubbed the club's habit of alternating between its favourite son and foreign managers, has produced little except frustration, and the fanbase's patience with president Ali Koç's successors is thin. Emre, as captain of the last Fenerbahçe title-winning side in 2011, carries real goodwill.

He takes over with the [Süper Lig](/leagues/super-lig) season roughly a third gone and the club still in the Champions League league phase. Early results after the November break will shape the mood quickly; a derby, a European night and a cup run are all waiting. His record as a coach is mixed, his status as a Fenerbahçe icon is not, and the job will test which matters more.
