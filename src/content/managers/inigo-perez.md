## Biography

Iñigo Pérez Soto was born in Pamplona on 18 January 1988 and is one of the fastest-rising coaches in Spain. A midfielder for Osasuna, Athletic and Numancia, he retired at 33, became [Andoni Iraola](/managers/andoni-iraola)'s assistant and then, in February 2024, took over Rayo Vallecano and turned the smallest budget in Madrid into one of La Liga's most admired teams. [Villarreal](/clubs/villarreal) appointed him in June 2026 on a contract to 2029 to replace Marcelino.

## Playing career

Pérez came through Osasuna's academy, debuted in La Liga in 2008 and spent most of his career as a tidy, left-footed central midfielder: Osasuna, a spell at [Athletic Bilbao](/clubs/athletic-bilbao) under Marcelo Bielsa, where he was a squad player in the 2012 double-final side, then Mirandés and Numancia. He played more than 250 senior games and retired in 2021 with a reputation as a thinker.

## Coaching career

He joined Iraola's staff at Rayo in 2021 and followed him to [Bournemouth](/clubs/bournemouth) in 2023, learning the pressing game that Iraola has since taken to [Liverpool](/clubs/liverpool). When Rayo sacked Francisco in February 2024 they turned to Pérez, who had only ever been an assistant. He kept them up with room to spare, and in 2024-25 took them to eighth, matching the club's best-ever top-flight finish and earning a first European campaign.

That 2025-26 Conference League run became a story in itself: Rayo, from a ground with one open end, reached the final in Leipzig before losing. In La Liga they finished mid-table despite the European load. Villarreal, who had just qualified for the [Champions League](/tournaments/champions-league) under Marcelino but could not agree a contract extension with him, moved quickly. Pérez was announced in June 2026 and presented at the Estadio de la Cerámica on a three-year deal.

## Tactical identity

Pérez coaches an intense 4-2-3-1 built on [pressing traps](/tactics/pressing-traps). His Rayo invited the opponent to play into a specific zone, usually the full-back, then jumped with the winger, the number ten and a midfielder to win the ball within 30 metres of goal. It is [gegenpressing](/tactics/gegenpressing) by design rather than by chaos, and it demands a [high line](/tactics/high-line) and a goalkeeper comfortable sweeping.

With the ball his teams play faster than the Iraola template, more vertical, with wingers attacking the byline and the striker (Jorge de Frutos and Isi Palazón were the Rayo stars) arriving late. He uses elements of [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) in the build-up but is happy to go long against a high press. At Villarreal, with a more technical squad and the European schedule, he has worked on making the press sustainable across three games a week and on a more patient possession phase, especially at home, where the club's identity has long been a passing one.

## Achievements

- Rayo Vallecano's joint-best La Liga finish (eighth, 2024-25) and first European qualification in 25 years.
- UEFA Conference League finalist 2026 with Rayo.
- Survival in 2023-24 after taking over mid-season as a first-time head coach.
- Appointed Villarreal head coach at 38, with a contract to June 2029.

## Storyline

Villarreal's 2026-27 season combines a Champions League return with a change of philosophy. Marcelino, who had rebuilt the club after a chaotic 2023-24, left in May 2026 wanting two more years when the club would offer one; Pérez is the long-term bet. He inherited a squad with Europa League and Champions League experience and the task of making it press like Rayo without losing the control that fans at the Cerámica expect.

The context in [La Liga](/leagues/la-liga) is unforgiving: [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona) are champions twice over under [Hansi Flick](/managers/hansi-flick), [José Mourinho](/managers/jose-mourinho) is back at [Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid), and [Atlético Madrid](/clubs/atletico-madrid) under [Diego Simeone](/managers/diego-simeone) remain the third force. Villarreal's realistic target is to defend their top-five place and to make the Champions League league phase matter. As of November 2026 the early reports have been encouraging about the intensity of the side, and Pérez's youth and the club's track record with young coaches (Unai Emery among them) make this one of the most interesting projects in Spain.
