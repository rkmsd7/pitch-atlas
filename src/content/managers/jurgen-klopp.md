## Biography

Jürgen Norbert Klopp was born in Stuttgart on 16 June 1967 and grew up in Glatten in the Black Forest, the son of a travelling salesman who was also a demanding amateur coach. Klopp is the great communicator of modern management: a coach whose teams play with the intensity of their manager's touchline presence. He turned [Borussia Dortmund](/clubs/borussia-dortmund) into Bayern's equal, gave [Liverpool](/clubs/liverpool) their first league title in thirty years, and in July 2026, after eighteen months away from the touchline, accepted the hardest job in German football.

## Playing career

Klopp spent his entire senior career at Mainz 05, from 1990 to 2001, first as a striker and later as a right-back in the second division. He scored 52 goals in 325 league games and often joked that he had "a fourth-division head and a first-division heart". His playing days taught him what a limited team needs to compete, which became the core of his coaching.

He became Mainz coach the day after retiring, kept them up, took them into the Bundesliga for the first time, and then into Europe. At Dortmund he won back-to-back titles with Mario Götze, Robert Lewandowski and Shinji Kagawa, beat Bayern 5-2 in the 2012 cup final, and lost the 2013 Champions League final at Wembley. At Liverpool he built the side of [Mohamed Salah](/players/mohamed-salah), Sadio Mané, Virgil van Dijk and Alisson that won every available trophy, reached three Champions League finals and recorded 97 and 99 points in losing title races to Pep Guardiola's City. He left in May 2024, worked as Red Bull's global head of soccer, and stepped in for Germany after Julian Nagelsmann resigned on 3 July 2026.

## Tactical identity

Klopp is the public face of [gegenpressing](/tactics/gegenpressing): the idea, inherited from [Ralf Rangnick](/managers/ralf-rangnick) and Wolfgang Frank, that the moment after losing the ball is the best moment to win it back. His Dortmund side pressed in a 4-2-3-1 with Götze and Kagawa triggering [pressing traps](/tactics/pressing-traps) on the touchline. At Liverpool the shape became a 4-3-3 with a workaholic midfield, inverted-running wingers and marauding full-backs Trent Alexander-Arnold and Andy Robertson, and the [high line](/tactics/high-line) held by Van Dijk. Over time the side matured from a pure [counter-attacking](/tactics/counter-attacking) outfit into one that could dominate possession, but the identity never changed: run more, sprint more, and make the opponent uncomfortable. With Germany, where he cannot drill players daily, he has spoken about simplifying the pressing triggers and building around Florian Wirtz and [Jamal Musiala](/players/jamal-musiala).

## Storyline

Germany's 2026 [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup) ended in the round of 32 against Paraguay on penalties, the third consecutive tournament that a four-time champion has fallen before the quarter-finals. Nagelsmann resigned within 48 hours. The DFB had already opened talks with Klopp, and on 24 July 2026 he was confirmed, with reports that his contract runs to the 2030 World Cup. He had always said he would not coach a club again in the near future; a national team, with its long breaks and short camps, was the one job he could not turn down.

The next steps are the 2026-27 [Nations League](/tournaments/nations-league), including a home match against [Xavi's Netherlands](/managers/xavi-manager) on 16 November 2026, and then the qualifiers for [Euro 2028](/tournaments/euro). The big calls are about the spine: who replaces Manuel Neuer and Toni Kroos for good, whether Antonio Rüdiger has another cycle in him, and how to get the best out of Wirtz, Musiala and Kai Havertz, who scored in the 2026 Champions League final for Arsenal. Klopp has the public, the federation and the Bundesliga coaches on his side, which is more than any Germany coach has enjoyed since 2014.
