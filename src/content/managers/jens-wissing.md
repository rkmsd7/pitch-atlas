## Biography

Jens Wissing, born in Germany in 1988, is one of the youngest coaches ever to lead a major Saudi club and one of the least expected. On 10 July 2026, at 38, he was unveiled by [Al Ittihad](/clubs/al-ittihad) on a two-year contract running to 2028, succeeding Sergio Conceição, who had left by mutual consent in June after a disappointing 2025-26. His credentials were earned in Japan: he had just led Gamba Osaka to the 2026 AFC Champions League, the most prestigious prize in Asian club football, and the Jeddah club decided a young, pressing-oriented German was the profile they wanted after a season of drift.

Arab News noted the parallels with Jeddah neighbours [Al Ahli](/clubs/al-ahli), who had hired another young German, [Matthias Jaissle](/managers/matthias-jaissle), to great effect before he left for [Newcastle United](/clubs/newcastle-united) in August 2026.

## Playing career

Wissing's playing career was modest and short. He played in Germany's regional leagues and amateur divisions, never reaching the professional tiers, and moved into coaching while still in his twenties, following the path of a growing number of German "laptop coaches" who built their expertise through academies, analysis and youth development rather than a playing record. He has spoken of being drawn to the methodological side of the game early, and of studying the pressing schools of Ralf Rangnick and [Jürgen Klopp](/managers/jurgen-klopp).

## Coaching career

He worked his way through German youth football, including a spell on the staff of [Borussia Dortmund](/clubs/borussia-dortmund)'s under-19s, where he was exposed to the club's famous high-intensity principles. The move abroad came via Japan: he joined Gamba Osaka as an assistant in 2023 under Dani Poyatos, and when the Spaniard's time ended he was promoted to head coach.

Gamba under Wissing were a revelation. A club that had drifted in the mid-table of the [J1 League](/leagues/j1-league) became a high-pressing, fast-breaking side, and in the 2025-26 AFC Champions League Elite they progressed through the Asian knockout rounds to lift the trophy, beating a succession of better-funded West Asian clubs on the way. That run was effectively his job interview for Saudi Arabia.

Al Ittihad, champions in 2023 and 2025, had endured a frustrating 2025-26 under Conceição despite a squad containing [Karim Benzema](/players/karim-benzema), N'Golo Kanté, Fabinho and Moussa Diaby. Wissing's first competitive match was a King Cup of Champions round-of-32 tie against Al-Najma.

## Tactical identity

Wissing is a product of the German pressing school. His teams defend forward: a [high line](/tactics/high-line), a compact block that shifts aggressively to the ball side, and a [gegenpressing](/tactics/gegenpressing) trigger whenever possession is lost in the opposition half. At Gamba the base shape was a 4-2-3-1 that became a 4-3-3 out of possession, with the wingers pressing the full-backs and the number ten blocking the pivot. He uses [pressing traps](/tactics/pressing-traps) to funnel play into the wide areas and attacks in waves of vertical passes as soon as the ball is recovered.

In possession he is more structured than the stereotype suggests, using the principles of [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) to create numerical superiority in build-up before accelerating. The Saudi Pro League feature on the summer's new managers described him as a coach who wanted Al Ittihad to "press, press, press", and the early question has been whether a squad of veteran stars can sustain that intensity in the Jeddah heat. Benzema, as a pressing centre-forward, is an intriguing experiment.

## Achievements

- AFC Champions League Elite 2025-26 with Gamba Osaka, the club's first Asian title since 2008.
- Transformed Gamba from mid-table into a continental champion within two seasons.
- Became one of the youngest head coaches of a reigning Saudi Pro League power at 38.

## Storyline

Al Ittihad's 2026-27 season is a bet on youth in the dugout and experience on the pitch. The club's hierarchy saw what Jaissle did at Al Ahli, winning the AFC Champions League in 2025 with a modern, pressing team, and wants the same identity. The risk is obvious: Wissing has never coached in a league with the scrutiny of the [Saudi Pro League](/leagues/saudi-pro-league), where rivals [Al Hilal](/clubs/al-hilal) under [Simone Inzaghi](/managers/simone-inzaghi) and champions [Al Nassr](/clubs/al-nassr) under [Ange Postecoglou](/managers/ange-postecoglou) have star power and deep benches.

Early-season coverage in late July still introduced him as the "new first-team coach" and the autumn has been about bedding in a system rather than chasing records. The two-year contract is short enough to be a trial and long enough to build something. If Al Ittihad press their way back to the title, Wissing will be the most sought-after young German coach outside the Bundesliga; if the veterans cannot run as he demands, Jeddah is not a patient city.
