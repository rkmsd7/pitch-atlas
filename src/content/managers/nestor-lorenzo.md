## Biography

Néstor Gabriel Lorenzo was born in Buenos Aires on 28 February 1966. A centre-back who played at the 1990 World Cup final, he spent most of his coaching career as José Pékerman's assistant with Argentina and then with [Colombia](/nations/colombia), where the pair took the national team to the 2014 World Cup quarter-finals. Appointed Colombia's head coach in June 2022, he has led them on a record unbeaten run, to the 2024 [Copa América](/tournaments/copa-america) final and to the 2026 [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup), after which the federation confirmed he would continue.

## Playing career

Lorenzo played for Argentinos Juniors, Bari in Italy, where he spent four seasons, and San Lorenzo, Chacarita and Racing, retiring in 1999. He won nine caps for [Argentina](/nations/argentina) and was a starter at the 1990 World Cup, playing in the final against West Germany in Rome.

His only previous head-coaching job was at Melgar in Arequipa, where he won the 2022 Apertura and took the Peruvian club to a Copa Sudamericana semi-final. The Colombian federation, which had just missed the 2022 World Cup under Reinaldo Rueda, chose him in June 2022 on a four-year deal for his knowledge of the group. Colombia went 28 games unbeaten between 2022 and the 2024 Copa América final, which they lost 1-0 to Argentina after extra time.

## Tactical identity

Lorenzo plays a 4-2-3-1 that is, in possession, a genuine [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) structure: full-backs high, two holding midfielders staggered, and [James Rodríguez](/players/james-rodriguez) given a free role between the lines. He was the first coach to make James the centre of the team again, and the result was the player of the 2024 Copa América. Without the ball Colombia press in coordinated [pressing traps](/tactics/pressing-traps) and are dangerous on the [counter](/tactics/counter-attacking) through [Luis Díaz](/players/luis-diaz) and Jhon Arias. He has also found a settled defence in Davinson Sánchez, Jhon Lucumí and Daniel Muñoz, and uses Jhon Córdoba or Jhon Durán as a physical nine.

## Storyline

Colombia went to the 2026 World Cup with their most complete squad since 2014. The tournament ended in the knockout rounds and provoked the usual Colombian debate about whether a coach with a 60-year-old captain-in-spirit in James Rodríguez could take the team further. The federation settled it by confirming in the summer that Lorenzo would continue into the next cycle, closing months of speculation.

What comes next is the 2027 Copa América, the start of CONMEBOL qualifying for 2030 in 2027, and a generational handover: James is 35, Díaz and Arias are in their peak years, and younger players such as Durán, Richard Ríos and Kevin Castaño are the base for 2030. Lorenzo has made Colombia a consistent, organised team for the first time since the Pékerman era; the next step is a trophy.
