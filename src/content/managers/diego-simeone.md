## Biography

Diego Pablo Simeone was born in Buenos Aires on 28 April 1970 and has been [Atlético Madrid](/clubs/atletico-madrid)'s manager since 23 December 2011, a span that makes him the longest-serving coach at any major European club. He is Cholismo in human form: the fist-pumping, black-clad figure who turned a chaotic, perennially second-tier-threatened club into a European power on the principle that effort is non-negotiable. In November 2026 he is in his fifteenth season, contracted to June 2027.

## Playing career

Simeone was a ferocious, intelligent central midfielder who won 106 caps for [Argentina](/nations/argentina) and played at three [World Cups](/tournaments/world-cup), most famously the 1998 incident with David Beckham. He won the 1996 double with Atlético under Radomir Antić, Serie A with [Inter](/clubs/inter-milan) in 1998 (plus the UEFA Cup) and with [Lazio](/clubs/lazio) in 2000, and two Copa Américas with Argentina. He returned to Atlético for a last spell in 2003-05 before finishing at Racing Club.

## Coaching career

He coached in Argentina within months of retiring, winning the Apertura with Estudiantes in 2006 and the Clausura with [River Plate](/clubs/river-plate) in 2008, with spells at San Lorenzo, Catania and Racing. Atlético, mired in mid-table, appointed him in December 2011. Within five months he had won the Europa League, in 2013 the Copa del Rey at the Bernabéu, and in 2014 the [La Liga](/leagues/la-liga) title, clinched with a draw at Camp Nou, breaking a decade of Barcelona-Madrid duopoly. [Champions League](/tournaments/champions-league) finals followed in 2014 and 2016, both lost to [Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid).

A second Europa League in 2018 and a second league title in 2021, built around Luis Suárez and Jan Oblak, confirmed that the first was no fluke. Recent seasons have been about sustaining a top-three presence with [Antoine Griezmann](/players/antoine-griezmann) and [Julián Álvarez](/players/julian-alvarez), with [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona) winning the last two titles under [Hansi Flick](/managers/hansi-flick).

## Tactical identity

Simeone's foundational shape is the 4-4-2 in a compact [low block](/tactics/low-block), two banks of four no more than 25 metres apart, strikers working back to make it a 4-4-2 of ten defenders. From there his sides are lethal in [counter-attacking](/tactics/counter-attacking) transition and dominant at [set pieces](/tactics/set-pieces), long a source of a third of their goals. He uses [pressing traps](/tactics/pressing-traps) on the touchline rather than a high press.

The identity has evolved more than caricature allows. Since 2020 he has frequently used a [back three](/tactics/back-three) or 5-3-2, giving Griezmann freedom between the lines and asking wing-backs to carry the ball. The 2021 title was won with more possession than any Simeone team before it, and the Álvarez-era sides press higher and attack with more players. What never changes is the emotional contract: every player runs, every player defends, and the Metropolitano demands it.

## Achievements

- Two La Liga titles (2014, 2021) and two Europa Leagues (2012, 2018) with Atlético.
- Two Champions League finals (2014, 2016) and two UEFA Super Cups.
- More than 800 matches as Atlético manager, the most in the club's history.
- League titles in Argentina with Estudiantes and River Plate, and two Copa Américas as a player.

## Storyline

Atlético's 2026-27 season carries the quiet question of succession. Simeone's contract runs to June 2027 and the departure of sporting director Andrea Berta prompted reports that the club's new football leadership was considering candidates for the long term, with Marcelino among the names floated; nothing has been confirmed and Simeone himself has given no sign of leaving. He passed 700 and then 800 matches in charge, numbers no modern coach at a top club comes close to.

On the pitch the landscape is harder than ever. Barcelona are champions twice over with [Lamine Yamal](/players/lamine-yamal), and [José Mourinho](/managers/jose-mourinho) has returned to Real Madrid with [Kylian Mbappé](/players/kylian-mbappe) and the €125m signing Yan Diomande. Atlético's Argentine core, Álvarez among them, came back from a World Cup final defeat to [Spain](/nations/spain) on 19 July 2026. For Simeone the aim is what it always is: to be the third force that nobody wants to play, and to win the one trophy that has escaped him. As of November 2026 the derby and the Champions League remain the measures, and the sense around the club is that the Cholo era, whenever it ends, will end on his terms.
