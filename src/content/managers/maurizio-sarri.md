## Biography

Maurizio Sarri was born in Naples on 10 January 1959 but grew up in Figline Valdarno, Tuscany, and did not coach a professional club until he was in his forties. For most of his working life he was a banker at Monte dei Paschi di Siena, coaching amateur sides in the evenings. He chain-smokes, wears tracksuits to formal occasions and is prone to a bleak, dry wit. He is also the man who, with Sarriball, produced possibly the most aesthetically complete possession football [Serie A](/leagues/serie-a) has seen.

## Playing career

Sarri was an amateur centre-back for Figline in Tuscany's regional leagues. He never played professionally, and he has often pointed out that this gave him time to watch, think and study tactics without the distractions of a career. He is among the most prominent examples of the coach who learned the game from the stands rather than the pitch.

## Coaching career

His climb was long. He coached Stia, Faellese, Cavriglia, Antella, Valdema and Tegoleto in Tuscany's amateur ranks, then took Sansovino into professional football with two promotions. Pescara, Arezzo, Avellino, Verona, Perugia, Grosseto, Alessandria and Sorrento followed before Empoli, where he won promotion to Serie A in 2014 and kept them up in style with a 4-3-1-2 that caught the eye of Aurelio De Laurentiis.

[Napoli](/clubs/napoli) hired him in 2015. Over three seasons with Marek Hamšík, Lorenzo Insigne, Dries Mertens as a [false nine](/tactics/false-nine) and Jorginho as the metronome, Napoli finished second, third and second, the last with 91 points in 2017-18, the highest total ever by a side that did not win the title. [Chelsea](/clubs/chelsea) signed him in 2018; he finished third and won the 2019 [Europa League](/tournaments/europa-league) 4-1 against [Arsenal](/clubs/arsenal) in Baku. [Juventus](/clubs/juventus) followed, and he won the 2019-20 Scudetto, but the relationship ended after a Champions League exit.

At [Lazio](/clubs/lazio) from 2021 to 2024 he finished second in 2022-23 before resigning in March 2024. He returned to Lazio in 2025 for a season that ended with a parting by mutual consent on 27 May 2026. Within three weeks, in mid-June 2026, [Atalanta](/clubs/atalanta) announced him on a three-year contract after sacking Raffaele Palladino, who had taken them to seventh and the Conference League.

## Tactical identity

Sarriball is a 4-3-3 built on [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) and a particularly fast, short-passing version of it that owes something to [tiki-taka](/tactics/tiki-taka). The regista sits in front of the defence and receives constantly; the two mezzali make staggered runs; the wingers cut inside and the full-backs overlap in choreographed patterns that are drilled relentlessly in training, often without opponents. Sarri's teams keep a [high line](/tactics/high-line) and press in a tight, compact 4-5-1 with the first pressing [trap](/tactics/pressing-traps) set on the opposition full-back.

His dogma has a cost: he needs a particular profile in each position and takes time to install the idea, which is why early seasons at Chelsea and Juventus were uncomfortable. The evolution has been minimal by design. Sarri would rather adapt players to the system than the system to players, though at Lazio he accepted more direct transitions and a slightly deeper block.

At Atalanta he inherits a squad raised on [Gian Piero Gasperini](/managers/gian-piero-gasperini)'s man-marking and a [back three](/tactics/back-three), the stylistic opposite of his zonal four. Early-season reports suggested a shift to a 4-3-3 and a slower, more controlled build-up.

## Achievements

- Serie A champion 2019-20 with Juventus.
- Europa League winner 2018-19 with Chelsea.
- Serie A Coach of the Year 2017 and Panchina d'Oro 2016-17 for Napoli's football.
- Promotion with Empoli in 2014 and with Sansovino, twice, in the amateur ranks.

## Storyline

Atalanta's choice of Sarri is a philosophical rupture. Bergamo's identity for nine years was Gasperini's chaos, and the sackings of Ivan Jurić in November 2025 and Palladino in June 2026 showed a club impatient for a new direction. Sarri is the third permanent coach since Gasperini left for [Roma](/clubs/roma), and the Percassi family's three-year commitment is a bet that his structure will give Atalanta a sustainable top-four model.

The context is the most competitive Serie A of recent years: [Cristian Chivu](/managers/cristian-chivu)'s [Inter](/clubs/inter-milan) are champions, [Massimiliano Allegri](/managers/massimiliano-allegri) is at Napoli, [Luciano Spalletti](/managers/luciano-spalletti) at Juventus and [Ruben Amorim](/managers/ruben-amorim) at [AC Milan](/clubs/ac-milan). Atalanta also have a Conference League campaign to manage.

As of November 2026 the state of the league campaign cannot be confirmed here. Sarri's history says the first half of a season is where he suffers and the second half is where his football appears. Bergamo, a city used to watching ideas take root, will have to decide how long it is prepared to wait.
