## Biography

Julian Nagelsmann was born in Landsberg am Lech, Bavaria, on 23 July 1987. A knee injury ended his playing career before it began, and by 28 he was the youngest head coach in Bundesliga history. He took Hoffenheim into the Champions League, RB Leipzig to a European semi-final, won the title with [Bayern Munich](/clubs/bayern-munich) at 34, and then rebuilt [Germany](/nations/germany) for a home Euro 2024 that ended in a stoppage-time quarter-final defeat to Spain. His Germany lost on penalties to Paraguay in the round of 32 at the 2026 [World Cup](/tournaments/world-cup), and he resigned on 3 July 2026, two years before his contract was due to expire. As of November 2026 he has not taken another job.

## Playing career

Nagelsmann was a centre-back in the youth teams of Augsburg and 1860 Munich. Persistent knee problems forced him to retire at 20 without a senior appearance. [Thomas Tuchel](/managers/thomas-tuchel), then Augsburg's reserve coach, gave him a scouting role, and he was coaching Hoffenheim's under-17s by 24 and their under-19s to a national title by 26.

He saved Hoffenheim from relegation in 2016 and took them to fourth and third in his two full seasons. At [RB Leipzig](/clubs/rb-leipzig) he beat Tottenham and Atlético Madrid on the way to the 2020 Champions League semi-final. Bayern paid a record compensation fee for him in 2021; he won the league in his first season and was sacked in March 2023 with the team second. The DFB hired him in September 2023 with Germany in crisis, and the Euro 2024 campaign, with Florian Wirtz and [Jamal Musiala](/players/jamal-musiala) at its heart, restored national pride.

## Tactical identity

Nagelsmann is a restless tactician in the [gegenpressing](/tactics/gegenpressing) tradition who changes shape more than most. He has used a [back three](/tactics/back-three), a 4-2-2-2 inherited from the Red Bull system, and at Euro 2024 a 4-2-3-1 built on Toni Kroos's return, with Wirtz and Musiala as twin tens and Kai Havertz as a fluid striker. In possession his sides follow [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) principles, with asymmetric full-backs and third-man runs, and he was among the first to use drone footage and video walls in training. The criticism, at Bayern and with Germany, has been of over-complication and of a tendency to tinker in moments that called for stability.

- German Football Manager of the Year 2017, the youngest winner

## Storyline

The 2026 World Cup was supposed to confirm the recovery that Euro 2024 had begun. Instead a group-stage wobble and a round-of-32 penalty shoot-out defeat to Paraguay sent Germany home earlier than any tournament since 1938, and Nagelsmann's statement two days later said the result "truly pains me". The DFB had already opened talks with [Jürgen Klopp](/managers/jurgen-klopp), who took over on 24 July.

Nagelsmann is 39, with a Bundesliga title, a European semi-final and a tournament quarter-final on his record. No new appointment had been verified as of November 2026. The usual suspects were mentioned in the German press, from a return to the Bundesliga to the Premier League, where several clubs were struggling by the autumn. His next job will tell us whether the Germany experience matured him or simply tired him. His status should be checked against the live data on this site.
