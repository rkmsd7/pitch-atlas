## History

Al Nassr was founded in 1955 by the Al-Ja'ba brothers in Riyadh and spent its first decades as the smaller of the capital's two clubs, overshadowed by Al Hilal, before the arrival of a striker who changed its history. Majed Abdullah, "the Arabian Jewel", scored 189 league goals between 1977 and 1998 and led the club to its first championships in 1975, 1980 and 1981 and the King Cup of 1986, 1987 and 1990. The nickname "Al-Alami", the global one, dates from 1998, when the club won the Asian Cup Winners' Cup and the Asian Super Cup and became the first Saudi club to play in the inaugural FIFA Club World Championship in Brazil in 2000, where it lost to Corinthians and Real Madrid and beat Raja Casablanca.

The 2000s were a long trough, with the club never finishing higher than third and often in the bottom half, until the president Prince Faisal bin Turki's 2010s rebuild. Titles came in 2013-14 and 2014-15, the second under the Uruguayan coach Jorge da Silva, in a period of star signings from Brazil and Morocco. A ninth title, in 2018-19 under the Portuguese Rui Vitória, with the Moroccan striker Abderrazak Hamdallah scoring a league-record 34 goals, was the last for seven years, until the 2025-26 title won with Cristiano Ronaldo.

## Trajectory

On 30 December 2022 Al Nassr announced the signing of [Cristiano Ronaldo](/players/cristiano-ronaldo) on a contract reported at more than €200m a year, the first of the Saudi Pro League's superstar signings and the one that made the league a global story. The Public Investment Fund took 75 per cent ownership of the club in June 2023, along with Al Hilal, Al Ittihad and Al Ahli, and Sadio Mané, Marcelo Brozović, Aymeric Laporte and Otávio arrived that summer. Ronaldo scored 35 league goals in 2023-24, a Saudi record, and the club won the 2023 Arab Club Champions Cup, but the league title has stayed out of reach: second in 2022-23 and 2023-24, third in 2024-25, with Al Hilal and then Al Ittihad winning. Ronaldo signed a new contract in June 2025 to 2027.

[Jorge Jesus](/managers/jorge-jesus) coached the club in 2025-26, with João Félix and Kingsley Coman joining that summer, before leaving to take the [Portugal](/nations/portugal) national team after the 2026 World Cup. The club's current squad data lists Ange Postecoglou, the former Tottenham and Celtic manager, as head coach as of November 2026, and Al Nassr are third in the 2026-27 Saudi Pro League with 28 points from 11 games, two behind Al Hilal.

## Players the club produced

Al Nassr's youth system produced Majed Abdullah, Fahad Al-Harifi, Mohaisen Al-Jam'an and the defenders Abdullah Al-Suwayed and Sultan Al-Ghannam, the long-serving captain of the Ronaldo era, as well as the goalkeeper Nawaf Al-Aqidi. The club's reputation now rests on its imports: Ronaldo, Mané, Brozović, Félix, Coman, the Colombian goalkeeper David Ospina, the Brazilian Talisca and the Moroccan Hamdallah.

## Identity and playing style

Since 2023 Al Nassr have been built around a single idea: give the ball to Ronaldo in the box. Luís Castro's 2023-24 team played a 4-2-3-1 with Talisca as the second scorer and Mané and Otávio feeding crosses; Stefano Pioli's 2024-25 team was more controlled and less productive. Jesus brought a high line and sustained pressure, the approach he had used at Flamengo and Benfica, with Félix as a false ten and Coman and Mané running the channels; Postecoglou's instinct is the same, an aggressive 4-3-3 with inverted full-backs and relentless possession, which suits a squad built to attack but exposes an ageing defence. See [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) and [high line](/tactics/high-line).

## Rivalries

The Riyadh derby against [Al Hilal](/clubs/al-hilal) is the biggest fixture in Saudi football, two clubs from the same city with the two largest followings in the country, and Al Hilal's dominance of the 2020s has made it one-sided enough to hurt. The games with [Al Ittihad](/clubs/al-ittihad) of Jeddah, Benzema against Ronaldo, became the league's marquee television fixture, and Al Shabab, the third Riyadh club, is the local third wheel.

## Current situation

As of November 2026 Al Nassr are third in the Saudi Pro League under Postecoglou, two points behind Al Hilal after 11 games, with Ronaldo, now 41 and still the top scorer, Mané, Félix, Coman, Brozović and Al-Ghannam as the core. The club's ambition is the one it has chased since 2023, a first title in the PIF era, and the AFC Champions League Elite, where it has yet to go beyond the semi-finals. Ronaldo's contract runs to 2027, and whether he plays beyond it is the question every Saudi broadcaster asks weekly.
