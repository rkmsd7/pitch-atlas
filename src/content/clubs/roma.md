## History

Roma were created by decree. In 1927 the fascist official Italo Foschi merged three of the capital's clubs, Roman, Alba-Audace and Fortitudo, to build a side capable of challenging the northern powers; Lazio, protected by a general in the regime, refused to join, which is why Rome has two clubs and one of the bitterest derbies in the game. The first Scudetto came in 1941-42 with Amedeo Amadei, the baker's son from Frascati who had debuted at 15, as the striker. The post-war decades were lean, with a relegation in 1951 and a single Inter-Cities Fairs Cup in 1961, until the Swedish coach Nils Liedholm built the side of the early 1980s around Falcão, the Brazilian midfielder known in Rome as the eighth king, Bruno Conti, Agostino Di Bartomei and Roberto Pruzzo. That side won the 1983 title and lost the 1984 European Cup final on penalties to Liverpool in their own stadium, a wound that has never fully closed.

The third title, in 2000-01, was Fabio Capello's: [Francesco Totti](/players/francesco-totti), [Gabriel Batistuta](/players/gabriel-batistuta), bought from Fiorentina at 31 for a huge fee, Vincenzo Montella, [Cafu](/players/cafu), Aldair and Walter Samuel. It remains the club's last league title, and the two decades since have been a succession of near misses, second to Juventus and Inter six times, nine Coppa Italias, a Conference League in 2022 under [José Mourinho](/managers/jose-mourinho) that was the first European trophy of the club's history, and a Europa League final lost on penalties to Sevilla in Budapest in 2023.

## Trajectory

Roma have never been relegated since 1951 and have finished in the top half of Serie A in every season this century, mostly between second and sixth. The Sensi family's ownership ended in debt in 2011 and the American group led by James Pallotta took over, promising a stadium that never got built; Luciano Spalletti and Rudi Garcia produced fine, trophyless sides, and Eusebio Di Francesco's 2018 team beat Barcelona 3-0 to reach a Champions League semi-final. Dan and Ryan Friedkin bought the club in 2020 for €591m, hired Mourinho in 2021, and have since cycled through [Daniele De Rossi](/managers/daniele-de-rossi), Ivan Jurić and Claudio Ranieri in a single chaotic 2024-25 before the appointment of [Gian Piero Gasperini](/managers/gian-piero-gasperini) in June 2025. Gasperini took a squad that had finished fifth and drove it to third in 2025-26 with 73 points and a return to the Champions League, the club's first since 2019.

The 2022 Conference League, won 1-0 against Feyenoord in Tirana with a Nicolò Zaniolo goal, was the first trophy of any kind since the 2008 Coppa Italia and the first European one in 61 years, and the celebration at the Circus Maximus was attended by more than 100,000 people. Mourinho's second season ended with the Europa League final in Budapest, lost 4-1 on penalties to Sevilla after a 1-1 draw and a famous confrontation with the referee Anthony Taylor in the car park, and he was sacked in January 2024 with the club ninth. De Rossi's half-season and Ranieri's third spell, which took Roma from 15th in November 2024 to fifth, were the bridge to Gasperini.

## Players the club produced

Totti is the whole story for most supporters: born in Porta Metronia, debuted at 16 in 1993, 307 goals in 786 games, one club, one city, a World Cup in 2006, retired in 2017 at 40 in a ceremony the stadium still talks about. [Daniele De Rossi](/players/daniele-de-rossi-player), born in Ostia, played 616 games alongside him and later coached the club. Bruno Conti came through the youth ranks to win the 1982 World Cup, Giuseppe Giannini was the captain before Totti, and Alessandro Florenzi and Lorenzo Pellegrini, the current captain, are the latest Romans to lead Romans. The club has also been a launchpad: [Mohamed Salah](/players/mohamed-salah) scored 29 goals in two seasons before Liverpool, [Alisson](/players/alisson-becker) went the same way, Marquinhos and Leandro Paredes went to Paris, and Edin Džeko scored 119 goals before joining Inter. [Paulo Dybala](/players/paulo-dybala), signed free from Juventus in 2022, is the current talisman at 32.

## Identity and playing style

For most of its history Roma has been a club of individual brilliance rather than system, from Falcão to Totti to Dybala. Gasperini has changed that. He brought from Atalanta the man-to-man [pressing](/tactics/gegenpressing) across the whole pitch, a [back three](/tactics/back-three) with the centre-backs following their forwards into midfield, wing-backs who become wingers and a pair of attacking midfielders behind one striker. Manu Koné and Bryan Cristante run the midfield, Evan Ndicka and Gianluca Mancini anchor the three, Mile Svilar has been the league's best goalkeeper for two seasons, and the summer signings, Donyell Malen from Aston Villa, Santiago Castro from Bologna for €35m, Rodrigo Mora from Porto and the 35-year-old Marten de Roon, Gasperini's old captain, were all chosen to fit the model.

## Rivalries

The Derby della Capitale against [Lazio](/clubs/lazio) is the city's dividing line: Roma, the club of the centre and the working-class districts, against Lazio, older and historically associated with the north of the city and the provinces. Totti's selfie celebration in 2015 and the 2013 Coppa Italia final, which Lazio won, are the modern flashpoints. The Derby del Sole with [Napoli](/clubs/napoli) is the biggest fixture in the south, and the matches against [Juventus](/clubs/juventus) carry the resentment of the 1981 Turone disallowed goal and the title races Roma lost to Turin. The 2-2 draw with [Inter](/clubs/inter-milan) at San Siro in September 2026 was a title-race marker.

## Current situation

As of November 2026 Roma are third in Serie A with 24 points from ten matches, unbeaten with seven wins and three draws, 28 goals scored and only 10 conceded, four points behind Lazio and one behind Inter. The 4-0 wins over Fiorentina and Lecce and the 3-3 draw at Napoli showed the attack; the 0-0 at Como in October showed the defence. Malen was the league's player of the month for the opening weeks. In the Champions League Roma have one win, over Slovan Bratislava 4-0, and defeats by Real Madrid 4-3 at the Olimpico and Manchester United 3-2 away in a thrilling but costly league phase.

The club's long-promised stadium at Pietralata has planning approval and the Friedkins hope to open it by 2028. With Lazio leading the table, the derby on the horizon and Gasperini in the second year of a contract that runs to 2028, the city's football is as tense as it has been in two decades. Roma have not won the title since 2001; for the first time in years the supporters are allowed to think about it.
