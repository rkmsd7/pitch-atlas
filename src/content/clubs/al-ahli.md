## History

Al Ahli was founded in Jeddah in 1937 and holds the royal title "Al-Raqi", the elegant one, with a history of royal patronage that gives it a different social profile from its cross-town rival Al Ittihad. The club won the King Cup, then the most prestigious competition in the kingdom, thirteen times between 1962 and 2016, more than any other club, and the league title in 1978 and 1984 before a long wait. Its 2000s were strong in cups but short of championships: the 2002 Arab Club Champions Cup, three Gulf Club Champions Cups, and the 2012 AFC Champions League final, lost to Ulsan Hyundai.

The third league title came in 2015-16 under the Swiss coach Christian Gross, when a side led by the Syrian striker Omar Al-Somah, the Saudi midfielder Taisir Al-Jassim and the Brazilian Victor Simões went unbeaten through the league season and added the King Cup. The club finished second in the following two seasons and then declined, and in May 2022, after a disastrous campaign under several coaches, Al Ahli were relegated for the first time in their history.

## Trajectory

The response was the most remarkable rebuild in Saudi football. Al Ahli won the second division at a canter in 2022-23, were included in the Public Investment Fund's June 2023 takeover of the four big clubs, and signed Riyad Mahrez from Manchester City, Roberto Firmino from Liverpool, Édouard Mendy from Chelsea, Franck Kessié from Barcelona and Allan Saint-Maximin that summer, with [Matthias Jaissle](/managers/matthias-jaissle), the young German from Salzburg, as head coach. Third place in 2023-24 and Ivan Toney's arrival from Brentford in 2024 followed.

The peak came on 3 May 2025 in Jeddah, when Al Ahli beat [Kawasaki Frontale](/clubs/kawasaki-frontale) 2-0 in the final of the first AFC Champions League Elite, Galeno and Firmino scoring, to win the club's first continental title in front of 58,000 at the King Abdullah Sports City. The win qualified the club for the 2025 FIFA Intercontinental Cup and the 2029 [Club World Cup](/tournaments/club-world-cup) cycle. Jaissle left for [Newcastle United](/clubs/newcastle-united) in August 2026; the identity of the current head coach is not confirmed in the club's squad data as of November 2026, so none is listed here. Al Ahli are sixth in the 2026-27 Saudi Pro League with 21 points from 11 games.

## Players the club produced

Al Ahli's academy produced Al-Jassim, the long-serving captain and Saudi international, the defenders Hussein Abdulghani and Mohammed Al-Khilaiwi's Jeddah contemporaries, the goalkeeper Yasser Al-Mosailem, who played at the 2018 World Cup, and the forward Abdullah Al-Majed. Since 2023 the club has been defined by its signings: Mahrez, Firmino (released in 2025), Mendy, Kessié, Toney, the Brazilian winger Galeno from Porto, the French midfielder Enzo Millot from Stuttgart and the Belgian defender Matteo Dams.

## Identity and playing style

Jaissle brought the Red Bull school to Jeddah: a 4-2-3-1 or 4-4-2 that presses high, counter-presses after losing the ball and attacks quickly through Mahrez's inverted dribbling and Toney's hold-up play, with Kessié as the engine. It was the most intense pressing team in the league and the first Saudi side to win the Champions League with that approach, which took the 2025 final away from a more technical Kawasaki side. See [gegenpressing](/tactics/gegenpressing). Without Jaissle the structure has loosened, and the 2026-27 team has been less consistent out of possession.

## Rivalries

The Jeddah derby against [Al Ittihad](/clubs/al-ittihad) is the city's great divide, green against yellow and black, played in a stadium the two clubs share, and Al Ahli's continental title in 2025 was the first time in a generation that the green half could claim the bigger trophy. [Al Hilal](/clubs/al-hilal) and [Al Nassr](/clubs/al-nassr), the Riyadh powers, are the national rivals, and the fixture against Al Hilal has produced the league's best matches since 2023.

## Current situation

As of November 2026 Al Ahli are sixth in the Saudi Pro League with 21 points from 11 games, nine behind Al Hilal, with no head coach confirmed in the current squad data following Jaissle's departure to Newcastle. Mahrez, now 35, Toney, Kessié, Mendy, Galeno and Millot are the core, and the club's immediate task is to settle the bench and defend its Asian title in the 2026-27 AFC Champions League Elite. The 2025 triumph remains the proudest moment in the club's history, and the fan base expects a league title to follow.
