## History

Elche Club de Fútbol were founded in 1923 in the city of palm trees in the province of Alicante, and reached [La Liga](/leagues/la-liga) for the first time in 1959 after winning Segunda. The 1960s were the golden decade: a fifth-place finish in 1963-64, the club's best, a Copa del Rey final in 1969 (lost 1-0 to Athletic) and a team of Vicente Pastor, Juan Manuel Asensi (later Barcelona's captain) and the Paraguayan striker Cayetano Ré, who topped the league scoring chart in 1965. The Altabix stadium gave way in 1976 to the Nuevo Estadio, now the Manuel Martínez Valero, built with a capacity far beyond the city's size for the 1982 World Cup.

Elche were relegated in 1971, returned for single seasons in 1973, 1978, 1984 and 1988, and then disappeared from the top flight for 25 years, including spells in Segunda B and a financial crisis in the 1990s. The modern revival began with the Segunda title in 2012-13 under Fran Escribá, two seasons in La Liga that ended with an administrative relegation in 2015 for unpaid tax debts despite a 13th-place finish, a fall to Segunda B in 2017, and a second rise: promotion in 2018 and then, in the 2020 play-offs played behind closed doors in August, a last-minute goal from Pere Milla at Girona that took Elche back to La Liga.

The Argentine businessman Christian Bragarnik bought the club in 2019 and has run it since. Elche survived in 2021 and 2022, were relegated bottom in 2023 with only 25 points, and returned in 2025 as Segunda runners-up under Eder Sarabia.

## Trajectory

Elche have been promoted to the top flight four times this century (2013, 2020, 2025 and via the 2017-18 play-offs to Segunda) and relegated three times (2015, 2017, 2023). Sarabia's two seasons were the best in a decade: promotion in 2024-25 and then a 15th-place finish in 2025-26 with 43 points, survival sealed by a final-day win over Girona that sent the Catalans down. Sarabia then announced he would not continue despite a year left on his contract, and Bragarnik appointed Martín Anselmi, the Argentine who had won titles with Independiente del Valle and coached Porto, on a one-season contract on 13 June 2026.

## Players the club produced

Asensi is the greatest player to come out of Elche's youth system, a 41-cap Spain midfielder who won six titles with Barcelona. Nino, from nearby Vera, scored more than 100 goals across two spells and played until 40; Fidel Chaves and Gonzalo Verdú were the leaders of the 2020-22 team; Pedro Bigas, the Mallorcan centre-back, is the current captain at 36. Elche's recent history has been built on loans and short-term signings rather than on an academy, with Lucas Boyé, Enzo Roco and Edgar Badía among the modern favourites.

## Identity and playing style

Sarabia's Elche were a technically ambitious side, building from the back in a 3-4-3 with high full-backs and a midfield designed for numerical superiority, in the Barcelona tradition in which he was trained. Anselmi's Independiente del Valle were similar in principle, a pressing, possession-based 4-3-3, and he has tried to maintain that identity with Gonzalo Villar and Federico Redondo as the deep midfielders, Thomas Lemar (on loan from Atlético since 1 September) and Facundo Buonanotte (on loan from Brighton) between the lines, and Ezequiel Ponce and Fer Niño as the strikers. The results have been harder to come by than the ideas.

## Rivalries

The Alicante derby against Hércules is the oldest rivalry, rarely played in recent years because of Hércules's decline. [Villarreal](/clubs/villarreal) and [Valencia](/clubs/valencia) are the Comunitat Valenciana rivals in the top flight, and Levante the other.

## Current situation

Elche are 17th on 7 November 2026 with ten points from 11 games (two wins, four draws, five defeats), level with Levante and just outside the relegation zone on goal difference, with Valencia and Málaga below them. Anselmi's start was a run of three defeats broken by a draw at San Mamés and then a 0-0 at home to Celta on 11 October, and the team's 14 goals scored mask a defence that has conceded 18.

The squad includes Matías Dituro in goal, Bigas, Víctor Chust and Kevin Lomónaco in defence, Villar, Redondo, Marc Aguado and Josan in midfield and Lemar, Buonanotte, Ponce, Grady Diangana and Niño in attack. Bragarnik's model, a rotating cast of Argentine and loan players around a small Spanish core, has kept Elche in the division before. Survival is again the only aim.
