## History

Frosinone is a town of 45,000 in the Ciociaria, the hill country between Rome and Naples, and its football club, founded in 1906 as Unione Sportiva Frusinate and refounded in 1928, 1959 and 1990, spent the first century of its life in Serie C, Serie D and the regional leagues. The yellow and blue colours were adopted in 1930. The transformation began when the industrialist Maurizio Stirpe took over in 2003: promotion from Serie C2 in 2004, Serie B for the first time in the club's history via the 2006 play-offs under Ivo Iaconi, five seasons there, relegation in 2011 and then the Lega Pro title in 2014 under Roberto Stellone that started the climb to the top.

Stellone's side finished second in Serie B in 2015 and took Frosinone to Serie A for the first time, where the club played its home games at the ancient Stadio Matusa, a 9,000-seat ground wedged into the town, and went down with 31 points. The Stadio Benito Stirpe, a compact English-style 16,000-seat ground named after the president's father, opened in 2017, in time for the second promotion, via the 2018 play-offs under Moreno Longo, and a second immediate relegation.

## Trajectory

Frosinone's modern record is a pattern: 18th in 2015-16, 19th in 2018-19, 18th in 2023-24, three relegations at the first attempt, each by a narrow margin. The 2022-23 Serie B title under Fabio Grosso, with Matías Soulé on loan from Juventus, Samuele Mulattieri and Luca Mazzitelli, was the best season in the club's history, and the 2023-24 side, coached by Eusebio Di Francesco with Soulé scoring 11 and Walid Cheddira up front, played bright football before losing on the final day to Udinese and going down on goal difference. The 2024-25 Serie B season was a struggle to 15th. Massimiliano Alvini, a 56-year-old Tuscan who had coached Perugia and Cremonese, was appointed in the summer of 2025 and finished second with 81 points, one behind Venezia, to win direct promotion at the fourth attempt.

## Players the club produced

The club's academy has produced little at the top level, but the loan model has produced a lot: Soulé went back to Juventus and then to Roma for €30m; Cheddira, Marco Brescianini, now at Fiorentina, Stefano Turati, Giorgi Kvernadze and Caleb Okoli all passed through. The home-grown exception is the midfielder Francesco Gelli, who has played more than 250 games across four spells. The icons of the Stirpe era are Daniel Ciofani, the striker whose goals won two promotions, Federico Dionisi, the captain of the 2018 play-off side, Camillo Ciano and Luca Paganini. Roberto Soldado, the former Valencia and Tottenham striker, spent 2015-16 there.

## Identity and playing style

Alvini is a coach in the Zeman tradition of the Italian provinces, committed to a 4-3-3 that attacks with width and pace and accepts the risk. The 2026-27 side has been the most entertaining promoted team in years: Antonio Raimondo, the 22-year-old striker on loan from Bologna, won the Serie A rising star award for the opening weeks; Alessio Zerbin and Giorgi Kvernadze provide the wide running; the Austrians Romano Schmid and Florian Grillitsch, signed from Werder Bremen and Hoffenheim, give the midfield a quality the club has never had; Ben Lhassine Kone was bought from Como. Matteo Pisseri, 34, is the experienced goalkeeper. The 4-4 draw at Atalanta and the 2-0 home win over Como in September were the games that announced the approach.

## Rivalries

The derby that matters is with Latina, the other main town of southern Lazio, though the two have rarely met in recent years. Frosinone's supporters dislike [Lazio](/clubs/lazio) and, to a lesser extent, [Roma](/clubs/roma), the capital's clubs that regard the Ciociaria as their hinterland, and the games against Napoli carry a south-against-south edge from the 2023-24 relegation fight.

## Current situation

As of November 2026 Frosinone are sixth in Serie A with 20 points from ten matches, six wins, two draws and two defeats, 24 goals scored, the best start in the club's history and currently a Europa League place. Only the 3-0 defeat at Napoli and a loss to Roma have interrupted it. The Coppa Italia is at the round of 16. Nobody at the Stirpe believes the position will hold through a winter with a squad this thin, and Alvini has said that 40 points remains the target, but a club that has never finished higher than 18th in the top flight has given itself a cushion that the three previous Serie A seasons never had.
