## History

FC Twente were created in 1965 from the merger of Sportclub Enschede and Enschedese Boys, and within a decade they were one of the best teams in the Netherlands, finishing second in 1974 and reaching the 1975 UEFA Cup final, where they lost 5-1 on aggregate to Borussia Mönchengladbach. Epi Drost and Theo Pahlplatz were the stars of that side, and Kees Rijvers the coach, before he moved on to [PSV](/clubs/psv-eindhoven). The club won the KNVB Cup in 1977 and 2001, the latter on penalties against PSV, but spent most of the 1980s and 1990s as a mid-table side, including a brief relegation in 1983.

The great era came in the late 2000s. Fred Rutten took the club to fourth and second, and Steve McClaren, rebuilding his reputation after England, won the 2009-10 Eredivisie with a team of Blaise Nkufo, Bryan Ruiz, Miroslav Stoch, Theo Janssen and Sander Boschker. It was clinched on the final day at NAC Breda, a first title in the club's history and the first for a club outside the big three since AZ the year before. Michel Preud'homme followed with the 2011 KNVB Cup and a second-place finish, and Co Adriaanse's side of 2011-12 played some of the most attractive football in the country.

The decline was brutal. Years of overspending under chairman Joop Munsterman caught up with the club in 2016, when a third-party ownership scandal brought a points deduction and nearly a licence withdrawal, and in 2018 Twente were relegated. They bounced straight back as Eerste Divisie champions in 2019, and under Ron Jans and then Joseph Oosting they re-established themselves as a European contender, finishing third in 2023-24.

## Trajectory

Twente's modern curve is one of the sharpest in Dutch football: from 13th in 2001 to a title in 2010, second in 2011, then steady decline to relegation in 2018, promotion in 2019, and recovery to third in 2024. Since returning to the top flight they have finished in the top six in five of the last six completed seasons, with 2020-21 the exception. The club's Europa League campaign in 2024-25 was their first group-stage football since 2011-12.

## Players the club produced

Twente's academy produced Quincy Promes, who went on to Spartak Moscow, Sevilla and Ajax, and the club was the launching pad for Wout Weghorst, Dušan Tadić (signed from Groningen and sold to Southampton), Bryan Ruiz, Kennedy Bakircioglu, Luuk de Jong, Ola John, Hakim Ziyech and Jesús Corona. Sander Boschker, the goalkeeper who played 555 league games for the club, is its all-time appearance record holder. Sem Steijn, the attacking midfielder who was the Eredivisie's top scorer in 2024-25, is the most recent product to make a major move, joining Feyenoord.

## Identity and playing style

Twente under John van den Brom, appointed in 2025, play an organised 4-3-3 that is more direct than the possession sides of Amsterdam and Eindhoven: quick wide play, late runs from midfield and a willingness to defend deep against the big three before breaking. The Grolsch Veste is one of the loudest grounds in the country and the club's identity is built around being the standard-bearer for the east of the Netherlands, far from the Randstad.

## Rivalries

The Twentse derby against Heracles Almelo, a few miles down the road, is the one that matters most to supporters. Matches against [PSV](/clubs/psv-eindhoven) carry the memory of the 2010 title race, and [AZ](/clubs/az-alkmaar) and Utrecht are the recurring rivals for the European places.

## Current situation

As of November 2026 Twente are fourth in the Eredivisie after eleven rounds, level on points with PSV and only two behind leaders Feyenoord, a strong start under Van den Brom that puts a Champions League qualification place within reach. The club remains financially careful after the near-death experience of 2016-18, and the aim for the season is European football and a KNVB Cup run.
