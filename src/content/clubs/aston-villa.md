## History

Aston Villa were founded in 1874 by members of the Villa Cross Wesleyan Chapel cricket team, and within 20 years they were the best club in England. William McGregor, a Villa director, wrote the letter in 1888 that created the Football League. Villa won it five times between 1894 and 1900 and completed the Double in 1897, the year they moved into Villa Park. A sixth title came in 1910 and a sixth FA Cup, then a record, in 1920. The 1957 FA Cup final win over the Busby Babes was the seventh and last.

The post-war decline reached the Third Division in 1970. Ron Saunders rebuilt the club from there: promotion in 1975, League Cups in 1975 and 1977, and in 1980-81 the league title, won using only 14 players, with Dennis Mortimer as captain, Gordon Cowans in midfield and Peter Withe scoring. Saunders resigned in February 1982 after a row with the board; his assistant Tony Barton took the same team to Rotterdam, where Withe's shin bounced the winner in off the post against [Bayern Munich](/clubs/bayern-munich) and 23-year-old reserve goalkeeper Nigel Spink, on for the injured Jimmy Rimmer after nine minutes, kept them out. Villa were champions of Europe on 26 May 1982.

The fall was just as fast: relegation in 1987. Graham Taylor brought them back and to second place in 1990, and Ron Atkinson's side finished second in the first Premier League season and beat Manchester United in the 1994 League Cup final. Brian Little won the League Cup again in 1996, the last trophy for 30 years. Doug Ellis's long chairmanship ended with Randy Lerner's purchase in 2006; Martin O'Neill's sixth-place teams with Gabriel Agbonlahor, Ashley Young and James Milner came close to the Champions League, then Lerner's money ran out. Relegation in 2016 under Rémi Garde, and three seasons in the Championship, were the low point.

Nassef Sawiris and Wes Edens bought the club in 2018. Dean Smith, a Villa supporter, won promotion through the play-offs in 2019 with [Jack Grealish](/players/jack-grealish) as captain, survived on the last day in 2020 after Hawk-Eye failed at Sheffield United, and built a team around [Ollie Watkins](/players/ollie-watkins) and [Emiliano Martínez](/players/emiliano-martinez). [Unai Emery](/managers/unai-emery) replaced Steven Gerrard in October 2022 with the club 17th, took them to seventh, then fourth and the Champions League in 2024, and in 2025-26 won the Europa League, beating Freiburg 3-0 in Istanbul with goals from Youri Tielemans, Emiliano Buendía and [Morgan Rogers](/players/morgan-rogers).

## Trajectory

Villa's quarter-century has the shape of a valley. The O'Neill years (sixth three seasons in a row from 2008 to 2010) were followed by a slow decline under Gérard Houllier, Alex McLeish, Paul Lambert, Tim Sherwood and Garde, with finishes of 15th, 15th, 17th and then last in 2015-16 with 17 points.

The Championship years were 13th, fourth (play-off final defeat to Fulham in 2018) and fifth, with promotion through the play-offs against Derby in May 2019. Back in the Premier League, 17th, 11th and 14th under Smith and Gerrard gave way to Emery's climb: seventh in 2023, fourth in 2024, sixth in 2025 (losing a Champions League quarter-final to Paris Saint-Germain), and fourth again in 2026 with the European trophy that secured direct Champions League entry. The side also reached the FA Cup final in 2015 and the League Cup final in 2010.

## Players the club produced

Bodymoor Heath has produced a long line of internationals: Gabriel Agbonlahor, who holds the club's Premier League scoring record, Gary Cahill, [Jack Grealish](/players/jack-grealish), the club's most gifted homegrown player of the modern era, who left for Manchester City for £100m in 2021, Jacob Ramsey and the current midfielder Lamare Bogarde all came through. Earlier graduates include Gordon Cowans, the playmaker of the 1981 title, and, in the 1990s, Dwight Yorke, signed from Tobago as a teenager and sold to Manchester United as the Premier League's best striker.

The signings who became legends include Peter Withe, Paul McGrath, the Villa supporters' Player of the Century, Gareth Southgate, Dion Dublin, Juan Pablo Ángel, Stiliyan Petrov, [John McGinn](/players/john-mcginn), signed from Hibernian for £2.75m in 2018 and captain since 2022, [Ollie Watkins](/players/ollie-watkins), bought from Brentford in 2020 and the club's leading scorer of the modern era, and [Emiliano Martínez](/players/emiliano-martinez), the World Cup-winning goalkeeper who left for Chelsea in summer 2026. [Morgan Rogers](/players/morgan-rogers), signed from Middlesbrough for £8m in January 2024, became a British-record £117m sale to Chelsea in 2026.

## Identity and playing style

[Unai Emery](/managers/unai-emery) is the great European-cup specialist of his generation, with four Europa League wins at Sevilla and Villarreal before the fifth with Villa. His football is detailed and reactive: a 4-2-3-1 or 4-4-2 that defends with a mid-block and a very high defensive line that catches opponents offside more than any team in Europe, then attacks with quick combinations through the half-spaces. Villa's [high line](/tactics/high-line) is their signature; so is Emery's habit of changing shape for each opponent.

The 2026-27 squad was reshaped after Martínez, Rogers, Tielemans and Marco Asensio departed. Zion Suzuki and Marco Bizot arrived as goalkeepers; Leon Goretzka came on a free from Bayern Munich; Johan Manzambi was signed from Freiburg for £59.5m and João Gomes from Wolves for £38m to deepen the midfield with Amadou Onana and Boubacar Kamara; Taylor Harwood-Bellis and Matteo Ruggeri joined the defence; and in attack Nicolas Jackson arrived, Alejandro Garnacho came on loan from Chelsea with an obligation to buy at around £42.5m, and Tammy Abraham returned. Watkins remains the centre-forward and McGinn the captain.

## Rivalries

The Second City derby with [Birmingham City](/clubs/birmingham-city) is the one that matters, dating to 1879; Villa won the 2002-03 league meetings in a season of fire and fury, and Birmingham's recent promotion back to the Championship has not yet brought the fixture back. [West Bromwich Albion](/clubs/west-bromwich-albion) and [Wolverhampton Wanderers](/clubs/wolverhampton-wanderers), relegated in 2026, are the other West Midlands rivalries. In Europe, the Villa Park nights against Bayern in 2024 and Paris Saint-Germain in 2025 revived memories of 1982.

## Current situation

Villa are tenth in early November 2026, with three wins, three draws and three defeats from nine: 10 goals scored, 13 conceded, 12 points. The best result was a 2-1 win over Manchester City at Villa Park on 24 October; before that they won 1-0 at Newcastle on 17 October, and on 31 October drew 1-1 at home to Fulham. The Champions League league phase has run alongside, with the squad rotation that Emery's ten summer signings were designed to allow.

The summer exits of Martínez, Rogers and Tielemans took out three of the Europa League final's decisive players, and the squad is thinner in wide attacking positions than at any point under Emery. The ambition is a fourth straight European season and another cup run; the realistic target is the top six, with the Champions League knockout rounds the measure of progress. Emery is in his fifth year and remains the most important figure in the club's modern revival.
