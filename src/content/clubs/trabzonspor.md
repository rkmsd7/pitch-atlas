## History

Trabzonspor were founded in 1967 by the merger of four clubs in Trabzon, the Black Sea port city, and within a decade they had done what no Anatolian club had done before or has done since: broken the Istanbul monopoly. Promoted in 1974, Trabzonspor won the Süper Lig in 1976, their second top-flight season, and added five more titles by 1984 under Ahmet Suat Özyazıcı and Özkan Sümer, with Şenol Güneş in goal, Necmi Perekli and Ali Kemal Denizci in attack and a style of football built on fitness and aggression that the rest of the country could not match. Six titles in nine seasons made Trabzon a footballing city unlike any other in Turkey.

Then the drought began. Trabzonspor finished second in 1995 and 1996 under Güneş as coach, with Hami Mandıralı the local hero and the Russian pair of Dmitri Kirichenko and Oleg Salenko adding goals, and again in 2004 and 2005 with Fatih Tekke scoring freely. Cup wins in 1992, 1995, 2003, 2004 and 2010 kept the trophy cabinet ticking over, but the title that mattered most was the one lost in 2011: level on points with Fenerbahçe, beaten on head-to-head record, and then watching the match-fixing scandal unfold without the title ever being reassigned. Trabzonspor still refer to themselves as the 2011 champions.

The wait ended on 30 April 2022, when Abdullah Avcı's team, with the Dane Andreas Cornelius, Anastasios Bakasetas, Marek Hamšík and goalkeeper Uğurcan Çakır, clinched the title with a 2-2 draw against Antalyaspor in front of a delirious Papara Park, 38 years after the last. The Super Cup followed. The seasons since have been unsettled, with coaching changes and finishes of sixth, third and seventh, the last with a Turkish Cup final lost to Galatasaray.

## Trajectory

Trabzonspor's seven titles, six of them before 1985, leave them fourth in Turkey, but they have been the most consistent challenger to the big three for half a century. Since 2000 the club has finished second four times (2004, 2005, 2011 and 2020), won the 2022 championship and finished outside the top eight only once, in 2016, when a twelfth-place finish came amid financial trouble. Europe has brought the 2004-05 UEFA Cup group stage, Champions League qualification in 2011-12 after Fenerbahçe's ban, and a 2022-23 Champions League group with Inter, Barcelona and Bayern, Trabzonspor's first since 2011.

## Players the club produced

Trabzonspor's academy produced Şenol Güneş, the goalkeeper who went on to coach Turkey to third place at the 2002 World Cup; Hami Mandıralı; Fatih Tekke; Burak Yılmaz, who became Turkey's record scorer of the modern era; and Uğurcan Çakır, the goalkeeper who captained the 2022 title side and Turkey. Abdülkadir Ömür, Yusuf Yazıcı (sold to Lille) and Abdülkadir Parmak came through in the 2010s. Foreign players who became icons include Alexander Sørloth, whose 24 goals in 2019-20 won the Golden Boot, José Sosa and Marek Hamšík.

## Identity and playing style

Trabzonspor's identity is regional pride: the Black Sea against Istanbul, a city that identifies with its club more completely than anywhere else in Turkey. The teams of the golden era were physical and fast; the 2022 champions under Avcı played a controlled 4-1-4-1 built on Hamšík's distribution and Bakasetas's late runs. As of November 2026 the team is led by Thomas Reis, the German coach who previously managed Bochum and Schalke, bringing a pressing style in a 4-2-3-1 with [gegenpressing](/tactics/gegenpressing) and [direct play](/tactics/direct-play).

## Rivalries

The rivalry with [Fenerbahçe](/clubs/fenerbahce) has been the most bitter since the disputed 2011 title, and matches at Papara Park have produced several ugly scenes. [Galatasaray](/clubs/galatasaray) and [Beşiktaş](/clubs/besiktas) complete the set; the Black Sea derby against Rizespor is local but minor.

## Current situation

As of November 2026 Trabzonspor are third in the Süper Lig after ten rounds, three points behind Galatasaray and well placed for a Champions League qualification spot. Reis's side has started strongly, with Çakır in goal and Paul Onuachu providing a physical focal point in attack; a return to the title race for the first time since 2022 is the ambition, and the early-season table suggests it is a realistic one.
