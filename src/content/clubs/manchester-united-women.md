## History

Manchester United had a women's team until 2005, when the Glazer family disbanded it, and for 13 years the biggest club in England had no female side at all. The team was reformed in 2018 under Casey Stoney, won the Championship at the first attempt with [Alessia Russo](/players/alessia-russo), Ella Toone, Katie Zelem and Lauren James among a startlingly good first squad, and finished fourth in each of its first three WSL seasons.

Marc Skinner took over in 2021 after Stoney left frustrated at the club's commitment. His 2022-23 team finished second and reached a first FA Cup final; a year later United beat Tottenham 4-0 at Wembley to win the 2024 FA Cup, the club's first major women's trophy, with Toone scoring a spectacular opener. Russo (to Arsenal), Mary Earps (to PSG) and James (to Chelsea) all left in a two-year span, and the pattern of losing the best players became the story.

Fifth in 2024, third in 2025 and fourth in 2025-26, when United lost the League Cup final 2-0 to Chelsea in March 2026, was a respectable record but the club wanted more. Skinner departed in the summer and in August 2026 United appointed Eva Olid, the Spanish coach who had built Real Betis and coached Huddersfield, with a brief to play a more progressive game. The summer window brought [Fridolina Rolfö](/players/fridolina-rolfo) from Barcelona, [Lea Schüller](/players/lea-schuller) from Bayern and Elisabeth Terland.

## Trajectory

Promoted in 2019, United have never finished below fifth in the WSL and have been top four in six of seven seasons, but have only broken into the top two once. The move of marquee fixtures to Old Trafford, where United drew the WSL's first 40,000-plus crowd outside London, has been the main growth lever.

## Players the club produced

The reformed club's first great product was Russo, though she arrived from the United States college system; Toone, from the youth setup at Blackburn, and Maya Le Tissier, signed from Brighton, are the current captain-and-vice-captain axis. Grace Clinton and Jess Park, both England internationals, Zelem and Earps all became stars at United, and the club's willingness to sell them has funded the squad.

## Identity and playing style

Skinner's United were a 4-2-3-1 built on a strong defensive block, Zelem's set pieces and Toone's late runs. Olid's brief is possession and a higher line, with Rolfö's left-sided attacking play and Schüller's finishing adding a continental edge to a side that has often lacked goals. Le Tissier, one of the best ball-playing centre-backs in England, is the anchor; Phallon Tullis-Joyce has replaced Earps in goal.

## Rivalries

The Manchester derby against [Manchester City](/clubs/manchester-city-women), lost 3-0 in March 2026, is the one that matters. Liverpool are the historic rival, and Chelsea have beaten United in the 2023 FA Cup final and the 2026 League Cup final.

## Current situation

As of November 2026 Olid's first season is a work in progress: a new system, a reshaped attack and the challenge of a 14-team league. Toone, Le Tissier, Park and the new signings give United a squad that should compete for the Champions League places, and the FA Cup remains a realistic target.
