## History

Mumbai City were founded in 2014 as one of the eight original franchises of the [Indian Super League](/leagues/indian-super-league), owned by the actor Ranbir Kapoor and businessman Bimal Parekh. The early years were all about marquee names. Freddie Ljungberg played in the first season, Nicolas Anelka was player-manager in 2015 and [Diego Forlán](/players/diego-forlan) arrived in 2016, when Alexandre Guimarães's team topped the league for the first time before losing the semi-final to ATK.

The turning point came in November 2019, when the [City Football Group](/clubs/manchester-city), owners of Manchester City, bought a 65 per cent stake. CFG brought scouting, structure and money, and hired Sergio Lobera, who had built a brilliant attacking side at [FC Goa](/clubs/fc-goa). Lobera's first season, 2020-21, was the club's best: Mumbai won the League Winners' Shield and then the ISL Cup, beating ATK Mohun Bagan 2-1 in the final in Goa with Bipin Singh's late winner. They were the first club to win both in the same season.

## Trajectory

Des Buckingham took over in 2021 and led Mumbai into the AFC Champions League, where they beat Iraq's Air Force Club in 2022 to become the first Indian club to win a group-stage game. His 2022-23 side was the best regular-season team the league has seen: unbeaten in their first 18 games and champions of the Shield with a record 46 points, although they lost the semi-final on penalties to Bengaluru. Petr Kratky, who replaced Buckingham in December 2023, won the 2023-24 ISL Cup, beating Shield winners [Mohun Bagan](/clubs/mohun-bagan) 3-1 at Salt Lake Stadium in Kolkata.

Since then the club has slipped. Kratky's side finished sixth in 2024-25 and went out in the knockout round, then came third in the shortened, points-only 2025-26 season, one point behind [East Bengal](/clubs/east-bengal) and Mohun Bagan. Kratky left in 2026 and Manolo Márquez, the former India coach who had won the Super Cup twice with Goa, was appointed in July 2026.

## Players the club produced

Mumbai's model has been to sign India internationals at their peak and polish them. Lallianzuala Chhangte became one of the league's best wingers in a Mumbai shirt, Apuia emerged as an elite midfielder, Rahul Bheke captained the double side, and Bipin Singh, Vikram Partap Singh and Akash Mishra became internationals. Among the foreigners, Bartholomew Ogbeche, Adam Le Fondre, Ahmed Jahouh, Greg Stewart, Jorge Pereyra Díaz and Mourtada Fall are the names supporters remember.

## Identity and playing style

The CFG influence shows: Mumbai have usually played a possession-based 4-2-3-1 or 4-3-3 built on [positional play](/tactics/positional-play), with full-backs high and wingers wide, and Buckingham's 2022-23 side pressed aggressively in the opponent's half. Márquez is a more cautious, structured coach, and his short spell produced a team that struggled to create chances.

## Rivalries

The rivalry with [FC Goa](/clubs/fc-goa), the "west coast derby", is the oldest and most bitter, sharpened by players and coaches crossing between the two clubs. Finals and Shield races with [Mohun Bagan](/clubs/mohun-bagan) and play-off battles with [Bengaluru FC](/clubs/bengaluru-fc) have produced the biggest modern matches.

## Current situation

Mumbai made a dreadful start to 2026-27. After a 2-1 defeat at Churchill Brothers, draws with Punjab and Kerala Blasters and a 3-2 home loss to Sporting Club Delhi, they were tenth with two points from four games, and on 6 November 2026 the club and Márquez agreed to part ways. As of 8 November no permanent successor has been named, with assistant Anthony Fernandes expected to take interim charge. Brandon Fernandes, Akash Mishra, Vikram Partap Singh and the Uruguayan forward Leandro Otormín form the core of a squad that needs a coach, and points, quickly.
