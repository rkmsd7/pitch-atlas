## History

Bologna were founded in October 1909 in the back room of the Ronzani beer hall by the Swiss Emilio Arnstein and a group of students, and within two decades were one of the great powers of Italian football. The 1925 title was won after a five-match final against Genoa so contentious it took shots fired at a train to settle it; four more followed between 1929 and 1941 under the Hungarian coach Árpád Weisz, a Jew who was sacked by the racial laws in 1938 and murdered at Auschwitz with his family in 1944. Angelo Schiavio, the striker who scored the winning goal for Italy in the 1934 World Cup final, scored 242 goals for his home-town club. The 1930s Bologna, "the team that made the world tremble", beat Chelsea 4-0 to win the 1937 Tournoi de Paris, a precursor of the European competitions.

The seventh and last Scudetto came in 1963-64 under Fulvio Bernardini, with Giacomo Bulgarelli, the midfielder who played 488 games and whose name is on the stand, the Danish striker Harald Nielsen and Ezio Pascutti. The title was decided by a one-off play-off against Inter at the Olimpico, won 2-0, after a doping accusation against five Bologna players had been thrown out on appeal. Bologna have never come close since. The club won the 1970 and 1974 Coppa Italias, was relegated in 1982 and fell into Serie C1 by 1983, went bankrupt in 1993, and climbed back under Renzo Ulivieri with two straight promotions to reach Serie A in 1996. The late-1990s side with [Roberto Baggio](/players/roberto-baggio), who scored 22 goals in 1997-98, Giuseppe Signori and Kennet Andersson reached a UEFA Cup semi-final in 1999, and then the club settled into mid-table life.

## Trajectory

Bologna were relegated in 2005 after a play-out against Parma, spent three seasons in Serie B and returned in 2008 with Marco Di Vaio as the goalscorer. They went down again in 2014, came straight back via the play-offs in 2015 and have been in the top flight since. The Canadian billionaire Joey Saputo bought the club in October 2014, and the model built by sporting director Giovanni Sartori from 2022, buying young and selling high, bore fruit under Thiago Motta's 2023-24 side, which finished fifth and reached the Champions League for the first time since 1964, and under Vincenzo Italiano, who won the Coppa Italia in May 2025 against Milan with a Dan Ndoye goal, the first trophy in 51 years. Italiano's 2025-26 side finished eighth and lost the Supercoppa final to Napoli in December 2025; he left by mutual consent on 28 May 2026. Domenico Tedesco was appointed on 2 June, lasted five league games, and on 16 September [Raffaele Palladino](/managers/raffaele-palladino), sacked by Atalanta in the summer, took over.

The 2024-25 Coppa Italia run was the stuff of the club's folklore: Monza, Atalanta and Empoli were beaten on the way, the final at the Olimpico on 14 May 2025 was won 1-0 with Ndoye's 53rd-minute finish from Orsolini's cross, and the open-top bus the next day drew the largest crowd in Piazza Maggiore since the 1964 title. The Champions League campaign the previous season, the first since the 1964-65 European Cup, produced a 0-0 draw with Liverpool at Anfield and a win over Borussia Dortmund but no knockout place. Sartori's recruitment has made Bologna the model club for the Italian mid-table: a net transfer profit of more than €150m over four years alongside the best league finishes since the 1960s.

## Players the club produced

Bologna's academy gave the 1960s side Bulgarelli and Romano Fogli, and more recently Riccardo Orsolini, bought from Ascoli at 20 and now the club's top scorer of the century with more than 70 league goals, and Giovanni Fabbian, sold to Fiorentina for €13m in 2026. The Sartori machine has turned unknowns into exports: Joshua Zirkzee to Manchester United for €42m, Riccardo Calafiori to Arsenal for €45m, Dan Ndoye to Nottingham Forest, Sam Beukema to Napoli, Santiago Castro, the Argentine striker, to Roma for €35m in summer 2026 and Jhon Lucumí to Juventus for €20m. Lewis Ferguson, the Scottish captain signed from Aberdeen, and Lukasz Skorupski, the 35-year-old goalkeeper, are the constants. Historically the club nurtured Gianluca Pagliuca, [Marco Di Vaio](/players/marco-di-vaio) and, for two seasons, Baggio at his most joyful.

## Identity and playing style

Motta's Bologna played [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) with full-backs stepping into midfield; Italiano's pressed high and attacked with width through Orsolini and Ndoye, and the Coppa Italia was won on that energy. Tedesco tried a possession-heavy [back three](/tactics/back-three) that the squad did not take to. Palladino has reverted to a 3-4-2-1 with Orsolini and Federico Bernardeschi, signed on a free from Toronto in 2025, behind Artem Dovbyk, on loan from Roma, with Ferguson and Nikola Moro in midfield and the 36-year-old Matteo Darmian, signed as a free agent in October, bringing experience to a defence that has Martin Vitík, Arthur Theate, on loan from Frankfurt, and Torbjørn Heggem competing for places.

## Rivalries

The Derby dell'Appennino with [Fiorentina](/clubs/fiorentina), the two cities separated by a mountain pass and 100 kilometres of the A1, is the fixture the Curva Bulgarelli waits for. The regional derbies with [Parma](/clubs/parma) and Modena are intense but intermittent, and Bologna's 1964 play-off against [Inter](/clubs/inter-milan) gives that game a historic edge. The 4-2 win at Milan in October 2026 was the result of the season so far.

## Current situation

As of November 2026 Bologna are ninth in Serie A with 14 points from ten matches, four wins, two draws and four defeats, level with Napoli. Palladino's start has been mixed: 1-0 defeats to Lazio, Atalanta and Napoli, then the 4-2 at San Siro and a 2-1 home win over Monza. There is no European football this season, the Coppa Italia defence begins at the round of 16, and the stated target is a return to Europe. The Dall'Ara renovation, which will cut capacity for two years, is due to begin in 2027 and the club is building a temporary ground at the Fiera. Palladino, 42, is on his third club in 18 months; Sartori's recruiting is the reason the fans remain patient.
