## History

Mohun Bagan was founded on 15 August 1889 at Mohan Bagan Villa, a house in north Calcutta belonging to the Mitra family, with the barrister Bhupendra Nath Bose as its first president. That makes it one of the oldest football clubs in Asia, and for most of the twentieth century it was something more than a club: a vehicle for Bengali, and later Indian, national pride under colonial rule.

The defining afternoon came on 29 July 1911. Mohun Bagan, playing barefoot, beat the East Yorkshire Regiment 2-1 in the IFA Shield final at the Calcutta FC ground to become the first Indian team to win the trophy, which until then had been a preserve of British military and civilian sides. Captain Shibdas Bhaduri equalised and Abhilash Ghosh scored the winner, and the crowds that flooded the Maidan turned a football match into a moment in the independence movement. The date is still celebrated every year as Mohun Bagan Day, and the "Immortal Eleven" of 1911 are the founding myth of Indian football.

The decades that followed produced the club's great individuals. Gostha Pal, the "Chinese Wall" of a defender, captained India's first overseas tour in 1924; [Sailen Manna](/players/sailen-manna) led India to the 1951 Asian Games gold; [Chuni Goswami](/players/chuni-goswami), a one-club man, captained the 1962 Asian Games winners. Mohun Bagan collected Durand Cups, Rovers Cups, IFA Shields and Calcutta league titles by the dozen, and in 1977 drew 2-2 with [Pelé](/players/pele)'s New York Cosmos in front of a vast crowd at Eden Gardens, a match Calcutta still talks about.

When a genuine national league arrived, the club adapted quickly. Mohun Bagan won the National Football League in 1997-98, 1999-2000 and 2001-02, with Brazilian forward José Ramirez Barreto the star of the later sides, and then the I-League in 2014-15 under Sanjoy Sen and in 2019-20 under Kibu Vicuña, the latter clinched with four rounds to spare. Through it all the club stayed a members' society in the old Calcutta tradition, and finance was a recurring struggle. The cup record is the deepest in the country: a record 17 Durand Cups, 14 Federation Cups and 14 Rovers Cups, plus 22 IFA Shields and more than 30 Calcutta league titles, a haul that underpins its claim to be India's national club.

## Trajectory

The modern club was born in January 2020, when RP-Sanjiv Goenka Group, owners of the ISL's three-time champions ATK, bought a controlling stake in Mohun Bagan's football operations and merged the two. ATK Mohun Bagan entered the [Indian Super League](/leagues/indian-super-league) in 2020-21, keeping the green and maroon colours and the members' heritage, and inherited ATK's coach Antonio López Habas. They were beaten 2-1 by [Mumbai City](/clubs/mumbai-city) in the 2021 final and reached the semi-finals in 2021-22.

Juan Ferrando's side won the 2022-23 ISL Cup, beating [Bengaluru FC](/clubs/bengaluru-fc) on penalties after a 2-2 draw in the final in Goa. The ATK prefix had never sat comfortably with supporters, and after a long fan campaign the club was renamed Mohun Bagan Super Giant from 1 June 2023. The new name came with a run of silverware: the 2023 Durand Cup, won 1-0 against [East Bengal](/clubs/east-bengal) with a Dimitri Petratos free-kick; the 2023-24 League Winners' Shield, followed by a final defeat to Mumbai City at Salt Lake; and in 2024-25, under José Molina, a domestic double of Shield and ISL Cup, the final won 2-1 after extra time against Bengaluru.

Sergio Lobera took over for the delayed, shortened 2025-26 season, which began on 14 February 2026 and was decided on points alone. Mohun Bagan lost only once in 13 games and finished on 26 points, level with East Bengal, but their goal difference of +14 was five worse than their rivals', and the title crossed the Maidan for the first time in the ISL era.

## Players the club produced

The roll of honour runs from Gostha Pal and Sailen Manna through Chuni Goswami, Subrata Bhattacharya, the long-serving centre-back, and Shyam Thapa to [Bhaichung Bhutia](/players/bhaichung-bhutia), who had spells at both Kolkata giants. [Sunil Chhetri](/players/sunil-chhetri) began his senior career at Mohun Bagan in 2002. The Super Giant era has been built around India internationals rather than academy graduates: Lallianzuala Chhangte, Anirudh Thapa, Apuia, Liston Colaco, Manvir Singh, Sahal Abdul Samad and goalkeeper Vishal Kaith, plus foreign stars such as Hugo Boumous, Dimitri Petratos and the Australian striker Jamie Maclaren.

## Identity and playing style

Mohun Bagan's identity is institutional before it is tactical: the expectation is to win everything, every season. On the pitch the ISL-era teams have usually been possession-heavy Spanish-school sides, from Habas's compact 4-4-2 to Ferrando's [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) and Molina's balanced 4-2-3-1 with full-backs pushing on. Greek coach Panagiotis Dilmperis, who arrived from Punjab FC for 2026-27, is head coach for 2026-27; the early weeks suggest a pragmatic, front-foot side built around Thapa and Miguel Figueira, the 2025-26 ISL Golden Ball winner, with Maclaren and Chhangte as the goal threat. See [pressing traps](/tactics/pressing-traps).

## Rivalries

The Kolkata Derby against [East Bengal](/clubs/east-bengal), first played in 1925, is one of the great rivalries in Asian football, a contest rooted in the divide between the ghotis of West Bengal and the bangals who came from the east, and it has drawn crowds well above 100,000 at Salt Lake. Mohun Bagan dominated the ISL derbies until 2025-26 reversed the hierarchy. Contests with [Bengaluru FC](/clubs/bengaluru-fc), their opponents in two ISL finals, and [Mumbai City](/clubs/mumbai-city) have become the sharpest modern rivalries.

## Current situation

As of 8 November 2026, Mohun Bagan are top of the 2026-27 ISL with 12 points from five games (four wins, one defeat, goals 8-5), one point ahead of NorthEast United, whose 1-0 win in Guwahati is the only blemish so far. Narrow wins over Sporting Club Delhi, Chennaiyin, Punjab and Odisha suggest a side grinding out results rather than dominating. The season, the first under the club-led commercial model agreed with the AIFF on 8 July 2026, is about one thing for the green and maroons: taking the title back from East Bengal. Dilmperis has a squad deep in India internationals, with Maclaren and Chhangte providing the goals and Kaith behind a defence led by Alberto Rodríguez, and expectations to match. The first league derby of the season against Antonio López Habas, their former coach now in the East Bengal dugout, is the date every supporter has circled.
