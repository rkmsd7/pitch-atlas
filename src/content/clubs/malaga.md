## History

Málaga Club de Fútbol were founded in 1994 from the reserve team of CD Málaga, the city's historic club, which had been dissolved after bankruptcy in 1992. The new club climbed from Segunda B to [La Liga](/leagues/la-liga) in five years, winning Segunda in 1998-99 under Joaquín Peiró, and settled into the top flight with a team of Dely Valdés, Kiki Musampa and the former Real Madrid defender Fernando Sanz, who later became president. Málaga won the 2002 Intertoto Cup, beating Villarreal in the final, and reached the UEFA Cup quarter-finals in 2002-03. Relegation in 2006 and a return in 2008 under Juande Ramos's protégé Juan Ramón López Muñiz followed.

The transformation came in 2010 when Sheikh Abdullah Al Thani of the Qatari royal family bought the club. Manuel Pellegrini was appointed, Ruud van Nistelrooy, Santi Cazorla, Joaquín, Julio Baptista and Jérémy Toulalan were signed, and Málaga finished fourth in 2011-12 to qualify for the Champions League for the first time. The 2012-13 campaign, with Isco as its star, took them to the quarter-finals unbeaten through the group, past Porto in the last 16, and to within seconds of the semi-final: leading 2-1 at Dortmund in the 90th minute, they conceded twice in stoppage time, the second from an offside position, and went out 3-2. That summer UEFA banned the club from Europe for unpaid debts.

Al Thani stopped investing, the stars were sold and Málaga drifted to relegation in 2018. The years that followed were grim: a Segunda play-off defeat in 2019, administrative chaos, the removal of Al Thani from control by a court-appointed administrator in 2020, and relegation to the third tier in 2023, the lowest point in the club's history. Sergio Pellicer's side won promotion through the 2024 play-offs, finished 14th in Segunda in 2024-25, and in 2025-26 finished fourth with 73 points and won the play-offs, beating Las Palmas in the semi-final and Almería in the final (0-0 at La Rosaleda, 2-1 in Almería on 20 June 2026) to return to La Liga after eight years.

## Trajectory

Málaga have spent 17 of the 27 seasons since their 1999 promotion in the top flight. The peak of 2011-13 (fourth, then sixth with a European quarter-final) was followed by five seasons of mid-table decline, relegation, five seasons in Segunda, one in the third tier, two more in Segunda and now the return. The 2026-27 season began with the appointment of Juan Francisco "Juanfran" Funes, promoted from the club's B team, as head coach after Pellicer's departure.

## Players the club produced

La Academia has been the club's lifeline through the lean years. Isco, born in Benalmádena, was signed from Valencia at 19 but became a star at La Rosaleda before his €30m move to Real Madrid in 2013; Ignacio Camacho, Samu Castillejo, Pablo Fornals, Brahim Díaz (sold to Manchester City at 16) and the current squad's David Larrubia, Izan Merino and Adrián Niño all came through. The Al Thani-era signings, Cazorla, Joaquín, Van Nistelrooy, Baptista, Roque Santa Cruz, Willy Caballero, Weligton and Salomón Rondón, remain the names on the fans' lips.

## Identity and playing style

Pellegrini's Málaga played a smooth 4-2-3-1 with Isco and Cazorla between the lines and Joaquín wide, a possession game that briefly made them Spain's third-best team. Pellicer's promotion side was pragmatic and physical, strong at [set pieces](/tactics/set-pieces) and on the counter, built around the striker Chupete, who was the Segunda's revelation, and Larrubia's running. Funes has inherited that structure, a 4-4-2 or 4-2-3-1, and added five academy players to a squad that includes the Swedish midfielder Jens Cajuste, the Moroccan left-back Adam Aznou and Pablo Martínez in midfield. The team is organised but has struggled badly to score.

## Rivalries

The Andalusian derbies against [Sevilla](/clubs/sevilla) and [Real Betis](/clubs/real-betis) are the biggest fixtures, and the regional rivalries with Cádiz, Granada and Almería, forged in Segunda, carry real heat. The 2013 Dortmund tie gave the club a lasting grudge against the referee rather than a rival.

## Current situation

Málaga are bottom of La Liga on 7 November 2026 with five points from 11 games, five draws and six defeats, eight goals scored and 21 conceded, and still without a win in the top flight this season after a 1-1 draw at Alavés on 23 October. Funes's position has been under discussion in the local press, with reports before the home game against Racing Santander on 7 November suggesting another defeat could prompt a change; the club has confirmed nothing.

The squad is largely the promotion side: Alfonso Herrero in goal; Einar Galilea, Fernando Calero, Juan Berrocal and Aznou in defence; Cajuste, Pablo Martínez, Ramón Enríquez and Merino in midfield; Larrubia, Niño, Chupete and Joaquín Muñoz in attack. La Rosaleda, with more than 25,000 season-ticket holders, has backed the team through the fall and the climb. Staying up would be the club's biggest achievement since 2013.
