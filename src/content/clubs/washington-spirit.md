## History

The Spirit were founded in 2012 from the DC United Women franchise and were an original [NWSL](/leagues/nwsl) club in 2013. The early years produced one great side, the 2016 team that lost the Championship final on penalties to Western New York after leading in stoppage time, and a lot of mediocrity, including last place in 2017. [Mallory Swanson](/players/mallory-swanson), then Pugh, began her professional career here in 2017.

The 2021 season was the turning point. Richie Burke's team, with rookie [Trinity Rodman](/players/trinity-rodman), Ashley Hatch and Andi Sullivan, won the Championship, beating Chicago 2-1 in extra time, while the club was in open civil war: Burke was fired for abuse during the play-off run, interim coach Kris Ward finished the job, and a boardroom battle ended with Michele Kang buying the club in 2022 for a record $35m. Kang has since added [Lyon](/clubs/lyon-women) and London City Lionesses to a multi-club group.

Jonatan Giráldez arrived from Barcelona in 2024 and took the Spirit to the Championship final, lost 1-0 to Orlando. When he left for Lyon in June 2025, assistant Adrián González, who had coached the team in 2023, returned to the job on 18 July 2025 and won the Challenge Cup before losing the 2025 Championship final to [Gotham FC](/clubs/gotham-fc). The 2025-26 Concacaf W Champions Cup also ended in a final defeat. In 2026 Washington finished third with 55 points, behind Gotham and San Diego, and drew Portland in the quarter-final on 8 November.

## Trajectory

Last in 2017, champions in 2021, 11th in 2022, finalists in 2024 and 2025. Since Kang's takeover the Spirit have been a consistent top-three club with the league's best training facility and, since 2023, full-time residence at Audi Field, which hosts the 2026 Championship.

## Players the club produced

Rodman is the club's own: the second pick in the 2021 draft, Rookie of the Year, 2024 Olympic champion and, after a three-year contract signed in January 2026 worth more than $2m a season, the highest-paid player in women's football. Sullivan and Aubrey Kingsbury, the captain, have been at the club for most of a decade; Hatch, Croix Bethune and Tara Rudd came through as young players, and Italy's Sofia Cantore arrived in 2025.

## Identity and playing style

Giráldez brought [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) to Washington and González has kept the structure: a 4-3-3 with Sullivan at the base, Bethune creating, and Rodman isolated on the right to attack full-backs one-v-one. Kingsbury's goalkeeping is the foundation and the back four, with Casey Krueger and Tara McKeown, is among the league's best. Rodman's 12 goals in 30 games in 2026 were a career high.

## Rivalries

Gotham, who have beaten the Spirit in the 2025 final and in regular-season deciders, are the main rival. [Orlando Pride](/clubs/orlando-pride), the 2024 final opponent, and North Carolina Courage complete the East Coast set.

## Current situation

As of November 2026 the Spirit are in the play-offs with the prospect of a home Championship on 21 November. Rodman, Sullivan and Kingsbury lead; González is in his second full season. A third straight final appearance would make Washington the most consistent club of the era, and winning one at Audi Field is the stated goal.
