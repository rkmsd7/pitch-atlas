## History

Getafe Club de Fútbol were founded in 1983 after the collapse of Club Getafe Deportivo, in an industrial town of 180,000 on the southern edge of Madrid. The club climbed from the regional leagues to Segunda in 1994, dropped back and returned in 1999 under the presidency of Ángel Torres, who has run it since 2002 and remains in charge. Promotion to [La Liga](/leagues/la-liga) for the first time came in 2004 under Quique Sánchez Flores, and the club has spent 20 of the 22 seasons since in the top flight.

The best years followed immediately. Bernd Schuster took Getafe to ninth in 2006 and the 2007 Copa del Rey final (lost 1-0 to Sevilla), and Michael Laudrup's 2007-08 side reached a second consecutive Copa final (lost 3-1 to Valencia) and the UEFA Cup quarter-final, where Bayern Munich needed a Luca Toni goal in the last minute of extra time to go through on away goals after Getafe had led 3-1 at the Coliseum. Daniel Güiza, the league's top scorer in 2008, and Roberto Soldado were the strikers of that era.

Relegation in 2016 after a dozen seasons was followed by an immediate return under José Bordalás, who then built the most successful Getafe side of all: fifth in 2018-19, a Europa League last-16 win over Ajax in 2020, and a reputation as the hardest team in Spain to play against. Bordalás left in 2021, Quique Sánchez Flores returned briefly, and Bordalás came back in 2023 for a second spell that is still running.

## Trajectory

Getafe's story in the top flight splits into the Schuster-Laudrup years (2005-08), a survival era of mid-table finishes, the 2016 drop, and the Bordalás years. Under Bordalás the club finished eighth, fifth and eighth from 2017-18 to 2019-20, then 15th three years in a row after his departure. His return produced 12th, 13th and, in 2025-26, a seventh-place finish with 51 points from only 32 goals scored, which earned a Conference League play-off place and the club's first European qualification since 2019-20.

## Players the club produced

Getafe have never had a productive academy; their model is cheap recruitment and resale. Güiza, Soldado, Pedro León, Diego Costa (briefly, on loan), Adrián Colunga, Ángel Rodríguez, Jaime Mata, Cristhian Stuani and Mauro Arambarri were all bought cheaply and either sold at a profit or became long-serving regulars. Djené Dakonam, the Togolese centre-back signed in 2017, and the goalkeeper David Soria are the longest-serving members of the current squad.

The headline signing of 2026 was Dani Carvajal: Real Madrid's six-time Champions League winner, out of contract at 34 after 12 years at the Bernabéu, signed for Getafe until June 2027 on 13 October 2026, after weeks of wrangling with the league's salary limit. He could not be registered for the Conference League league phase.

## Identity and playing style

Bordalás's Getafe are the modern definition of a [low block](/tactics/low-block) with teeth: a 4-4-2 that sits deep, fouls tactically, wastes time expertly, wins second balls and scores from [set pieces](/tactics/set-pieces) and long throws. Critics call it anti-football; Bordalás's defenders point out that Getafe have finished above richer clubs in most of his seasons. The 2025-26 campaign was built on 38 goals conceded, a league-best defensive record outside the top five, and striking contributions from Borja Mayoral, Christantus Uche and Enes Ünal. The style has evolved slightly with Carvajal as a right wing-back in a 5-3-2 and Orel Mangala and Nemanja Gudelj as deeper passers, but the identity is non-negotiable.

## Rivalries

The southern Madrid derby against Leganés, five kilometres away, is the closest rivalry, though it is only played when both are in the same division. [Rayo Vallecano](/clubs/rayo-vallecano) are the other small Madrid club and a regular opponent. Matches against [Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid), the giant next door, carry the feeling of a derby for Getafe fans if not for Madrid's.

## Current situation

Getafe are 13th on 7 November 2026 with 12 points from 11 games (three wins, three draws, five defeats), and have scored only eight goals, the fewest in the league. The Conference League campaign is the first European football at the Coliseum since 2020, and Carvajal's arrival in October gave a famously unglamorous club a rare headline signing.

The squad is Bordalás through and through: Soria in goal; Carvajal, Djené, Abdel Abqar, Johan Mojica and Kiko Femenía in defence; Gudelj, Mangala, Francho Serrano and Uche in midfield; Mayoral, Ünal and Juanmi up front. Survival, as every year, is the baseline, and the coach has made it into a culture.
