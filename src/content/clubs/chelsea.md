## History

Chelsea were founded on 10 March 1905 in the Rising Sun pub opposite Stamford Bridge, by Gus Mears, who had bought the athletics ground and, having failed to persuade [Fulham](/clubs/fulham) to move in, created a club to fill it. They were elected to the Second Division without having kicked a ball and were in the top flight by 1907. For half a century Chelsea were famous for big crowds, music-hall jokes and not winning anything, until Ted Drake, the former Arsenal striker, modernised the club, dropped the Pensioner badge and won the league in 1955.

Tommy Docherty's young team of the 1960s and Dave Sexton's side of the early 1970s gave the club its first glamour: Peter Osgood, the King of Stamford Bridge, Charlie Cooke, Peter Bonetti and the FA Cup of 1970, won in a brutal replay at Old Trafford against Leeds, then the Cup Winners' Cup in 1971 against [Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid). The cost of rebuilding the East Stand nearly killed the club. Ken Bates bought it for £1 in 1982, Chelsea went down to the Second Division twice, and the club's identity in the 1980s was its hooligan element as much as its football.

Glenn Hoddle, Ruud Gullit and Gianluca Vialli brought continental players and cup wins in the late 1990s, with Gianfranco Zola the most loved of them: the FA Cup in 1997 and 2000, the League Cup and Cup Winners' Cup in 1998. Then, in July 2003, Roman Abramovich bought the club and changed English football. Claudio Ranieri's £100m summer took Chelsea to second; [José Mourinho](/managers/jose-mourinho) arrived in 2004, announced himself as "a special one", and won back-to-back titles in 2005 (95 points, 15 goals conceded) and 2006 with [John Terry](/players/john-terry), [Frank Lampard](/players/frank-lampard-player), Claude Makélélé, [Petr Čech](/players/petr-cech) and [Didier Drogba](/players/didier-drogba).

[Carlo Ancelotti](/managers/carlo-ancelotti) won the Double in 2010 with 103 league goals. Roberto Di Matteo, an interim manager, won the Champions League in 2012, Drogba equalising with two minutes left and scoring the decisive penalty against [Bayern Munich](/clubs/bayern-munich) in their own stadium. Rafael Benítez won the Europa League in 2013, Mourinho returned for a title in 2015 and then self-destructed, Antonio Conte's back three won the league in 2017, and Thomas Tuchel, appointed in January 2021, won the Champions League in Porto four months later against [Manchester City](/clubs/manchester-city) with Kai Havertz's goal. [Eden Hazard](/players/eden-hazard) was the great player of the decade.

Abramovich was sanctioned by the UK government after Russia's invasion of Ukraine in March 2022 and forced to sell. A consortium led by Todd Boehly and Clearlake Capital paid £4.25bn, spent more than £1bn on players in two years, and burned through Tuchel, Graham Potter, Frank Lampard (as caretaker), Mauricio Pochettino, [Enzo Maresca](/managers/enzo-maresca), Liam Rosenior and Calum McFarlane before appointing [Xabi Alonso](/managers/xabi-alonso) in July 2026.

## Trajectory

Chelsea's modern arc runs from Abramovich's arrival to the present in three movements. From 2003-04 to 2016-17 they never finished below sixth and won five titles, with a one-season blip to tenth in 2015-16 after Mourinho's implosion. From 2017-18 to 2021-22 they were a top-four team with a trophy most seasons, culminating in the 2021 Champions League and the 2022 Club World Cup.

The Boehly era has been volatile. Twelfth in 2022-23, the worst finish since 1994, was followed by sixth and then fourth under Maresca, whose first season brought the Conference League in May 2025 and, in July 2025, the FIFA Club World Cup, won 3-0 against [Paris Saint-Germain](/clubs/paris-saint-germain) in New Jersey with two goals from [Cole Palmer](/players/cole-palmer).

The 2025-26 season unravelled. Maresca left on 1 January 2026; Rosenior, hired from Strasbourg on a six-and-a-half-year contract, was sacked after 106 days; McFarlane finished the season as interim. Chelsea lost the FA Cup final 1-0 to Manchester City on 16 May and finished tenth on 52 points, outside Europe for 2026-27. Alonso, who had left Real Madrid mid-season, was appointed in July as the sixth permanent head coach of the Clearlake era.

## Players the club produced

Cobham's academy produced the heart of the 2021 Champions League team. Reece James, the captain, Mason Mount, Trevoh Chalobah, Callum Hudson-Odoi and Tammy Abraham all played in that run, and Levi Colwill, Josh Acheampong and Tyrique George have followed. Earlier graduates include Peter Osgood, Ray Wilkins, [John Terry](/players/john-terry), the club's most successful captain with five league titles, and Jimmy Greaves, who scored 132 goals before leaving for Milan in 1961.

The signings define the Abramovich era. [Frank Lampard](/players/frank-lampard-player), bought from [West Ham](/clubs/west-ham-united) in 2001 for £11m, became the club's record scorer with 211 goals from midfield. [Didier Drogba](/players/didier-drogba), from [Marseille](/clubs/marseille), scored in four FA Cup finals and the 2012 Champions League final. [Petr Čech](/players/petr-cech), Claude Makélélé, Michael Essien, Ashley Cole, [Eden Hazard](/players/eden-hazard), N'Golo Kanté, [Thibaut Courtois](/players/thibaut-courtois) and Diego Costa were all bought. [Cole Palmer](/players/cole-palmer), signed from Manchester City for £42.5m in 2023, is the signature player of the current era and scored twice in the Club World Cup final. [Morgan Rogers](/players/morgan-rogers), bought from Aston Villa for a British-record £117m in summer 2026, is the latest.

## Identity and playing style

[Xabi Alonso](/managers/xabi-alonso) built an unbeaten Bundesliga champion at [Bayer Leverkusen](/clubs/bayer-leverkusen) in 2023-24 with a 3-4-2-1 and then spent a season at Real Madrid before leaving mid-way through 2025-26. At Chelsea he has used a [back three](/tactics/back-three) with Reece James and Malo Gusto as wing-backs, Moisés Caicedo and Romeo Lavia as the double pivot, and [Cole Palmer](/players/cole-palmer) and [Morgan Rogers](/players/morgan-rogers) as the two creators behind [João Pedro](/players/joao-pedro). The system is [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) in structure, with the centre-backs splitting wide and the wing-backs providing width, but it has far more verticality than Maresca's version: Alonso wants the ball into the two tens early and quickly.

The defence was rebuilt in the summer: Emiliano Martínez, the Argentina goalkeeper, arrived from Aston Villa for £7.5m and immediately displaced Robert Sánchez; Maxence Lacroix came from Crystal Palace for £52m; Jorrel Hato and Marco Palestra added young depth. Jordan Henderson, 36, and Danny Welbeck, 35, were signed on short deals to add experience to a squad whose average age had been the youngest in the league. The attack has Pedro Neto, Jamie Gittens, Geovany Quenda and Estêvão, 19, who has been the most exciting of the young Brazilians.

Chelsea press in Alonso's coordinated, trigger-based way, with the two tens cutting passing lanes into midfield, and they are strong at [set pieces](/tactics/set-pieces), where Lacroix and Colwill are the targets.

## Rivalries

[Tottenham Hotspur](/clubs/tottenham-hotspur) are the sharpest rivalry: the 1967 FA Cup final, the Battle of the Bridge in May 2016 that ended Spurs's title hopes, and Spurs's 2-0 win at Stamford Bridge on 24 October 2026 under Roberto De Zerbi. [Arsenal](/clubs/arsenal) are the big-club rivalry of the Abramovich years, with the 2017 FA Cup final and 2019 Europa League final. [Fulham](/clubs/fulham) are the nearest neighbours, two miles down the Fulham Road. [Leeds United](/clubs/leeds-united) are the historic enmity of the 1970 FA Cup final, revived whenever Leeds are in the top flight. [Liverpool](/clubs/liverpool) were the rivalry of the Mourinho-Benítez years, with five Champions League meetings between 2005 and 2009.

## Current situation

Chelsea are fourth in early November 2026: five wins, one draw and three defeats from nine, 19 goals scored and 15 conceded, 16 points. October brought a 2-0 win at Everton on 17 October, a 2-0 home defeat to Tottenham on 24 October and a 2-0 win over Manchester United on 31 October. Rogers, used as a left-sided ten, was the league's leading scorer in the opening weeks, and Martínez has been the clear first choice in goal.

The summer window was enormous even by Chelsea standards: ten senior arrivals, including Rogers, Lacroix, Martínez, Welbeck and Henderson, and 39 departures, including [Enzo Fernández](/players/enzo-fernandez) to Manchester City for £125m, [Andrey Santos](/players/andrey-santos) to Manchester United, Alejandro Garnacho on loan to Aston Villa, and the loan of Mykhailo Mudryk to Tottenham. Several outlets named Chelsea the winners of the window.

The season is unusual in one respect: no European football, for the first time since 2016-17, which gives Alonso a clear run at the league and the cups. The ambition is a Champions League place at minimum and a title challenge if the structure holds. The ownership's patience with head coaches has been short; Alonso's contract and standing give him more room than his predecessors had.
