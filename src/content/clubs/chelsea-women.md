## History

Chelsea Ladies were founded in 1992 and spent their first two decades as a mid-table side, joining the WSL at its 2011 launch. The transformation began in August 2012 when [Emma Hayes](/managers/emma-hayes) was appointed. By 2015 Chelsea had won a first WSL title and FA Cup double, and over the following decade Hayes built the most successful English club side of the professional era: six league titles, five FA Cups, two League Cups and the club's only [Champions League](/tournaments/womens-champions-league) final, lost 4-0 to [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona-women) in 2021.

The signings tell the story. [Ji So-yun](/players/ji-so-yun) arrived in 2014, [Fran Kirby](/players/fran-kirby) for a British record fee in 2015, [Sam Kerr](/players/sam-kerr) in January 2020, and later [Lauren James](/players/lauren-james), [Lucy Bronze](/players/lucy-bronze) and [Hannah Hampton](/players/hannah-hampton). Hayes left in May 2024 to coach the [United States](/nations/united-states-women), signing off with a fifth straight title clinched on goal difference at Old Trafford.

[Sonia Bompastor](/managers/sonia-bompastor), who had won the Champions League as Lyon's coach, replaced her and went unbeaten through 2024-25 to win a domestic treble. The 2025-26 season was harder: a League Cup win over Manchester United in March 2026, but a 5-1 defeat at [Manchester City](/clubs/manchester-city-women) in February and a third-place finish, 6 points behind the champions, meant Chelsea entered the Champions League via the qualifying rounds. Kerr, having broken Kirby's club WSL scoring record with 116 goals in all competitions, left in May 2026 for [Gotham FC](/clubs/gotham-fc).

## Trajectory

From seventh in 2011 to a first title in 2015, then seven championships in the eight seasons between 2017-18 and 2024-25. The 2025-26 third place was the first finish outside the top two since 2019. From 2026-27 all WSL home games are at Stamford Bridge, with cup and Champions League league-phase games at Plough Lane, the former Kingsmeadow base having been outgrown.

## Players the club produced

Chelsea's academy produced [Lauren James](/players/lauren-james) only indirectly (she left for Arsenal and Manchester United before returning in 2021), but Niamh Charles, Aggie Beever-Jones and Jess Carter all came through the pathway. The club's real gift to the game has been its signings: Kerr, Kirby, Ji, Millie Bright, Magdalena Eriksson, Pernille Harder, Erin Cuthbert, Guro Reiten and Bronze all played their best football in blue.

## Identity and playing style

Hayes's Chelsea were pragmatic and adaptable, switching between a [back three](/tactics/back-three) and a 4-2-3-1 and winning through game management. Bompastor plays a more fixed 4-2-3-1 with a high press, wide overloads from Sandy Baltimore and Johanna Rytting Kaneryd, and James as the free-roaming creator. Keira Walsh, signed from Barcelona in January 2025, runs the midfield; [Naomi Girma](/players/naomi-girma), the world's most expensive defender when she arrived, and Bronze give it a spine. Hampton, the 2025 Yashin Trophy winner, is as good with her feet as her hands, which suits a team that builds short.

## Rivalries

[Arsenal](/clubs/arsenal-women) is the biggest fixture in English women's football, a London derby that has decided titles. Manchester City have been the title rival of the 2020s. In Europe, Barcelona have eliminated Chelsea repeatedly, including in the 2021 final and successive semi-finals.

## Current situation

As of November 2026 Bompastor's second rebuild is underway. Kerr has gone, Alyssa Thompson has arrived from Angel City, Girma, Mayra Ramírez and Cuthbert, the captain, remain, and Bronze, 35, has signed on for another year as vice-captain. The aim is to recover the title from City and finally win the Champions League that eluded Hayes.
