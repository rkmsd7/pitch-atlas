## History

Orlando Pride joined the [NWSL](/leagues/nwsl) in 2016 as the sister club of Orlando City SC and immediately became the league's glamour signing destination: [Alex Morgan](/players/alex-morgan) arrived from Portland in the first season, Ashlyn Harris and Ali Krieger followed, and in 2017 [Marta](/players/marta), the six-time world player of the year, signed from Rosengård. That side reached the play-off semi-finals, losing to Portland, but the years that followed were a long disappointment: last place in 2019, a 2020 season withdrawn from the Challenge Cup after a COVID-19 outbreak, and a run of coaches that included Marc Skinner and Amanda Cromwell, who was fired in 2022 after a league investigation.

Seb Hines, promoted from interim, rebuilt the club around Marta and Zambian striker Barbra Banda, signed for a then-record fee in 2024. The 2024 season was perfect: an 18-6-2 record, the Shield, a 23-match unbeaten run and a 1-0 Championship final win over the Washington Spirit, with Banda scoring and Marta, 38, lifting her first NWSL trophy. Ownership by the Wilf family, who also own the Minnesota Vikings, had finally paid off.

The decline was quick. A fourth-place finish and semi-final exit in 2025 was followed by a 2026 season in which the Pride never found their 2024 form; Hines was sacked on 31 August 2026 with the team outside the play-off places, Yolanda Thomas took over as interim, and a 1-0 defeat to Seattle on Decision Day, 1 November, confirmed an 11th-place finish.

## Trajectory

Third in year two, last in year four, champions in year nine, 11th in year 11. The Pride's story is one of extremes, and the 2024 season remains the club's only sustained period at the top. Inter&Co Stadium, shared with Orlando City, is one of the league's best venues.

## Players the club produced

The Pride have been a destination rather than an academy. Marta is the club's greatest player and, at 40, still captain and number 10; Morgan scored 29 goals in Orlando before returning to San Diego; Harris, Krieger and Sydney Leroux were the other big names. Banda's 2024 Golden Boot-level form made her the league's best striker, and Angelina, the Brazilian midfielder, and Emily Sams, the centre-back, are the homegrown-adjacent core.

## Identity and playing style

Hines's champions played a 4-2-3-1 built on defensive solidity, [counter-attacking](/tactics/counter-attacking) through Banda's pace and Marta's creativity between the lines, and Anna Moorhouse's goalkeeping. The 2026 side lost that balance, conceding freely and scoring less, and Thomas's interim brief has been to restore the structure while giving younger players minutes.

## Rivalries

[Gotham FC](/clubs/gotham-fc), who beat Orlando in the 2025 semi-final, and the [Washington Spirit](/clubs/washington-spirit), beaten in the 2024 final, are the natural rivals. Kansas City Current, the other big-spending club of the mid-2020s, is a frequent play-off opponent.

## Current situation

As of November 2026 the Pride are searching for a permanent head coach and deciding how long Marta, who married team-mate Carrie Lawrence in January 2026 and has a contract through 2026, will keep playing. Banda remains the centrepiece of any rebuild. The 2027 season, interrupted by the World Cup in Marta's home country, is the next chance to recover.
