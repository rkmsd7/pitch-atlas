## History

Udinese were founded in 1896 as the football section of a gymnastics society in Udine, the capital of Friuli in the far north-east of Italy, which makes them one of the oldest clubs in the country, though their first appearance in Serie A did not come until 1950. The 1954-55 side finished second, the club's best ever position, with the striker Lorenzo Bettini's successor Arne Selmosson, and was then relegated for match-fixing. The next decades were spent mostly in Serie B and Serie C, with a 1978-79 Serie B title bringing the club back to the top flight, and the signing that changed its image: in 1983 Udinese bought [Zico](/players/zico), the greatest Brazilian of his generation, from Flamengo for a fee that required the intervention of the Italian federation and a demonstration by 20,000 Friulians in the streets. Zico scored 19 goals in 1983-84 and lifted a provincial club into the national conversation before injury and a tax case ended his stay.

The modern Udinese was built by Giampaolo Pozzo, the local industrialist who bought the club in 1986 and has owned it since, longer than any other owner in Serie A. The Pozzo model, scouting the world for undervalued players, buying them cheaply, developing them and selling them at a profit, was decades ahead of the rest of Italy and is the reason the club has been in Serie A without interruption since 1995, 31 consecutive seasons, the fourth-longest current run in the league.

## Trajectory

Alberto Zaccheroni's 1997-98 side finished third with Oliver Bierhoff scoring 27 goals, and Luciano Spalletti's 2004-05 side finished fourth and took Udinese into the Champions League for the first time, where they beat Sporting and Panathinaikos in the group and went out to Barcelona. Francesco Guidolin's teams of 2010 to 2013 finished fourth, third and fifth with [Alexis Sánchez](/players/alexis-sanchez), Antonio Di Natale, Kwadwo Asamoah and Samir Handanović, and reached the Champions League play-off twice, losing to Arsenal and Braga. Di Natale, the striker from Naples who refused every offer to leave, scored 191 league goals for the club, won the Capocannoniere in 2010 and 2011, and retired in 2016 with the Bluenergy Stadium's new stands rebuilt around him.

Since 2013 the club has drifted between 10th and 16th, a long sequence of narrow survivals and short-lived coaches, including a 2016-17 season of Luigi Delneri's and the Gabriele Cioffi and Andrea Sottil years. Kosta Runjaić, the German-Croatian coach appointed in 2024, finished 12th and then tenth in 2025-26 with 50 points, the best for a decade. The 2026-27 start was a disaster, one win in 11, and Runjaić was dismissed on 2 November 2026; Paolo Zanetti, the former Empoli and Verona coach, was appointed the same day.

## Players the club produced

Udinese's academy is secondary to its scouting, but it produced Pierpaolo Bisoli's contemporaries, the Italy goalkeeper Simone Scuffet and the midfielder Lazar Samardžić's teammate Simone Pafundi, a prodigy capped at 16. The Pozzo pipeline is the real story: Márcio Amoroso, sold to Parma for a record fee in 1999; Vincenzo Iaquinta; Sánchez, bought from Colo-Colo at 17 and sold to Barcelona for €26m; Juan Cuadrado, Handanović, Asamoah, Allan, Piotr Zieliński, Rodrigo De Paul, bought from Valencia and sold to Atlético for €35m, Gerard Deulofeu, Nahuel Molina, Destiny Udogie, sold to Tottenham at 19, Roberto Pereyra, Beto, Lazar Samardžić and Lorenzo Lucca. [Enzo Francescoli](/players/enzo-francescoli) and Sulley Muntari also wore the shirt. Thomas Kristensen was sold to Atalanta for €22m in summer 2026, and Arthur Atta to Fiorentina for €25m.

## Identity and playing style

Udinese have played a 3-5-2 almost without interruption since Guidolin, a system that suits the fast, physical, inexpensive players the scouting network finds. Runjaić's side was built on transition and set pieces, with Oumar Solet, the French centre-back found at Salzburg, as the best defender outside the top six and Sandi Lovrić as the creator. Zanetti has inherited a squad with an unusual headline signing in David Alaba, the 34-year-old Austrian who left Real Madrid on a free in summer 2026, alongside Nicolò Zaniolo, Keinan Davis, the Scottish teenager Lennon Miller, bought from Motherwell, Jurgen Ekkelenkamp and the Nigerian goalkeeper Maduka Okoye. The defence, which conceded 48 last season, has conceded 25 in 11 games this one.

## Rivalries

The historic derby is the Derby del Friuli-Venezia Giulia against Triestina, long dormant with Triestina in Serie C. In Serie A the Derby del Triveneto against [Venezia](/clubs/venezia) and Verona are the regional fixtures, and Udinese's 2-1 Coppa Italia win over Venezia in September 2026 was the one success of the autumn. The supporters have a friendship with Atalanta's and a historic hostility to Roma from the 1980s Zico years.

## Current situation

As of November 2026 Udinese are bottom of Serie A, 20th with five points from 11 matches, one win, two draws and eight defeats, four points from safety. Runjaić's dismissal after the Lecce defeat was the first time the Pozzos had sacked a coach in November since 2016. Zanetti, who kept Empoli up twice, has a contract to the end of the season with an option. The club's advantages are the stadium, rebuilt in 2016 and wholly owned, and a model that has never produced a relegation in three decades; the disadvantage is a squad that was sold down in the summer and has not yet been rebuilt. A first relegation since 1994 is a real possibility.
