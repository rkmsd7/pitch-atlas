## History

Genoa Cricket and Football Club was founded on 7 September 1893 by British consular officials and businessmen, which makes it the oldest football club still active in Italy, and its first Italian champions: the 1898 title, won in a single afternoon's tournament in Turin, was the first of six in the championship's first seven years. The Scotsman James Spensley, a doctor, was the club's first goalkeeper, coach and organiser; William Garbutt, an Englishman who arrived in 1912, was the first professional coach in Italy and the man Italian footballers still call "Mister" because of him. Garbutt's sides won the 1915, 1923 and 1924 championships, the last with the midfielder Renzo De Vecchi, "the son of God". Nine Scudetti, all before 1925, and the 1937 Coppa Italia make up the club's honours; no Italian club has gone so long between titles.

The post-war club lived between Serie A and Serie B. The 1990-91 side under Osvaldo Bagnoli, with the Czech striker Tomáš Skuhravý, the Uruguayan Carlos Aguilera and the captain Gianluca Signorini, finished fourth and reached the UEFA Cup semi-final in 1992, beating Liverpool at Anfield on the way, the first Italian side to do so. Signorini died of motor neurone disease in 2002 and the Gradinata Nord sings his name every match.

## Trajectory

The 2000s were the club's worst decade and its most dramatic rescue. Genoa won Serie B in 2004-05 under Enrico Preziosi, the toy magnate who had bought the club in 2003, and were then demoted to Serie C1 and docked points for fixing the final game against Venezia. The club won promotion from the third tier at the first attempt and from Serie B at the second, returning to Serie A in 2007 under [Gian Piero Gasperini](/managers/gian-piero-gasperini), whose 2008-09 side with Diego Milito and Thiago Motta finished fifth, the best position since 1991, and reached Europe. The next decade was a grind of 11th to 17th-place finishes and coaching changes, Preziosi sacking more than 20 managers, until relegation in 2022 under the American group 777 Partners, who had bought the club the previous year.

Alberto Gilardino took Genoa straight back as Serie B runners-up in 2023 and finished 11th in 2023-24 before being sacked. 777 collapsed in 2024 and the Romanian businessman Dan Șucu, owner of Rapid Bucharest, bought a controlling stake in December 2024. Patrick Vieira saved the 2024-25 season and left; [Daniele De Rossi](/managers/daniele-de-rossi) was appointed in November 2025 and kept the club up in 16th place with 41 points.

## Players the club produced

The academy's most famous product is Mattia Perin, the goalkeeper who came through in the 2010s before Juventus, and more recently Nicolò Rovella, now at Lazio, and Jeff Ekhator, the striker sold to Juventus for €16m in summer 2026. Brooke Norton-Cuffy went to Hull for €14m the same window. The club's history is one of signings who became icons: Skuhravý, Aguilera, Milito, who scored 24 goals in 2008-09 before Inter bought him, Goran Pandev, Domenico Criscito, the captain across two spells, Krzysztof Piątek, bought from Cracovia and sold to Milan after 19 goals in six months, Albert Guðmundsson and Mateo Retegui. Stephan El Shaarawy, now 34, joined on a free from Roma in summer 2026.

## Identity and playing style

Gasperini's Genoa was the laboratory for the 3-4-3 man-marking game he later perfected at Atalanta. De Rossi, who played his whole career at Roma and coached there briefly, uses a 3-5-2 built on [counter-attacking](/tactics/counter-attacking) and a hard-working midfield of Morten Frendrup, Djibril Sow, signed from Sevilla, and Tommaso Baldanzi, on loan from Roma. Johan Vásquez and Leo Østigård are the centre-backs in front of Justin Bijlow, bought from Feyenoord. The attack is a collection of loans and bargains, Lorenzo Colombo from Milan, Milutin Osmajić from Preston, the Portuguese winger Vitinha and the 18-year-old Brazilian Robinho Júnior, on loan from Santos.

## Rivalries

The Derby della Lanterna with Sampdoria is the fixture the city lives for, named for the medieval lighthouse that is Genoa's symbol; with Sampdoria in Serie B since 2023 and nearly in Serie C in 2025, the derby has been on hold, which Genoa fans enjoy and miss in equal measure. The rivalry with [AC Milan](/clubs/ac-milan) is bitter and dates from the 1995 killing of the Genoa supporter Vincenzo Spagnolo. There are friendships with Napoli and Torino's Granata, and a dislike of [Fiorentina](/clubs/fiorentina), Verona and Pisa.

## Current situation

As of November 2026 Genoa are 11th in Serie A with 13 points from ten matches, four wins, one draw and five defeats, having lost the first three games of the season to Napoli, Lazio and Como and then recovered with a run of wins. The Coppa Italia produced a 4-1 win over Ascoli in August. De Rossi, in his first full season, has the backing of Șucu and the sporting director Marco Ottolini, and the target is a calm mid-table finish and the retention of the players, Frendrup above all, that bigger clubs want. The Luigi Ferraris, shared with Sampdoria and 115 years old, is to be bought from the city by the two clubs and renovated.
