## History

Real Club Deportivo de La Coruña were founded in 1906 in the Galician port city and spent most of the twentieth century as a yo-yo club, with a 1950 second-place finish and the Riazor stadium (opened 1944) as the high points. The era that made them famous began with the election of Augusto César Lendoiro as president in 1988 and a promotion in 1991. Arsenio Iglesias' team of Bebeto, Mauro Silva, Fran, Donato and Djukić finished third in 1993 and second in 1994, losing the title on the final day when Djukić's last-minute penalty against Valencia was saved. The 1995 Copa del Rey, won against Valencia in a final finished days later because of a storm, was the first major trophy.

Super Dépor became Spanish champions in 1999-2000 under Javier Irureta, with Roy Makaay, Djalminha, Mauro Silva, Fran, Donato and the young Diego Tristán, the first and only title for a Galician club. They were runners-up in 2001 and 2002, won the 2002 Copa del Rey at the Bernabéu on Real Madrid's centenary ("el Centenariazo"), three Supercopas, and became a Champions League regular: Makaay was Europe's Golden Shoe in 2003, and on 7 April 2004 Deportivo beat holders Milan 4-0 at Riazor to overturn a 4-1 first-leg deficit, the greatest night in the club's history, before losing the semi-final to Porto.

Lendoiro's spending, however, had been built on debt. From 2005 the club sold its stars, fell into administration in 2013 with liabilities above €150m, and was relegated three times in eight seasons (2011, 2013, 2018). In 2020 Deportivo dropped to the third tier for the first time since 1975 and spent four years there, losing play-off finals in 2022 and 2023 before winning the Primera Federación in 2023-24. Promotion back to [La Liga](/leagues/la-liga) came in 2025-26 as Segunda runners-up behind Racing Santander, ending a six-year absence.

## Trajectory

The arc is unusually clean: seven straight top-three finishes from 1998 to 2004, a decade of decline, and a fall from the Champions League quarter-finals to the third tier in 16 years. The club's ownership passed in 2020 to the Galician bank Abanca, which cleared the debt and funded the rebuild, with Fernando Vidal and later Juan Carlos Escotet's management restoring order.

The two seasons in Segunda were a tale of two halves: 11th in 2024-25 under Óscar Gilsanz after a mid-season change, then a 77-point promotion campaign in 2025-26 with Yeremay Hernández the league's outstanding player and Riazor regularly above 25,000. Antonio Hidalgo, the former Huesca coach who had taken over for the promotion push, was given a one-year contract with an option for a second to lead the return to the top flight, with a reported €33m budget for signings.

## Players the club produced

The academy at Abegondo produced Fran, the one-club captain of the Super Dépor years, and in the modern era the two players around whom the current side is built: Yeremay Hernández, a dribbling left-footed winger born in the Canaries and trained in Galicia, who rejected offers from Spain and abroad to sign a long contract and lead the promotion, and David Mella, a winger who played at the 2025 Under-20 World Cup. Lucas Pérez, born in A Coruña, returned from Cádiz in 2023, paying part of his own transfer fee, to lead the climb out of the third tier.

The signings of the golden era remain the pantheon: Bebeto, the 1994 World Cup winner; Mauro Silva, the Brazilian midfielder who stayed 13 years; Donato, playing until 40; Djalminha; Makaay; Tristán; Juan Carlos Valerón, the most elegant Spanish playmaker of his generation; José Molina in goal; Noureddine Naybet; Aldo Duscher.

## Identity and playing style

Irureta's Deportivo played a 4-2-3-1 with a solid pivot (Mauro Silva and Sergio), a playmaker (Valerón or Djalminha) and quick wingers (Fran, Víctor), direct when needed and intelligent in possession: Spanish football before [tiki-taka](/tactics/tiki-taka). Hidalgo's side is more pragmatic, a 4-2-3-1 or 4-4-2 that defends in a mid-block, uses Marc Casadó (on loan from Barcelona) and Diego Villares to control the centre, and attacks on the break through Yeremay, Adama Traoré and Mella. The 37-year-old Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang, signed from Marseille, has been limited by a knee meniscus operation but is close to a return in November.

The Milan night of 2004 remains the measure of everything. Deportivo had lost the first leg 4-1 at San Siro with Kaká and Shevchenko scoring, and Riazor expected a formality. Walter Pandiani scored after five minutes, Valerón after 35, Luque after 44, and Fran, the captain, sealed it with 14 minutes left. It was the first time a team had overturned a three-goal first-leg deficit in the Champions League era, and it was done by a club from a city of 250,000 people. Deportivo had also beaten Juventus and Manchester United on the road in previous campaigns, and the club's Champions League record, five seasons, two quarter-finals and one semi-final, is better than that of most clubs in Spain.

## Rivalries

The Galician derby against [Celta Vigo](/clubs/celta-vigo) is one of the most emotional in Spain, a contest between Galicia's two cities, A Coruña and Vigo, that returns to La Liga in 2026-27 for the first time since 2017-18; the first league derby of the season is at Balaídos in January 2027. Sporting Gijón and Real Oviedo are the north-western rivals from the Segunda years, and the 2002 Copa final left a lingering edge with [Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid).

## Current situation

Deportivo have made a respectable start to life back in La Liga. On 7 November 2026 they are eighth with 14 points from 11 games (three wins, five draws, three defeats), a goal difference of +1 and an unbeaten opening month that ended with a 3-2 defeat at Real Sociedad on 11 October. The squad mixes the promotion core (Yeremay, Mella, Villares, Ximo Navarro, Dani Barcia) with experienced arrivals: José María Giménez from Atlético in defence, Angeliño, Casadó, Lorenzo Amatucci, Zakaria Eddahchouri, Adama Traoré and Aubameyang.

The objective is simple: stay up, keep Yeremay, and rebuild the club's standing one season at a time. Espanyol away on 7 November is the next fixture.
