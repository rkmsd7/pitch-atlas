## History

The club began as FC Lyon's women's section in 1970 and was absorbed by Olympique Lyonnais in 2004, when president Jean-Michel Aulas decided to fund it like a men's team. The result was the first superclub in women's football. Lyon won 14 consecutive French titles from 2007 to 2020 and eight [Champions Leagues](/tournaments/womens-champions-league) between 2011 and 2022, including five in a row from 2016 to 2020, with [Wendie Renard](/players/wendie-renard), [Ada Hegerberg](/players/ada-hegerberg), Eugénie Le Sommer, Amandine Henry, Sarah Bouhaddi, [Lucy Bronze](/players/lucy-bronze) and Dzsenifer Marozsán among the best players of their generation.

The 2022 final win over [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona-women) in Turin, 3-1, was the last European title. Since then Barcelona have beaten Lyon in the 2024 and 2026 finals, the latter a 4-0 defeat in Oslo on 23 May 2026 that was the heaviest in the club's European history. In between, Lyon lost the 2025 semi-final to [Arsenal](/clubs/arsenal-women) after winning the first leg in London.

American businesswoman Michele Kang bought control of the women's team from OL Groupe in 2024, rebranded it OL Lyonnes in 2025, moved home games to the 59,000-seat Parc OL and hired Jonatan Giráldez, who had won three Champions Leagues with Barcelona, in June 2025 on a contract to 2028. His first season brought a 19th French title via a 5-0 play-off final win over Paris FC, the Coupe de France and the Coupe LFFP, plus the Oslo final.

## Trajectory

One second place in 19 seasons, in 2020-21 when PSG won the title. Lyon have reached 11 Champions League finals. The move to the Parc OL and Kang's investment, which also covers the [Washington Spirit](/clubs/washington-spirit) and London City Lionesses in her multi-club group, has positioned Lyon as the club most able to compete with English wages.

## Players the club produced

Lyon's academy produced Renard's partner-in-crime Griedge Mbock, Selma Bacha, Delphine Cascarino, Amel Majri and Melvine Malard, a spine of the French national team. The signings became legends: Hegerberg (2014), Le Sommer (2010), Henry, Bronze (2017-2020), Marozsán, [Lindsey Heaps](/players/lindsey-horan) (2022-2026), [Kadidiatou Diani](/players/kadidiatou-diani) (2023-2026) and Melchie Dumornay, the Haitian playmaker who has become the team's creative hub.

## Identity and playing style

For a decade Lyon simply had better players and played a direct, powerful 4-3-3 with Renard dominating [set pieces](/tactics/set-pieces) and Hegerberg finishing. Giráldez has brought Barcelona's [positional play](/tactics/positional-play): a [high line](/tactics/high-line), short build-up through Christiane Endler, Dumornay and Daniëlle van de Donk as interiors, and width from Tabitha Chawinga and now [Salma Paralluelo](/players/salma-paralluelo), signed from Barcelona in July 2026 on a four-year deal. Marie-Antoinette Katoto, signed from PSG, shares the striking load with Hegerberg.

## Rivalries

[Paris Saint-Germain](/clubs/paris-saint-germain-women) is the domestic rivalry, decided by the 2021 title and a run of Coupe de France finals. Barcelona are the great European rival, with four finals since 2019. [Wolfsburg](/clubs/wolfsburg-women) were the opponent in three finals in the 2010s and Arsenal the newest nemesis.

## Current situation

As of November 2026 Lyon lead the Première Ligue again and are through the Champions League league phase. Renard, 36, still captains; Hegerberg, 31, scored three in Norway's April qualifiers; Paralluelo scored twice in her first four league games. The objective is explicit: a ninth Champions League, and revenge on Barcelona.
