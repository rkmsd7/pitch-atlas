## History

Club Brugge were founded in 1891 in the medieval Flemish city of Bruges and are one of the oldest clubs in Belgium, though their first title did not arrive until 1920. The golden era came in the 1970s under the Austrian coach Ernst Happel, the same man who had taken [Feyenoord](/clubs/feyenoord) to the 1970 European Cup. With Raoul Lambert up front, Happel's Brugge won three consecutive titles from 1976 to 1978, reached the 1976 UEFA Cup final, lost over two legs to Liverpool, and in 1978 became the first and still the only Belgian club to reach the European Cup final, losing 1-0 to Liverpool at Wembley to a Kenny Dalglish goal.

Jan Ceulemans, the great Belgian forward, joined in 1978 and stayed for 14 years, and the club won titles in 1980, 1988, 1990 and 1992. The 1990s and early 2000s brought five more championships under Hugo Broos and Trond Sollied, with Franky Van der Elst, Daniel Amokachi and Gert Verheyen, and a 2002-03 Champions League group campaign in which Brugge beat Milan at San Siro. A quieter decade followed, with Anderlecht and Standard Liège dominant, until Michel Preud'homme's appointment in 2013.

Preud'homme won the 2015 Belgian Cup and the 2016 title, the club's first in 11 years, and Ivan Leko, Philippe Clement and Alfred Schreuder added championships in 2018, 2020, 2021 and 2022, with Hans Vanaken winning the Belgian Golden Shoe twice and Ruud Vormer and Simon Mignolet providing experience. Brugge reached the Champions League last 16 in 2022-23, topping a group with Porto, Leverkusen and Atlético without conceding in their first four matches, and under Nicky Hayen won the 2024 title and reached the 2024 Conference League semi-final. In 2024-25 Hayen's team beat Aston Villa and Atalanta to reach the Champions League last 16 for a second time and won the Belgian Cup.

## Trajectory

Club Brugge's 19 titles are second in Belgium only to Anderlecht's 34, but in the last decade they have been the country's dominant force, winning six championships between 2016 and 2024 and finishing in the top two in all but one season since 2015. The twenty-first century as a whole has brought eight titles and only four finishes outside the top three. European consistency is the club's distinguishing achievement: Champions League group or league-phase football in eight of the last nine seasons, two last-16 appearances and the 2024 Conference League semi-final. Brugge are in the 2026-27 Champions League league phase and, as of November 2026, sit ninth with nine points from four matches, their best start to a European campaign.

## Players the club produced

Club Brugge's recent business has been the envy of Belgium: Charles De Ketelaere (sold to AC Milan), Noa Lang (to PSV), Ignace Van der Brempt, Maxim De Cuyper, Antonio Nusa (to RB Leipzig), Chemsdine Talbi and Ardon Jashari (to AC Milan) were all developed or launched at the club and sold for large fees. Earlier products and launches include Thibaut Courtois' rival for Belgium's goalkeeping shirt, Simon Mignolet, who arrived from Liverpool, and Timmy Simons, Hans Vanaken, Ruud Vormer and Daniel Amokachi, whose two seasons in Bruges in the early 1990s led to Everton. Jan Ceulemans and Franky Van der Elst remain the club's greatest home players.

## Identity and playing style

Club Brugge's identity is a combination of Flemish pride, a fiercely loyal crowd at the Jan Breydel and a modern, data-led recruitment model that buys young and sells high. On the pitch the club has favoured a 3-4-3 or 3-5-2 under Clement, Schreuder and Hayen, with [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) in build-up, aggressive wing-backs and a [back three](/tactics/back-three) that allows Vanaken to drift between the lines. Hayen's team of 2024-25 was notable for its composure in possession against far richer opponents. The head coach as of November 2026 should be checked against the club's latest announcements.

## Rivalries

The Bruges derby against Cercle Brugge, with whom Club share the Jan Breydel Stadium, is the local fixture; the rivalry with Anderlecht is the national one, Flanders against Brussels, and the two have contested most of the titles of the last 50 years. Matches against Gent and Antwerp have grown in importance as those clubs have strengthened.

## Current situation

As of November 2026 Club Brugge are in the Champions League league phase with nine points from four matches, ninth in the table and well placed for a third knockout appearance in four seasons. Domestically the aim is to recover the title from the 2024-25 season, when Brugge finished second, and to keep the squad together through January; Vanaken and Mignolet remain the leaders of a team that continues to regenerate through sales.
