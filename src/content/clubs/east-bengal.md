## History

East Bengal was founded on 1 August 1920 in the Jorabagan neighbourhood of north Calcutta, after Jorabagan Club left out the half-back Sailesh Bose from a Coochbehar Cup match against Mohun Bagan. Suresh Chandra Chaudhuri walked out in protest and, with Raja Manmatha Nath Chaudhuri, Ramesh Chandra Sen and Aurobinda Ghosh, started a club for the people of eastern Bengal living in the city. From its first day it was a club of identity: the "bangals" from the east against the established "ghotis" of Calcutta, an identity that deepened after Partition in 1947 sent millions of refugees from East Pakistan into the city.

The club's golden eras came in waves. The "Pancha Pandava" forward line of the late 1940s and early 1950s, built around Ahmed Khan and P. Venkatesh, won the IFA Shield and Durand Cup; the 1970s side, coached for much of the decade by P. K. Banerjee, won six straight Calcutta league titles from 1970 and in 1975 beat Mohun Bagan 5-0 in the IFA Shield final, still the derby's biggest win. East Bengal became the record IFA Shield winners, with 29, and collected 17 Durand Cups.

When the National Football League arrived it won three titles, in 2000-01, 2002-03 and 2003-04, the last under Subhash Bhowmick, who also led the club to the 2003 ASEAN Club Championship in Jakarta, the first international club trophy won by an Indian side. Then came a long drought. East Bengal finished second in the I-League four times, won Federation Cups in 2009, 2010 and 2012, and took eight straight Calcutta league titles from 2010, but the national title never came back.

## Trajectory

East Bengal's route into the [Indian Super League](/leagues/indian-super-league) was messy. The club stayed in the I-League while the ISL grew, went through an investor partnership with Quess Corp from 2018, and finally entered the ISL in 2020-21 as SC East Bengal, backed by Shree Cement, with Robbie Fowler as coach. The finishes were grim: ninth, eleventh, ninth, ninth, ninth. Emami became the principal investor in 2022, and under Carles Cuadrat the club won the 2024 Super Cup, beating Odisha 3-2 after extra time in Bhubaneswar for their first major trophy in over a decade.

Óscar Bruzón arrived early in 2024-25 and turned the club into a contender. The 2025-26 season, delayed by the governance crisis around the expiry of the AIFF's Master Rights Agreement, began on 14 February 2026 as a shortened, points-only competition with no play-offs. East Bengal lost once in 13 games and went into the final day needing a win. On 21 May 2026 at the Kishore Bharati Krirangan, they came from behind to beat Inter Kashi 2-1, Youssef Ezzejjari equalising and Mohammed Rashid heading the 72nd-minute winner from Bipin Singh's cross. East Bengal and [Mohun Bagan](/clubs/mohun-bagan) finished on 26 points, but East Bengal's goal difference of +19 beat their rival's +14. It was the club's first ISL title and first national league crown in 22 years, and Ezzejjari took the Golden Boot.

## Players the club produced

East Bengal's greats include the 1950s forward Ahmed Khan, defender Sudhir Karmakar, the playmaker Krishanu Dey, the Iranian midfielder Majid Bishkar, whose 1980s spell remains a Kolkata legend, and Nigerian striker Chima Okorie. [Bhaichung Bhutia](/players/bhaichung-bhutia) scored a hat-trick in the 1997 Federation Cup semi-final derby watched by an estimated 131,000, and [Sunil Chhetri](/players/sunil-chhetri) spent a season at the club. The title-winning squad featured India internationals Anwar Ali, Naorem Mahesh Singh, Prabhsukhan Gill and Bipin Singh, with Rashid and the Spanish midfielder Dani Ramírez.

## Identity and playing style

East Bengal are a working-class, emotional club whose supporters prize fight over elegance, and Bruzón's champions reflected that: a compact 4-2-3-1, strong at set pieces and quick in transition. For 2026-27 the club has appointed Antonio López Habas, the Spaniard who won two ISL titles with ATK and later coached ATK Mohun Bagan, a crossing of the Kolkata divide that raised eyebrows. Habas favours a disciplined [low block](/tactics/low-block) and fast [counter-attacks](/tactics/counter-attacking).

## Rivalries

The Kolkata Derby against [Mohun Bagan](/clubs/mohun-bagan), first played in 1925, is the heart of the club. It is ghoti against bangal, green-and-maroon against red-and-gold, hilsa against prawn on the fish market stalls on derby day. Mohun Bagan won most ISL-era derbies, which made the 2025-26 title, taken from them on goal difference, the sweetest in the club's history. Matches with Mohammedan Sporting, the third great Kolkata club, carry their own history.

## Current situation

As of 8 November 2026, East Bengal are sixth in the 2026-27 ISL with 6 points from only two games, having beaten Churchill Brothers 4-0 and Inter Kashi 5-1, with a match at Chennaiyin on 7 November and two or three games in hand on the clubs above them. The defending champions want to show the 2025-26 title, won in a shortened season, was no accident, and to make an impact in Asia as India's league champions.
