## History

Espérance Sportive Troyes Aube Champagne, universally known as ESTAC, were founded in 1986 as the successor to Troyes AF, a club that had been liquidated after reaching Division 1 in the 1950s and the 1970s. The new club climbed from the regional leagues to Division 2 in 1996 and to the top flight in 1999 under Alain Perrin, who then produced the club's best two seasons: seventh in 2000 and 2001 with Blaise Nkufo, Jérôme Rothen and Gharib Amzine, qualification for the Intertoto Cup, and in 2001 an Intertoto win over Newcastle on away goals (4-4 in a famous second leg at St James' Park) that took Troyes into the UEFA Cup, where they beat Villa and lost to Leeds.

Perrin left for Marseille in 2002 and Troyes were relegated the next spring. Since then the club has been the archetypal French yo-yo side: promoted in 2005, down in 2007, down again to the National in 2009, up in 2010 and 2012, down in 2013, up as champions in 2015, down with 18 points in 2016, up via the play-off in 2017, down in 2018, up as champions in 2021 under Laurent Batlles, down in 2023, and up again as champions in 2025-26 under Stéphane Dumont. Benjamin Nivet, the playmaker who played for the club into his 40s, was the constant of a decade of promotions and relegations.

City Football Group bought ESTAC in September 2020, making Troyes the tenth club in the Manchester City network, and used the club as a development stop for young players such as Metehan Güçlü, Savinho and Rony Lopes. The 2022-23 relegation was followed by a near-disaster in 2023-24, when only a final-day result kept them out of the National. The recovery since, seventh in 2025 and champions in 2026, has been built under Dumont on a settled squad and the Stade de l'Aube, renovated in the 2000s to hold 20,000.

## Trajectory

Six promotions to Ligue 1 and six relegations in a quarter of a century, with no top-flight spell longer than two seasons since 2003: Troyes's trajectory is a saw-tooth. The CFG era has not yet changed the pattern, but the 2026 promotion came with a stronger squad and more stable coaching than previous ones, and the first months of 2026-27 have been competitive.

## Players the club produced

Troyes's academy has produced Rothen, who left for Monaco in 2002 and became a France international, Blaise Matuidi (signed as a teenager from Clairefontaine, sold to Saint-Étienne), Adil Rami (a brief academy spell before Fréjus and Lille), Jessy Moulin, the goalkeeper of the 2010s, and Mama Baldé. Among signings, Nkufo, Amzine, Nivet, Yoann Touzghar, Renaud Ripart and Metehan Güçlü are the names of the promotion seasons; Tawfik Bentayeb and Kévin Fortuné led the 2025-26 attack.

## Identity and playing style

Perrin's Troyes played an attacking 4-4-2 that overachieved for two seasons; the promotion sides since have been pragmatic, organised teams built on a strong defence. Dumont's 2025-26 champions used a 4-3-3 with a mid-block, [pressing traps](/tactics/pressing-traps) in the middle third and quick [counter-attacking](/tactics/counter-attacking) transitions, with [set pieces](/tactics/set-pieces) a significant source of goals. In Ligue 1 the side has kept its shape, conceding fewer than most predicted, and has been the promoted team making the biggest impression.

## Rivalries

The Derby de Champagne against Reims, 120 kilometres away, is the regional rivalry, now a Ligue 2 fixture after Reims's 2025 relegation; Sedan, in the Ardennes, were the rival of the 2000s. [AJ Auxerre](/clubs/auxerre), 90 kilometres south in Burgundy, are the nearest Ligue 1 opponent and the derby of 2026-27.

## Current situation

As of 7 November 2026 Troyes are 16th in Ligue 1, in the relegation play-off place, on eight points from nine games (two wins, two draws, five defeats, seven scored, 16 conceded). They lost 3-1 at home to Lens on 17 October, drew 0-0 at Toulouse on 25 October and beat fellow promoted side Le Mans 2-0 at the Stade de l'Aube on 31 October. The next fixture is at the Parc des Princes against PSG on 7 November.

Dumont's team is hard to beat but struggles to score, which is the standard profile of a promoted club built on defence. The objective is to break the club's pattern and stay up for a third consecutive season for the first time since 2003; the gap to safety is a single point.
