## History

Levante Unión Deportiva trace their origins to Levante Football Club, founded in 1909 in Valencia's maritime district, which merged with Gimnástico in 1939. The club's most remarkable piece of history came during the Civil War: Levante won the Copa de la España Libre in 1937, the Republican zone's cup, a trophy the club has campaigned for decades to have recognised by the federation. They did not reach the top flight until 1963 and spent most of the century in the second and third tiers, with brief first-division spells in 1963-65 and 1965-66. The most surreal episode came in 1981, when [Johan Cruyff](/players/johan-cruyff), aged 33, played ten games for Levante in Segunda, a transfer that astonished Spain and did nothing to prevent the club's financial collapse.

The modern Levante arrived with the Segunda title in 2004 under Manuel Preciado, with Predrag Mijatović and Juan Carlos Valerón's brother in the side. Two promotions and two relegations in five years left the club in administration in 2008, with players unpaid for months and Segunda B a real threat. A fan-led rescue and promotion in 2010 under Luis García Plaza preceded the greatest season in the club's history: in 2011-12, with a squad of veterans on minimal wages (Juanfran, Sergio Ballesteros, Vicente Iborra, Arouna Koné), Levante briefly led La Liga in October, finished sixth and reached the Europa League last 16 the following year.

Relegation in 2016 was followed by the Segunda title in 2017 and five seasons in the top flight under Paco López, with José Luis Morales, Roger Martí and Enis Bardhi producing famous wins over Barcelona (5-4 in 2018) and Real Madrid. Relegation in 2022 was followed by a play-off final defeat to Alavés in 2023, decided by a goal in the 101st minute of the second leg, and promotion in third place in 2024-25.

## Trajectory

Levante have been promoted to La Liga five times this century (2004, 2006, 2010, 2017, 2025) and relegated four times (2005, 2008, 2016, 2022). The 2025-26 season was a survival epic: Julián Calero was removed in November with the team 19th, the caretaker Álvaro del Moral lasted three weeks, and the Portuguese coach Luís Castro, formerly of Shakhtar, Botafogo and Al-Nassr, took over on 20 December with the team bottom. Castro's "revolution" turned the season around and Levante finished 16th on 42 points, staying up on the final day as Mallorca, Girona and Oviedo went down.

## Players the club produced

Levante's academy, long starved of resources, has produced few stars; the city's talent historically went to Valencia CF. The modern exceptions are the striker Roger Martí (from nearby Torrent), the midfielder Pepelu, now at Valencia, Jorge de Frutos, Carlos Clerc and the current goalkeeper Pablo Cuñat. The 2011-12 veterans and Morales, "El Comandante", who played nine seasons and 300 games, are the club's emotional heroes.

The signing that caught attention in 2025 was Etta Eyong, the Cameroonian striker whose goals in the first half of 2025-26 made him one of the league's most sought-after forwards before a long dry spell; he began 2026-27 with a thigh injury but returned to the starting line-up in late October.

## Identity and playing style

Levante's football has usually been built on organisation and a big striker. Juan Ignacio Martínez's 2011-12 side was a deep 4-4-2 that counter-attacked and fouled with discipline; Paco López's teams were open and fast, with Morales running from the left. Castro, by contrast, is a possession coach whose Botafogo won the 2024 Copa Libertadores with a compact 4-2-3-1, intense pressing and quick wide combinations. At Levante he has used a 4-2-3-1 with Jon Ander Olasagasti and Hugo Sotelo in midfield, Roger Brugué and Iván Romero wide and Etta Eyong or Petar Ratkov as the striker, with the Australian goalkeeper Mathew Ryan and the veteran Aïssa Mandi as the defensive leaders.

## Rivalries

The Valencia derby against [Valencia CF](/clubs/valencia) is the one that matters, a contest between the big club of the city and its humbler neighbour from the fishing and port districts, with Levante's wins in 2015 and 2019 cherished. [Villarreal](/clubs/villarreal) and [Elche](/clubs/elche) are the regional rivals.

## Current situation

Levante are 18th on 7 November 2026 with ten points from 11 games (two wins, four draws, five defeats), level with Elche and a point above Valencia, which makes the city derby of 2026-27 a relegation six-pointer. The squad, assembled on a small budget with Castro's input, includes Ryan in goal; Mandi, Jeremy Toljan, Víctor García and Manu Sánchez in defence; Olasagasti, Sotelo, Enzo Bardeli and Brugué in midfield; and Etta Eyong, Ratkov and Romero in attack. A Copa del Rey tie at Tarancón on 5 November was the week's warm-up before the league resumed.

The objective is the one Castro achieved last spring: survival, by whatever margin, and then the chance to build something longer-term at the Ciutat de València.
