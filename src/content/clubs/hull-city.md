## History

Hull City were founded in 1904 in a rugby league city and joined the Football League's Second Division a year later. For a century they were the largest city in England never to have had a top-flight club: an FA Cup semi-final in 1930, the Raich Carter years after the war, the goals of Chris Chilton and Ken Wagstaff in the 1960s, and otherwise long stretches in the third and fourth divisions, with a spell near bankruptcy under David Lloyd in the 1990s when the club was locked out of Boothferry Park by bailiffs.

Adam Pearson's purchase in 2001 and the move to the KC Stadium in 2002 started the climb. Peter Taylor won back-to-back promotions in 2004 and 2005, and Phil Brown's team, with Dean Windass's volley in the 2008 play-off final against Bristol City, took Hull into the top flight for the first time in their 104-year history. They won at Arsenal and Tottenham that autumn, and Brown's half-time team talk on the pitch at Manchester City became part of the folklore; survival on the last day in 2009 was followed by relegation in 2010.

Steve Bruce brought them back in 2013 and, in 2014, took Hull to their only FA Cup final, where they led Arsenal 2-0 after eight minutes and lost 3-2 in extra time, and into the Europa League. Owner Assem Allam's attempt to rename the club Hull Tigers was blocked by the FA. Relegation in 2015, promotion through the play-offs in 2016 under Bruce, and relegation again in 2017 under Marco Silva followed. The Allam years ended with relegation to League One in 2020; Grant McCann won the League One title in 2021. Acun Ilıcalı, the Turkish television mogul, bought the club in January 2022, and after Liam Rosenior's near-miss in 2024 and a 21st-place scramble in 2025, [Sergej Jakirović](/managers/sergej-jakirovic), the Bosnian-Croatian coach who had won titles with Dinamo Zagreb, led Hull to sixth and the play-offs in 2025-26. They beat Millwall in the semi-final and Middlesbrough 1-0 at Wembley on 23 May 2026, Oli McBurnie scoring in the 95th minute. All three of Hull's play-off final wins have been 1-0.

## Trajectory

Hull's trajectory is the most volatile in the division: fourth tier in 2004, Premier League in 2008, three relegations and three promotions between 2010 and 2017, League One in 2020, and now a fourth spell in the top flight. The 2026 play-off final was also controversial: Southampton were removed from the play-offs over a spying scandal and Middlesbrough took their place, and Hull reportedly explored whether they should be promoted automatically before the final went ahead.

## Players the club produced

Hull's academy has produced Jarrod Bowen, sold to West Ham in 2020 and an England international, Keane Lewis-Potter, sold to Brentford for £16m in 2022, Jacob Greaves, sold to Ipswich in 2024, Andy Robertson, who arrived from Dundee United and was developed into a Liverpool and Scotland left-back, and Harry Maguire, bought from Sheffield United and sold to Leicester. Dean Windass, born in Hull, is the local hero; Nick Barmby, another Hull native, returned to play and manage. Ian Ashbee, who captained the club in all four divisions, and Ahmed Elmohamady are the other modern legends.

## Identity and playing style

[Sergej Jakirović](/managers/sergej-jakirovic) favours a 4-2-3-1 that defends deep and compact and breaks quickly, with a physical target in Oli McBurnie, Joe Gelhardt and Mohamed-Ali Cho running beyond him and Sorba Thomas delivering from wide. It is [direct play](/tactics/direct-play) with discipline, and the defensive numbers of the autumn, with Hull conceding far fewer goals than their expected-goals-against suggested, point to a well-drilled block and to Jack Butland's form in goal.

The summer recruitment was unusual: 18 signings, with an average age of about 24, including Hidemasa Morita from Sporting CP, Lucas Gourna-Douath, Eliot Matazo, Jens Hjertø-Dahl from Tromsø, Tim Iroegbunam, John Egan, Semi Ajayi, Matt Targett and Butland, while the promotion-winning core of McBurnie, Thomas, Regan Slater, Lewie Coyle and Ryan Giles was kept. Jakirović signed a new contract to 2028 in the summer.

## Rivalries

[Leeds United](/clubs/leeds-united) are the Yorkshire rivalry of the modern era; [Sheffield United](/clubs/sheffield-united) the other; and [Grimsby Town](/clubs/grimsby-town), across the Humber, the oldest, though the two clubs have not met in the league for years.

## Current situation

Hull are 11th in early November 2026, with two wins, five draws and two defeats from nine: 11 goals scored and 10 conceded, 11 points. The run includes a 2-2 draw with Everton at home on 11 October, a 1-0 defeat at Fulham on 17 October, 1-1 with Brentford on 25 October and 2-2 with Ipswich on 31 October. Earlier in the season Hull beat Manchester United and Coventry and drew 2-2 at Chelsea, and at one point sat as high as third. Jakirović said after three games that he "would have signed immediately" for seven points.

The ambition is survival, and the early weeks have made that look more achievable than any pre-season projection. The squad's depth after 18 signings is a strength; its lack of Premier League experience is the risk once the winter fixtures accumulate.
