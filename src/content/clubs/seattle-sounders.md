## History

Seattle has had a team called the Sounders since 1974, through the NASL, the APSL and the USL, and when the MLS version kicked off in 2009 it inherited a name, a culture and a city that already knew what it wanted. The ownership group of Adrian Hanauer, Joe Roth, Paul Allen and the comedian Drew Carey introduced a members' association with a vote on the general manager, a marching band, and a stadium (then Qwest Field) that drew an average of 30,000 from day one. The club won the U.S. Open Cup in each of its first three seasons under Sigi Schmid, with Kasey Keller in goal and Fredy Montero up front.

The signing of Clint Dempsey from [Tottenham](/clubs/tottenham-hotspur) in 2013 was the club's statement of intent, and alongside Obafemi Martins and the midfield anchor Osvaldo Alonso he led the team to the 2014 Supporters' Shield. The step to MLS Cup needed one more move: in July 2016, with the team near the bottom of the West and Schmid gone, assistant Brian Schmetzer took over and the club signed Nicolás Lodeiro from Boca Juniors. Seattle won MLS Cup that December on penalties against Toronto without having a shot on target.

## Trajectory

Schmetzer, a Seattle native who played for the old Sounders, has been in charge ever since. His teams reached four MLS Cup finals in five years (2016, 2017, 2019, 2020), winning two, the second a 3-1 win over Toronto in front of 69,274 at Lumen Field in 2019 with Raúl Ruidíaz scoring. The 2022 season was the odd one: the club became the first MLS side to win the CONCACAF Champions League, beating [Pumas UNAM](/clubs/pumas-unam) 3-0 in the second leg of the final in front of 68,741, then missed the play-offs for the first time as the squad ran out of energy.

That title took Seattle to the 2022 [Club World Cup](/tournaments/club-world-cup) in Morocco and, in 2025, to the expanded tournament on home soil, where they lost to Botafogo, Atlético Madrid and PSG at Lumen Field. The summer of 2025 brought a trophy instead: a 3-0 Leagues Cup final win over [Inter Miami](/clubs/inter-miami) that ended in a melee started by Luis Suárez. The 2026 regular season was a step back, tenth in the West with 43 points and no play-off place for only the second time in the club's history.

## Players the club produced

The Sounders academy has become one of the best in the country. Jordan Morris, a Stanford product and Seattle native, has scored more than 100 goals for the club. Cristian Roldan came through the draft and has played over 300 games. DeAndre Yedlin was sold to Tottenham in 2014, Obed Vargas and Reed Baker-Whiting are more recent academy graduates, and the club's second team has fed the first team steadily. The great imports were Dempsey, Martins, Lodeiro, Ruidíaz, João Paulo and the goalkeeper Stefan Frei, whose save from Jozy Altidore in the 2017 final is the most famous moment in club history.

## Identity and playing style

Schmetzer's Sounders are a 4-2-3-1 team that defends in a compact block and attacks through quick vertical passes to the wingers and Morris's runs in behind, with Lodeiro, and later Albert Rusnák and Pedro de la Vega, operating between the lines. The team is rarely the most possession-heavy in the league but is consistently among the best at transition, which is why it has been so dangerous in knockout football. Schmetzer's insistence on continuity, with the same assistants and a core of players over nearly a decade, is itself the club's identity.

## Rivalries

The Cascadia Cup, contested with Portland Timbers and Vancouver Whitecaps, is MLS's most authentic regional rivalry and dates back to the 1970s. Seattle-Portland is the fiercest fixture in the league; Vancouver, 2025 MLS Cup finalists, have become the sharper competitive rival. Games with [LAFC](/clubs/lafc) have decided two Western Conference finals.

## Current situation

As of November 2026 the Sounders have missed the play-offs after a tenth-place finish in the West. Schmetzer is listed as head coach in the club's squad data, Morris and Roldan remain the leaders, and the club is heading into a rebuild of the attacking line. The Leagues Cup and Champions League wins keep Seattle's status as the most decorated MLS club of the 2010s and 2020s outside the LA pair, and the fan base, still the largest in the league, will expect a return to the top four in 2027.
