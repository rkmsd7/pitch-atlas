## History

The club began life as Sky Blue FC in 2007, won the inaugural Women's Professional Soccer title in 2009 with Christie Pearce as player-coach, and survived the collapse of WPS to become a founder member of the [NWSL](/leagues/nwsl) in 2013. For most of the next decade Sky Blue were a byword for mismanagement: a one-win season in 2018, players housed in substandard conditions, and a constant churn of coaches. The rebrand to NJ/NY Gotham FC in 2021, the move to Red Bull Arena (now Sports Illustrated Stadium) and the arrival of Carli Lloyd for her final seasons began the turnaround.

The real change was Juan Carlos Amorós. The Spanish coach took over for 2023 after Gotham had finished last in 2022 and won the Championship in his first season, beating OL Reign 2-1 in San Diego in Ali Krieger's farewell game. The 2025 season was stranger still: eighth in the regular season, the last play-off seed, then wins over Kansas City, Washington and Orlando to lift a second Championship, having already won the Concacaf W Champions Cup in May 2025.

In 2026 Gotham were the best team from start to finish. [Sam Kerr](/players/sam-kerr), signed from Chelsea on 29 June on a contract to 2030, scored four in 17 games, breaking the club's single-season record in August and becoming the first player to 80 NWSL regular-season goals. The Shield was won with a club-record 61 points and a quarter-final against Seattle Reign on 8 November opened the defence of the title.

## Trajectory

Last in 2018 and 2022, champions in 2023 and 2025, Shield winners in 2026. Few clubs in world football have swung so violently. Ownership, which includes Kevin Durant, Sue Bird and Eli Manning among its investors, has backed Amorós with the league's deepest squad.

## Players the club produced

Gotham's identity has been built on signings rather than development. [Rose Lavelle](/players/rose-lavelle), Tierna Davidson (captain), Emily Sonnett, Midge Purce and Jaedyn Shaw are American internationals; Guro Reiten and Ann-Katrin Berger, the German goalkeeper who was the hero of the 2024 Olympics, are European imports. Lynn Biyendolo, the 2023 Championship MVP, left in 2025. Kerr is the biggest signing in the club's history.

## Identity and playing style

Amorós plays a possession-based 4-3-3 with Spanish principles: build-up through Berger and Davidson, Lavelle as the free interior, width from Purce and Shaw, and a striker, now Kerr, attacking the [high line](/tactics/high-line) in behind. The press is coordinated rather than frantic, which sets Gotham apart in a league that runs on transitions, and the 2025 play-off run showed a side that could absorb pressure and strike on the [counter-attack](/tactics/counter-attacking).

## Rivalries

The [Washington Spirit](/clubs/washington-spirit), beaten in the 2025 Championship final, are the East Coast rival; [Orlando Pride](/clubs/orlando-pride) were the 2024 champions and 2025 semi-final victims. North Carolina Courage were the dominant club of the previous era.

## Current situation

As of November 2026 Gotham are the top seed in the play-offs, with the Championship at Audi Field on 21 November the target. Kerr, Lavelle, Davidson and Berger lead a squad that also includes Reiten and Shaw. A third title in four years would confirm Amorós's team as the NWSL's dynasty.
