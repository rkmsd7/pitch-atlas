## History

Clube Atlético Mineiro was founded in March 1908 by a group of students in Belo Horizonte and is the oldest of the state's big clubs. The rooster mascot, the Galo, dates from the 1940s and gives the club its nickname, and the "Massa", the mass of fans, is proud of being the most popular club in Minas Gerais. Atlético won the first Campeonato Brasileiro in 1971, with Dadá Maravilha's goals and the coach Telê Santana, and then spent the rest of the century as the best team never to win it again, finishing second in 1977 (losing a final on penalties to São Paulo after Reinaldo's goals), 1980 and 1999.

Reinaldo, the "Rei", who scored 255 goals and raised a clenched fist in celebration during the dictatorship, is the club's greatest player; Éder Aleixo, Toninho Cerezo, Luizinho and the goalkeeper João Leite were the 1980s core; and Gilberto Silva, the future Arsenal midfielder, came through in the late 1990s. The club won the Copa Conmebol in 1992 and 1997 and was relegated in 2005, returning as Série B champions in 2006.

## Trajectory

The 2010s changed everything. [Ronaldinho](/players/ronaldinho) signed in 2012, Cuca built a team around him with Diego Tardelli, Bernard, Jô and the goalkeeper Victor, and in July 2013 Atlético won the Copa Libertadores for the first time, after a series of improbable comebacks: Victor's foot save from a penalty against Tijuana, a 4-1 extra-time win over Newell's Old Boys from 2-0 down, and a 2-0 second-leg win over Olimpia followed by penalties in the final. Victor's save from the Tijuana penalty is known in Belo Horizonte as "the miracle". The 2014 Copa do Brasil, won against Cruzeiro, and the Recopa followed.

The second great season came in 2021, when Cuca returned and Hulk, signed from Shanghai SIPG, led a team with Nacho Fernández, Diego Costa and Keno to the Brasileirão and Copa do Brasil double, the first league title in 50 years. The Arena MRV, the club's own 46,000-seat stadium, opened in 2023, and in 2024 the club reached both the Copa Libertadores final (lost 3-1 to Botafogo) and the Copa do Brasil final (lost to Flamengo) under Gabriel Milito. Jorge Sampaoli's 2025 team lost the Copa Sudamericana final on penalties to Lanús. The club's current squad data lists Eduardo Domínguez, the Argentine who had won titles with Estudiantes, as head coach as of November 2026; Atlético are seventh in the 2026 Brasileirão with 50 points from 34 games.

## Players the club produced

Atlético's academy produced Reinaldo, Éder, Toninho Cerezo, Gilberto Silva, Bernard (sold to Shakhtar in 2013), Marcos Rocha, the goalkeeper Victor's understudies and the striker Alan Kardec. The signings were the legends of the modern club: Ronaldinho, Hulk (the all-time top scorer of the Arena era), Tardelli, Jô, Victor, Nacho Fernández and the Argentine Matías Zaracho.

## Identity and playing style

Galo's identity is passion and chaos, "Galo doido", the mad rooster, and its most successful coach, Cuca, has twice harnessed it with an attacking 4-2-3-1 built around a dominant striker (Jô, then Hulk) and quick wingers, pressing in waves rather than systematically. Sampaoli's 2025 team was the most intense in Brazil and the most defensively exposed. Domínguez has brought an Argentine emphasis on structure, a compact 4-4-2 that defends its box and attacks through Hulk, now 40 and still the top scorer, and Gustavo Scarpa's left foot. See [counter-attacking](/tactics/counter-attacking) for the family of approaches the Arena MRV team has favoured.

## Rivalries

The Clássico Mineiro against [Cruzeiro](/clubs/cruzeiro) is the only derby that matters in Belo Horizonte and one of the most heated in the country; Atlético's 2021 title, Cruzeiro's 2019 relegation and the Mineirão's division between the two have all been weaponised in chants. The 2024 Libertadores and Copa do Brasil finals created national rivalries with [Botafogo](/clubs/botafogo) and [Flamengo](/clubs/flamengo).

## Current situation

As of November 2026 Atlético are seventh in the Brasileirão, in the Sudamericana places and pushing for a Libertadores spot, with Domínguez listed as head coach and Hulk, Scarpa, Everson, Paulinho and Igor Gomes as the core. The SAF, owned by the Galo Forte Vencedor group of businessmen since 2022, has invested heavily and expects a return to a continental final; the Arena MRV, which hosted no World Cup matches, has become one of the most intimidating grounds in Brazil.
