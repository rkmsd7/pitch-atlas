## History

PSG's women's team was founded in 1971 but only became a serious club after Qatar Sports Investments bought Paris Saint-Germain in 2011 and decided the women should match the men's ambition. Within six years PSG had reached two [Champions League](/tournaments/womens-champions-league) finals, losing to Frankfurt in 2015 and to [Lyon](/clubs/lyon-women) on penalties in 2017 in Cardiff, and had assembled a squad that included [Lindsey Heaps](/players/lindsey-horan), Laure Boulleau and Shirley Cruz.

The breakthrough came in 2020-21. Under Olivier Echouafni, with [Kadidiatou Diani](/players/kadidiatou-diani) and Marie-Antoinette Katoto scoring freely and Grace Geyoro running midfield, PSG ended Lyon's 14-year reign in France and reached a Champions League semi-final. It remains the only title. The years since have been a series of second places, Coupe de France wins in 2022 and 2024, and a reputation for self-sabotage: the 2021 Kheira Hamraoui affair, in which a player was assaulted and a team-mate arrested and later cleared, poisoned the dressing room for years, and the 2023 departures of Diani to Lyon and Katoto's later move there drained the attack.

A third-place finish in 2025-26 behind Lyon and Paris FC marked a low, but it was also year one of a stated youth project under Paulo César. Romée Leuchter, the Dutch striker signed from Ajax, won the Golden Boot with 18 goals; Mary Earps, the England goalkeeper signed from Manchester United in 2024, retired from international football but remained the club's number one.

## Trajectory

Ten consecutive seasons in the top three, nine of them finishing second or third behind Lyon, with the single title of 2021. In Europe PSG have reached four semi-finals and two finals but never won. Home games are split between the 1,100-seat Campus PSG in Poissy and the Parc des Princes for big occasions.

## Players the club produced

PSG's academy produced Geyoro, Sandy Baltimore (now at Chelsea), Oriane Jean-François and Katoto, who scored over 100 goals for the club before her move to Lyon. Diani, signed from Paris FC, became the best winger in France here. The club has also been a destination for international stars: Heaps, Nadia Nadim, Sara Däbritz, Ramona Bachmann and Earps.

## Identity and playing style

Echouafni's champions were a direct, [counter-attacking](/tactics/counter-attacking) side using Diani's pace. César's project is more possession-based, a 4-3-3 with Geyoro and Jackie Groenen controlling the middle, Sakina Karchaoui overlapping from left-back, Leuchter as the focal point and young academy wingers rotating. Griedge Mbock, the France captain, leads the defence. The gap to Lyon remains large, and the club's willingness to invest at Lyon's level is the open question.

## Rivalries

Lyon is the rivalry that defines PSG, a fixture that has decided titles and produced some of the biggest crowds in French women's football. Paris FC, who finished above PSG in 2025-26, have become a city rival.

## Current situation

As of November 2026 PSG are in the second year of César's rebuild, in the Première Ligue top three and competing in the Champions League qualifying rounds after missing out on direct entry. Leuchter, Geyoro, Karchaoui and Mbock give the side an international-class spine; whether the owners back it to reclaim the title is the question for 2027.
