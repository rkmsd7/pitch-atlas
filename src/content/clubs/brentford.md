## History

Brentford were founded in 1889 by members of a rowing club looking for a winter sport, and the bee nickname came from a supporter's misheard chant of "buck up, Bs". They moved into Griffin Park in 1904, a ground with a pub on every corner, and joined the Football League in 1920. The 1930s were the golden age: the Second Division title in 1935 and three top-six finishes in the First Division, including fifth in 1936, with Jack Holliday scoring the goals. Relegation in 1947 began 74 years outside the top flight.

The intervening decades were spent in the third and fourth tiers, with occasional escape attempts: the 1985 Freight Rover Trophy final, Steve Perryman's promotion in 1992, Ron Noades's player-chairman era and the 1999 Second Division title, a 2002 play-off final defeat to Stoke, and relegation to League Two in 2007 after a financial collapse that nearly closed the club. Supporters' trust Bees United took control and, in 2012, sold a majority to Matthew Benham, a lifelong supporter who had made his fortune in sports betting.

Benham's Brentford became the most discussed experiment in English football. He closed the academy in 2016 and replaced it with a B team, used statistical models to find undervalued players in Scandinavia and the lower leagues, and sold them at a profit: Andre Gray, Scott Hogan, Neal Maupay, [Ollie Watkins](/players/ollie-watkins), Saïd Benrahma, Ivan Toney. Mark Warburton won promotion from League One in 2014; Thomas Frank, promoted from assistant in 2018, lost the 2020 play-off final to Fulham and won the 2021 one against Swansea, taking Brentford into the top flight for the first time since 1947. The club moved to the 17,250-seat Gtech Community Stadium in 2020. Frank left for Tottenham in June 2025 and [Keith Andrews](/managers/keith-andrews), the club's set-piece coach, was promoted to head coach.

## Trajectory

The arc is 25 years from the third tier to the Premier League's upper mid-table. The 2007 relegation to League Two was the floor; the 2009 League Two title, the 2014 promotion and the 2021 play-off win the steps up. In the Premier League Brentford have finished 13th, ninth, 16th, 10th and ninth, never in a relegation fight after the first autumn. The 2025-26 finish, ninth on 53 points, missed the Conference League on goal difference and the Europa League by a single point after Andrews had lost eight of his first 15 games and then turned the season around. Igor Thiago's 22 league goals were second only to the Golden Boot winner.

## Players the club produced

Brentford's B-team model has produced Mads Roerslev, Marcus Forss, Yehor Yarmoliuk, who is now a first-team regular, and Keane Lewis-Potter, though most of the club's modern heroes were bought. Ivan Toney, signed from Peterborough for £5m in 2020, scored the goals that took Brentford up and became an England striker before leaving for Al-Ahli in 2024. Bryan Mbeumo, from Troyes for £5.8m in 2019, scored 20 league goals in 2024-25 and was sold to Manchester United for £65m. Pontus Jansson, Christian Nørgaard, the captain until his 2025 move to Arsenal, David Raya, sold to Arsenal and now the champions' goalkeeper, and Kevin Schade are the others. Jim Towers, with 153 goals in the 1950s, remains the record scorer.

## Identity and playing style

[Keith Andrews](/managers/keith-andrews), a former Republic of Ireland midfielder, was Brentford's set-piece coach before his promotion, and his team's identity reflects that: [set pieces](/tactics/set-pieces) remain the club's edge, with long throws, choreographed corners and Nathan Collins and Kristoffer Ajer as targets. The open-play shape is a 4-2-3-1 or 3-5-2 that defends in a mid-block and attacks on the counter through Schade's pace, Mikkel Damsgaard's creativity and Thiago's finishing, with Caoimhín Kelleher, signed from Liverpool in 2025, as a goalkeeper who starts attacks.

Mamadou Sangaré, signed from Lens for £39m in August 2026, is the club's record signing and the new holding midfielder; Jaidon Anthony (from Burnley), El Hadji Malick Diouf (from West Ham), Callum Wilson and Jannik Schuster were the other summer arrivals. Director of football Phil Giles said the club kept the core of Andrews' group and added five first-team signings. Andrews signed a six-year contract extension in early 2026.

## Rivalries

[Fulham](/clubs/fulham), four miles east along the Thames, are the local rivalry, and [Queens Park Rangers](/clubs/queens-park-rangers), the other west London club of similar size, the older one. [Chelsea](/clubs/chelsea) are the big neighbours, and Brentford's 4-1 at Stamford Bridge in April 2022 remains a favourite result.

## Current situation

Brentford are seventh in early November 2026, with three wins, five draws and one defeat from nine: 16 goals scored, nine conceded, 14 points. The autumn results include a 2-1 home defeat to Liverpool on 17 October, a 1-1 draw at Hull on 25 October and a 2-0 home win over Nottingham Forest on 31 October. The only defeat so far is the Liverpool game.

The ambition is the comfortable mid-table of the Frank era with a cup run and, if the form holds, a first European qualification. With three promoted clubs in the division, Brentford's floor looks secure; the question is whether Thiago, Damsgaard and the set-piece machine can take them higher.
