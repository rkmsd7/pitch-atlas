## History

Athletic Club were founded in 1898 by Basque students returning from Britain and British shipyard workers in Bilbao, which is why the name is spelled the English way and the stripes, adopted in 1910, mirror Southampton's. The club won the first Copa del Rey editions with a ferocity that became its identity, and the striker Rafael Moreno "Pichichi" gave his name to the league's top-scorer award. Athletic were founders of [La Liga](/leagues/la-liga) in 1929 and, with four titles between 1930 and 1936 and four Copas in a row, the dominant Spanish club of the pre-war era.

Telmo Zarra, the greatest goalscorer in Spanish league history until Messi, led the post-war side to the 1943 title and a cluster of cups. The 1950s "once de aldeanos" (the eleven villagers) won the 1956 league and three Copas with a team drawn entirely from the Basque provinces, and goalkeeper José Ángel Iribar, 614 matches between 1962 and 1980, bridged the generations to the Javier Clemente era: back-to-back titles in 1983 and 1984, the latter a double, with Andoni Goikoetxea, Dani and Manu Sarabia.

What makes the club unique is the cantera policy: since 1912 Athletic have fielded only players born or trained in the Basque Country. The policy has never been written into the statutes and has never been broken, and the club has never been relegated, a record it shares only with Real Madrid and Barcelona. The post-1984 decades had lows (17th in 2006-07, saved on the last day) and highs: Marcelo Bielsa's 2011-12 side that beat Manchester United at Old Trafford and reached the Europa League and Copa finals, Ernesto Valverde's two spells, and the 2024 Copa del Rey, won on penalties against Mallorca after a 40-year wait, celebrated by a million people along the Nervión river on the gabarra barge.

## Trajectory

Athletic have spent the twenty-first century oscillating between European places and mid-table. The Valverde side of 2013-14 finished fourth and reached the Champions League for the first time since 1998; the 2015-16 team won the Supercopa against Barcelona and finished fifth. The nadir was 2017-18, 16th, after which a rebuild under Gaizka Garitano, Marcelino (who won the 2021 Supercopa against Real Madrid and Barcelona) and Valverde produced the modern team.

Valverde's third spell was the most consistent since Clemente: fifth in 2023-24 with the Copa, fourth in 2024-25 with a Europa League semi-final, and the Champions League league phase in 2025-26. That season turned difficult: 12th, 45 points, the worst league finish since 2018, and no European place for 2026-27. Valverde left and the club made the boldest appointment in its modern history, hiring the Dortmund coach [Edin Terzić](/managers/edin-terzic) on a contract to 2028.

## Players the club produced

Every Athletic player is, by definition, a product of the Basque system. Pichichi and Zarra are the historic names; Iribar, Goikoetxea, Julen Guerrero, Joseba Etxeberria, Fernando Llorente, Javi Martínez and Ander Herrera carried the club through the modern era; Aritz Aduriz scored 172 goals and won the 2016 Puskás Award at 36. Iker Muniain captained the 2024 cup winners.

The current generation is the most gifted in a generation. [Nico Williams](/players/nico-williams), born in Pamplona to Ghanaian parents, was Euro 2024's breakout star and a World Cup winner with [Spain](/nations/spain) in 2026, having turned down Barcelona in 2024 and 2025 to extend his contract to 2035. His brother Iñaki holds the record for consecutive league appearances (251) and captains Ghana. Unai Simón won the 2026 World Cup Golden Glove with seven clean sheets, Dani Vivian and Oihan Sancet were in the squad, and Aymeric Laporte, who returned from Saudi Arabia in 2025, anchors the defence.

## Identity and playing style

Athletic's identity is intensity: a vertical game, crosses into a big striker, physical duels and the roar of San Mamés, "La Catedral", rebuilt in 2013. Clemente's teams were hard and direct; Bielsa's were a laboratory for man-marking and relentless [Gegenpressing](/tactics/gegenpressing); Valverde's balanced a 4-2-3-1 built on Sancet's late runs and the Williams brothers' pace in [counter-attacking](/tactics/counter-attacking) transition.

Terzić has brought the Dortmund template: a 4-2-3-1 with a high press, quick wide combinations, Beñat Prados and Mikel Jauregizar as the pivots, and Sancet as the playmaker. The early months have been uneven. The first competitive game was a 1-3 home defeat to Sevilla in August, Nico Williams was eased back after a summer with no pre-season, and after ten league games the team sits seventh with 15 points.

The Copa del Rey is the trophy that fits the club. Athletic's 24 titles are second only to Barcelona's, and the finals are the markers of the club's history: 1984 against Barcelona and the brawl that followed Maradona's challenge, the lost finals of 2009, 2012, 2015, 2020 and 2021 that made the 2024 win feel like a release. The gabarra, the barge on which the 1983 and 1984 teams paraded along the river, had been moored in a museum for 40 years; its return in April 2024 was watched by a crowd the police estimated at a million.

## Rivalries

The Basque derby against [Real Sociedad](/clubs/real-sociedad) is the fixture that matters most, a rivalry between Bilbao and San Sebastián played with more mutual respect than most, and the one that produces the biggest atmosphere at San Mamés. Historic matches with [Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid) (the two have met more than any other pair in Spain) and [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona) (the 1984 Copa final brawl, the 2015 Supercopa 4-0, the 2021 Supercopa win) are the big-club rivalries. [Osasuna](/clubs/osasuna) and [Alavés](/clubs/alaves) are the other Basque and Navarrese derbies.

## Current situation

As of 7 November 2026 Athletic are seventh in La Liga with 15 points from ten games (four wins, three draws, three defeats), with a game in hand. Without European football for the first time since 2022-23, the season's targets are a return to Europe and another Copa run; the club has not won the league since 1984 and the Copa remains the realistic prize. Before the November international break the fixture list runs through Rayo, Valencia, Levante, Getafe, the Basque derby against Real Sociedad and the trip to Osasuna, with Nico Williams and Yuri Berchiche back in full training on 5 November after load management.

The squad is unchanged in character: Simón, Vivian, Laporte, Yeray and Jesús Areso in defence; Ruiz de Galarreta, Prados, Jauregizar and Sancet in midfield; the Williams brothers, Gorka Guruzeta, Álex Berenguer and Maroan Sannadi in attack. Terzić's first autumn has raised some doubts in the local press, but the club's board has historically given its coaches time, and the Basque-only model, as always, is non-negotiable.
