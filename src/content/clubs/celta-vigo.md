## History

Real Club Celta de Vigo were formed on 23 August 1923 by the merger of Vigo's two clubs, Real Vigo Sporting and Real Fortuna, with the explicit aim of creating a Galician side strong enough to compete nationally. The name comes from the Celtic heritage of the region. Celta reached the top flight in 1936 and spent the post-war decades moving between divisions, finishing fourth in 1948 and reaching Copa finals in 1948 and 1994, both lost.

The club's golden age was "EuroCelta", from 1998 to 2003. Under Javier Irureta and then Víctor Fernández, a team of Alexander Mostovoi, Valeri Karpin, Haim Revivo, Michel Salgado, Claude Makélélé, Gustavo López and Jesuli played the best football in the club's history, won the 2000 Intertoto Cup, reached UEFA Cup quarter-finals in 1999, 2000 and 2001, lost the 2001 Copa del Rey final to Zaragoza, and finished fourth in 2002-03 to reach the Champions League for the first time. That campaign, in 2003-04, brought a famous group-stage draw at the San Siro and a last-16 exit to Arsenal, but the league season collapsed: Celta were relegated in 19th while still in Europe.

The years since have been a cycle. Promotion in 2005 and a sixth-place finish in 2006 was followed by relegation in 2007 and five seasons in Segunda, some close to financial ruin, before Paco Herrera's 2012 promotion. Luis Enrique's single season (2013-14) and Eduardo Berizzo's three (2014-17) brought sixth place in 2016 and the 2017 Europa League semi-final, lost 2-1 on aggregate to Manchester United. Two narrow escapes in 2019 and 2020 followed before the club rebuilt around its academy.

## Trajectory

Celta have been in [La Liga](/leagues/la-liga) continuously since 2012-13, their longest spell in the top flight. The low point was two consecutive 17th-place finishes in 2018-19 and 2019-20; the recovery began with Rafa Benítez's difficult 2023-24 and accelerated when Claudio Giráldez, the B-team coach with no previous first-team experience, was promoted in March 2024. Giráldez's first full season ended seventh with a Europa League place, the club's first European qualification in eight years, and 2025-26 was better still: sixth with 54 points and a second consecutive Europa League campaign. The coach signed a new contract in April 2026.

## Players the club produced

A Madroa, the academy, has become Celta's greatest asset. Iago Aspas, born in Moaña across the bay, came through in 2008, left for Liverpool and Sevilla, and returned in 2015 to become the club's all-time top scorer, a Spain international and the symbol of modern Celta; he renewed in May 2026 for one final season, with a role at the club to follow. Hugo Mallo captained for a decade, Santi Mina, Brais Méndez, Gabri Veiga (sold to Al-Ahli for €40m in 2023), Hugo Álvarez, Fer López and Pablo Durán are recent graduates, and Ilaix Moriba, back from Leipzig, is a Vigo-raised product of Barcelona's academy.

Signed stars of the EuroCelta years, Mostovoi, Karpin and Revivo, remain the fans' favourites, alongside Nolito, whose 2013-16 spell under Luis Enrique and Berizzo is remembered with particular warmth.

## Identity and playing style

Celta's identity, from EuroCelta through Berizzo to Giráldez, is possession football with a Galician temperament: patient build-up, wingers who come inside and a playmaker who dictates. Giráldez's team plays a 3-4-3 or 4-3-3 that builds from the goalkeeper, uses the wing-backs Javi Galán and Sergio Carreira for width and relies on the intelligence of Aspas, Borja Iglesias and Moriba between the lines. The style is close to [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) with a counter-pressing edge, and the club now invests little in the transfer market, preferring academy promotion.

## Rivalries

The Galician derby with [Deportivo La Coruña](/clubs/deportivo-la-coruna) is the one that matters, a rivalry between Vigo's industrial port and A Coruña's administrative capital, back in La Liga in 2026-27 for the first time since 2018; the first league meeting is at Balaídos in January 2027. Sporting Gijón are a northern rival from the Segunda years, and the 2001 Copa final gave Zaragoza a place in the club's memory.

## Current situation

Celta are 11th in La Liga on 7 November 2026 with 12 points from 11 games (two wins, six draws, three defeats), with the tightest defence outside the top three (ten goals conceded) but only ten scored. Giráldez is balancing a Europa League league phase with a squad that lost Borja Iglesias's partner-in-crime Alfon and the striker Jørgen Strand Larsen in recent windows and has added Borja Iglesias, Moriba, the Manchester United goalkeeper Altay Bayındır and the veteran Marcos Alonso. Aspas, 39, is used selectively but remains the dressing-room's centre.

The season's aim is a third consecutive European place and a fitting farewell for Aspas. Balaídos, being rebuilt stand by stand, has rarely been fuller.
