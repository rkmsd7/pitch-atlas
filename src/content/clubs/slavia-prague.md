## History

Slavia were founded in 1892 by students of Prague's Czech university as a literary and debating society that soon took up football, and the club has carried an intellectual, bourgeois reputation ever since, in contrast to working-class Sparta across the river. The red-and-white halved shirt with the red star dates from the earliest years, and the club's pre-war era was one of the strongest in Europe: Slavia won the Mitropa Cup in 1938, the forerunner of the European Cup, and Josef Bican, the Austrian-Czech striker who joined in 1937, scored more than 500 goals for the club and is counted among the most prolific players in history. František Plánička, the goalkeeper of the 1934 World Cup final, was Slavia's captain.

The Communist era was a punishment. The regime favoured Sparta and the army and police clubs, Slavia were renamed Dynamo for a time and did not win a championship between 1947 and 1996. The post-1989 revival produced the 1996 title under František Cipro, with Karel Poborský, Patrik Berger, Vladimír Šmicer and Radek Bejbl all starting at Euro 96 for the Czech side that reached the final, and a UEFA Cup semi-final the same year, lost to Bordeaux.

Titles in 2008 and 2009 under Karel Jarolím came as the new Eden stadium opened, but the club's finances collapsed afterwards, with relegation narrowly avoided in 2011 and 2014. Chinese investment from CEFC and later CITIC from 2015 brought stability and ambition, and the appointment of Jindřich Trpišovský in December 2017 transformed the club. Trpišovský won the 2018 cup, the 2019 double, the 2020 and 2021 titles and the 2023 cup, took Slavia to Europa League quarter-finals in 2019 (losing to Chelsea after beating Sevilla) and 2021 (beating Leicester and Rangers before losing to Arsenal), and in 2024-25 went through the league season unbeaten to win his fifth championship.

## Trajectory

Slavia's eight Czech titles since 1993 place them second to Sparta's twelve, but the balance has swung firmly to Eden in the Trpišovský era: five championships between 2017 and 2025 against Sparta's two. The lean years are visible in the table, with thirteenth-place finishes in 2011 and 2014 and five seasons outside the top five, before the recovery from 2016. In Europe the two Europa League quarter-finals and Champions League group and league-phase campaigns in 2019-20 and 2025-26 are the modern peaks. Slavia are in the 2026-27 Champions League league phase, where they have one point from four matches as of November 2026.

## Players the club produced

Slavia's Euro 96 generation of Poborský, Berger, Šmicer, Bejbl and Jan Suchopárek all played for the club, and the modern era has produced or launched Tomáš Souček and Vladimír Coufal, sold to West Ham in 2020; Alex Král (to Spartak Moscow); Abdallah Sima (to Brighton); Peter Olayinka; Nicolae Stanciu; Ondřej Kolář; and Lukáš Provod, the current captain. Milan Škoda was the club's talismanic striker of the Trpišovský revival, and the club's academy continues to supply the Czech national team.

## Identity and playing style

Slavia under Trpišovský play the most intense pressing football in Central Europe: a 4-2-3-1 or 3-4-3 that presses high, defends with a [high line](/tactics/high-line), uses [gegenpressing](/tactics/gegenpressing) to win the ball in the opponent's half and attacks with speed through the wide players. The club's recruitment focuses on physical, athletic players from the Czech league, Africa and the Balkans, and Trpišovský, now in his ninth year, has become the longest-serving coach at any major European club outside the biggest leagues. The club's self-image is of the thinking fan's club, with a strong association with Czech cultural and academic life.

## Rivalries

The Prague derby against Sparta is the only fixture that truly matters, red-and-white against maroon, the oldest rivalry in Czech football and one with a political edge from the Communist decades. Matches against Viktoria Plzeň, the third force of the last 15 years, decide many title races.

## Current situation

As of November 2026 Slavia are in the Champions League league phase with one point from four matches, near the bottom of the table and facing an uphill battle to reach the play-off round. Domestically the aim is to retain the title won unbeaten in 2025 and extend the club's hold over Sparta; the squad is built around Provod, the striker Tomáš Chorý and the goalkeeper Jindřich Staněk, with Trpišovský's contract and succession the club's longer-term question.
