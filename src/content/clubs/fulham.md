## History

Fulham were founded in 1879 as Fulham St Andrew's Church Sunday School FC, which makes them London's oldest professional club, and they have played at Craven Cottage, on the north bank of the Thames by Bishop's Park, since 1896. The ground's Johnny Haynes Stand, designed by Archibald Leitch in 1905, and the cottage in the corner are both listed buildings. The club joined the Football League in 1907 and spent most of the century in the second tier, with two spells in the First Division: 1949 to 1952 and 1959 to 1968.

Johnny Haynes, the first £100-a-week footballer, who stayed at Fulham for 18 years and 658 games when he could have gone anywhere, is the club's greatest player; Bobby Robson and George Cohen, the 1966 World Cup winner, played alongside him. The 1975 FA Cup final, reached as a Second Division side with Bobby Moore and Alan Mullery, was lost 2-0 to West Ham. By the mid-1990s Fulham were in the fourth tier, and in 1996 they finished 17th in the Third Division, their lowest ever.

Mohamed Al-Fayed, the owner of Harrods, bought the club in 1997 and promised the Premier League in five years. Kevin Keegan won the Second Division in 1999 with 101 points; Jean Tigana's team of Louis Saha, Luís Boa Morte and Steed Malbranque won the First Division in 2001 with 101 points too, and Fulham were in the top flight for the first time in 33 years. They stayed 13 seasons. Roy Hodgson's side, built around Brede Hangeland, Danny Murphy, Bobby Zamora, Clint Dempsey and Zoltán Gera, reached the 2010 Europa League final in Hamburg after beating Juventus 4-1 at the Cottage from 3-1 down on aggregate, losing to Atlético Madrid in extra time.

Shahid Khan bought the club in 2013; relegation followed in 2014. Slaviša Jokanović's promotion in 2018, relegation in 2019, Scott Parker's promotion in 2020 and relegation in 2021 made Fulham the archetypal yo-yo club. Marco Silva, appointed in 2021, won the Championship with 90 points and Aleksandar Mitrović's record 43 goals, then kept Fulham up for four seasons with finishes of tenth, 13th, 11th and 11th. He left for Benfica in July 2026, and [Álvaro Arbeloa](/managers/alvaro-arbeloa), the former Real Madrid full-back who had coached Castilla and then the first team in 2025-26, was appointed on a contract to 2029.

## Trajectory

Fulham's 25 years are Al-Fayed's 13-season stay in the top flight (never higher than seventh, in 2009, nor lower than 17th), three promotions and three relegations between 2014 and 2021, and the Silva stability since 2022. The Riverside Stand, which has been under construction and partial opening since 2019, is slowly raising the capacity towards 30,000 and the club's revenue with it.

## Players the club produced

Fulham's academy produced Johnny Haynes, Alan Mullery, who left for Tottenham and came back, Tony Gale, Sean Davis, Zat Knight, Ryan Sessegnon, who returned in 2024 after a spell at Tottenham, Harvey Elliott, who left for Liverpool at 16, and the current midfielder Josh King. The signings who became legends include Gordon Davies, the record scorer with 178 goals, Louis Saha, Brian McBride, Clint Dempsey, who became the first American to score a Premier League hat-trick, Danny Murphy, Mark Schwarzer, Aleksandar Mitrović, Tom Cairney, captain since 2017, and Antonee Robinson, the United States left-back who has been the club's most valuable player of the Silva years.

## Identity and playing style

[Álvaro Arbeloa](/managers/alvaro-arbeloa) coached Real Madrid's youth teams and Castilla before stepping up to the first team when Xabi Alonso left mid-way through 2025-26. His football is a compact 4-3-3 or 4-2-3-1 with an emphasis on pressing from the front, quick wide combinations and an organised defensive block, closer to Silva's transition-based style than to the possession-heavy approach of his old club. He brought two Real Madrid youngsters with him: Gonzalo García, the striker who scored four goals at the 2025 Club World Cup, and the midfielder César Palacios, both on five-year contracts.

With Harry Wilson and Raúl Jiménez gone (they had been involved in 57 per cent of the goals in 2025-26), the attack relies on Rodrigo Muniz, García, Alex Iwobi, Emile Smith Rowe and Oscar Bobb, signed from Manchester City. Bernd Leno remains the goalkeeper, Joachim Andersen and Calvin Bassey the centre-backs, Robinson and Kenny Tete the full-backs, with Sander Berge, Hugo Larsson and Shea Charles in midfield.

## Rivalries

[Chelsea](/clubs/chelsea) are the West London derby, two miles along the Fulham Road, though the gap in resources has made it a lopsided affair since 2003. [Queens Park Rangers](/clubs/queens-park-rangers) are the older rivalry of the Championship years, and [Brentford](/clubs/brentford), five miles west along the river, the newest, with the two clubs meeting in the 2020 play-off final.

## Current situation

Fulham are 15th in early November 2026, with two wins, three draws and four defeats from nine: nine goals scored and 11 conceded, nine points. October brought a 1-0 home win over Hull on 17 October, a 1-0 win at Coventry on 24 October and a 1-1 draw at Aston Villa on 31 October, which steadied a season that had begun with a home defeat to Chelsea on the opening day.

The ambition under Arbeloa is to build on Silva's five seasons and push for the top half, with the Riverside Stand finally generating the revenue to compete. Early-season goals have been scarce, and the January window is expected to add a wide forward.
