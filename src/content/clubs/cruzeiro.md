## History

Cruzeiro was founded in 1921 as Palestra Itália de Belo Horizonte by the city's Italian community and, like its São Paulo namesake, was forced to change its name during the Second World War; it chose the Southern Cross, Cruzeiro do Sul, and the celestial blue of the Brazilian flag's stars. Its great era was the 1960s, when the Mineirão opened in 1965 and the team of Tostão, Dirceu Lopes, Piazza, Natal and Raul Plassmann won the 1966 Taça Brasil, beating Pelé's Santos 6-2 and 3-2 in the final, and became the first side to dominate from outside Rio and São Paulo.

The 1976 Copa Libertadores, won against River Plate in a play-off in Santiago with Nelinho and Palhinha, was the first continental title for a club from Minas Gerais, and the 1990s brought the Supercopa Libertadores of 1991 and 1992, a 17-year-old [Ronaldo](/players/ronaldo-nazario) scoring 44 goals in 47 games in 1993-94 before his sale to PSV, and the second Libertadores in 1997, won against Sporting Cristal with the goalkeeper Dida and the Argentine Juan Pablo Sorín. Cruzeiro's six Copa do Brasil titles are a record, and the 2003 season under Vanderlei Luxemburgo, with Alex as the playmaker and 100 points from 46 games, was the first treble of league, cup and state championship in Brazilian history.

Marcelo Oliveira's side won back-to-back Brasileirão titles in 2013 and 2014 with Everton Ribeiro and Ricardo Goulart, and Mano Menezes added Copa do Brasil wins in 2017 and 2018, but the debts behind those teams were ruinous.

## Trajectory

In 2019 the club's finances, mismanaged by its directors to the point of criminal investigation, caught up with it. Cruzeiro were relegated for the first time, the final home match against Palmeiras ending in a riot, and the club spent three seasons in Série B. In December 2021 Ronaldo, the club's most famous former player, bought 90 per cent of the newly created SAF company for a nominal sum plus the assumption of debts, and under Paulo Pezzolano the club won Série B in 2022 with five rounds to spare. Ronaldo sold to the supermarket magnate Pedro Lourenço in 2024.

Fernando Diniz's brief 2024 spell produced a Copa Sudamericana final, lost to Racing Club in Asunción, and in 2025 Leonardo Jardim, the former Monaco coach, built the club's best team in a decade, with Kaio Jorge and Matheus Pereira, finishing fourth. Jardim left for [Flamengo](/clubs/flamengo) in 2026; the club's current squad data lists Artur Jorge, the Portuguese who won the 2024 double with Botafogo, as head coach as of November 2026. Cruzeiro are third in the 2026 Brasileirão with 60 points from 34 games.

## Players the club produced

The Toca da Raposa academy produced Tostão's generation, and later Dida, Ronaldo (he arrived at 16 from São Cristóvão and was made in Belo Horizonte), Alex, Fábio (the goalkeeper, 976 games), Maicon, Luisão, Lucas Silva (to Real Madrid) and, in the 2020s, the midfielder Vitor Roque's contemporaries in the Minas youth leagues. The Bolivian striker Marcelo Moreno and the Argentine Sorín were the most loved imports.

## Identity and playing style

Cruzeiro's identity, "Cabuloso", is of a club with an edge, and its best teams have combined a strong defensive base with an elite number ten: Dirceu Lopes, Alex, Everton Ribeiro, Matheus Pereira. Jardim's 2025 side played a European-style 4-2-3-1 with high pressing and quick wide play, and Artur Jorge has kept a similar structure with more emphasis on set pieces and a [low block](/tactics/low-block) away from home. Fabrício Bruno anchors the defence, Cássio, the former Corinthians goalkeeper, is in goal, and Gabigol, signed from Flamengo in 2025, has been the finisher alongside Kaio Jorge.

## Rivalries

The Clássico Mineiro against [Atlético Mineiro](/clubs/atletico-mineiro) is one of the most violent and passionate derbies in Brazil, two clubs that share the Mineirão and now play in separate stadiums since Atlético opened the Arena MRV in 2023. The 2014 Copa do Brasil final and Atlético's 2013 Libertadores win over Cruzeiro's absence are sore points. Games with [Flamengo](/clubs/flamengo) and [Palmeiras](/clubs/palmeiras) have decided recent cup ties.

## Current situation

As of November 2026 Cruzeiro are third in the Brasileirão with four games left, certain of a Libertadores place and still mathematically in the title race, with Artur Jorge listed as head coach and Kaio Jorge, Matheus Pereira, Gabigol, Fabrício Bruno and Cássio as the core. The SAF model has stabilised the club's finances and the aim for 2027 is a first continental final since 2009.
