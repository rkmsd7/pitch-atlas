## History

Real Racing Club de Santander were founded on 23 February 1913 in the Cantabrian capital and were one of the ten founder members of [La Liga](/leagues/la-liga) in 1929, finishing second in 1930-31 with a team led by the great goalkeeper Óscar Álvarez. The 1930s were their peak until modern times, and the half-century after the Civil War was spent largely in Segunda with occasional top-flight spells, including a run from 1950 to 1960 and promotions in 1973 and 1977.

The modern Racing were a stable top-flight club from 1993 to 2012, 19 consecutive seasons that included the emergence of Pedro Munitis, Iván de la Peña's loan, Nikola Žigić's goals and, under Marcelino García Toral, a sixth-place finish in 2007-08 that brought the club's only European campaign, a UEFA Cup first-round exit to Manchester City. [Sergio Canales](/players/sergio-canales), a Santander boy, broke through in 2009 and was sold to Real Madrid at 19.

Then came the collapse. Ahsan Ali Syed, an Indian businessman based in Bahrain, bought the club in 2011 with promises of investment that never arrived; Racing were relegated in 2012, again in 2013 to Segunda B, and in January 2014, with players unpaid for months, the team refused to play a Copa del Rey quarter-final second leg against Real Sociedad in protest, standing still at kick-off. The years in the third tier (2013-14, 2015-19, 2020-22) were punctuated by promotions in 2014 and 2019 and an immediate relegation in 2020. The 2022 Primera RFEF title under Guillermo Fernández Romo began the climb that José Alberto López, appointed in December 2022, completed: fifth and a play-off defeat in 2024-25, then the Segunda title in 2025-26 with 82 points and 90 goals.

## Trajectory

Racing's 2026-27 season is their first in the top flight since 2011-12, a 14-year gap. The promotion was confirmed on 16 May 2026 and the title on the final day, with Deportivo second. The club, now owned by the Sebastián Ceria family's group after the chaos of the 2010s, moved quickly to strengthen for La Liga: Canales, 35, returned from Monterrey on a contract to 2028 as the first signing of the summer, and the Athletic goalkeeper Julen Agirrezabala followed in August.

## Players the club produced

Racing's academy has produced more than its size suggests: Munitis, who returned to captain the club, Canales, Quique Setién (a one-club midfielder of the 1970s and 1980s who later coached Barcelona), Iván Helguera, Diego Mariño, Íñigo Vicente's generation and the current captain Íñigo Sainz-Maza. Andrés Martín, Vicente and Asier Villalibre were the attacking core of the promotion season, and the Moroccan striker Yassir Zabiri and the Georgian Giorgi Guliashvili add depth.

## Identity and playing style

José Alberto's Racing are an attacking, high-tempo side that won Segunda by outscoring everyone, with a 4-2-3-1 built on Vicente's creativity, Andrés Martín's running from the left and full-backs who join the attack. The flip side is a defence that conceded 61 goals in Segunda and has been exposed in the top flight: 29 goals conceded in 11 La Liga games, the worst record in the division, including a 3-0 home defeat to Real Madrid on 1 November in which Mbappé, Bellingham and Yan Diomande scored by half-time. Canales, used as a deeper playmaker, is the team's quality and its tempo.

## Rivalries

The Cantabrian club's rivalries are with the Asturian pair, Sporting Gijón and Real Oviedo, the derbies of the north coast that were fierce in Segunda and lapse when divisions separate the clubs. [Athletic Bilbao](/clubs/athletic-bilbao), the nearest big club, are the top-flight rival Racing fans care about most, and the 2014 Copa protest left a strange bond with [Real Sociedad](/clubs/real-sociedad).

## Current situation

Racing are 16th on 7 November 2026 with 11 points from 11 games (three wins, two draws, six defeats), level with Osasuna and Espanyol and a point above the relegation zone, with goals flowing at both ends (15 for, 29 against). The visit to bottom club Málaga on 7 November is a direct survival fixture. José Alberto, under some pressure after the autumn's defensive problems, remains in charge; the club's September health scare, a vascular complication after minor surgery, kept him out briefly.

El Sardinero, full for the first time in a generation, has made the return a celebration regardless of results. Staying up would make it the club's best season since Marcelino.
