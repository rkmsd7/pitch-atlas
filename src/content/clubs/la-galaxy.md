## History

The Galaxy were one of the ten founding clubs when MLS kicked off in 1996, and from the start they were the league's marquee team: the Los Angeles market, the Rose Bowl as a first home, and a Salvadoran playmaker, Mauricio Cienfuegos, who gave the early years a Latin flavour. They reached the first MLS Cup final in 1996 and lost it, a pattern repeated in 1999 and 2001 before Sigi Schmid's side finally won the trophy in 2002, the year the club moved into its purpose-built stadium in Carson.

The second half of the 2000s belonged to Landon Donovan, who arrived in 2005 and became the club's greatest player, and then to [David Beckham](/players/david-beckham), whose 2007 signing changed MLS's relationship with the rest of the world. The Beckham years started chaotically (Ruud Gullit's short reign, Donovan's public frustration) but under Bruce Arena they became the league's dominant team: Supporters' Shields in 2010 and 2011 and back-to-back MLS Cups in 2011 and 2012, with Robbie Keane the signing that turned a good team into a great one. A fifth MLS Cup followed in 2014, Donovan's farewell.

The decline from 2016 was steep. Steven Gerrard's spell was underwhelming, 2017 produced the worst record in the league, and even [Zlatan Ibrahimović](/players/zlatan-ibrahimovic), who scored a 40-yard volley on his debut in 2018 and 52 goals in two seasons, could not drag the club back to a final. Javier "Chicharito" Hernández gave the 2021-23 period its goals but not its trophies.

## Trajectory

Greg Vanney, a 2002 and 2005 champion as a player, returned as head coach in 2021 and rebuilt the squad around Riqui Puig, the former [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona) midfielder signed in 2022. The reward came in 2024: second in the West, a play-off run that included a 6-2 demolition of Minnesota, and a 2-1 MLS Cup win over the New York Red Bulls in Carson on 7 December 2024, with Puig playing through a torn ACL suffered in the conference final. The sixth MLS Cup restored the club's status as the league's record champions.

What followed was the strangest season in the club's history. Without Puig for most of 2025 and with a thin, ageing squad, the champions went winless for the first 16 games and finished bottom of the Western Conference, missing the play-offs. Vanney survived. In 2026 the Galaxy recovered to seventh in the West with 44 points, enough for a best-of-three first-round series in the play-offs that are being played as of November 2026.

## Players the club produced

The Galaxy's academy is one of the most productive in the United States. Gyasi Zardes, Jose Villarreal, Efraín Álvarez, Julian Araujo (sold to Barcelona in 2023) and Jalen Neal all came through it. The club's signings matter more to its mythology: Cobi Jones, the all-time appearance leader; Cienfuegos and Carlos Ruiz from Central America; Donovan; [Beckham](/players/david-beckham); Keane; Gerrard; [Ibrahimović](/players/zlatan-ibrahimovic); Chicharito; and Marco Reus, who joined from [Borussia Dortmund](/clubs/borussia-dortmund) in 2024.

## Identity and playing style

Vanney's Galaxy play a patient possession game, closer to [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) than most MLS sides, with Puig as a roaming playmaker who takes the ball in every zone and the wingers Gabriel Pec and Joseph Paintsil attacking space on the outside. The 2024 champions were the most attacking team in the league; the 2025 version showed what happens to a possession team when the playmaker is missing and the defensive midfield has no legs. Historically the club's identity is simpler: star power. From Cienfuegos to Beckham to Zlatan, the Galaxy have always been the team that brings the famous name to Los Angeles.

## Rivalries

El Tráfico with [LAFC](/clubs/lafc), born in 2018 with a 4-3 Galaxy win sealed by Zlatan's debut volley, is now the biggest rivalry in MLS. The California Clásico with San Jose Earthquakes is older and was defined by the Donovan transfer in 2005. The 2024 MLS Cup final against the Red Bulls, and the Beckham link, give the games with [Inter Miami](/clubs/inter-miami) an extra edge.

## Current situation

As of November 2026 the Galaxy are seventh in the West and in the play-offs, with Vanney listed as head coach in the club's current squad data. Puig is back and fit, Pec and Paintsil remain the attacking outlets, and Reus is in the final year of his deal. The 2026 World Cup brought the eyes of the world to Los Angeles; the club's ambition is to turn the 2024 title and the new attention into a sustained return to the top of the West rather than another one-off.
