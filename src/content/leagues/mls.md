## Overview

Major League Soccer is the top division in the United States and Canada: 30 clubs (San Diego FC joined in 2025) split into Eastern and Western Conferences, each playing 34 regular-season matches from February to October. The best overall record wins the Supporters' Shield; the top seven in each conference qualify for the MLS Cup play-offs, with the eighth and ninth playing single-match wild cards, a best-of-three first round, then single-leg knockouts to MLS Cup in December. There is no relegation.

MLS is a single-entity league: the league owns player contracts, and a salary cap with Designated Player exceptions (the "Beckham rule") lets each club sign a few stars outside it. Apple TV holds global broadcast rights. The 2026 World Cup, co-hosted by the [United States](/nations/united-states), [Canada](/nations/canada) and [Mexico](/nations/mexico), was the league's biggest marketing moment since its founding.

## History and eras

MLS was created as a condition of the United States hosting the 1994 World Cup and kicked off in 1996 with ten teams; D.C. United won the first two titles. The league nearly folded in 2001 and contracted to ten clubs. David Beckham's arrival at the [LA Galaxy](/clubs/la-galaxy) in 2007 and the Designated Player rule began the growth era: Henry, Kaka, Pirlo, Lampard, Gerrard and Ibrahimovic followed. The Galaxy won five MLS Cups (the last in 2024), Seattle Sounders built the league's model fanbase, Atlanta United set attendance records from 2017, and LAFC won in 2022.

[Lionel Messi](/players/lionel-messi) joined [Inter Miami](/clubs/inter-miami) in July 2023 with Busquets, Alba and Suarez. Miami won the 2023 Leagues Cup, set the regular-season points record (74) to win the 2024 Supporters' Shield, and on 6 December 2025 won their first MLS Cup, 3-1 against Vancouver Whitecaps at Chase Stadium, Messi named MVP with two assists; Busquets and Alba retired after the match.

## Style and tactical trends

MLS has moved from a physical, transition-heavy league to a tactically varied one. The Red Bull group ([gegenpressing](/tactics/gegenpressing) at New York Red Bulls), Columbus Crew's [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) under Wilfried Nancy (champions 2023), Philadelphia Union's narrow 4-4-2 diamond and Inter Miami's Messi-centred 4-3-3 ([relationism](/tactics/relationism) in all but name on the left) all coexist. Travel distances, summer heat, altitude in Denver and Salt Lake and artificial pitches make the home-away split pronounced.

The academy system has improved rapidly; the United States' World Cup squad in 2026 was mostly Europe-based but increasingly MLS-trained. The league's young South Americans (Almada, Cuypers, Evander) are its best exports.

## Great clubs and dynasties

[LA Galaxy](/clubs/la-galaxy) have five MLS Cups and the Beckham era. D.C. United won four of the first nine. Seattle Sounders (two Cups, the 2022 CONCACAF Champions League, the first MLS winner) and Sporting Kansas City, Columbus Crew (three Cups), LAFC (2022) and [Inter Miami](/clubs/inter-miami) (2025) are the modern powers. Atlanta United (2018) drew 70,000 crowds in their first seasons.

## 2026-27 season storylines

- **Nashville's Shield.** Nashville SC won the 2026 Supporters' Shield, the club's first, clinching it with a 2-0 win over CF Montreal on 24 October and securing home advantage through the play-offs; they were chasing Inter Miami's 74-point record into the final weeks.
- **MLS Cup 2026.** The play-offs run through November with the final in mid-December (reported dates of 15 and 18 December differ; check the official schedule). Nashville's Geodis Park would host if they go all the way.
- **Messi at 39.** Inter Miami, the holders, are in the play-offs with Messi still the league's focal point; his contract and the club's new Miami Freedom Park stadium, opening in 2026, are the long-term stories.
- **World Cup legacy.** The 2026 World Cup final at MetLife Stadium on 19 July, Spain 1-0 Argentina after extra time, played out in MLS stadiums across the continent; Vancouver, Seattle, Atlanta, Kansas City and others hosted matches.
- **Expansion.** The 30-club league is debating a switch to the international calendar (autumn to spring) from 2027, which would align MLS with the transfer windows of Europe.
