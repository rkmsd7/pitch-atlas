## Overview

The Frauen-Bundesliga, officially the Google Pixel Frauen-Bundesliga under its current sponsor, is the top tier of German women's football. Founded in 1990 as a two-group competition and merged into a single division in 1997, it expanded from 12 to 14 teams for 2025-26. The top three go into the [Champions League](/tournaments/womens-champions-league) and the bottom two are relegated.

In 2025-26 [Bayern Munich](/clubs/bayern-munich-women) won their fourth straight title with 74 points, clinching it with a 3-2 win at Union Berlin on 22 April 2026 and finishing 16 clear of [Wolfsburg](/clubs/wolfsburg-women). Eintracht Frankfurt took the third Champions League place, Hoffenheim and Bayer Leverkusen were next, and Werder Bremen's Larissa Mühlhaus won the Golden Boot with 17 goals. Carl Zeiss Jena and SGS Essen were relegated.

## History and eras

The league's first giants were 1. FFC Frankfurt, who won seven titles and four European trophies around [Birgit Prinz](/players/birgit-prinz), and Turbine Potsdam, the East German powerhouse that won six Bundesligas and two Champions Leagues. Then came Wolfsburg: backed by Volkswagen, they won back-to-back Champions Leagues in 2013 and 2014, seven league titles between 2013 and 2022 and a record nine straight DFB-Pokals, with [Alexandra Popp](/players/alexandra-popp), [Ewa Pajor](/players/ewa-pajor) and [Caroline Graham Hansen](/players/caroline-graham-hansen) among the stars.

Bayern, who had won in 2015, 2016 and 2021, took over from 2023 under Alexander Straus, adding [Giulia Gwinn](/players/giulia-gwinn), Lena Oberdorf, Pernille Harder and Magdalena Eriksson, and the 2025-26 title under José Barcala came with a DFB-Pokal double. Frankfurt, now Eintracht, remain the best of the rest.

## Style and tactical trends

German football means intensity, and the women's league is no different: high pressing, physical duels and quick transitions, with Bayern increasingly adding [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) and Wolfsburg traditionally built on [set pieces](/tactics/set-pieces) and crossing. The national team's record of eight Euros and two World Cups comes from this league. Its challenge is retention: Popp's move to Borussia Dortmund, the men's giants' fast-rising women's team, in summer 2026 was a rare transfer within Germany, while Lea Schüller's departure to [Manchester United](/clubs/manchester-united-women) and Pajor's earlier move to [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona-women) show the pull of England and Spain.

## 2026-27 season storylines

- Bayern chasing a fifth straight title and a deeper Champions League run after losing the 2026 semi-final 4-2 to Barcelona.
- Wolfsburg under Stephan Lerch trying to end a four-year wait, with Svenja Huth captaining and Chelsea loanee Mara Alber adding quality.
- Dortmund's rise with Popp as captain, a story that could reshape the league's geography.
- Frankfurt, Hoffenheim and Leverkusen contesting the third Champions League place.
- Germany's qualification for the [2027 World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup), confirmed in March 2026, giving the league's internationals a clear target.
