## Overview

The Indian Super League is the top division of Indian men's football, recognised by the AIFF and the AFC as the country's premier league since 2019-20, when it replaced the I-League at the top of the pyramid. Fourteen clubs took part in 2025-26. The league was founded in 2013 as a franchise competition modelled on the IPL and run by Football Sports Development Limited (FSDL), a Reliance-IMG venture, under a Master Rights Agreement with the All India Football Federation (AIFF).

That agreement expired in December 2025 and its collapse plunged Indian football into crisis. The 2025-26 season was put on hold in July 2025, the Supreme Court directed the AIFF to run an open tender for a commercial partner and placed the federation under the National Sports Governance Act 2025, and the league only kicked off on 14 February 2026, five months late, as a shortened competition. In July 2026 the AIFF and the clubs agreed a club-led commercial model, with a special purpose vehicle owned by the clubs handling sponsorship and broadcasting and the AIFF retaining regulatory control, for at least the 2026-27 and 2027-28 seasons.

## History and eras

The ISL launched in October 2014 with eight franchises, marquee veterans (Del Piero, Pires, Trezeguet, Anelka) and celebrity owners, running for three months a year alongside the I-League. Atletico de Kolkata (ATK) won the first title and again in 2016 and 2019-20. Chennaiyin won in 2015 and 2017-18; [Bengaluru FC](/clubs/bengaluru-fc), the I-League's best club, joined in 2017 and won in 2018-19 under Albert Roca with Sunil Chhetri. [Mumbai City](/clubs/mumbai-city), part of the City Football Group since 2019, won the 2020-21 and 2023-24 ISL Cups and two League Shields.

The merger of ATK with Kolkata's historic [Mohun Bagan](/clubs/mohun-bagan) in 2020 created ATK Mohun Bagan, later Mohun Bagan Super Giant, who won the ISL Cup in 2022-23 and 2024-25 and the Shield in 2023-24 and 2024-25. East Bengal, their century-old rivals, joined in 2020 and struggled for years. Then came 2025-26: with no play-offs for the first time, the league table decided the champion, and East Bengal beat Inter Kashi 2-1 on 21 May 2026 at the Kishore Bharati Krirangan in Kolkata, Mohammed Rashid scoring the 72nd-minute winner, to finish level on points with Mohun Bagan and win on goal difference. It was East Bengal's first ISL title and first national league title in 22 years. Youssef Ezzejari won the Golden Boot, Miguel Figueira the Golden Ball and Hrithik Tiwari the Golden Glove.

## Style and tactical trends

ISL football has been shaped by its foreign coaches: Spaniards (Antonio Habas, Sergio Lobera, Carles Cuadrat, Albert Roca) brought [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) and 4-2-3-1 structures; Englishmen and Scots (Owen Coyle, John Gregory) brought [direct play](/tactics/direct-play) and [set pieces](/tactics/set-pieces); Mumbai City's CFG link introduced Guardiola-school build-up. The league allows six foreign players (four on the pitch), one of them Asian, and foreign strikers score most of the goals, which makes [wing play](/tactics/wing-play) and crossing the default.

The pace of play and defensive organisation lag behind the top Asian leagues, but the rise of Indian playmakers and the Kolkata derby's intensity have raised the standard. The 2025-26 points-only format removed the play-off lottery and rewarded consistency.

## Great clubs and dynasties

The ATK-Mohun Bagan lineage has five ISL Cups (three as ATK, two as Mohun Bagan Super Giant) and two Shields. [Mumbai City](/clubs/mumbai-city) have two Cups and two Shields. Chennaiyin have two Cups. [Bengaluru FC](/clubs/bengaluru-fc) have one Cup and the 2016 AFC Cup final. FC Goa and Kerala Blasters, the best-supported club in the league, are the great nearly-teams; East Bengal, the 2025-26 champions, are India's oldest major club with a title at last.

## 2026-27 season storylines

- **A full season again.** The 2026-27 ISL began on 4 September 2026 as a seven-month, home-and-away competition for 14 clubs, the first run under the club-led commercial model agreed with the AIFF in July. The clubs' committee was issuing requests for proposals for a broadcast partner and sponsors through the summer.
- **Governance.** The AIFF's 15-year commercial rights tender, floated in March 2026, produced conflicting reports (one named Genius Sports as highest bidder, another said no takers); the club-led SPV is the working solution for at least two seasons. The National Sports Governance Act now applies to the federation.
- **The Kolkata derby.** East Bengal's title on goal difference over Mohun Bagan sets up the most charged derby season in ISL history.
- **The 14.** East Bengal, Mohun Bagan Super Giant, Mumbai City, Bengaluru FC, FC Goa, Kerala Blasters, Chennaiyin, NorthEast United, Odisha, Punjab FC, Inter Kashi, Sporting Club Delhi and Churchill Brothers, the Goan I-League veterans, make up the division (the live data lists 13; the 14th should be verified).
- **Promotion and relegation** with the I-League remains an AFC requirement that the new model is expected to address.
