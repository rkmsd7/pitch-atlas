## Overview

The Saudi Pro League, officially the Roshn Saudi League, is the top division of Saudi Arabia: 18 clubs, 34 rounds, August to May. The top three qualify for the AFC Champions League Elite, the bottom three are relegated to the First Division League. Since June 2023 the Public Investment Fund has owned 75 percent of the four biggest clubs, [Al Hilal](/clubs/al-hilal), [Al Nassr](/clubs/al-nassr), [Al Ittihad](/clubs/al-ittihad) and [Al Ahli](/clubs/al-ahli), and the league has spent billions on players from Europe: [Cristiano Ronaldo](/players/cristiano-ronaldo), Benzema, Neymar (briefly), Mane, Mitrovic, Ruben Neves, Milinkovic-Savic, Kante, Coman, Mahrez, Joao Felix and Theo Hernandez among them.

Each club may register ten foreign players, with eight in a matchday squad. The project is tied to Vision 2030 and the 2034 World Cup, which Saudi Arabia will host, and the league's ambition is to become a top-five competition by then.

## History and eras

Saudi club football was organised into a national league in 1976. Al Hilal of Riyadh won the first title and have dominated since, their 2022 title taking them to 18 and 2021-22 and 2023-24 adding more. Al Ittihad of Jeddah were the first Asian club to retain the AFC Champions League (2004, 2005); Al Hilal have won it four times, most recently in 2021. [Al Nassr](/clubs/al-nassr) had nine titles, the last in 2018-19, when Ronaldo arrived in January 2023.

The 2023-24 season was Al Hilal's masterpiece: a world-record 34 consecutive wins and the title under Jorge Jesus. Al Ittihad under Laurent Blanc won in 2024-25 with Benzema and Kante. Then, in 2025-26, Al Nassr finally delivered. They beat Damac 4-1 at Al-Awwal Park on the final day, 21 May 2026, with a Ronaldo brace and goals from Coman and Mane, to finish on 86 points, two clear of Al Hilal, who were runners-up for the 17th time. It was Al Nassr's tenth title and Ronaldo's first league championship in Saudi Arabia, at the age of 41; he finished with 28 league goals.

## Style and tactical trends

The league's football is a hybrid. The big four play European systems under European coaches: Jorge Jesus' attacking 4-3-3 and later Simone Inzaghi's [back three](/tactics/back-three) at Al Hilal, Blanc's [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) at Al Ittihad, Matthias Jaissle's Red Bull pressing at Al Ahli (before he left for Newcastle in August 2026) and Jorge Jesus again at Al Nassr. The smaller clubs, with fewer foreign stars, sit deep and counter, so the league table is often decided by who breaks down the most [low blocks](/tactics/low-block). The heat means matches are played at night and tempos drop in the second half, which favours [set pieces](/tactics/set-pieces) and individual quality.

Ronaldo's effect on the league has been to make the penalty box the centre of attention: Al Nassr's whole structure was built to deliver crosses and cut-backs to him.

## Great clubs and dynasties

[Al Hilal](/clubs/al-hilal) have 19 titles, four AFC Champions Leagues and were the first Saudi side to reach a Club World Cup quarter-final (2025, beating Manchester City). [Al Nassr](/clubs/al-nassr) have 10. [Al Ittihad](/clubs/al-ittihad) have 10 and two Asian titles. [Al Ahli](/clubs/al-ahli) have three and the 2025 AFC Champions League Elite. Al Shabab (6), Al Ettifaq (2) and Al Fateh (2017) are the others.

## 2026-27 season storylines

- **Al Hilal's response.** After losing the title by two points, Al Hilal started 2026-27 fastest: a 6-0 win over Al Taawoun in September put them top, with Al Ittihad second and Al Nassr third after a 1-1 draw with Al Khaleej. A later snapshot had Al Hilal leading with 27 points from ten matches, though this could not be verified for November.
- **Ronaldo at 41.** The champions' captain, contracted through 2027, remains the league's face; whether he can repeat 28 goals is the obvious question.
- **Coaching changes.** Jaissle's departure from Al Ahli to Newcastle was the summer's most notable exit; the 2026-27 benches at the big four should be checked against club sites.
- **New names.** Neom SC, the club of the planned megacity, and Al Diriyah are in the 18 for 2026-27 after promotion, alongside Al Qadsiah, backed by Aramco.
- **2034 and the AFC.** With the World Cup eight years away, the league's AFC Champions League Elite performances are the main measure of progress.
