## Overview

The UEFA Champions League is the continent's premier club competition and, since 2024-25, a league as much as a cup. Thirty-six clubs play eight matches each against eight different opponents (four at home, four away) in a single table, the "league phase", from September to January. The top eight go straight to the round of 16; the clubs finishing ninth to 24th play two-legged play-offs for the remaining eight places; the bottom twelve are out, with no drop into the Europa League. From the last 16 the competition is a conventional two-legged knockout to a one-off final.

Qualification comes through domestic leagues (the top five from England and, depending on the UEFA coefficient, Spain or Italy; four each from Germany and France) plus champions of smaller leagues through qualifying rounds. The prize money, more than 2.5 billion euros a season, makes the league phase the central economic fact of European club football.

## History and eras

The European Cup began in 1955-56, an idea of the French newspaper L'Equipe, and [Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid) won the first five with Di Stefano and Puskas. [Benfica](/clubs/benfica) (1961, 1962), [Inter Milan](/clubs/inter-milan)'s [catenaccio](/tactics/catenaccio) (1964, 1965), [Celtic](/clubs/celtic)'s Lisbon Lions (1967) and [Manchester United](/clubs/manchester-united) (1968) followed. The 1970s were the era of [total football](/tactics/total-football) at [Ajax](/clubs/ajax) (1971-73), then [Bayern Munich](/clubs/bayern-munich) (1974-76) and the English years, [Liverpool](/clubs/liverpool) and Nottingham Forest winning seven of eight between 1977 and 1984.

The Champions League rebrand of 1992 brought group stages and television money. [AC Milan](/clubs/ac-milan) of Sacchi and Capello, [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona)'s Dream Team, Juventus, Dortmund and Manchester United's 1999 treble filled the decade. The 2000s produced Real Madrid's Galacticos, Porto's 2004 shock under [Jose Mourinho](/managers/jose-mourinho), Liverpool's Istanbul comeback (2005) and [Pep Guardiola](/managers/pep-guardiola)'s Barcelona (2009, 2011). Real Madrid then won six in ten years (2014, 2016-18, 2022, 2024), [Carlo Ancelotti](/managers/carlo-ancelotti) and [Zinedine Zidane](/managers/zinedine-zidane-manager) sharing them. [Manchester City](/clubs/manchester-city) completed their treble in 2023.

[Luis Enrique](/managers/luis-enrique)'s [Paris Saint-Germain](/clubs/paris-saint-germain) won the first league-phase edition in 2025 with a 5-0 final against Inter, then retained the trophy on 30 May 2026 in Budapest: 1-1 against [Arsenal](/clubs/arsenal) (Havertz 6', Dembele penalty) and 4-3 on penalties.

## Style and tactical trends

The Champions League rewards control and punishes naivety. The great winners of the 2010s were counter-punchers, Ancelotti's Madrid with [Vinicius Junior](/players/vinicius-junior) running the channels; the winners of the 2020s have been pressing possession teams, City's [box midfield](/tactics/box-midfield) and PSG's [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) with aggressive [pressing traps](/tactics/pressing-traps). Knockout football still rewards the [low block](/tactics/low-block) in a tie's second leg and makes [set pieces](/tactics/set-pieces) decisive: the 2026 final's only open-play moment of note was Havertz's early header from a dead ball.

The league phase has changed preparation. Eight different opponents in four months mean coaches cannot scout a group in depth, and squads rotate more. Clubs that finish in the top eight save two matches and three weeks, which has become its own competitive advantage.

## Great clubs and dynasties

[Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid) lead with 15 titles. [AC Milan](/clubs/ac-milan) have seven, [Liverpool](/clubs/liverpool) and [Bayern Munich](/clubs/bayern-munich) six, [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona) five, [Ajax](/clubs/ajax) four, [Manchester United](/clubs/manchester-united) and [Inter Milan](/clubs/inter-milan) three, [Benfica](/clubs/benfica), [Juventus](/clubs/juventus), Nottingham Forest, [Porto](/clubs/porto), [Chelsea](/clubs/chelsea) and now [Paris Saint-Germain](/clubs/paris-saint-germain) two. [Celtic](/clubs/celtic), [Feyenoord](/clubs/feyenoord), [Aston Villa](/clubs/aston-villa), [Borussia Dortmund](/clubs/borussia-dortmund), [Marseille](/clubs/marseille) and [Manchester City](/clubs/manchester-city) are among the single winners.

After one round PSG, Bayern and Barcelona shared the lead on three points; as of early November no club had pulled clear and the league table should be checked live.
- **Managers in new chairs.** Mourinho's Real Madrid, [Xabi Alonso](/managers/xabi-alonso)'s Chelsea are absent after a poor 2025-26, [Enzo Maresca](/managers/enzo-maresca)'s City, [Andoni Iraola](/managers/andoni-iraola)'s Liverpool and Michael Carrick's United all debut in the competition this season.
- **PSG's three-peat.** No club has won three in a row since Real Madrid in 2016-18. Luis Enrique, now with three European Cups as a coach, is chasing it without Barcola, sold to Liverpool.
- **The final.** The 2027 final venue and the knockout play-offs in February will decide whether the league phase again produces a winner from the top eight.
