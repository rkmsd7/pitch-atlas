## Overview

The NWSL kicked off in 2013 as the third attempt at a professional women's league in the United States, after the WUSA and WPS both folded. It is the one that lasted. Backed initially by the US, Canadian and Mexican federations, it now runs independently, with club valuations in the hundreds of millions, a national television deal with CBS, ESPN, Prime Video and ION, and expansion fees that reached $110m for the 2026 arrivals, Boston Legacy FC and Denver Summit FC.

The 2026 season was played with 16 teams over 30 rounds. Eight clubs reach the single-elimination play-offs, with quarter-finals on 6-8 November, semi-finals on 13-15 November and the Championship on Saturday 21 November 2026 at Audi Field in Washington, DC. The regular-season winner takes the NWSL Shield.

## History and eras

The early years were defined by the Portland Thorns, who drew crowds of 15,000 when the rest of the league averaged a fraction of that, and by the Western New York Flash / North Carolina Courage franchise, which won three Championships in four years from 2016 to 2019 under Paul Riley. The pandemic Challenge Cup of 2020 kept the league alive; the 2021 abuse scandals, which led to the Sally Yates report and the removal of multiple coaches, forced a reckoning that produced the collective bargaining agreement of 2024, the first in American sport with no draft and full free agency.

The current era belongs to [Gotham FC](/clubs/gotham-fc). Last in 2022, they won the 2023 Championship under Juan Carlos Amorós, the 2025 Concacaf W Champions Cup, the 2025 Championship, beating the [Washington Spirit](/clubs/washington-spirit) in the final, and the 2026 Shield with a club-record 61 points after signing [Sam Kerr](/players/sam-kerr) in June. The [Orlando Pride](/clubs/orlando-pride) of [Marta](/players/marta) and Barbra Banda swept the Shield and Championship in 2024.

## Style and tactical trends

The NWSL is the most athletic league in the world: transitions are faster, pressing is more aggressive and games are more open than in Europe. The best teams, Gotham under Amorós and Washington under Adrián González, now combine that with real possession structure. Ashley Sanchez won the 2026 Golden Boot with 20 goals for North Carolina; [Trinity Rodman](/players/trinity-rodman), re-signed by Washington in January 2026 on a deal worth more than $2m a year, is the league's highest-paid player, and [Sophia Wilson](/players/sophia-smith) returned from maternity leave to score 15 for Portland. The international influx continues: Kerr, Guro Reiten and Ann-Katrin Berger at Gotham, [Lindsey Heaps](/players/lindsey-horan) at Denver, Barbara Bonansea at Utah.

- The expansion sides: Boston and Denver, the latter having signed Heaps and built a new stadium.
- Kerr's assault on the league record books: she became the first player to 80 NWSL regular-season goals in August 2026.
- Preparations for a 2027 season that will be interrupted by the [Women's World Cup](/tournaments/womens-world-cup) in Brazil.
