## Overview

La Liga, officially LaLiga EA Sports, is Spain's top division, played since 1929 by 20 clubs over 38 rounds. The top four go to the [Champions League](/leagues/champions-league) league phase, with a fifth place awarded in seasons when Spain tops the UEFA coefficient ranking, as it did in 2025-26. The bottom three drop to the Segunda Division.

For most of its history the league has been a duopoly, [Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid) and [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona) sharing the vast majority of titles, but [Atletico Madrid](/clubs/atletico-madrid) under [Diego Simeone](/managers/diego-simeone) have broken it twice (2014, 2021) and the league produced the 2026 World Cup winners: [Spain](/nations/spain)'s squad was built on Barcelona's midfield and Real Madrid's, Athletic's and Real Sociedad's players.

## History and eras

Barcelona won the first championship in 1929. Athletic Bilbao dominated the 1930s, Atletico and Barcelona the 1940s and early 1950s. Then Real Madrid, with Alfredo Di Stefano, won the first five European Cups (1956-1960) and set the template of global superclub. The 1970s brought [Johan Cruyff](/players/johan-cruyff) to Barcelona as a player and the 1988-96 Dream Team under Cruyff as manager, four straight titles and the 1992 European Cup. Madrid's Quinta del Buitre won five in a row from 1986 to 1990.

The Galacticos era (Zidane, Figo, Ronaldo, Beckham) gave way to [Pep Guardiola](/managers/pep-guardiola)'s Barcelona (2008-2012), the greatest club side of the century, and then the decade of [Lionel Messi](/players/lionel-messi) against [Cristiano Ronaldo](/players/cristiano-ronaldo), with [Jose Mourinho](/managers/jose-mourinho)'s 100-point Madrid of 2012 and Luis Enrique's 2015 treble. [Zinedine Zidane](/managers/zinedine-zidane-manager) won three straight Champions Leagues from 2016 to 2018; [Carlo Ancelotti](/managers/carlo-ancelotti) added two more in 2022 and 2024.

[Hansi Flick](/managers/hansi-flick) arrived at Barcelona in 2024 and won back-to-back titles (2025, 2026), the club's 28th and 29th, with the teenage [Lamine Yamal](/players/lamine-yamal) as the face of a new generation. At Madrid, [Xabi Alonso](/managers/xabi-alonso)'s first season unravelled and he left mid-season in 2025-26; Mourinho returned on 13 July 2026 on a contract to 2029.

## Style and tactical trends

La Liga is the laboratory of [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) and [tiki-taka](/tactics/tiki-taka). Cruyff's Barcelona, Guardiola's Barcelona and the national team's 2008-2012 treble of tournaments made short passing and the [false nine](/tactics/false-nine) the Spanish identity. Flick added German pressing and the most aggressive [high line](/tactics/high-line) in Europe, catching opponents offside at record rates.

The counter-tradition is just as strong. Mourinho's Madrid of 2010-13 was the fastest [counter-attacking](/tactics/counter-attacking) side ever seen, Simeone's Atletico the definitive [low block](/tactics/low-block), and Ancelotti's Madrid a hybrid of calm possession and devastating transitions through [Vinicius Junior](/players/vinicius-junior). Beyond the big three, the league is tactically rich: Real Sociedad and [Villarreal](/clubs/villarreal) play possession football, Athletic under Ernesto Valverde press with Basque intensity, Girona's 2023-24 Champions League qualification came from a Guardiola-school 4-3-3.

## Great clubs and dynasties

[Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid) have 36 league titles and a record 15 European Cups. [Barcelona](/clubs/barcelona) have 29 and the Messi era of 2008-2021 that produced 10 league titles and four Champions Leagues. [Atletico Madrid](/clubs/atletico-madrid) have 11 and have been a consistent top-three side since 2012. [Athletic Bilbao](/clubs/athletic-bilbao) (8 titles, Basque-only policy, never relegated) and [Valencia](/clubs/valencia) (6, two titles under Rafael Benitez in 2002 and 2004) complete the historic five. [Sevilla](/clubs/sevilla) are the kings of the Europa League with seven; [Real Sociedad](/clubs/real-sociedad), [Real Betis](/clubs/real-betis) and [Villarreal](/clubs/villarreal) are the modern European regulars.

## 2026-27 season storylines

- **Mourinho's return.** Thirteen years after leaving, Mourinho opened the season at the Bernabeu against Real Sociedad with [Kylian Mbappe](/players/kylian-mbappe), Vinicius, [Jude Bellingham](/players/jude-bellingham) and the summer's big signing Yan Diomande (around 125 million euros from RB Leipzig). On matchday 10, 25 October, his Madrid won the Clasico at Camp Nou; Mourinho told his players not to "go over the moon".
- **Barcelona's three-peat bid.** Flick's champions remained the bookmakers' favourites even after the Clasico defeat, with Yamal, [Pedri](/players/pedri), Raphinha and [Pau Cubarsi](/players/pau-cubarsi) all fresh from Spain's World Cup win. Lamine Yamal finished third in the 2026 Ballon d'Or and won a third Kopa Trophy.
- **Atletico and the rest.** Simeone's Atletico, Villarreal, Athletic and Real Betis contest the Champions League places; Osasuna sacked Luis Miguel Ramis on 12 October 2026 after a poor start.
- **World Cup hangover.** Spain's players returned late from the July final in New Jersey; [Rodri](/players/rodri), the Golden Ball winner, came home to Barcelona in August, joining Unai Simon (Golden Glove), Cubarsi and Yamal as La Liga players.
- **Next up.** Valencia host Real Madrid on 8 November; the return Clasico at the Bernabeu comes in the spring.
