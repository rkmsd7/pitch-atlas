## Overview

Ligue 1, officially Ligue 1 McDonald's, is France's top division: 18 clubs since 2023-24, 34 rounds. The top three qualify directly for the [Champions League](/leagues/champions-league) league phase, fourth enters the qualifying rounds, fifth and the cup winners go to the Europa League, the bottom two are relegated and the 16th-placed club plays off against a Ligue 2 side.

The league is a paradox: the best producer of young talent in Europe (Mbappe, Camavinga, Tchouameni, Doue, Barcola, Zaire-Emery all came through French academies) and, since 2011, a one-club championship dominated by Qatari-owned [Paris Saint-Germain](/clubs/paris-saint-germain). PSG's back-to-back Champions League wins in 2025 and 2026 changed the conversation: they are no longer the rich underachievers but the best team in the world.

## History and eras

The professional championship began in 1932. Saint-Etienne won ten titles between 1957 and 1981 and reached the 1976 European Cup final. [Marseille](/clubs/marseille), under Bernard Tapie, won four in a row (1989-92) and became the only French club to win the European Cup in 1993, before a match-fixing scandal sent them down. Nantes, [Monaco](/clubs/monaco) (with Wenger, then Deschamps), Bordeaux and [Lyon](/clubs/lyon), seven straight champions from 2002 to 2008, defined the 1990s and 2000s. [Lille](/clubs/lille) (2011, 2021) and Montpellier (2012) were the last outsiders.

Qatar Sports Investments bought PSG in 2011. With Ibrahimovic, then Neymar and [Kylian Mbappe](/players/kylian-mbappe), then [Lionel Messi](/players/lionel-messi), PSG won eleven titles in thirteen seasons but kept failing in Europe. [Luis Enrique](/managers/luis-enrique), appointed in 2023, rebuilt the club around a young, pressing collective after Mbappe left for Real Madrid in 2024, and won the 2025 Champions League with a 5-0 final against Inter, then retained it in Budapest on 30 May 2026, beating Arsenal 4-3 on penalties after a 1-1 draw. Dembele won the 2025 Ballon d'Or. The 2025-26 Ligue 1 title was PSG's fifth in a row and 14th overall.

## Style and tactical trends

Ligue 1 is a physical, athletic league with open spaces and fast transitions, which is why its wingers and full-backs are so prized abroad. PSG under Luis Enrique play [positional play](/tactics/positional-play) with a [gegenpressing](/tactics/gegenpressing) edge: Vitinha as the pivot, Hakimi as a wide forward, a [false nine](/tactics/false-nine) in Dembele, and coordinated [pressing traps](/tactics/pressing-traps) on the opponent's first pass.

Below PSG, [Marseille](/clubs/marseille) under [Roberto De Zerbi](/managers/roberto-de-zerbi) (2024-26) played the most elaborate build-up in France before his move to Tottenham; [Lyon](/clubs/lyon) under Paulo Fonseca favour possession; [Lille](/clubs/lille), [Monaco](/clubs/monaco) and Brest have built Champions League runs on counter-pressing and [counter-attacking](/tactics/counter-attacking). The French academy system (Clairefontaine, and the clubs' own centres) produces athletes who can play any system, which is the league's real export.

## Great clubs and dynasties

[Paris Saint-Germain](/clubs/paris-saint-germain) have 14 titles and two Champions Leagues. [Marseille](/clubs/marseille) have nine and the 1993 European Cup. Saint-Etienne have ten, all before 1982. [Monaco](/clubs/monaco) have eight, [Lyon](/clubs/lyon) seven, Nantes eight, Bordeaux six. [Lille](/clubs/lille) have four, the last in 2021 under Christophe Galtier.

## 2026-27 season storylines

- **PSG's sixth straight?** Luis Enrique's team began 2026-27 as overwhelming favourites and were again top in the autumn; they beat Le Havre 5-1 on 31 October and hosted promoted Troyes on 7 November. Bradley Barcola left for Liverpool for around 125 million euros, but Doue, Kvaratskhelia and Dembele remained.
- **Mbappe's shadow.** Kylian Mbappe, the 2026 World Cup Golden Boot winner with 10 goals and Ballon d'Or runner-up, plays in Madrid, but every French winger is now measured against him.
- **Zidane's France.** [Zinedine Zidane](/managers/zinedine-zidane-manager) replaced Didier Deschamps as national coach on 28 July 2026 after France lost the World Cup semi-final to Spain and the third-place match 6-4 to England.
- **The chasers.** Marseille without De Zerbi, Lyon under Fonseca, Lille, Monaco and Nice are fighting for the two or three places behind PSG. Troyes are the promoted side making the biggest impression.
- **Europe.** PSG travelled to Villarreal on 3 November and host Roma on 25 November in the Champions League league phase; the Ligue 1 run-in ends on 29 May 2027 with PSG at home to Toulouse.
