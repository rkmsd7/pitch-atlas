## Overview

Liga F is the professional top flight of Spanish women's football, a 16-team league played over 30 rounds between September and May. The top three qualify for the [Champions League](/tournaments/womens-champions-league), the bottom two drop into the Primera Federación. The league was founded as the Liga Nacional in 1988, became the Primera División in 2001 and was reconstituted as the fully professional Liga F in 2022 after a players' strike over minimum wages.

[Barcelona Femení](/clubs/barcelona-women) are the gravitational centre. Their 2025-26 title, an 11th overall and seventh in a row, was won with 87 points, 15 clear of [Real Madrid](/clubs/real-madrid-women); [Clàudia Pina](/players/claudia-pina) took the Golden Boot with 21 goals. Real Sociedad finished third, Tenerife, Atlético Madrid and Granada completed the top six, and Levante and Alhama were relegated.

## History and eras

Before Barcelona there was Levante, champions six times around the turn of the century, and Athletic Bilbao, who won five titles between 2003 and 2016 with an all-Basque squad and drew 48,000 to San Mamés in 2019. Rayo Vallecano won three in a row from 2009 to 2011. Atlético Madrid broke through with three straight titles from 2017 to 2019 and set a world attendance record of 60,739 at the Wanda Metropolitano against Barcelona.

Then Barcelona went professional, hired Lluís Cortés and later Jonatan Giráldez, and the league became a procession. [Alexia Putellas](/players/alexia-putellas) won two Ballons d'Or, [Aitana Bonmatí](/players/aitana-bonmati) three, and the club won four Champions Leagues in six seasons. Real Madrid, who entered the women's game only in 2020 by absorbing CD Tacón, have finished second in five of six seasons but have yet to win a trophy. The 2023 World Cup triumph of a [Spain](/nations/spain-women) side built almost entirely from Liga F players cemented the league's reputation for producing technicians.

## Style and tactical trends

Liga F is the home of [positional play](/tactics/positional-play). Barcelona's 4-3-3 with inverted full-backs and a counter-press set the template; Real Madrid under Pau Quesada, Real Sociedad and Atlético have all adopted possession-based approaches, and the league's referees allow more contact than in England. The weakness is competitive balance and money: Bonmatí herself warned in September 2026 that the gap between Liga F and the English league in wages and infrastructure was widening, and the summer of 2026 saw Putellas, [Salma Paralluelo](/players/salma-paralluelo) and Fridolina Rolfö all leave Barcelona for England and France.

- Real Sociedad and Atlético competing for the third Champions League spot.
- The broadcast and sponsorship renewals that will determine whether Liga F can keep its Spain internationals at home.
- The November 2026 qualifying window, when the league pauses for Spain's friendlies with Chile and Venezuela.
